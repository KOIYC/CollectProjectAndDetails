Clue Me — A daily word game  ## Get the AI to guess the word.  We give you a word. Type clues without using the word itself. The AI guesses as you type.  Five words. 30 seconds each.  How to play+  Full rules +  The game picks your word but doesn’t tell the guessing AI which one it picked. The AI chooses from the word pool using only your clues, and the game checks its answer.  Describe what it is or what it does, without saying the word itself, its parts, or their word forms. The parts to avoid are shown below your clue. Rounds four and five add one extra word to avoid, shown in the same place. No spelling or rhyming clues.  The AI guesses when you pause typing. Keep typing, edit, or clear the box. Only the current text counts. Each word has thirty seconds; skips and timeouts add thirty seconds to your total. If a guess is slow, the clock runs at quarter speed, with up to eight extra seconds per round.  The same five words for everyone, everywhere. Practice gives you fresh sets of five whenever you like.
