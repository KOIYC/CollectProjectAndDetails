## 摘要正文

The idea comes from Chrome Music Lab, which was addictive and satisfying. I felt it could be that fun and easy to put down musical ideas and hear them grow into something more substantial. Simple at first, but with progressively more depth if the player wants to go there. That was the seed, and it spiraled from there into Jam.You start with one instrument, and you write parts on a piano roll. Create your own music or play gigs (levels) to earn coins and buy more gear. Each gig is built around one musical idea, so you pick up some music knowledge as you go. Friends can join your studio with a link and write parts alongside you.In my other Show HNs I've always come with my own code. In this case I didn't write the code, but I'm still proud of the outcome and I hope some of you will enjoy it too.The audio code is Rust/WASM running in a worklet. This is the part I had prior experience in, and it's been fun getting that into a browser. The instruments are decent and the signal chains hold their own.
