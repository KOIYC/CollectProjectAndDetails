So I built a town. What is Tablif?  Tablif gives every launched product its own office in a 3D town. Founders claim their office, change its design, and verify their site to show the office really belongs to them. There is also a game in the town, with seasons and weekly progress.  What did I write in the first part?  Tablif started as a stock market for launches, where people invested virtual money. Then I started collecting launches every day. But a launch was a one-day flame, and we were no different from other launch sites. I started using AI to read products' websites and produce competitor analysis and leads. The results were useful, but the people who came wanted to add their own product to the list, and then they left.  Not getting approval from my payment provider for the lead generation service was a big disappointment. …
