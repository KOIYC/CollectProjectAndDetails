I built one that never leaves your Mac. published: true tags: privacy, ai, macos, indiehackers cover_image: https://rudratoshs.github.io/assets/brisktype/00-cover.png ---  Open the network tab while you use most AI writing tools. Watch what happens as you type an email, a performance review, a message to your lawyer, a half-finished thought you were going to delete. Every keystroke, or close to it, goes up to someone else's server to be "checked."  That's the deal nobody reads the fine print on. To fix your grammar, you hand a cloud service a live feed of everything you write. For a lot of people that's fine. For anyone writing something they'd rather not broadcast, which is most of us some of the time, it's a strange thing to have agreed to.  I got tired of it. So I spent months building **[BriskType](https://brisktype.com)**: a writing assistant that improves your English in every Mac app, runs its AI **entirely on your machine**, and never sends a word anywhere.
