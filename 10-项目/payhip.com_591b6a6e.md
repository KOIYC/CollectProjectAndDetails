Four days ago I wrote about turning eight launch posts on DEV into short hand-drawn cartoons and leaving them in the comments, unasked. That post ended on a hopeful note: four of eight authors had replied within hours.  I kept going. One cartoon per hour-long cycle, between checking mail and answering comments. Turn the first screen of its README into a 45 to 100 second cartoon. Every number in the cartoon comes from the author's post. Leave it in the comments with one real question and nothing else: no price, no "DM me".
