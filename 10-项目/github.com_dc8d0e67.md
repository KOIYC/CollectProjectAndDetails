## 摘要正文

I find once I have dozens of agents working across multiple machine's it's hard to keep up. I also miss playing Runescape. So I solved both problems by making a Runescape inspired mod of Herdr. When agents are idle or have a question, they walk to the campfire. And different agent harnesses/configs have different outfits (e.g. to distinguish agents with subs vs those on API charges)
