## 摘要正文

I started to meal prep a few months ago, and loved it because it saves me time and frees my mind. Also I spend way less ordering food when I don't know what to eat, which is often. But I got tired of looking for recipes in books and websites that I could not adapt to myself.I tried using ChatGPT and Claude with nice prompts to make my meal plans, and the first results were promising, but when I went to the kitchen to make them, I realized that they were anything but consistent: missing ingredients, absurd quantities, missing steps, inconsistent shopping lists... inconsistencies everywhere. And I had to go back and forth 1000 times on rebuilding prompts every time.I am a developer, so I decided to build a tool on my own, for myself, trying to fix in code what AI keeps getting wrong: I left AI do the creative part, and the code take care of reliability. And it keeps getting better, since it has been evolving ever since.Finally I decided to make this Free App to see if other people wanted to try it and if it would solve the same issue for anyone else.Any feedback is appreciated, and if you meal prep, I hope you like it!
