Published: 2026-07-27 Author: Usmanajmal  ‎Digital Detox Lock: Disconnect App - App Store  # Digital Detox Lock: Disconnect  Focus timeout screen lockdown  Only for iPhone  Free · In‑App Purchases · Designed for iPhone. Not verified for macOS.  Share  iPhone  Discipline is giving yourself no choice. When willpower isn’t enough, lock the apps you can’t quit — and give someone you trust the key. Most app blockers fail for the same reason: they trust you to stop yourself. But research shows people are significantly more likely to break a habit when someone else is involved. Disconnect App is built on that insight. You set the rules. A trusted person holds the key. When you try to unblock a blocked app, they decide — not you. No bypass. No weak moments. Just a system that works when willpower doesn't. HOW IT WORKS Choose the apps you want to block and when. Then pick your lock level: Soft Lock — unblock instantly, but tap through a confirmation first. Enough friction to break the reflex. Medium Lock — wait 5 minutes before access is granted. Enough time to actually reconsider. Hard Lock — you cannot unblock it yourself. Your buddy gets a notification and decides whether to let you in. …
