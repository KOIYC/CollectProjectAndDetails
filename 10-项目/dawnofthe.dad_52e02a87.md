Anarchy Chess Hint Reset board Share result Post score Daily Leaderboard Fewest unattacked squares, fewest hints, then fastest time Options Show threatened squares Show timer Highlight available spaces After placed pieces: How to Play Place every piece from the tray onto the 8×8 board so that no piece attacks any other piece in either direction. Click or drag a piece from the tray onto any square to place it; placing your first piece starts the clock. Drag a placed piece on the board to move or swap it, or click it (or drag it off the board) to return it to the tray. Subtle dots mark empty squares currently threatened by placed pieces; red squares indicate an attack conflict. Stuck? Click Hint (1 per 30s) to place a valid piece, or highlight a misplaced piece if your board has reached a dead end. Daily Puzzles & Leaderboard New Easy , Medium , and Hard daily puzzles unlock every day at 00:00 UTC . Once you solve a puzzle, any unattacked empty squares are highlighted and your solve time locks in immediately. You can keep rearranging pieces to try for 0 unattacked squares (full board coverage)!
