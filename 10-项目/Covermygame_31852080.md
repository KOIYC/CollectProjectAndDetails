coverMyGame - Indie Hackers  # coverMyGame  September 19, 2026 Why I am building coverMyGame  Most platforms that connect game developers with creators ask for a subscriber count or a quality bar before you can use them properly. Those are fair rules that happen to lock out everybody who is starting. This is the one I wanted when I started, and what I am trying to do differently. There are already plenty of places where game developers and creators find each other, and most of them work. I am not here to tell you they are broken. A subscriber count. A channel that has been going a while. On the other side, a game that clears somebody’s quality bar, with a trailer that looks like it had a budget and a store page that looks like a studio wrote it.  None of that is unfair.
