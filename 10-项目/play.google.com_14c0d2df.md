## 摘要正文

I’m currently pricing [Penwright](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.penwright.penwright&pcampaignid=web_share), an offline Android writing app for novelists that is pretty close to release. The core editor, local files and word counts are free (no feature is gated). Paid extras (genre calendars, series linking, specialist exports, aesthetic settings, project limits) unlock with either $1.99USD/month or a one-time $24.99USD.  As a fairly new developer, I'm a bit unsure whether a subscription is hard to justify for a user if the app has no cloud sync features? But at the same time, a one-time lifetime payment might be underselling the manuscript writing capabilities of the app.  I'm not interested in running free trials, but I also don't want to price out some niche demographics I'm targeting (e.g., fan fiction communities). So my question is how do I make this fair? Does anyone here know what the typical monthly vs lifetime ratio is? Am I charging too much?  Thanks for any help!
