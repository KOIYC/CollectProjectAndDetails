## 摘要正文

Hello,  I'm not diagnosed with ADHD, but there are about a hundred projects still needing to be finished or marketed and for years people around me have said I can't focus on one thing.  It's been amplified with Claude code / Chatgpt, where I spend days planning and procrastinating until I burn out with nothing tangible at the end of it. Even if finishing would help me tremendously, I still seem to get stuck for days/weeks on minor things.  Every productivity classics like todo-lists etc lasted few days at most.  I got sick of it, so shipped a web app that break down overwhelming tasks into small doable ones. Honest feedback is welcome. [kapigo.app](https://kapigo.app)
