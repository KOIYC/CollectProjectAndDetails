It's a text expander for Mac: type a short abbreviation like ;sig and it turns into your signature, a reply or a whole email with blanks to fill in, in any app. Where I posted today: Hacker News, X, Indie Hackers and a Reddit comment thread.  The best feedback so far came from a comment on my launch post. Someone pointed out that people only switch text expanders if their existing snippets come over in one click. Textfold can already import from TextExpander, Espanso, Apple text replacements, aText and CSV, but I'd made importing a Pro feature. That's backwards: if you can't bring your snippets over to try it, you never get to the point of buying.
