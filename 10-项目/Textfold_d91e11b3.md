Home Starting Up Case Studies DB Products Ideas DB Vibe Coding Tools Subscribe to IH+ Starting Up Case Studies  Ideas DB Products DB Sign in Join Textfold  Text Expander for Mac Visit Website Textfold Text Expander for Mac  Posts 2  Revenue $0 / mo  Website Twitter October 15, 2026  Day 2: I shipped what the comments on my launch post asked for Yesterday I launched Textfold, a text expander for Mac, and asked for feedback on pricing and switching. The comments were better than I expected, so today's update (1.0.4) is built almost entirely from them.  1. Importing is now free.  Someone pointed out that people only switch if their existing snippets come over in one click. Textfold could already import from TextExpander, Espanso, aText, Apple text replacements and CSV, but I'd made it a Pro feature. That was backwards: if you can't try it with your own snippets, you never get to the point of buying. Now anyone can import everything.  2. A clear screen at snippet 11.  Another comment said the moment that sells Pro is when someone saves snippet 11, so that screen deserves as much care as the landing page. Mine showed a small orange note, and worse, a new snippet could quietly switch off…
