You keep what you've read and what you want to read, on paper or on your Kindle or Kobo. Pecia suggests what comes next and watches the price. It's mainly for second-hand books, in any edition and with shipping included, but it also covers new books and ebooks. For now that means bookshops in Spain.  A few decisions I made early:  Mobile-first. Most reading decisions happen on a phone, in a queue or on the sofa, so Pecia is designed for the phone first.  The library comes first, the price second. The point isn't saving a few euros. It's knowing what you want to read next.  Try it without an account. Look up a book's price right away, with no sign-up.  Free reading tools.
