## 摘要正文

I built a social media scheduler (you don't say), that is actually profitable. (The goal is not MRR talk here.)  It was started pre-AI era though, as now it seems kinda impossible as platforms make approvals more than a month. Anyway to the subject at hand. I am quite happy with the progress so far, but I want to focus more on B2B.  If you're doing B2C and succeeding, **you're a hero**. The churn here is way higher, as I have both B2C and B2B clients, and the latter's churn is maybe less than 2%.  I've achieved almost all my success via SEO, and still focusing harder there. The thing is AI search is way harder, and more "gated" in that niche. How are you getting B2B customers without sales calls?!  I try to avoid calls altogether, as it's quite time-consuming and restrictive for me, but I am starting to think that I won't be able to scale [PostFast](https://postfa.st) otherwise to the point of 6-7 figures.  I really wanna hear how you scale B2B?!
