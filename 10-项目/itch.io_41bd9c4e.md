A little about me first.  I am Kyxoan, if you played Warcraft 3 as a kid (or adult I guess) and played custom games you may remember me and my main passion project "Ice Troll Tribes".  I also created several other games (maps) over the years as a kid.  Kyx's Kings and Knights, Battle Dome, a version of a football game, etc.  I am now working on an idle RPG because I am a big enjoyer of old school style RPGs with really deep systems and a slow burn without huge huge silly numbers like most idle games or ARPGs do now a days.  I have been working on this game for about 6 months now at this point and am adding to it daily, I would say I am about 60-70% finished with the game as of today.  I have let a few close friends play it to try it and pretty much all of them tell me I got something good here... so I decided it is time to let others who have no clue who I am try it and let me know what they think.
