It also did basically nothing, because a thank-you is a dead end. Nobody replies to it, and the person who was half interested just scrolls on.  So now I try to sort comments into three kinds and answer each one differently.  1. "Looks cool" comments get a question back. Not a fake engagement question, a real one. Something like "thanks, what are you using right now for this?" Maybe one in five answers, and that answer is the most useful thing I'll read all day, because it tells me what I'm actually competing with.  2. Feature questions get a straight yes or no first.
