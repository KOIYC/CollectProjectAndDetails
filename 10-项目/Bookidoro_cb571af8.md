Visit Website Bookidoro The micro-reader for busy workdays.  Post 1  Revenue $0 / mo  Website September 29, 2026  Bookidoro Hi IH 👋 I'm Niko.  For years I've had the same problem: I want to read more, but "finding time to read" never happens. What does happen, every 25 minutes, is a break. And I spent those breaks scrolling.  So I built Bookidoro , a tiny Mac menu bar app that turns your work breaks into reading breaks.  How it works  You work. Bookidoro sits quietly in the menu bar. When it's break time, the next page of your book opens in the middle of the screen, with everything else blurred. You read a page (or two), click Done , and you're back to work. It remembers your place, so a book gets finished a page at a time without you ever "making time". The math is what convinced me: a few pages per break, a handful of breaks a day, is a lot of books a year for zero extra time.
