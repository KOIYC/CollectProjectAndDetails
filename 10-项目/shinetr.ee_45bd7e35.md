Programming used to be the fun part. Sit with a problem for a week, get it working at night, the thing is yours. The idea of bootstrapping your SaaS and breaking out in the industry used to be exciting, but now everything is a prompt and can be built by anyone, in a weekend.What nobody wants to do is keep it running and maintain it. I think big SaaS will survive, but a small SaaS business is hard to sell. I know building is cheap, maintaining is the actual work, and the part I decided to take on.Since the end of July I have been building this alone. Consultants, Coaches, tutors, I know are selling sessions with linktree + calendly + stripe link or "dm me on discord", and also struggle using discord as the place to host the session, like Google Meet or Zoom. Which means making a voice channel by hand for every call. Now it's one page: people pick a time, pay if it's a paid session, and the call link is made for them on discord, zoom or google, sent in the email.I built a discord bot that turns discord into a meeting platform like Zoom or Google Meet.
