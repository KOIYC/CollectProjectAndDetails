Works fully offline  Know exactly how many days your supplies will last .  Track your emergency supplies, bug-out bags, and everyday pantry in one place. Open PPantry No account needed · Free forever Know how long you’ll last  See food and water duration in days based on your household. Start-from-templates for bug-out, vehicle, and first-aid kits. Never miss an expiration  Configurable reminders for food, meds, and batteries. A rotation queue shows what to use next before it expires.
