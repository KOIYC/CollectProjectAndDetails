## 摘要正文

I started building Aggregate Finance a couple of years ago because I was manually updating a Google Sheet to track my credit card statements, monthly recurring transactions, and spending habits. This seemed perfect for automation as I was having to log in to six separate banking apps to pull this information together.My original intention was to use this tool locally, but I figured it would be an excellent learning opportunity to understand how to scale the application for others to use. The core functionality of Aggregate Finance revolves around syncing banks and brokerages through Plaid to create visuals and data analytics in Google Sheets and OneDrive/Excel, but I've also added Stripe for payments, passkey and TOTP support for MFA (wanted to better learn how WebAuthn works, mostly), and Resend for emailing account updates.The first question you're likely thinking is: isn't this just reinventing the wheel?
