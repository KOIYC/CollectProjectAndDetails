## 摘要正文

When my partner got pregnant all the apps I tried was written for her. I wanted something that told me what was going on that week and what I could actually do about it.  So I built **ExpectDad**. It’s free on iPhone (US only for now):  * **This week:** her week in a few lines, the baby’s size (eggplant, pumpkin…), and three small things you can do today, picked for how she’s feeling. “Ten-minute back rub, because she has lower back pain.” * **Visit guides:** what each appointment is, what to bring, questions to ask, and somewhere to note what you learned. * **Gear:** what you actually need and by when, with picks at three budgets. * **Dad’s Corner:** a private space to check in with yourself and plan money and leave.  No ads. Your data is yours, and you can delete it any time.  **What I’m asking:** try it for a week and tell me honestly:  * What’s useful, and what’s noise? * What did you go looking for and not find? * Anything confusing, broken, or a bit cringe? * What else would be useful ?
