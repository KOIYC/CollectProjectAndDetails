## 摘要正文

I run a small site that sells shipping labels (USPS, UPS, FedEx) to people who sell on eBay, Etsy and similar. You type what you're shipping, it works out the box and weight, shows prices, and you buy the label. We make a small fee on each label.  There's also a paid plan. The problem: during beta I made everything free for everyone, so today the paid plan adds only one thing, which is covering surprise carrier adjustments up to a monthly limit. That's real value, but it's insurance, and people don't pay monthly for insurance they rarely need.  The obvious move is to take features away from the free tier. I don't want to: the free calculator is how people find us, and the biggest competitor in this space is completely free.  What I'm weighing:  \- no per-label fee on the paid plan, so it pays for itself for anyone shipping more than a handful a month  \- buying labels for all open eBay orders in one click  \- a yearly shipping-expense export for taxes  \- scheduled carrier pickups  For those of you whose free tier is "good enough": what did you put behind the paywall that people actually paid for?
