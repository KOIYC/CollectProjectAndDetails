## 摘要正文

2 popular Indie Hackers built these websites:  [https://canivibecodeit.com/](https://canivibecodeit.com/) [https://trustmrr.com/](https://trustmrr.com/)  And they literally started them as side projects.  I think the ideas behind both are genius. Not because they’re incredibly complicated, but because they’re original.  They solved problems that most people didn’t even realize they had until these websites existed.  That got me thinking:  How do you actually come up with an idea like this?  Not another AI wrapper. Not another SaaS that already has 50 competitors.  How do you find an idea that feels genuinely original, where people see it and think:  I didn’t even know I needed this.  For those of you who have built something like this, how did you come up with the idea?
