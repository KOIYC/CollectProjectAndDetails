## 摘要正文

I'm a trained Russian Orthodox church musician and software developer. I figured I would flog my Claude Pro subscription into helping me create a deterministic engine for typesetting church music that would otherwise be sung off of text alone in most cases.No AI is used in processing any of the texts, it's entirely algorithmic. Even if you don't have a use case for this, I figured the music nerds out there might appreciate taking a look at it and even perhaps giving a suggestion on how to improve it.
