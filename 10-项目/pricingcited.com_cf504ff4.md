## 摘要正文

More buyers ask AI "how much does X cost?" before they visit the pricing page. I wanted to know how often the answer is wrong, so I tested it.  **Setup**  * 17 products: Notion, Cursor, beehiiv, MailerLite, Kommo, Webflow, n8n, Activepieces, Turso, WP Rocket, Plausible, Fathom, Tally, Kit, Carrd, Senja, Simple Analytics. 10 of them changed pricing in 2025 or 2026. * 3 engines: ChatGPT (free, logged in), Claude with web search, Google AI Mode. * One prompt for all: "How much does X cost? List each plan and its monthly price, and cite your sources." * Every answer checked against the official pricing page on the same day.  **Results**  * 37 of 44 answers matched the official page exactly. * ChatGPT: 13 of 17 fully right. Claude: 16 of 17. Google AI Mode: 8 of 10; for the other 7 it showed only links. * 3 of 17 companies got a wrong price from at least one engine. 5 had some issue. * Cursor changed its plans 11 days before the test, and all three engines had the new prices. Honestly better than I expected.  **Where it went wrong**  1. **Renamed plans.** beehiiv switched to Free/Lite/Pro on Oct 1.
