Social Reps: block Instagram until you do your push-ups  # Earn your scroll.  Your feeds start every day locked. Ten push-ups opens them. The camera counts, so there's nothing to negotiate.  One email the day it drops. Nothing else.  The camera runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, ever.  ## You don't have a willpower problem. You have a friction problem.  Instagram is one thumb-tap away, all day, built by hundreds of people whose job is keeping you there. "Just be disciplined" was never a fair fight — the feed is free and instant. So we made it cost something.  ### Lock your feeds  Pick the apps that eat your day — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X. They start every morning closed.  ### Do your reps  Prop the phone up and start. It counts push-ups, sit-ups and burpees on its own.  ### They open  Hit the day's goal and your feeds unlock until tomorrow. Miss it and the lock holds.  ## Don't take my word for it.  This is the exact rep counter from the app, running in this page. No install, no signup, no account. Do a few push-ups and watch it count.  Turn on my camera  Loads about 19 MB, once.
