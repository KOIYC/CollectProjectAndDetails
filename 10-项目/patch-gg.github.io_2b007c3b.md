# pixelfx.  **<!-- count -->244<!-- /count --> pixel art effects and animations, every one a small piece of code.** Fire, explosions, magic, weather, UI, pickups, critters and more. Play them live, recolor and mirror them, drop them into a browser game, or export sprite sheets for any engine. Plus a Claude Code skill that makes new ones the way a picky pixel artist would: draft, render, look, refine.  Every effect that involves a character is **acted out**: he winds up, lets go, freezes on contact and recovers, and a target takes the hit. Twelve whole moves chain the effects into attacks from wind-up to recovery.  **[open the gallery](https://patch-gg.github.io/pixelfx/)** · [catalog](docs/CATALOG.md) · [acting](#acting) · [the skill](#the-skill) · [make your own](#make-your-own) · [how it works](#how-it-works)  The method is [Hugo Duprez](https://x.com/HugoDuprez)'s, from [How to make (good) pixel art effects with Claude](https://www.spritefusion.com/blog/how-to-make-good-pixel-art-effects-with-claude): code is clay, trust your eyes, feed your references.
