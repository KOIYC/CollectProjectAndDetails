## 摘要正文

My name is Raphael, and I'm the founder of Their First Words LLC, where we make apps for our daughters. My mom took me to a typing class when I was a kid, and that laid the foundation for me in my adult years. I think everyone deserves to learn to type. We made Their First Typing thinking of our daughters, who we are raising speaking in multiple languages. It starts with individual keys and grows into useful words, messages, and connected text in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Tagalog, and Italian. It runs in the browser with a physical keyboard. Every course has instructions in English and the local language. It is completely free and ad-free, and no account is needed. Website: [https://theirfirstwords.org/typing/](https://theirfirstwords.org/typing/) 38-second overview: [https://youtu.be/vL9xrQggD7g](https://youtu.be/vL9xrQggD7g)
