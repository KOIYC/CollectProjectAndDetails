## 摘要正文

Hi HN! I'm Matthew, a software engineer tired of paid budgeting apps doing fuzzy averages and making me categorize every card swipe.Example: which category is Rent on the 1st? Which category is my $114 "Valuemart" purchase last week? It doesn't matter IMO. What matters is that Rent repeats every 1st, I have $218 left for spending until Friday, and I can contribute $500 to my Roth IRA next paycheck and have $2,000 left in that account.Bills just cost what they cost, and I need to see exactly when they will come out and how much money that leaves me on that day.For spending, I just move a hunk of cash to a separate "safe to spend" account every Friday and try not to blow through it. This means I can see exactly when spending money will come out too, and how much money that leaves me. I might spend more on gas this week and more for the party next week; there's no 'normal' week. It sounds simple, but it took me years of trial and error to find this system that consistently works for me and make a halfway-decent app to track it.Cashcraft is free, and doesn't require an account to start locally.
