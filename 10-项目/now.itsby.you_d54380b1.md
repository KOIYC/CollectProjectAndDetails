## 摘要正文

Hi HN! "Now It's by You" is a folk song passed between people and computers exploring music's ability to take on a life of its own, in verse and by embedding the software that sings it. Every copy is both an audio file and the same program that synthesized the song's instruments and vocals.Run it to get a new version, shaped by your computer and the season. The singing voice is a tiny 0.9M param model trained on other synthetic inputs and then fine-tuned on the vocal takes of a few volunteer singers. You can open the song's embedded studio app to change the words, melody, style, and mix. Or sing along yourself, and a bit of your timing and inflection can influence the voice of future versions of the song.It's released to the public domain, and I'd love to hear to what others can do with it!
