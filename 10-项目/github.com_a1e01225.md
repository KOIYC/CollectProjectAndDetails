## 摘要正文

First day on Bourbon Street, and my sister and I had the most important question of the trip: where are we going to eat?  It should have been easy. Between the two of us, we had added sixty-eight restaurants, bars, cafés, and attractions to [Le Voyage](https://github.com/earlgreyhot1701D/le-voyage), a travel-planning app I vibecoded the better part of a year earlier. We were standing in the middle of the French Quarter, surrounded by food, carrying a small database of places we already wanted to try, and we were still hungry and indecisive.  So we opened the app.  Le Voyage started as something I built for a trip to Paris. The idea was simple. Instead of sending restaurant links back and forth, saving places in three different apps, losing recommendations in group chats, and trying to remember why somebody added a particular café three weeks ago, we could keep the whole trip in one shared place. Everyone sees the same restaurants, attractions, neighborhoods, notes, maps, and itinerary. It runs as a hosted web app with a free database behind it, and for the handful of people I travel with, it costs essentially nothing.  I built it for Paris.
