Ms. Penrose in the Library by forgottenmachine  # Ms. Penrose in the Library  A downloadable ebook reader for Windows and Android  Ms. Penrose in the Library is an e-book reader because I was looking for a custom e-book reader that would allow me to:  - build a library and fill it up visually (the bookshelves get populated as you add books) - journal in your books. Whenever I read books, I have very different experiences with them. It is a combination of when you read a book, what state of mind you're in etc. and so the ability to take notes, and make a journal per book you read - all your notes are stored in plain text and are exportable at any time, any where - use RSVP to "get into" a book without any distractions.
