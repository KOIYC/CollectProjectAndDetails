## 摘要正文

Hey there! We're making a 2D narrative cozy-horror adventure game "Vale's Echo". Last time I posted 6 months ago when we had 8k wl and said that we should have 20k by September. Looks like the game exceeded my guess, which I’m really happy about. So I will cover what happened in these past 6 months. Well, major news, that we got invited to the Telltale's Choice Matter Festival. More about it in festivals breakdown chapter below. If you’re interested in checking the previous parts, I will leave links to them at the end.  [Every higher step is mostly a festival\/showcase](https://preview.redd.it/zfjg1id2cqmh1.png?width=868&format=png&auto=webp&s=cdeaafbc616864727d8b2745f19ed509bb0eda47)  Because we’re making a narrative game and the demo is basically chapter 1 of the game, the development until now was quite linear - we just made level after level. Now, because the demo is done, we want to make a framework of the complete game and then polish each level from there. So we’re trying to block out the full game until the end. We think it would give us a better understanding of the scope, which parts we should pay extra attention to, and if we’re missing something that needs to be added. …
