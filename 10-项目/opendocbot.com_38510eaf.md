Use the models and keys you already have , without another license.  By OpenDocBot · November 2, 2026  Back to all posts  Most organizations already pay for AI. They hold API keys with a model provider, a contract with a cloud platform, or a local model running on their own servers. When the work happens in Word, Excel or PowerPoint, though, the AI feature usually arrives as another per-user license , tied to one model and one vendor.  Today we are releasing OpenDocBot, an open interface for agentic Office . It runs in the Office task pane, connects to the models you already use, and adds no per-seat fee . You bring your own models and keys, you choose where it runs, and you can change providers without changing how your team works. OpenDocBot is source-available . You can read the code that handles your documents, run it on your own infrastructure, and connect the keys you already have.
