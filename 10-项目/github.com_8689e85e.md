## 摘要正文

Lucas Larroche declared on Oct 4, 2026 that he was archiving PicoCSS and it really upset me. Here was a library that I promoted, used in several websites, and really saw as the future of minimalistic web development.Starting last month sometime, I decided that I would rewrite the library to not only address some of the issues Lucas outlined such as using OKLCH colors, cascade layers, and the Popover API, but to also make some architectural decisions that I didn't agree. Last week, I finally pushed everything up on GitHub. There is a full demo on jsfiddle too: https://jsfiddle.net/bLpsf2jd/The name I decided to go with is T'Pico, which is a double entendre not only representing the minimalistic nature of Pico's original vision but also as a play on the Cajun norm of putting "T" in front of people's names (like father "Bob" and son "T'Bob"); in a sense T'Pico is a child born out of PicoCSS.T'Pico wasn't a one-to-one rewrite of PicoCSS, it is a complete rewrite, not a drop-in replacement. Outside of adding a few new components such as the offcanvas, classless version of the dropdown component, the toolbar component, etc., there were some things I left out entirely.
