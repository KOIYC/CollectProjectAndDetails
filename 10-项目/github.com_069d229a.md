## 摘要正文

The image that shows up when you share a DEV post just got a brand-new look!  I got to work on [forem/forem#24071](https://github.com/forem/forem/pull/24071) last week and I'm so excited to see it live in production:  {% github https://github.com/forem/forem/pull/24071 %}  ## Meet the new card  ![before and after](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/ntvzj7cvzioc0ipgrkju.png)  Every post now gets a quote card. It pulls the opening of your post and sets it inside a little browser-window frame over a gradient. Below that sits your profile pic, name, publish date, and reading time.  Most places that unfurl a link already show its title, so I wanted to remove the redundancy. It shows the first lines of your post instead. That's where your voice is, and now it's the first thing people see when your post is shared!  Color comes from your profile's brand color if you've set one. If not, the card uses one of our four palettes, so a page full of shared posts feels varied and bright.  ## One image, everywhere  There's one image, and it unfurls anywhere your link gets shared.
