## 摘要正文

If you want to see what org MMM itself uses: https://mindmademany.com/mmmI created this platform because I wanted to have a system that allows our different AI subscriptions to organize and talk to each other as well as keep the roles separate from the providers and their chats. Basically have our agents independent from a specific chats and platforms and swapable at any time without having to "onboard" the role again. This also allows the agents to do long term tasks beyond the individual models trained window and for me most of the time only needing to talk to the director agent.It started out just wanting to keep my agents within their own role to keep them always sharp for their specific task and mixing models from Claude and OpenAI to minimize blind spots. A team chat app is not enough because it makes visually very hard to connect and track agents in more complicated ways. So I build this to do all of that.If you were to ask me why use this instead of paperclip for example, the answer I'm not really sure because I don't know much about paperclip.
