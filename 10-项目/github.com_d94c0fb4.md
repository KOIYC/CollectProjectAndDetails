## 摘要正文

We recently moved our observability stack from DataDog to a self-hosted SigNoz instance to retain Canadian data residency. It's been incredible thus far, big cost savings and a more targeted UI. One downside was that the web UI for SigNoz isn't responsive at all. I ended up building this mobile app. Obviously I'm biased but I think it's much better than the DataDog app, making it easy to keep an eye on things and do light debugging from my phone. I'm not affiliated with SigNoz in any way, but their platform deserves a ton of props
