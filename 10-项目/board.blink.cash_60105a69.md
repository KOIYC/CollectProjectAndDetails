## 摘要正文

Hi everyone,Board is a way to create, deploy, and fund a trading bot in 10 seconds. My co-founders and I built Board out of our own necessity. Every time we had a new trading strategy, we would need to manually input it into our broker accounts and monitor the situation ourselves.We looked at all of our trading strategies over the past 3 years and added everything to the board that would be required to rebuild them from scratch. Now, when we have a new strategy, we chat with AI to create the strategy. We review the board to confirm that it matches what we expect, and we deploy it. We don't have to worry about setting up webhooks for signals or keeping it running on our computer locally.The whole system is built on top of tokenized stocks and commodities. You always have access to withdraw your funds.
