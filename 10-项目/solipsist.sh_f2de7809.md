## 摘要正文

Hi HN, since some time I have been experimenting with a new way to work with AI agents, instead of chatting with them in the usual sense, I wanted to work with them collaboratively (like people usually do in google docs or confluence).I think the chat based interface we use for these coding agents doesn't extend well to other use cases, like researching, thinking... or just having a general introspective session with AI.I thought of having a simple markdown editor (like Notion), where you can just dump your thoughts (or documents) and then the AI agents can work off of them. The AI agents can give back nicely formatted tables, images and other content.
