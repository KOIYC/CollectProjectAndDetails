Journey Jotter: Travel journaling in 3 minutes using your | BetaList  ## Travel journaling in 3 minutes using your own voice  Most travelers want to document their trips, but sitting down to journal every day takes time. Miss a day or two, and you're a week behind. Photos and travel apps are great for remembering where you went, but not what happened. Journey Jotter lets you talk or type about your day, answers with thoughtful prompts, then turns it into a beautiful journal written in your own voice, all in just a few minutes.
