## 摘要正文

I started this because I kept trying planners and eventually abandoning them.  Super Productivity, Vikunja, Planify, TickTick — some of them were genuinely good. But I kept wanting the same thing: I didn't just want to plan tomorrow. I wanted to come back a week later and understand what actually happened.  So I started building Personal Planner.  The basic loop became:  plan the day → do the work → track the real time → review what happened → use that when planning again.  It now has an hour-by-hour Day view, timers that keep planned and actual time separately, daily and weekly reviews, a year-long consistency view, Experiments for trying routines for a few weeks, and an Inbox for things I want to schedule later.  Everything works locally first, so normal use doesn't depend on a server. Sync is optional.  The funny part is that the planner UI wasn't really the hard part. Sync was.  Offline changes, reconnecting, migrations, auth and getting two devices to agree took far more work than I expected.
