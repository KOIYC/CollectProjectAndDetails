## 摘要正文

I've been looking for a Yelp-style place to check out triathlons and marathons before signing up and couldn't find one. The info is out there, it's just scattered across race reports on personal blogs, Slowtwitch threads, review sites and hotel reviews.  So I pulled it together. Right now it covers 1,079 races: 116 full-distance and 221 70.3 triathlons, 164 Olympic, 230 sprint, 297 marathons and 51 ultras, built from about 5,800 reports and reviews. Every race gets a 1-5 score on things like swim, bike, run, conditions and travel, and each review links back to where it came from. You can filter for stuff like flat bike courses, wetsuit-legal swims or first-timer friendly races, and compare up to three side by side.  https://moku.coach/race-ratings/  Full disclosure: it lives on the site for Moku Coach, the training app my cofounder and I are building. The ratings are free and you don't need an account to browse.  Two things I'd love help with:  - If you've raced one of these, leave a review on its page. A lot of races only have a handful of reports, so first-hand reviews help a ton. - If a score looks off for a race you know, tell me. I'd rather fix it than leave it wrong.  Any fee…
