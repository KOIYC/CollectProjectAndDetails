## 摘要正文

*A 1969 Mission Control console for kids. Built in one day for NASA Space Apps 2026.*  > **TL;DR:** For NASA Space Apps 2026 I built Ghost Rovers, a free web app where kids sit at a 1969 Mission Control console and call the 12 machines NASA left on the Moon and Mars. Five Moon machines answer NO SIGNAL, the Apollo 11 Laser Mirror answers, and Mars stays quiet. I directed it in one day, with [Claude](https://claude.ai) planning and checking and [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/) building, and every fact traces back to NASA's own records.  The first commit went in at 10:47 a.m. PST on Saturday: a 15-minute test to see whether my phone could spin a 3D Moon without choking. At 7:32 p.m. I clicked Submit for Judging. In between, the project grew a second planet, a study kit for teachers, a 27-second demo video with Apollo radio beeps, and one correction I'm glad I asked for.  [NASA Space Apps](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/) is NASA's global hackathon, run over one weekend.
