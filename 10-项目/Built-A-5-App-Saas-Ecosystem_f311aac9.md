In construction and estimating, the estimate isn’t the end of the information. Details from that job may also need to reach a schedule, an invoice, or somebody doing the work.  Building the BubbaCode apps has me thinking about those handoffs. A tool might handle its own part well, but someone still ends up copying information into the next one.  That’s the part I’m curious about—not your whole software setup, just one thing you keep entering twice.  What information is it, and which two places does it have to go?  Customer details from an email into a job record? Measurements from your notes into an estimate? Booking dates into a separate calendar?
