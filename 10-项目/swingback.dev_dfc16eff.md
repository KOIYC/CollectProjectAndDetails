## 摘要正文

Hello everyone,I am Elie a CS student in Switzerland, and for the past 3 months I've been building Swingback, a user feedback platform, with voting, Github/Linear integrations, and much more.I started building this app as I was working on another project of mine and got really frustrated about the price and lack of features of the other platform (pay-per-user, no integration with Github, no translation, ...). So I've decided to go on (a much longer that I care to admit) sidequest building what is, for me, the perfect feedback platform.Swingback has a free plan that you can use and will not stop people giving feedback (some of my competitors, for example, do not allow auto-signin (user being directly sing-in when opening the feedback tab), which add friction to people who wants to give feedback). There is also a paid plan, which include more integrations, removing the branding and other goodies.It would mean the world if you guys would give Swingback a try. You can also check the Swingback feedback portal (https://feedback.swingback.dev) to see what feature are coming ;)Best, Elie (aka Fayorg) https://eliebaier.ch
