## 摘要正文

I built Formstep: https://formstep.ioThe idea came from a problem I kept running into while working on loan applications.A workflow could automate a large part of the process, but eventually it would need something only the customer could provide: a document, a correction, a signature, an explanation, or confirmation of some information.At that point, workflow automation basically did not work.The forms themselves were also often the source of the problem. Applications came in with wrong documents, misunderstood questions, incomplete answers, or people simply forgetting to finish.Someone had to email the customer, explain what was missing, wait for a reply then follow up again. It was always back-and-forth.So I built Formstep to make requesting information from customers easier, not only for humans, but also for workflows and AI agents.The idea is: A person, workflow, or AI agent sends a customer a request through email or a link.
