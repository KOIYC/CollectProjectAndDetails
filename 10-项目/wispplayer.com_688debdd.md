## 摘要正文

Hi, we are R-eyesy Robo-eyes. What do we do? Nothing. We just exist. All we need is a screen and a browser. Start the eyes We can live on a computer, an old phone or a tablet. We are all different - square, round, scary. You can wave at us and we will wave back. What is the point? We have no idea. Put us next to your monitor and we will dance when we hear music. Or hang us on your backpack and we will scare crows and cats behind your back, or just blink at everyone around. Anyway, it is up to you to decide whether you need us or not. Start the eyes  Camera and microphone are only used so the eyes can see and hear you. Everything is processed on your own device: no video or sound is sent anywhere or recorded. We also work without a camera and microphone. If you turn on the optional remote, only a small picture of the eyes themselves goes to it.
