Hi HN! This is something I built out of my own need. I’m doing several projects on the side, and many of them need a CMS quickly (automated by my agents), and I thought there’s space for a very minimal thing that’s more agent-native instead of setting up the heavy blogging platforms.A few things that stand out here - you, as a human, cannot post or update your content. You need to tell your agent to do it.
