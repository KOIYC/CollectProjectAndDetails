I'm finally feeling like I've figured out Reddit marketing to drive some traffic to my site. I used to just try to promote my app to various subreddits and hope I went viral. It didn't work and I lost motivation very quickly.  Over the last week, I started tracking everything meticulously and adding structure to my marketing. I now have a spreadsheet where I track my posts to various subs noting how many views each post got and how much traffic my website got as a result for the day.  Here are my stats after the first week:  * 2-3 posts per day (usually 3 but 2 on the weekends when I'm a bit lazier) * 2.3k (worst) to 14.9k (best) views per day. Averaging 7k views per day.    * I only track post views for the first 24 hours. For example, my 14.9k day is closer to 18k now after several days have past. * Avg 121 unique visitors to site per day * 31% bounce rate (pretty good imo)  I'm going to continue this and see if I can get better results.
