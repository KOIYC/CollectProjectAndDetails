Animus AI Blocker - Chrome Web Store  # Animus AI Blocker  Overview  Hide images and videos labeled as AI-generated on supported social websites. Reveal filtered content anytime.  Hide declared AI images and videos on supported social feeds. Animus AI Blocker Early Access replaces matching media with a reversible notice when it finds an explicit generation declaration, trusted synthetic Content Credentials when available, or one of your saved filtering choices. You stay in control: - Select Show once to reveal filtered content. - After revealing a post, select Not AI to save a local correction. - Enable or disable protection for each supported website, or turn it off everywhere. - Optionally hide Reddit posts with an exact AI-generated community flair. This personal rule is off by default and is not verified detection. - Reset local decisions in Settings. This is not a universal AI detector. Unlabeled content may remain visible, and content left visible is not certified authentic. Previews without a supported declaration may remain visible even when the opened post has a label.
