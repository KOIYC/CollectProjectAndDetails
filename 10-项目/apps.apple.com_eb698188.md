## 摘要正文

COOL + ALPHABETS = COOLPHABETSCOOLPHABETS teaches you to read and write in your mother tongue - for kids who are just starting, and adults who never did.Millions of families live outside the country they were born in. While parents can read and write in their native language, their kids - the second generation - can barely do so.Kids often speak their mother tongue fluently because it is the language they use at home. But reading and writing is rarely a "felt need" for them. And kids are unlikely to feel that need unless parents nudge them - preferably at an early age.Many parents genuinely want their kids to be able to read and write in their mother tongue. This usually means a textbook or a weekend language class. Both can feel like a chore.Most language apps teach you to 'speak' a language. COOLPHABETS teaches you to 'read' and 'write'. Reading and writing are a genuinely difficult problem to solve - especially on an app. Getting a child to write letters correctly is the harder part.That's where we built CoolphaSense - a collection of tiny AI models that run entirely on the device and provide instant feedback on handwriting.COOLPHABETS is designed for kids.
