## 摘要正文

Today I'm releasing Viola on macOS, Windows, Linux, and Android, with true/real-time sync available across all of them.  [](https://preview.redd.it/i-build-a-spiritual-successor-to-arc-cross-platform-mac-win-v0-5g3268w0xegh1.png?width=1080&format=png&auto=webp&s=c4dc7f7230151f35709ff8eaf44004b6fc6fb849)  Create a tab, workspace or group on your computer, pick up your Android phone and it’s already there. Supported browser organization and history are end-to-end encrypted before leaving your device. Cookies/logins obviously don't sync.  Viola includes:  \- Workspaces for separating projects  \- Isolated profiles with separate cookies, logins, storage, and extensions  \- Tab Groups (persistent or not!) and side-by-side Splits  \- One shared workspace model across every Viola window  \- Chrome Web Store extensions on desktop, installed per profile (Viola is Chromium-based)  \- A Mini Player that follows you between workspaces (YouTube, Twitch, etc)  \- Synced tabs, pins, groups, splits, bookmarks, history, and compatible extension catalogs  \- Full session recovery  \- In-app persistent notifications  The Android version is probably what I'm most excited about right now.
