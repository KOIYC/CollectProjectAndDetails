ArchLog — Engineering Institutional Memory  # Explore the context behind an engineering decision.  Engineering systems accumulate decisions, compromises, migrations, incidents and discussions. ArchLog connects that history across code, documentation and team conversations into a searchable engineering memory - so the reasoning survives longer than the people who made it.  Free to connect · Read-only by default · Nothing pushed without your approval  Decision Ledger entry  decision Decision  What was actually decided, stated in one line.  reason Reason  Why it made sense at the time - the part version control never keeps.  impact Impact  What changed as a result, in code and in operational behaviour.  risk · confidence Risk & confidence  How risky the change was, and how strongly the evidence supports the reading.  commits · pull requests Provenance  The commits, pull requests, authors and files the entry was derived from.  ## Watch ArchLog turn a workspace’s history into a decision record.
