## 摘要正文

In August I asked here how you find your first testers. Got a lot of good answers, and whoever told me not to start with friends was right.  What happened since:  * shipped the core thing. 3 short lessons a day picked for you * spent like a month making lesson generation faster and cheaper. went from \~4 min to \~2 min per lesson and about half the cost * set a price: $14.99/mo or $129.99/yr, 20 lessons a month. first lesson is free * paying users: 1 )  My biggest mistake was living in “almost ready” mode. I kept polishing instead of showing it to anyone.  Next 2 weeks I have one goal: 20 strangers try it and 5 of them tell me what’s wrong.  If you want to be one of them: [noemacreate.org](http://noemacreate.org), or DM me.  Where did your first 20 users actually come from?
