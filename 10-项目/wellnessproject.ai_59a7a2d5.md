## 摘要正文

One thing I didn’t fully appreciate before building a consumer app is how much the job changes once people actually start using it.  [Wellness Project](https://wellnessproject.ai/) is about six months old. We’re now at **almost 5,000 mobile downloads and signups, over 200 paying subscribers, more than $10K in revenue (32 days into launching subscription payments), around 2,000 daily active users, and 100+ new users a day.**  At this point, the problem isn’t finding something useful to work on. It’s deciding which useful thing matters most.  On any given morning, we could:  * Read reviews and respond to feedback * Check signups, subscriptions, cancellations and revenue * Fix bugs and sync issues * Build the next feature people are asking for * Improve onboarding, conversion and retention * Add another health or workout integration * Make the app faster * Work on SEO and international growth * Run ads and test new acquisition channels * Reach out to users who stopped coming back  The hard part is that almost all of those are good ideas.
