## 摘要正文

https://preview.redd.it/hbym145lfyph1.png?width=1555&format=png&auto=webp&s=22aad2db07ae2b4e05e8f77bc6d12365c161ed0d  Hi everyone.  I had this idea for a few days, to create a cool, immersive medieval looking website where you can learn about history and have fun.  Basically the loop would be something like this:  ***Learn → explore → take a quiz → progress through a roadmap → come back later***  The website could have different kingdoms selector, different eras, wars, inventions, etc.  I uploaded a few images that I made and I would love a feedback. Would you use something like this and why/why not ?  If you want to 'feel' my idea here is the concept as a dummy website: [https://historica-eight.vercel.app/](https://historica-eight.vercel.app/)  Most of the images for this concept are AI generated. For the MVP I would be looking to use real images for rulers/relics and of course I would do a facts double check for potential errors.  Thank you for your feedback !
