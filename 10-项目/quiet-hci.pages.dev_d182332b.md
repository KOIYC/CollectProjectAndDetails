## 摘要正文

I’m Ruggiero, a CS student working on a research project about humanComputer interaction and AI.I built QUIET because I’ve been thinking about a problem with current AI interfaces: they usually either wait for an explicit prompt, or try to take over a task.I wanted to explore a different relationship between humans and AI: an assistant that can understand when to stay out of the way, when to point something out, when to teach, and when to act. I’ve built a small interactive demo of this idea. It’s obviously still very early, and I want to understand whether the interaction itself makes sense before trying to build the actual AI system behind it.The prototype is intentionally not AI-powered yet.The four examples are scripted so I can test the interaction itself before spending time building the underlying AI system.The idea is that the assistant can choose different levels/forms of intervention:* stay silent * point at something * explain / teach * do the taskThe important question for me isn’t whether an AI can perform these actions.
