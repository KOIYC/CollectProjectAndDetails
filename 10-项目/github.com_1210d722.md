## 摘要正文

Let me paint a picture I know you’ve experienced recently.  You’re trying to book a flight, buy concert tickets, or just download a simple PDF converter. Suddenly, the website turns into an absolute warzone:  🚨 *"ONLY 2 SEATS LEFT AT THIS PRICE!"* ⏳ A blood-red countdown timer ticks down from 05:00. 🟢 Three massive, glowing "DOWNLOAD NOW" buttons appear, and you *know* two of them will install malware. 🥺 And when you try to close the popup? The only button says, *"No thanks, I'd rather pay full price and be miserable."*  **We call this "marketing." But let’s be honest: it’s just deceptive engineering.**  These are **Dark Patterns**—UI/UX choices intentionally designed to trick, guilt, or force you into doing things you didn't want to do. And honestly? It’s getting gross. As developers, we build the web. We shouldn't be okay with the web actively manipulating its users.  So, my team at [Tarkash Labs](https://github.com/Tarkash-Labs) decided to fight back. We built a cognitive visual shield to catch these patterns in real-time.
