## 摘要正文

Hey, I'm Matt. This is a free game I built in the spirit of the old internet. Just built for fun and no other reason. I built a version 2-3 years ago by hand. This time I built it assisted by Claude Code.Every day you set a toggle. On/off, dials, etc. Every single one of them follows some piece of public data.The board locks at 23:00 UTC and is scored. You then find out what was right or wrong. On some toggles there's also an indicator if you are hot or cold. The goal is to get them all right :).Hints are given along at checkpoints along the way.I have an open beta beginning November 30th. Would love to get feedback on the concept, what works, what doesn't, etc so that season 1 goes off without too many hitches.
