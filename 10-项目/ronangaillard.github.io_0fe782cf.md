I like giving prints to people I meet, or slipping them into postcards. I also like to travel without my phone as much as I can. But today the phone is the middleman: copy the photo to it, open the instax app, print from there. It works, but it is slow and it kills the fun. Naively, I thought a fairly recent Fujifilm camera would print to it natively. I had even seen the “instax printer print” entry in the menus. It doesn’t. The X-T30 II only knows the old Wi-Fi printers (SP-1, SP-2, SP-3). Newer ones like the Link 3 use a different protocol over Bluetoot…
