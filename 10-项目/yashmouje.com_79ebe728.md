## 摘要正文

Voyager 1 crossed one light-day from Earth today, and it’s still carrying the Golden Record: 115 images, sounds of Earth, greetings in 55 languages, and 90 minutes of music.I made one for myself with twelve things that fascinate me. Side A has things and Side B is ideas. Each entry is a halftone 3D object in WebGL you can drag to turn. And the end of the page shows Voyager’s live distance from Earth.
