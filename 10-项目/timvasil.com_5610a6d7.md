← Tim Vasil ‹ Features  Photos  Apps 41  Remote  Schematics  BOM › Features This is a personal hardware + software project, inspired by TheFlightWall ,  with the goal of pulling as much interesting live data from the internet as possible to showcase on a wide LED display over my living room fireplace.  It started with a simple question, “ What’s that ship passing by in San Diego Bay? ,” but now answers all sorts of questions including “What’s the best time to walk the dog today?” and “What are the trending streaming shows?” I built the display from seven 64 × 64 LED panels, driven by a Raspberry Pi. That gives me 448 × 64 pixels to work with, which is an interesting constraint (and aspect ratio) for 40+ apps!  Nonetheless, I was able to take advantage of a 30 fps frame rate (on a 100+ Hz panel refresh) with full 24-bit color to craft some pretty slick visuals and animations, including map imagery that zooms and pictures of baseball players during live games. The intro splash screen as LumiBridge boots, with sound. The Home screen alternates between app icons and a dashboard of live updates. Let the apps take turns.
