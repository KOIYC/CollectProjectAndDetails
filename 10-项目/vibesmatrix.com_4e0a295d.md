## 摘要正文

About ×  About Vibes Matrix Tools for humans with complex decisions and feelings. Big life decisions often make you feel excited and scared and confused all at the same time. Each path is a ball of different opportunities and risks, so you have conflicting feelings rather than clarity. I created the Vibes Matrix at a moment in my life where I faced a large and complex career decision that had very different paths that would have very different life impacts. I'm a big fan of breaking large unwieldy problems into smaller more discrete problems, and I realised that I needed a way to disambiguate the complex and conflicting feelings I had about the different paths in front of me. Vibes Matrix works by breaking down complex, multifaceted decisions into elements you can tune into the vibe you're feeling about that element. Start by listing the paths you see ahead for a given decision, and all of the criteria you can could think of that would make for an exciting or terrible decision. By choosing an emoji that represents a vibe or feeling, you can vote on the criteria of each path, and gain clarity about how the different parts of an equation make you feel.
