It navigates live information sources (web, slack, drive, wiki, CRM, MCP servers) to assemble context on demand - and builds its own wiki and CRM as it learns about your company.  YC's Summer 2026 RFS named "Company Brain" and "AI Operating System for Companies" — the same idea from two angles: pull knowledge out of fragmented sources and turn it into something AI can act on. The brain is the data layer. The OS runs on top of it. Neither exists as a finished product today, but the pieces do.  Scout stitches them together using patterns that already work: **navigation over search**, **context providers**, agentic SQL, and …
