Learn Piano Easy  # Learn Piano Easy  Watch the notes fall onto the keys, then play them yourself, one song at a time.  - No account - Works offline - No ads  Hi, I'm your piano teacher. Shall we play?  ## The notes fall, and you play  Every song looks like this. Each note falls onto its key, and you press the key as it lands.  Once it plays, press its keys to join in.  ## Learn step by step, or just play  Pick the part that fits your day. You can switch at any time.  Guided course  ### Learn Piano  A guided course of small steps. Each one teaches a skill, and a song puts it to use.  Any song  ### Just Play Piano  Open any song, watch its notes fly towards a 3D piano, and play along.  ## Four steps, again and again  Every lesson follows the same short loop, so you always know what comes next.  1. 1  ### Learn a skill  A short lesson shows one new thing, such as a note, a rhythm or a hand position. 2. 2  ### Practise it  Play it on your own keyboard.
