The setups people actually struggle with:  People with calendars from different organizations: two part-time jobs, a consultant with a client-issued Outlook account, a work and a personal calendar. Each calendar only knows its own meetings.  Microsoft Bookings users. Bookings only checks your default Outlook calendar, so a Google event or a second account doesn't block the slot.
