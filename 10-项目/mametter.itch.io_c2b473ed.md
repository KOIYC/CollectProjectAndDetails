Posting Puzzle by mametter  # Posting Puzzle  A little puzzle about sorting envelopes and stamps. Pair them up to deliver, mix them up to return.  ### How to play  Pushing a tray adds to both rows at once. Matching pairs go in the mailbox, a mismatch comes back. That is the whole rule.  - 1 / 2 / 3: push a tray - Z or Backspace: undo - R: reset - ← / →: change stage  ### About the puzzles  Mr. Post is named after Emil Post, and every stage here is an instance of Post's Correspondence Problem. Twelve in all: the first six are meant to be solved by hand, the last six are hard instances borrowed from research and are not. A small celebration of PCP[3,4] finally being settled.  An earlier version: mame.github.io/posting-puzzle. Font: Special Elite.
