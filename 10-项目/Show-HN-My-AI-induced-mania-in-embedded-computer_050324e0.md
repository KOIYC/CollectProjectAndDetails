## 摘要正文

Author: superchromat  20 years ago on the venerable DailyWTF programming blog, the inner-platform effect anti-pattern was named and shamed. Now, this pattern is an invariable sort of consequence of the collective insanity of enterprise software engineering where the engineers, in the name of hyper-flexibility, end up inventing their own programming language that they put inside the product that theoretically lets end users do whatever they want. Now, this is always a shitty programming language and it's an absolute anti-pattern. And in today's video, I want to tell you how, with the assistance of AI, I too have succumbed to the inner-platform effect and have invented a shader language for Sci-On. So, we've got vertex shaders and glitch shaders. Um for those of you who don't know, Sci-On is my device that makes it really easy to work with high-definition or high-quality video in the analog domain. It makes analog video electronics very approachable. Um and now it has shaders. I don't know why, but it does. Stick around, I'll tell you the story. Like a lot of my distractions, this side quest began with the best of intentions, namely to work on improving the color controls in Sci-On. …
