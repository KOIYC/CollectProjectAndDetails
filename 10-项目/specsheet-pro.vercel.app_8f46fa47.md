## 摘要正文

Hey everyone,  Creating technical specification sheets in Excel or Word often leads to broken layouts, inconsistent formatting, and date ambiguity issues across teams.  I built **SpecSheet Pro** to solve this. It's a lightweight web app that lets you input product parameters, custom tables, and logos, and outputs a clean, standardized PDF ready for production or quality control.  **Key Features:**  * Dynamic table creation for technical parameters (Specs, Methods, Values). * Custom logo support with instant client-side preview. * Configurable date formats (ISO 8601, localized, text-based) to prevent QA ambiguity. * Clean PDF compilation using html2pdf / html2canvas.  The core generator is free to use:[https://specsheet-pro.vercel.app/](https://specsheet-pro.vercel.app/)  I'd love to get your feedback on the UI/UX and any features you think are missing for technical workflows.
