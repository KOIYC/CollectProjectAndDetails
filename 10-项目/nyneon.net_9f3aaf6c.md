Rema 1000 Føtex Netto Lidl Bilka 365 Discount Spar Meny  Compare supermarket offers across Denmark  Find the best supermarket offers in Denmark in one place. This page collects the weekly grocery offers (tilbud) from eight Danish supermarkets: Rema 1000, Netto, Føtex, Lidl, Bilka, 365 Discount, Spar and Meny. Instead of going through eight different tilbudsavis leaflets, search for one product and see which shop has the lowest offer price right now.  The price compare tool works in English, so you don’t need to read Danish. Search for “eggs”, “coffee” or “chicken” and it finds the Danish product names for you. Prices are updated every night.  How to find the cheapest groceries in Denmark  - Compare the unit price, not the pack price. Look at kr./kg, kr./l or kr./stk. A bigger pack on offer is not always the better deal. - Check offers before you shop.
