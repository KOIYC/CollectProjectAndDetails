I've had plenty of ideas over the years that I never actually brought to life.  Recently, I decided to follow one all the way through. I built a conversational AI called **From Idea to Product**, designed to help people explore their ideas, develop them, and work toward creating tangible products.  It's now published and available for $9.  One of the biggest lessons from building it was how much you can only discover by actually doing the work. I encountered questions and problems that hadn't even occurred to me while I was still thinking about the idea.  Now I'm discovering something else.  Finishing a product and finding people who might want it are two entirely different challenges.  I've published an article about the experience, started participating in conversations with other creators, and recently began introducing the product directly in relevant communities.  I haven't made my first sale yet.
