Yogurt Private AI meeting notes, with no bot in your call.A free, open-source alternative to Granola, Otter and Fireflies.  Website  · GitHub  ## Introduction  Yogurt records your microphone and call audio, then transcribes and summarizes on your Mac, Windows or Linux computer. It names speakers from Zoom, Teams and Meet and answers questions across meetings with sources.  ## Why  Our conversations are our business. We want our meeting notes on our own computers, with no account to create. That's why we built Yogurt to record, transcribe and summarize locally. It goes online only to download models and check for updates.  ## Download  Yogurt is free for [Mac](https://github.com/FrigadeHQ/yogurt/releases/latest/download/Yogurt-mac-arm64.dmg), [Windows](https://github.com/FrigadeHQ/yogurt/releases/latest/download/Yogurt-windows-x64.exe) and [Linux](https://github.com/FrigadeHQ/yogurt/releases/latest/download/Yogurt-linux-x86_64.AppImage).
