## 摘要正文

I've always found it fun to explore the history of Chinese characters.Some modern characters are unrecognizable from what they represent, while others still look similar to the versions carved in turtle shells over 3,000 years ago!I built a Heardle-inspired game where you guess the meaning of a Chinese character as it gradually transforms back through time to a more pictographic version. Let me know what you think.
