## 摘要正文

Most websites cater to one side of the reading community, either the readers or the authors. Rising Hat is a bridge between the two.Readers can come, find books, rate, discuss, start forums, look for authors. Authors can claim authorship, submit unlaunched books, bring in their audience for marketing, directly interact with readers in forums, share their thoughts.The website currently contains 15000 books, and new books are added every week.
