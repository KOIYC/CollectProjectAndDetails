## 摘要正文

**The viral tweet:** [x.com/gathint/status/2111379569030939102](https://x.com/gathint/status/2111379569030939102)  On 17 October 2026 I posted a before and after of my parents' TV, cleaned up by Claude Code over Wi-Fi. It reached 7.4M views, people started doing the same to their own TVs, and their replies turned into the lists here: 170+ TV models from 200+ contributors in four days. The repo was called tv-vibehack until the 19th; old links work.  **Themes, switches and add-ons:** [cleanyour.tv/addons](https://cleanyour.tv/addons)  **Official links:** the website is [cleanyour.tv](https://cleanyour.tv) and the code, launcher and tools live only in this repo.
