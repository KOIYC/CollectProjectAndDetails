Please retry later.  # Sinabis Green Energy – Synthetic Email Dataset  More than 10 million emails and 1.4 million calendar files from the everyday business of a fictional renewable-energy group. They span ten and a half years, six countries and 29 languages, and they include real attachments. Every person, company, authority, law and event is made up. The content was written by large language models, and the `mailgen` generator built the structure around it.  > All figures in this file were computed from the files themselves (as of 7 Oct 2026).
