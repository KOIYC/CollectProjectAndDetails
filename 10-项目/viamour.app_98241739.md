## 摘要正文

Hey everyone! I'm just trying to share the app I made that launched this past Saturday on Google Play and the App Store. I work in non-profit and have an MFA in Screenwriting. Despite all this unrelated experience I was able to take an idea I've had in my head for about 4 years and bring it to life in 90 days with the help of Codex and Claude. I hadn't touched anything related to coding since probably the Myspace days and that definitely doesn't count.About the app: Viamour is a new app for finding friends, groups, local events, and curated experiences nearby. It’s not a dating app, and the goal isn’t to keep you endlessly swiping. It’s to help you actually meet people and build community offline.My Stack: Codex, Claude, React Native, Supabase, Resend, AdMob, Revenue Cat, Stripe.I would love an feedback and maybe to help spread the word since there's the whole "cold start" problem. I would be happy for anyone that checks it out.I got my first 50 users over the weekend, wish me luck!
