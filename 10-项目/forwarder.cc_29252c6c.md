TL;DR: 12 years ago I built a service that forwards simple HTML forms to an email address. I've been procrastinating on finishing it ever since, until today. You point your form at it and every submission lands in your inbox. It runs on credits, 1 credit = 1 message, no subscription. Perfect for static websites.—Hey folks, back around 2014, alongside my job as a software engineer, I was building a lot of static websites for my own clients, mostly small businesses that just wanted a simple site. To avoid headaches, I went fully static, often hosted on GitHub Pages and similar.But most clients also wanted a contact form, and a form needs a server running somewhere. I had my own weekend-hacked service for this, which literally just forwarded the form to the client's email.I didn't invent anything here: one service I particularly remember was FormKeep by thoughtbot, that I really liked.So I started building my own, mostly because I loved losing myself in side projects like this, and also because I was a cheap ass and thought I could do something similar for less, and without a subscription.By 2016 I had done about 90% of what I'm showing you today.
