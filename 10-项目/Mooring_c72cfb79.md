Mooring - Indie Hackers  # Mooring  Mailbox hosting on your own domain  September 19, 2026 Launching Mooring — real mailboxes on a domain you own (free while early)  I just put Mooring on Indie Hackers.  What it is: IMAP/SMTP mailboxes on a domain you already own — not forwarding,  not Google Workspace. Webmail, Outlook, iPhone. CalDAV/CardDAV too.  Why: most “custom domain email” is either forwarding or a per-seat bill.  I wanted a simple berth: point DNS, make a mailbox, open it in normal apps.  Status: live at https://mooring.email  Signup is open. Default is 1 domain / 1 mailbox. Free while I’m early.  I’m looking for a few domain owners to berth and tell me where setup hurts  (DNS and phone Mail especially). Happy to help if you get stuck.  If you’ve tried ImprovMX / Cloudflare Email / Workspace for a small brand —  what made you stay or leave?  ## About  People who own a domain still end up on forwarding or Google Workspace when all they want is a real mailbox on @theirbrand. Mooring exists to make that simple: IMAP/SMTP on your domain, webmail, Outlook and phone.  # iptvstreamingservices - Indie Hackers
