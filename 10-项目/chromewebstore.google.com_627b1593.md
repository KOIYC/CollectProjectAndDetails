Kora – Focus Reader & Website Shield - Chrome Web Store  # Kora – Focus Reader & Website Shield  Overview  Read one paragraph at a time, save your place, and block distracting websites. Private, local, and free.  Long pages shouldn’t feel like a wall of text. Kora is a calm focus reader that helps you finish more of what you open. It keeps your current paragraph clear, gently softens the surrounding text, and shows simple progress through the page. Start reading directly from the quiet page-edge button - no popup required. Move paragraph by paragraph, scroll normally, or use keyboard shortcuts. Choose the websites that regularly pull you away and block them during a Kora focus session—or turn on the shield manually. PRIVATE BY DESIGN Page text stays on the page.
