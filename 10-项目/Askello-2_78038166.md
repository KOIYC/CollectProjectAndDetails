Home Starting Up Case Studies DB Products Ideas DB Subscribe to IH+ Starting Up Case Studies  Ideas DB Products DB Sign in Join Askello  AI guest guides for vacation rentals Visit Website Askello AI guest guides for vacation rentals  Post 1  Revenue $0 / mo  Website September 21, 2026  I built a guest guide that refuses to make up an answer I’ve been building Askello, a guest guide for short-term rental hosts, and I want to write about one specific decision rather than pitch the whole product.  Every host answers the same questions repeatedly:  - What’s the Wi-Fi password?  - How does the oven work?  - Where can we park?  - What time is checkout?  The obvious solution is to put everything in a document and send guests the link. Hosts already do that with PDFs, laminated sheets and long check-in messages. But nobody reads an entire manual at 11pm when the oven won’t light.  With Askello, a host pastes the information they already give guests. AI organizes it into editable sections, the host reviews and publishes it, and guests receive a mobile guide they can browse or ask questions to.
