# 0.9.5: no computer has to stay on  - Tag: v0.9.5 - Repository: bryanzk/send-to-dot - Published: 2026-11-06T01:24:41Z - Author: bryanzk  ---  **Send to dot 0.9.5 — no computer has to stay on**  Share a photo (and an optional question) from Android or iPhone, and the original appears in your existing ChatGPT dot conversation, where dot answers you.  What this release means for you:  - **No computer has to stay on.** Everything runs on your own free Cloudflare account. Your Mac can sleep or be off; sharing keeps working. - **Set up by talking to your dot.** Paste the README “No Mac” message into your dot conversation. Dot finds this release and walks you through the few steps only you can do (Cloudflare Deploy page, your owner code, `/setup` for the phone code, adding the ChatGPT app), then creates its own task. No terminal, no server to maintain. - **Your original photo, once.** The exact file, not a compressed preview, arrives as an attachment and is never sent twice. Uncertain sends are held and checked, not retried. - **Private by default.** Encrypted, deleted from the inbox after 1 hour; the phone code can only upload; ChatGPT reads only after you approve with your owner code. …
