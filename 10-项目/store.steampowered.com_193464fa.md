## 摘要正文

A warning if you're launching on Steam soon: submit your build for review at least 3 weeks before your release date.  Here's my timeline:  * Oct 20: Submitted my build for review, 13 business days before launch (Valve's docs ask for at least 7). * Nov 4: Valve replied 11 business days later, asking for one small fix. I fixed it and resubmitted the same day. * Nov 6: Release day passed while I was still waiting for the re-review.  The most painful part is watching players join your Discord or post on the Steam forums asking when the game is coming out, and you can't give them an answer :(  The demo is still up (20+ reviews, 100% positive) if you want to give it a look: [King in the Mountain Demo on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5235350/King_in_the_Mountain_Demo/)
