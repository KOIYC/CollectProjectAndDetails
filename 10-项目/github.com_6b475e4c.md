Home · aschoerk/chapterdesk Wiki · GitHub  # Welcome to ChapterDesk  A local writing studio for scene-by-scene story work with language models.  You do not chat with the model. You give a short direction for the next scene, read what comes back, and keep going. When a plot could go two ways, you fork the thread and keep both versions. When you want to read, you open the book view instead of the prompt.  ChapterDesk is for steering stories — not for claiming authorship. The model drafts the beat; you decide what happens next.  ## Start here  New to ChapterDesk?
