## 摘要正文

Diskly explains what's actually taking up space on your Mac in plain language instead of macOS's vague "System Data: 70GB" bucket. Right-click any folder in Finder and it tells you what it is, which app it belongs to, and whether it's safe to remove. Nothing auto-deletes, you confirm every action.  Went with one-time pricing instead of subscription since this is a "run it occasionally" tool, not something people open daily, felt wrong to charge monthly for that.  Currently in beta ahead of a Product Hunt launch on Nov 17, Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 14+. If anyone wants to try it or has thoughts on the pricing model: [https://diskly.net](https://diskly.net)
