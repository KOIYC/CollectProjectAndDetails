You lead Rome, or one of its rivals, from a single province to an empire. Each year you choose  where to march, what to raise and how hard to tax; then the rival kingdoms take their turns. When  armies meet, you fight the battle yourself. A campaign across the Mediterranean 33 provinces from Britannia to Mesopotamia and 8 kingdoms. You win by  holding 8, 12 or 16 provinces, as you chose  before you began, and lose if your capital falls. How a campaign works Provinces to govern Set each province’s tribute, hold games, station garrisons and appoint generals. Public  mood, from 0 to 100, decides what a province pays and whether it rises against you.
