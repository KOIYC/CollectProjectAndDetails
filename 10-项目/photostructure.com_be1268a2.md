## 摘要正文

Howdy!I've been working on an open-source tool that helps lighten the burden of reviewing what your coding agents write. You still see all the lines of code, but the agents write a "tour guide" that links you through the local PR, and as you have questions or comments, you chat with your agent in the context of the code, in a threaded conversation.I called it "gavel" (you make your agents build a case that you judge). Hope you find it as useful as I do!https://github.com/photostructure/gavel
