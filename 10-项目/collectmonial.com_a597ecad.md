## 摘要正文

Hey, I'm 19 and work as a brand designer for SaaS companies. Most of my clients have come through Twitter/X inbound.  January 2026 was my peak month. I closed **$6,000** in brand design deals within a single week, because a bunch of my posts were blowing up on X — **2k+ likes** and **200k+ views**.  One of the companies I ended up working with was doing **$6 Million ARR**.  But X changed the algorithm somewhere along the way and designers got pushed out of the inbound funnel.  Reach has dropped. And no new client inquiries have come in for months.  So after 3-4 months of zero clients, I just decided I didn't want to keep freelancing anymore lol.  Figured building a SaaS would give me way more leverage if I can grow it well over the next few years (and yeah, I needed the income too).  So I started looking at existing tools to figure out where I could do better. Ended up going with **testimonial collection** because it's a proven market (reinventing the wheel felt too risky).
