## 摘要正文

Hi everyone! I typically do not put my projects out for others to see, so this is new to me, but I have created a privacy first budgeting app called KernelCash Budgets ( https://kernelcash.com ), and wanted to share it here in the event someone was interested in a product like this. This app has really helped my wife and I, as well as some family, friends, and colleagues, so I wanted to put this out to see if it could help anyone else.I created this app as a replacement to some of the other budgeting apps out there. Some of KernelCash's core features include:- Envelope and zero style budgeting - Expense, income, and savings goal tracking - Full analytics for income, expenses, and savings - Bank connection / sync free. We use transaction file imports (OFX / QFX) or manual entry to keep things private. You can freely share or not share what you choose. - Multi-collaborator workspaces. These are great for sharing your budget with a spouse, roommates, and have even been used by some parents to teach their kids how to budget.
