## 摘要正文

Making a polished 30-second SaaS demo can take way more editing than you'd expect.  Cutting and arranging clips, zooming into UI elements, animating text and highlights, adding cursor movements and clicks, making transitions feel natural, and constantly tweaking the timing.  And when the product UI changes, you often have to redo parts of the video.  I built Deamio to make that editing process easier and, more importantly, keep everything editable. You can replace assets, change timing, move elements around, restyle things, and keep refining the same project instead of starting over. So instead of making it all from scratch you have an almost finished result just need to tweak it change some icons and text and thats it, I also have a button where you can try a different veriation for a scene.  It also runs locally, so your project assets don't need to be uploaded to a cloud service. I believe I priced it kinda cheap(currently sale 25% generally 15 and might change it) after looking at fiverr listings and other stuff.
