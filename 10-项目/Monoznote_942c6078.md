I’ve always taken a lot of notes, but rarely went back to them.  Ideas, things I learned, and random thoughts slowly disappear into an endless pile of notes.  I didn’t want another system of folders and tags. I wanted my old thoughts to come back to me when they’re actually relevant.  So I’m building monoznote .  As you write, monoznote surfaces relevant notes from your past based on what you’re writing now.  It’s an early MVP, and I’m looking for feedback:  Would this help you rediscover things you’ve forgotten, or would it just create more noise?
