Several Linux VMs on different hosts, their disks on fault-tolerant storage and the VMs on one private network, from Debian's public cloud image to iperf3 between hosts, and a running VM moved live to another host. nos at work: a demo  The goal of the video is to run several Linux virtual machines on different nodes of a cluster, all booted into nos, with their disks on fault-tolerant storage and the machines on one private network, and then to move one of them to another node while it runs. It starts with an SSH session into the kernel of one host and goes on as one session at the management panel of a small nos cluster with three hosts. One is a bare-metal Intel server at Hetzner, an EX44, with nos running directly on the hardware. The other two are nos instances in KVM virtual machines on a second server, an AMD AX41.
