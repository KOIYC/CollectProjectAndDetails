I make small Mac apps, and Notch Radio is the newest one. It's been on the Mac App Store since September.  I listen to internet radio all day while I work. Every Mac radio app I tried wanted a window or a menu bar popover, so changing station meant finding something, clicking into it and closing it again. The notch sits right where I'm already looking and does nothing, so I put the radio there.  When it's collapsed it's a thin pill against the notch. Hover over it and it opens into a player with album art, live track titles and controls. Move the pointer away and it folds back.
