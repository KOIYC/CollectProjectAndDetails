An 11 year journey to play DOS Wing Commander together By danielrh Watch on YouTube Play it in your browser When we were kids, my brother Patrick and I would steer our space fighter with one of us on controls and the other on the lasers and afterburner boost. We always dreamed of playing the Wing Commander series cooperatively as wingmates rather than as a two headed hydra in a single craft. In the beginning we were searching for where we were On October 4, 2015 as I was playing the classic Wing Commander in Dosbox I realized the whole memory probably fit in that 640K Bill Gates apocryphally said “ought to be enough for anybody”. If I could dump the memory and identify the locations that held the X,Y,Z coords for the player & wingman we could let the network packets control the wingman.
