Nerdulator: Put something in. Get everything out.  # Nerdulator  Put something in. Get everything out.  What have you got? Paste or type anything  What have you got? Paste or type anything; Nerdulator works out what it is.  Or don’t decide: surprise me picks a page at random, and today’s page is the same one for everybody until midnight.  ## How it works  01  ### It works out what you gave it  A deterministic parser (regular expressions, lookup tables and range checks) decides whether you typed a number, a date, a measurement, a colour, an address or a word. If it is genuinely ambiguous, you get both readings rather than a silent guess.  02  ### It calculates everything it can  The matching engine runs every transformation it knows: factors, conversions, encodings, contrast ratios, subnet boundaries, calendar positions. Same input, same output, every time.  03  ### It links you onward  Every derived value is itself a page. The square of 47, the complement of a colour, the day after a date. All real, all one click away.  ## 22 engines, no models  Nerdulator makes zero AI calls. Every figure on every page is computed at request time from the input you gave it. That is the whole point…
