Repo: [https://github.com/libredb/libredb-studio](https://github.com/libredb/libredb-studio)  Demo: [https://app.libredb.org](https://app.libredb.org/)  Youtube : [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWANKEDszs4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWANKEDszs4)  Deploy it next to your database with Docker or Helm and open it in the browser. No SSH tunnels, no desktop client on every laptop.  Postgres, MySQL, Oracle, SQL Server, MongoDB, Redis, ClickHouse, DuckDB, Cassandra and more. MIT licensed, SSO and the AI assistant included.
