## 摘要正文

Hey yall! I needed a way to cap my own phone use so downloaded one of the many "do pushups to unlock your phone apps". I loved it! The only thing I didn't like was that I had to set my phone up to track myself doing the pushups/situps etc. That part was super annoying and embarrassing when people would see me doing pushups at a gas station lol. The app I built isn't for everyone, but instead of tracking you do the workout, it's the same idea just based on the honor system - no camera tracking! I have loved using it the past few months and maybe some of y'all will enjoy it as well!ios/android supported!
