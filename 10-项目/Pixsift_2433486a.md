PixSift - Indie Hackers  # PixSift  Make your screenshots instantly findable  September 18, 2026 Google for your screenshots, minus the part where your screenshots leave your PC  Screenshots has to be the easiest way to capture the source of truth at that moment. It costs nothing and can capture the current state in your screen. I personally take screenshots constantly. Error messages, receipts, UI references, invoices ,random things I was definitely going to need later.  At some point my Screenshots folder passed thousands of files and became a graveyard of things I knew I had saved, but could never actually find.  Windows Search wasn’t much help. It knows `Screenshot 2026-03-14 091522.png` exists. It doesn’t know there’s a receipt number, an error message, or some random piece of text sitting inside those pixels. It doesn't even work properly for pdfs and docs, escpecially the scanned ones.  So I built PixSift. It indexes screenshots, PDFs and documents locally and lets me search for the words actually inside them. I can type a phrase I only partially remember, find the exact screenshot, pull the text out, copy the file, or send it straight into whatever I’m already working on.  …
