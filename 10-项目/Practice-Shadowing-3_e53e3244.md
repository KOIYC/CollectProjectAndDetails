It launched on 18 October and is free while I find out whether people use it.  What it does?  Shadowing means repeating what a speaker says straight after them. Most players make you pause by hand. This one plays one sentence, waits until you have finished saying it, then goes on. The words you say light up as you speak, and you can play your attempt next to the speaker's. Sample clips at three levels need no account. With a free account you can use your own audio, a podcast or a YouTube video with captions. The screens are in English and Vietnamese, because my first market is Vietnamese learners of English.  What building it taught me?  Knowing when someone has finished speaking is the whole product.
