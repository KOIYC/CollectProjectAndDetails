## 摘要正文

Hi HN, I’m Rayhan. Crulon is a business phone system where an AI agent answers the line and your team works alongside it. You can talk with our demo agent right in your browser, no sign-up: https://crulon.ai/#talkWhy I built it: I’ve spent eight years as a software and network engineer, mostly building tools for internet providers, and kept watching small support teams lose customers in the seams. Calls missed after hours. Calls passed from a bot to a person who had to start over. Most voice AI products are a bot sitting on someone else’s phone line, so a handoff means forwarding to another number and losing the context. We built the phone system and the agent as one thing.What that gets you:The handoff happens inside the same system. When something isn’t the AI’s job, it asks the caller, then rings your team (in the browser or on a desk phone) with a note on why they’re calling. Live calls: anyone with permission can listen to an AI call (no one on the call can hear them) and take it over. It’s the same call, with no transfer and no hold music; the AI just leaves.
