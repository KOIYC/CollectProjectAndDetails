Hey r/saas,  I'm Yahia, used to post here a ton while building random stuff on the internet for years.  One of those projects was [stockalarm.io](http://stockalarm.io), which I grew to 250,000 users and sold.  Plenty of the others went absolutely nowhere (tons of fun though)...  Eventually I bootstrapped Brand.dev. Give us a domain, get back logos, colors, fonts, and company info. Customers started asking us to pull more data from websites, and we kept saying yes.  That grew into Context.dev.  We went through YC S26, grew to 7 people, and built infrastructure that now handles millions of requests an hour across search, web scraping, crawling, and structured data.  There’s a ridiculous amount of engineering behind that. Very proud of what the team’s built.  But the biggest thing I’ve learned in a market this competitive is how much comes from staying close to customers..  For me, that’s meant:  \> personally taking 300–400 customer/lead calls a month, my calendar is a disaster most days.  \> talking to the same people again after they’ve actually used the product.
