Each day, a new fictional clinical case is published with up to 6 progressive clues. Players search for ICD-10 codes and receive proximity feedback across 5 levels: exact ICD, same category, same group, same chapter, or different chapter. Beyond the daily case, NEXO offers a Continents mode (103 cases organized by regional epidemiology across Africa, Americas, Asia, Europe, and Oceania) and a Multimedia mode (123 cases with clinical imaging and auscultation, including X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, ECG, upper endoscopy, colonoscopy, dermatoscopy, fundoscopy, physical exam, histopathology, blood smear, karyotype, PET/CT, otoscopy, mammography, colposcopy, and cardiac and respiratory audio).
