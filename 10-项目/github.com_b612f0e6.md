## 摘要正文

Gondwana is a 2D/2.5D game engine for C#/.NET 8 that I’ve been building over the past 16 months, using SkiaSharp for rendering. I've spent my career creating web and distributed apps and that has influenced the engine's development, so it will feel very familiar to anyone with a similar background. The engine is deliberately code-first in its implementation but still includes visual authoring tools and a custom CLI.I started creating Gondwana to host my own RTS and SNES-style games, to offload the game ideas percolating in my brain. I wanted to focus more on creating gameplay systems and was less concerned with 3D graphics. The predictability and transparency of the old Win32/GDI era were the ideal for what I was shooting for, but I also wanted something faster and more portable than GDI32. To that end, I am not trying to emulate the bigger engines.Spot! became my primary dogfooding project that I’ve since pushed up to itch.io. Getting a game to that point really helped burn away the cruft that accumulates from trying to create something like this. I was able to get Spot! to render at a consistent 120 fps on my dev machine.
