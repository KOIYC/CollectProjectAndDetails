## 摘要正文

i’ve been working on a few side projects and kept running into the same annoying problem: setting up a simple contact email.  i didn’t want to pay for a mailbox for every project, configure dns records or set up a whole email service just so someone could contact me.  so i built https://mailcontact.app  the idea is pretty simple. you get a contact email address and a free contact form in seconds. messages from both end up in the same inbox, and you can reply directly from there.  you can also get notifications in the tools you already use, so you don’t have to keep checking another inbox. it’s mainly for indie devs, small saas projects, freelancers or anyone who just needs an easy way for people to reach them.  i’d love to hear what you think, especially if there’s something missing or something you’d do differently.
