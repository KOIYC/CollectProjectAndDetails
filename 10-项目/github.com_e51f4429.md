## 摘要正文

I hate leaving the terminal, so clv was born: it shows images, Markdown, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, SVGs, and HTML right in your kitty-compatible terminal.Supports light/dark modes, search, copy to clipboard, follow links (ala Vimium), browsing directories/zips/tars, open your default app.I hope others find it useful.I'd appreciate your feedback!--- Short boring history:clv began as the `view` subcommand I proposed for pdfcraft's cli (PR #2920). I immediately realized I wanted to support other types of docs.Prior to clv I was using icat for images, tdf for PDFs, mdv or mdcat for Markdown, and didn't have a solution for most other doc types. So I imagined a "display anything" command, and here we are.
