DER DIE DAS — Learn German Noun Genders with Spaced Repetition  # DER DIE DAS  Learn German noun genders — der, die, das — with spaced-repetition flashcards, eight practice games, and real-time duels.  - 8 solo games - 4 duel modes - telc A1.1–B2 - 17 interface languages - FSRS review engine - iOS, Android, Web  ## What is DER DIE DAS?  DER DIE DAS is an app that teaches German noun genders — der (masculine), die (feminine), das (neuter) — using spaced-repetition flashcards, eight standalone practice games, and real-time 1v1 duels. Reviews are scheduled by the FSRS algorithm, vocabulary spans telc levels A1.1 through B2, and the interface is available in 17 languages.  ## How it works  1. Pick a word source: the built-in ~3,000-word frequency list, a telc exam level (A1.1 through B2), or a category filter such as work, sports, health, or body parts. 2. Review the words due today. Each card can be read, listened to, or spoken aloud, depending on which card types are enabled in Settings. 3. Grade each answer Again or Good.
