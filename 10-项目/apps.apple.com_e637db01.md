## 摘要正文

Disclosure: I made this.  **Backstory:** I have ADHD and I forget my to-do list exists. So I built Resurface: a to-do app that shows one task at a time, mostly on a Lock Screen widget, and brings it back later if you ignore it. It went live on the App Store today.  **Tech stack:** Swift and SwiftUI, every line written by Claude Code. I can't read code; I wrote the spec and made every product call. Built on Windows: xtool through WSL builds and installs straight onto a real iPhone. I rented a Mac for one day for the App Store upload. No server, no accounts, no analytics: tasks stay on the phone, so there's no backend to pay for.  **Pricing and revenue:** first month free, then $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year. Kept cheap on purpose. Revenue so far: $0, it launched today.  **Lesson:** most of the work was deciding what to leave out. No list to pick from, no streaks, no "overdue", no due dates. A lot of features that look helpful quietly work against the people they're for.  App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6819368552  Site: https://resurfacehq.app  Roasts of the landing page welcome.
