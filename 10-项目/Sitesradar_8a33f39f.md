SitesRadar: Monitor client websites and get alerts when | BetaList  ## Monitor client websites and get alerts when anything regresses  SitesRadar monitors client websites for freelancers and small agencies and alerts you the moment something gets worse. It checks uptime, SSL and certificate expiry, broken links, redirects, domain and DNS changes, email deliverability, SEO tags, and even leaked file metadata. Then it emails exactly what changed and where. You can add multiple sites, see a single prioritized status view, and avoid noisy “still up” pings.
