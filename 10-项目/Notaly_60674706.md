Where it started  I've used Notion, Logseq, and Obsidian. Obsidian the longest, and I still use it. What kept bugging me was writing the same thing twice. I'd take meeting notes in one document, then write a summary or a plan somewhere else and type the same items again, or paste copies that slowly drifted apart.
