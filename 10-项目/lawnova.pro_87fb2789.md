I've been building a legal-tech project called **LAWNOVA**, and I've reached one of those slightly awkward founder moments where the original product has started turning into several products.  I'm not sure yet whether that's a feature or a mistake.  The original idea was pretty broad: instead of making another AI legal chatbot, could we build something that connects more of the actual workflow?  Cases, documents, research, transcripts, drafting, trial preparation, etc.  As we built it, certain pieces started becoming useful enough to almost stand on their own. LAWNOVA** [https://lawnova.pro](https://lawnova.pro/)  This is the main platform and the larger vision — more of a legal workspace / operating layer.  **2. PDF / Transcript Search** [https://pdf.lawnova.pro](https://pdf.lawnova.pro/)  This is much more focused.
