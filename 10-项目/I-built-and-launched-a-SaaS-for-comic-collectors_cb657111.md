## 摘要正文

I’m a lifelong collector of comics, and I was really unhappy with the apps being offered to collectors (the latest ones are actually from marketplaces that want to track what people own), So, managing a serious collection had become really fragmented.  Cataloguing in one place. Market research through Google. Spreadsheets for tracking. Price guides for values. Marketplace searches when I wanted actual comparable sales.  I wanted one mobile app that could make all of that feel simple.  So I built ComiCollector.  I started thinking that I could lean on AI to help out and keep things simple.  But I massively underestimated how complicated “simple” would become. AI isn't ready for these types of complex tasks. I could go on for hours about just how bad it is, but it forced me to leverage deterministic programming as the real bedrock for the application. Deterministic rules, structured data, market evidence, filtering, caching, validation and a lot of guardrails.  The principle became:  **AI can help interpret the market.
