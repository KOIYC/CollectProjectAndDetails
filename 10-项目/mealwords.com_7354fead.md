## 摘要正文

Last month I went to Indonesia, and was eating at a local place with friends who did not eat raw fish&green onions. We tried ChatGPT (of course) it worked, except it wasnt a smooth experience: taking too long, and difficult for the staff to provide proper feedback quickly.Then I realized I only needed to tell the waiter what we won't eat, and ask for recommendations, that's all. So I made MealWords (recommend to try on your phone):1. You pick allergies, dietary and preferences in your language (peanuts, no cilantro, not spicy etc) and it shows the card in the staff's language.2. The waiter taps anything they can't do on the card, or just confirms. You read the answer in your own language.On translations: nothing is machine-translated (well, llm-assisted for research but not mechanical translations). For allergens&terms I used all official guidance I could find (FDA, EFSA, Japan etc all official governments and local public health orgs) to make sure I used not only accurate but also understandable terms.
