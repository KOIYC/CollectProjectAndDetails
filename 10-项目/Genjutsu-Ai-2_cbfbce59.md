But I kept running into a different problem: sometimes the source video is already good — the motion, timing, camera movement, and composition all work. I only want to change one object, product, or part of the scene.  That’s why I built Genjutsu AI .  The idea is simple: upload an existing video, provide reference images, and transform only what you want while keeping as much of the original video as possible.  I’m currently experimenting with workflows like object swaps and motion-based transformations, and I’m still figuring out where this is genuinely useful versus where a normal video generator is good enough.
