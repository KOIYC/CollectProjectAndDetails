## 摘要正文

Word Spies A word-association spy game you play with friends, AI teammates, or both. Two teams share a grid of 25 words; each spymaster gives one-word clues so their team can find its words before the other team does, without hitting the assassin. Any seat can be a person or an AI model. After every clue, Word Spies shows how good the clue was and how each team's chance of winning changed. No account needed to play.
