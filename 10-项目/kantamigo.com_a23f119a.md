## 摘要正文

Heey everyone,It's probably not the best place to show a tool for offline parties with a guitar, but I still wanna try :DMy brain and fingers produced something great in the last few months and it's called Kantamigo. If you're a guitarist or participated a party where everybody sing together, then you probably had a "what's the lyrics?" situation when you want to sing but you have no idea what the guitarist is playing. So this is what Kantamigo for - realtime lyrics for guitar parties. It's also a personal songbook of course, with many cool features (smart import from ANY source, not-so-stupid auto-scroll, pedal / keyboard support, playlists, tuner, transponder and so on).So if it's relevant for you - please give it a try, and please please let me know what do you think. I've been having that idea in my head for ~4 years, finally created it and really want to hear if I'm not the only one who needs this :)Thanks!
