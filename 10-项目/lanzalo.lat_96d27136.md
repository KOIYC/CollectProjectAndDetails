## 摘要正文

Hey, I'm José. I built Lánzalo, a free place to launch and discover products made in Latin America.  You can publish a product page, browse projects by country or category, and leave comments. It's live, but still early beta.  The question I'm trying to figure out is what makes a regional discovery site worth coming back to after you've posted your own product. A page full of launch announcements is easy to build. Giving people a reason to stick around feels harder.  If you build in or for LATAM, would seeing the country and local context help you find products you'd actually try? Or would you mostly want a way to find products solving a specific problem, regardless of where they were built?  Here's the site if you want to have a look: [https://lanzalo.lat](https://lanzalo.lat)  I'd love a blunt take on whether the homepage makes that regional angle useful or just adds another filter.
