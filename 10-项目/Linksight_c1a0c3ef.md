LinkSight: Plan RF line-of-sight links and Fresnel zones | BetaList  ## Plan RF line-of-sight links and Fresnel zones instantly on the go  LinkSight helps you plan wireless links anywhere with precise line-of-sight and Fresnel zone analysis. It uses global topography, adjustable mast heights, and custom frequencies to validate point-to-point paths, estimate tower needs, and confirm clearances before fieldwork. You can generate professional PDF reports, drop GPS-accurate pins, and view live metrics for quick decisions. Whether for WISP, LoRa, UAV, or amateur radio, it puts RF planning in your pocket.
