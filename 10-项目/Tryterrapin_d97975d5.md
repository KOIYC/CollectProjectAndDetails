Terrapin: Turn receipts, email & photos into clean, | BetaList  ## Turn receipts, email & photos into clean, tax-ready insights, automatically  Terrapin is a receipt proof automation platform built for freelancers, 1099 contractors, and self-employed workers. No manual uploads, no spreadsheets, no shoeboxes.  We match each receipt to the right transaction, categorize it, and separate business from personal expenses.
