## 摘要正文

Hi everyone, I’m the solo developer of REFRACTION: The First Dream.  It’s a short psychological horror game with fixed camera angles, environmental storytelling, and puzzles. There’s no combat. You explore, look for clues, and try to make sense of the places you’re moving through.  Silent Hill, Resident Evil, and P.T. were big inspirations. This is a small proof of concept, roughly 20–30 minutes depending on exploration and puzzle solving.
