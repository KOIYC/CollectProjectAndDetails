Whether the new arm does better is what the trial is there to find out, and until it reads out, everyone involved works from some estimate of the odds. How good are those estimates? When 148 cancer specialists forecast 25 ongoing trials, their Brier score was 0.22, a little better than the 0.25 you get by saying 50% every time ( Doussau et al. 2023 ).
