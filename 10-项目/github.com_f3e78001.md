## 摘要正文

Hi HN, I’m building Hindsight, a free, open-source activity tracker for Windows and macOS.I know there are plenty of time trackers. I wanted something that combined automatic tracking, detailed charts, and cross-device sync in a lightweight app with an interface I enjoyed using. I couldn’t find a free option that fit, so I started building my own.Hindsight records active apps and window titles while it’s running, without requiring you to start and stop timers. You can explore your activity through daily, weekly, and monthly charts, drill down into individual apps and window titles, and use a yearly heatmap to spot patterns. Categories are customizable.There’s also an optional screenshot and OCR feature for finding something you previously saw on screen. Both are off by default. When enabled, you can search recognized text and jump back to the corresponding screenshot.The AI features can generate daily summaries or answer questions about your activity, with links back to the underlying records so you can check the answers.
