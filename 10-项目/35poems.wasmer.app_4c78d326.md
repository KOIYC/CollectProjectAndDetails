## 摘要正文

Author: John Muconto  35 Poems of John Muconto — A Journey Through Life  # 35  Poems  of John Muconto   “at 35, I do not claim resolution. only awareness.” — from the Foreword   A collection written before turning thirty-five — a record of a life lived in fragments, gathered across five movements. Not a search for answers, but an honest observation of experience as it is.  ## The Five Movements  1. Formation 2. Struggle 3. Release 4. Observation 5. Resolution  ## About the Author   John Muconto is a programmer and writer from Mozambique with professional experience in IT management and a parallel academic background in theology. His work sits at the intersection of analytical systems thinking and reflective spiritual inquiry.   He has practiced the Messianic faith for 22 years, a perspective that informs much of his worldview and writing. His poetry reflects structured introspection, personal observation, and thematic exploration of meaning, discipline, and belief.
