JustYourMenu - Indie Hackers  # JustYourMenu  Digital QR menu tool for restaurants, pubs, bars  September 19, 2026 Digital QR menus can be easy and fast  JustYourMenu was born out of frustration.  I went to a restaurant, scanned the QR code and got welcomed by 10s long loading screen with a logo of a company that made it.  Why? I have no idea.  I went to few other places, some had similar problems, and some had literal scanned PDFs and I had to zoom in just to see whats on the menu.  And that's how it all started. I started developing a solution that would allow restaurant owners to make a simple, fast and intuitive digital QR menu that I would myself enjoy scanning.  JustYourMenu is super focused product with no bloat. Signup, give your menu a name, fill out the info, add categories, add items. That's it.  No bloat, no online ordering or staff logins. Just the menu. And that's exactly what I believe most places want. A simple way to make a beautiful digital version of their menu that they can easily update on the go.
