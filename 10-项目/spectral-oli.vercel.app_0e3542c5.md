## 摘要正文

SpectralOli is a browser based livecoding environment for reshaping audio loops by treating the spectrogram as an editable two-dimensional canvas. The spectrogram as a canvas idea draws inspirations from many existing projects in different ways: Metasynth, Strudel, SuperCollider, Composers Desktop Project and a whole lot more
