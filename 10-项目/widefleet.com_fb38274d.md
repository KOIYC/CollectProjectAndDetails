## 摘要正文

Hi HN, we’re building Widefleet for teams building internal apps and workflows with coding agents.Think Vercel or Cloudflare, with company login (SSO), app sharing, and controls for IT to manage who can access each app and which company systems it can connect to. Apps run as separate workers with per-app databases and object storage. You can also fully customize and manage connectors to internal systems, so your other users don’t have to handle API keys etc. themselves. Cron jobs and queues support background tasks and recurring workflows.There’s now a hosted version, so give it a try and let us know what you think!Try it: https://app.widefleet.com Code: https://github.com/widefleet/widefleetCurious how you’re dealing with vibe-coded apps at work, whether you built them yourself or ended up maintaining someone else’s.
