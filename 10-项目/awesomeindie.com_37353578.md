## 摘要正文

One change I made before launching AfterLid: you can now download the Mac app without creating an account.  It’s a Mac utility with an iPhone and Android companion. The account is needed to connect those devices, but the Mac app also works on its own. Asking people to sign in before downloading felt too early.  The flow is now: download the Mac app, try it, then create an account if you want to connect your phone. Linking still requires signing in on both devices and approving the Mac.  The Mac modes, safeguards and viewing live status from your phone are free. Changing modes remotely is part of the paid Pro plan.  The next thing I want to measure is how many downloads turn into completed phone connections.  AfterLid is on Awesome Indie today: https://awesomeindie.com/product/afterlid  If you’ve shipped a desktop app with a phone companion, when did you introduce account creation?
