## 摘要正文

hey guys, musician here (music producer - studio geek) and the maker of SongBlast ([https://songblast.ai](https://songblast.ai)), a micro-SaaS that turns one song into a month of short promo videos for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook  some context: every release comes with the same advice — post three short videos a day with your song on them. I never did it. Cutting the hook, timing the lyrics on screen, exporting vertical, writing four captions, posting at the right hour, three times a day for weeks. That's a job. So I built the tool for my own releases, used it for a while, then put it online.  what it does: well, you upload a song and some footage — your own clips, or use free 4K stock (we have Pexels integrated as one of the libraries basically infinite cool footage which the app will also auto-cut so it's totally original in the "eyes" of the algorithmic push of the platforms) ;  it finds the catchiest 15–20 seconds, puts the synced lyrics on screen, and I trained an AI brain , **Otis**, who will constantly scan for cool captions and hashtags for the posts and will write them for you (posting 50 posts for example on 4 platforms - TikTok, Instagram, Youtub…
