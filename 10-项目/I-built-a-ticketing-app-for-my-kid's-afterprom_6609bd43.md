## 摘要正文

I joined our high school's afterprom committee as a parent volunteer, and the first thing I noticed was how old-school it all was. Paper tickets, a cash box, and a clipboard at the door.  The part that really bugged me came later. Once a kid was inside, nobody outside knew anything. Parents dropped them off and hoped. If a kid left at 1am, mom found out when they walked in the door, or didn't.  I'm a developer, so I did the thing developers do and said "I can build something for this." Online tickets, waivers signed ahead of time, QR check-in at the door, and the piece I cared about most: **a text to the parent when their kid checks in, and another one if they leave.**  The waivers turned out to be the sleeper hit. Before, it was a stack of paper forms and a line of kids at the door hunting down a parent signature at 11pm. With the waiver built into the ticket purchase, **we had 560 kids at afterprom and only 3 showed up without a signed waiver.** The committee, and our inflatable company who needed the waivers, couldn't believe it.
