When you plug in your USB or USB-C headphones, it switches your Mac's sound output to them.  I made it for my **Bose Noise Cancelling Wired Earbuds (USB-C)**. When I plugged them into my MacBook Pro, the microphone switched over but the sound kept coming out of the speakers, so I had to open Sound settings every time. With this app, the sound moves to the earbuds when I plug them in.
