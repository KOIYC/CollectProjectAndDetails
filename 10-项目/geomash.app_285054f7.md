## 摘要正文

Been working on a small side project called GeoMash. You get a photo from somewhere in the world and 90 sec to pin it on the map. There’s a hot/cold radar and some clues to help you. Started it to get better at geography myself and I am quite happy with the current version.  The part I enjoy most is multiplayer. You make a room, send the link to friends (up to 8) and everyone guesses the same place live. You can also send a challenge link so a friend plays your exact game and tries to beat your score.  No sign up, works on phone. [geomash.app](http://geomash.app)  Attached a short demo video if you just want a quick look without opening it. Would love any feedback, good or bad.  https://reddit.com/link/1x8b9qg/video/vz07syg7c1wh1/player
