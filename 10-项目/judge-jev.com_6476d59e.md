## 摘要正文

This is just a simple game with procedurally generated legal scenarios where you are accused of a crime and are given 5 pieces of evidence associated with said* crime. You then have 3 minutes to mount a defense to convince Judge Jev you didn't commit the crime. Sometimes the evidence has glaring gaps, other times not so much. I made this little game initially as an experiment for Jevs logical abilities, but I actually had a lot of fun and decided it was worth sharing.
