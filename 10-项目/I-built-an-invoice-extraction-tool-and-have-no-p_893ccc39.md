## 摘要正文

I’m a student building Paprly on the side. It reads supplier invoices (PDFs, scans, photos), pulls out the line items, and exports to Xero or QuickBooks. It’s live, but I have no paying customers.  What I’ve done so far: a handful of early testers gave useful feedback, I fixed things like European date formats and credit notes, and I’ve just emailed about 20 small accountancy firms.  What I’m hearing: one practice owner told me that invoice extraction will probably be overtaken by people writing their own tools, with the price heading to zero in 6 to 12 months. I think that’s partly right for raw extraction, but I’m not sure the review, error-flagging and clean-export side gets commoditised as fast.  I’d like honest opinions on a few things:  1. Is a standalone invoice parser still worth pursuing, or is it a race to the bottom? 2. If you’ve been through this, how did you decide when to stop, and what evidence made up your mind? 3. If you’d keep going, would you narrow to a specific niche?  I’m happy to hear “stop”. I’d just rather decide on real experience than on a bad week.
