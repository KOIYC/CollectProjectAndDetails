## 摘要正文

About a year ago, I built [Hyallo](https://hyallo.com), a website that lets you call real phone numbers directly from your browser. No apps, no subscriptions, just pay per minute. Credits never expire.  I'm a Brazilian data engineer, and I started building it while I was living in the Netherlands.  I used Skype Out to call banks and public services back in Brazil, since most of them don't have WhatsApp. When Skype shut down, I couldn't find a replacement without some catch: subscriptions, expiring credits, or requiring the other person to install an app.  So I built my own.  **Fast forward to today:**  * \~$4k in total revenue across September and October. * **$0 MRR.** Everything comes from one-time credit purchases. Some users don't return for months. * Most revenue comes from a handful of accounts that seem to belong to the same team calling Denmark. So it's basically one customer. * When those accounts slowed down, so did my revenue. * The free trial minute also got abused. One guy created \~70 Gmail accounts just to keep calling sex workers in Australia for free 😂  Now I'm trying to figure out what to do next.  **1.
