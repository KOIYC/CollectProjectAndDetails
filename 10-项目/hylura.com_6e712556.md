## 摘要正文

7 months. $6,000. Hundreds of bugs. Countless redesigns. And 4 paying customers who have no idea how much they mean to me.  Here's the crazy part. All 4 subscribed when my app looked like absolute vibe-coded garbage, and they came from a $100 Meta ad campaign with a single static image. 😂  I come from wholesale real estate. I've done deals before, but I'd never built software in my life. Hylura is my first product.  Four complete strangers spent their hard-earned money on something I made. I know it's only 4 people, but man, that meant everything to me.  So I kept building. Probably way more than I should have lol.  What started as a collectibles scanner became [Hylura](https://hylura.com/). Market values, collection tracking, interactive 3D trading card binders, and an entire collecting experience.  I became obsessed with making it feel like a real product instead of another AI-generated app thrown together in a weekend. The amount of times I rebuilt things because they didn't feel right is ridiculous.  Now I'm sitting at **$27.96 MRR**, and after 7 months, I'm finally proud enough to show the world what I've built.  Now comes the fun part. Getting people to actually discover it. …
