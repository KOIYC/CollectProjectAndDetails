# Free My TV  **A fast, ad-free home screen for Google TV, LG and Samsung TVs.**  Trending picks from your own apps, the weather at a glance, and a look that follows the sunset. Open source. Built with Claude Code. One button to undo.  [**freemytv.io**](https://freemytv.io) · [Install](#install) · [LG TVs](#lg-tvs-webos) · [Samsung TVs](#samsung-tvs-tizen) · [Any TV](#any-tv) · [Make it yours](#make-it-yours)  ## What you get  - **No ads.** No sponsored rows, banners or autoplaying promos. Not one. - **Your apps, front and center.** The streaming apps you actually use, on a glass shelf. Pick them and their order right on the TV. - **Trending, not sponsored.** Three fresh picks every hour (up to six, in Home Settings) from what's trending this week and the latest releases, only from apps you already have, and from the services you choose. The focused one opens wide and the others show their posters. Movies and Shows get their own tabs, and on Google TV your own [Plex library](#your-plex-library-optional) can join in. - **Continue Watching** (Google TV and Android TV). *Home Settings* → *Top of the screen* puts what you left halfway in your apps on top, with its progress, and OK pick…
