# Free My TV  **A fast, ad-free home screen for Google TV, LG and Samsung TVs.**  Trending picks from your own apps, the weather at a glance, and a look that follows the sunset. Open source. Built with Claude Code. One button to undo.  [**freemytv.io**](https://freemytv.io) · [Install](#install) · [LG TVs](#lg-tvs-webos) · [Samsung TVs](#samsung-tvs-tizen) · [Any TV](#any-tv) · [Make it yours](#make-it-yours)  ## What you get  - **No ads.** No sponsored rows, banners or autoplaying promos. Not one. - **Your apps, front and center.** The streaming apps you actually use, on a glass shelf. Pick them and their order right on the TV. - **Trending, not sponsored.** Three fresh picks every hour from what's trending this week and the latest releases, only from apps you already have. Movies and Shows get their own tabs. - **Weather and markets, one press away.** The hour-by-hour forecast (or the next 5 days), sunrise and sunset, and live crypto prices. - **Light by day, dark by night.** It follows your city's real sunset, the way your phone does. - **A screensaver to match.** Full-screen photos of the picks with a big glass clock. - **Your own photos** (Google TV and Android TV).
