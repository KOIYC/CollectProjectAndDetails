# Free My TV  **A fast, ad-free home screen for Google TV, LG and Samsung TVs.**  Trending picks from your own apps, the weather at a glance, and a look that follows the sunset. Open source. Built with Claude Code. One button to undo.  [**freemytv.io**](https://freemytv.io) · [Install](#install) · [LG TVs](#lg-tvs-webos) · [Samsung TVs](#samsung-tvs-tizen) · [Any TV](#any-tv) · [Make it yours](#make-it-yours)  ## What you get  - **No ads.** No sponsored rows, banners or autoplaying promos. Not one. - **Your apps, front and center.** The streaming apps you actually use, on a glass shelf. Pick them and their order right on the TV. - **Trending, not sponsored.** Three fresh picks every hour (up to six, in Home Settings) from what's trending this week and the latest releases, only from apps you already have.
