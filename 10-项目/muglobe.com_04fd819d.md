## 摘要正文

Hi HN. I’m Ed. I built MuGlobe solo. It’s a global music discovery platform. Click on any spot on the globe and stream music from there. It’s not playlists stuck to a globe. It’s a huge ethnomusicology database that paints > 1500 musical genres onto the world then maps the overlapping influences into a virtual radio station that then generates a dynamic list from 100,000 cataloged tracks on Youtube. It’s free and has no ads. I want it to be used to cure the music algorithm blues. Do you think its a fit or that use case or better in education? I find a lot of the music in Latin America and Africa can be left on all day.
