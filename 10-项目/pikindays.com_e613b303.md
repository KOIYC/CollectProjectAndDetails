## 摘要正文

If you grew up in Nigeria, there is one sentence that can stop your heart in the middle of cartoons:  **"My pikin, come o."**  You know what's coming. An errand. Go and buy bread. Go and buy salt. Go to Iya Toyin's shop, and *count your change o.* And as you're walking out of the gate, you hear the final warning:  "If I count this money and one naira is missing…"  So I did what any normal adult would do with that memory. I turned it into a 3D game. 😂  ![The Pikin Days title screen on a phone](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/9ytsp23svyz9ezuq01wg.jpg)  ## So what is Pikin Days?  [Pikin Days](https://pikindays.com) is a free 3D browser game where you **relive your Nigerian childhood, one memory at a time.** Each episode is a different slice of growing up.  **Episode 1 is "Hold Your Change"**: run errands for the family and bring back the *complete* change. You pick your child, pick who is sending you (Mama, Papa, Grandma, Aunty, Uncle…), and off you go.  On the way you have to:  - find the right shop and buy the **right** item (salt and sugar are not the same o!) - count your change, because sellers are not always honest - dodge okadas, kekes and bus…
