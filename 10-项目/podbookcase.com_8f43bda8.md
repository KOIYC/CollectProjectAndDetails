## 摘要正文

Saw HackerBooks shared the other day (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50326821) and wanted the same thing for books mentioned in popular podcasts instead.As an avid reader and lover of podcasts, I always found it super interesting to hear about the books that helped shape influential people.So I built PodBookCase (well Claude did) – a searchable directory of books mentioned across popular podcast episodes, using parsed transcript data (which I'll shortly make available)I originally wanted to build it for myself, as I keep accumulating podcasts I want to watch and books I want to read. But then I thought it might be useful to other curious readers too.
