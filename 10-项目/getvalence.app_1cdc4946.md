Valence: a self-hosted streaming platform  # Your films and programmes, on every screen in the house. Your films and programmes, on every screen in the house  A streaming platform you run on your own server, for films, programmes, music and books. Nothing you run depends on a server of ours.  See every feature open sourced, and always will be  Your library stays yours. So does what you watch, when you watch it, and who with. It all lives on your server, and with Valence, it never leaves.  - ### Direct play, or a transcode that earns its keep  Every device gets what it can actually take, negotiated per device rather than per server. Hardware transcoding runs on VideoToolbox, NVENC, VAAPI, QSV and AMF, several with AV1, and falls back to the processor cleanly when there's no GPU. - ### HDR stays HDR  Real PQ and HLG tone-mapping, in software or on the device, because a screen needs it, not because the transcoder gave up and flattened it. - ### Skips the parts you already know  Intros are found by fingerprinting the audio across episodes and credits by reading the picture, not by a hand-typed timestamp, so it works on a library you already own.
