Memex How it works Dreaming Your data GitHub Open source. Work in progress. Your repo remembers the code. Memex remembers you. The durable context about you and your work, like preferences, decisions and background, shouldn't live inside one AI app. Keep it in your own database and let any agent use it. Hi agent, set up Memex for me. Follow the instructions in SETUP.md at https://github.com/predsymod/memex  Copy Paste that into any coding agent. It walks you through the setup and does the typing for you. In Claude, on Monday save to memex: my favorite color is red Saved to Memex. kept in your own database In Muse, on Friday hi, sync memex One new note from Claude. Pulled. what's my favorite color? Red. You saved that from Claude on Monday. What goes in Memex The durable, cross-cutting context that every tool should know, and that no single repo can hold. Keeps  What follows you Your preferences and how you like to work, decisions and the reasons behind them, corrections, people and plans, and stable background on your products and projects.
