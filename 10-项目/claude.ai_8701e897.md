I just didn't plan on leaving it with a fever.  So here's the setup. We're on vacation in Mexico. I got sick in Oaxaca a few days ago. We made it to Mexico City, and then it was E's turn. I'm still sick. Now she is too. So she's in bed, and I'm the one pushing through, upright enough to fetch things.  I was proud of one thing on this trip. I didn't touch my Chromebook until day 8.
