## 摘要正文

*A small laptop is a full build kit. Here's how far mine went.*  > **TL;DR:** A reader suggested I publish the full bootstrapping path behind an app I built on a 4 GB Chromebook: editor, auth, build, deploy, and recovery. So I did, and then I pushed the same little laptop through three more builds and a few stress tests to see where it would break. The Chromebook never broke a sweat. Through five rounds, a broken deploy, and a 44-second fix, its CPU stayed between 5% and 15%.  The comment landed while I was still answering replies on my last post.  [Alex Shev](https://dev.to/alexshev), creator of [Terminal Skills](https://terminalskills.io/), a free, open-source library of skills that extend AI coding agents like Claude Code, wrote that building on constrained hardware shows which parts of a workflow are essential, and that publishing the bootstrapping path would make a useful guide for other builders working with limited machines.  That comment changed how I saw the whole thing. I built that app because my wife was sick and this little laptop was what I had with me. I usually build on a bigger one. I never thought of what I did as a guide. Alex's idea helped me see it could be one…
