## 摘要正文

At the end of 2024 I started Kaneo as a small, unserious side project: a project manager that's fast and simple, because every tool I tried was either bloated or locked into someone else's cloud.  It's MIT licensed and you can self-host it for free. Somehow it grew: 9k+ stars on GitHub, 100+ contributors, 150+ releases, and people running it on everything from homelabs to company servers.  A couple of months ago I decided to try making it a business. A few decisions I made, and I'd love your take on them:  1. The paid product is hosting, not features**.** Kaneo Cloud is $4/month for one person and $5/user for teams. The self-hosted version gets every feature, including SSO. I watched other open-source tools move features like SSO into paid tiers and their communities hated it, so I'm betting on convenience instead: backups, updates and EU hosting handled for you. 2. Early users stay free. Every workspace that existed before pricing launched stays free forever. They took a chance on a random side project, so it felt wrong to start charging them. 3. Cheap on purpose. Most of my competitors charge $10 to $25 per user. I'd rather be the obvious choice for small teams than squeeze them.…
