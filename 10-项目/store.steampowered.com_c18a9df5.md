Hey everyone!  I'm a solo indie dev from Australia and I'm making a fighting game but you both play pinball.  You pick a character and a stage, then rack up points to lob attack balls at each other.  Every character has an ultimate move you can save up attack balls to execute as well!  It's super duper fun I promise. I hope hahaha. Look, please give it a try and let me know what you think. I don't think this concept exists anywhere in the world yet so it's a new gameplay experience.  You can find the extended demo here: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3938260/Bumper\_Bout/  Thank you so much for giving me and my game a look in during this busy period! It means the world.
