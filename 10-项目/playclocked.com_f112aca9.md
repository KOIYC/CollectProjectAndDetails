## 摘要正文

I’ve been building a free daily trivia quiz on the side: 20 questions, 5 minutes, same quiz for everyone. Here’s what I’ve learned so far.  **Lessons on getting first players ($0 spent):**  1. **Niche lists beat big platforms.** Daily-game directories (the “dle” lists) sent steady traffic. Big subreddits mostly auto-removed my posts. 2. **Facebook groups surprised me.** Trivia and brain-game groups, with a mostly older audience, engaged far more than I expected. Leading with a fun fact from a past quiz worked better than “I made a game.” 3. **Sharing is the real growth engine.** A spoiler-free result grid (like Wordle’s) gets the game sent around group chats. 4. **Getting people back matters as much as new players.** I just added a 9 am reminder notification, and I’m curious whether it moves the numbers.  **Lessons on the game itself:**  * Ordering questions from easy to hard made a big difference. People quit when the first question is hard. * Forgiving small typos was essential.
