## 摘要正文

Follow-up to my earlier post here about DeployVet, the little website security and bug checker I've been building (I built it, beginner, solo, using AI tools).  Where I was: the free check ran 600+ times and nobody paid. Plenty of people were curious, but nobody needed the paid report enough to buy it.  What I changed: - I stopped trying to sell an automated report to strangers and started checking sites properly before anyone paid - Every report gets verified by hand and false alarms get removed, so what someone pays for is real - I simplified the page, using feedback people left in this sub (one price up top, plain language about what it checks)  Today the first paying customer bought five $7 scans for five of their sites, $35 on one invoice. They found me through an email plus my earlier Reddit post. They replied happy and offered a testimonial, which honestly meant more than the $35.  It's tiny, but it's the first proof that someone will pay when the output is trustworthy and not just "here's what a scanner said."  Lesson for me: the free tier got attention, but trust got the sale. If you've been through the "lots of free users, zero paid" stage, what moved the needle for you? …
