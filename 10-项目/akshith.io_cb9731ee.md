## 摘要正文

Hi HN! Since Macs have no Face ID, I built MacID: look at the webcam, press Return on the lock screen and you’re in. It runs fully on device and if anything fails, TouchID and the password prompt still work.However, since a webcam isn’t a depth sensor, a good photo can fool it (so I include a “require a blink” option).Free & open source: https://macid.akshith.io
