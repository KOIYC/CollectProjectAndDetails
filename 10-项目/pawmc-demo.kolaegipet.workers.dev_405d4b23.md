## 摘要正文

What pawmc is (from the blog post):It’s an LLM chat app with minimal system prompt and no tools/web-search by design. It stores most important context across your conversations in isolated buckets, called “diaries” and “topics”. This means that when you start a conversation in { diary: personal, topic: books }, the AI receives your first message along with summaries of that diary and that topic. It’s close in concept to Karpathy’s LLM Wiki, but simpler, easier to wrap your head around and maintain — no extra tooling and no different types of documents: it’s just your conversations as the source of truth and the summaries of topics and diaries as the “wiki”Post links a hosted demo with a scripted walkthrough of a typical flow, no LLM.And here is the repo: https://github.com/kol3x/pawmc
