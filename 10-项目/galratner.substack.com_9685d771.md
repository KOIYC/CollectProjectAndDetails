Published: 2026-10-23  From The Vault: SAFER Systems Is an Example of How Good Software Cannot Save a Bad Company  # From The Vault: SAFER Systems Is an Example of How Good Software Cannot Save a Bad Company  ### SAFER Systems was a company that saved lives. Unfortunately bad management lead to its untimely demise.  Gal Ratner  Oct 23, 2026  On February 15, 2017, SAFER Systems of Westlake Village, California, announced SAFER One, a real-time, cloud-based platform for chemical detection and toxic plume modeling. It ran on Amazon Web Services, drew its plumes over Google Maps, and opened in any browser. The release said the launch was “ushering in a new ERA” for the company. Eric Fishman, SAFER’s president, was the only person quoted, and he explained that users were no longer tied to PCs on desks in emergency operations centers.  I had a browser-based SAFER control panel running by July 2013. It had live sensor alarms, weather-station feeds, plume footprints drawn over Google Earth, and a map layer that could swap Google for Esri without a rewrite.
