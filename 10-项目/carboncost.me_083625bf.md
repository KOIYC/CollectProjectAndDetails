## 摘要正文

Often, we focus on the wrong things when trying to combat climate change such as plastic straws, paper towels and AI prompts. However, the impact of these things are negligible compared to meat, cars, clothes and new tech. I wanted an easy way to compare the impact of different activities and purchases to better focus our efforts.
