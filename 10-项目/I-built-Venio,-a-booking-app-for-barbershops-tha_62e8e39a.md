## 摘要正文

What it is: Venio is a booking and payments app built only for barbershops. Each shop gets its own branded booking site, so clients book in a few taps with no app to download.  What it does:  • Online booking for any barber in the shop, with each barber's own services, prices and hours  • Walk-in line: clients join from their phone and see their spot, so the waiting area stays clear  • Payments: pay at the shop, save a card, or pay ahead with Apple Pay, Google Pay or Cash App, and tip after the cut  • Each barber gets paid straight to their own bank  • Chair rent tracked every week, so owners always know who's paid and who's late  • No-show fees, promo codes, client list, stats for the whole shop, and a QR flyer for the window and mirrors  • Text or email reminders so fewer people forget their appointment  Why it's better:  • Most booking apps charge every barber a monthly fee. Venio is $25/mo for the whole shop ($35 with text reminders), plus a one-time $300 setup.
