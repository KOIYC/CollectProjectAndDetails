## 摘要正文

Our son was born at 35 weeks, so every ml counted and we were logging every feed anyway. I wanted to see it like my GitHub contributions: a square for every hour, darker when he ate more.  That turned into Milo, which my wife and I built together. The grandparents and the night nurse log from their own phones, and the favorite feature by far is the one-photo-a-day gallery (the grandparents comment on every single one).  Demo with a made-up family, no signup: https://fennimore.milo.baby/demo  It's on Product Hunt this week too. It's a side project, the price is just there to cover the Postgres bill. Feedback very welcome!
