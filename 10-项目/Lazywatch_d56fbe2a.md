LazyWatch - Indie Hackers  # LazyWatch  Track what you watch. Find your people  September 18, 2026 We missed TV Time, so three friends built our own tracker  A few months ago, TV Time shut down.  I’d used it for years, so naturally I started trying alternatives. Some were great at tracking. Some were good for discovery. Others had social features. But none of them felt like the app I actually wanted to keep using.  So I messaged two friends and we decided to build our own.  At first, LazyWatch was really just for us. We wanted a place to track shows and movies, know when the next episode was coming out, and keep our watch history without making it feel like work.  Then we kept adding the things we personally wanted.  Episode comments, so you can talk about what just happened without digging through a random Reddit thread.  Ratings and reviews.  Swipes to find something for movie night or your next binge.  Upcoming episodes.  Stats.  Lists and Top 5s.  Imports, because nobody wants to manually rebuild years of watch history.
