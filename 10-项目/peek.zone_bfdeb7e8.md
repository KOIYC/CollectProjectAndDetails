## 摘要正文

Hey HN, I made Peek, a Mac app for quickly checking the things you check often without switching apps.You just need to hold the hotkey ( Fn by default) to bring up the websites and widgets you need in a layer over what you're doing. Release it and you're back. To interact with the contents inside, you just tap the hotkey to keep it open.The idea came from FPS/MOBA/... games: you hold a key to check the scoreboard or map, then release it to get back to playing. I wanted that same quick interaction for the things I check while working.Personally, I keep calendar widget, reminders widget and X feed for my Peek zone.And few other things Peek can do:- Add websites, local PDF and HTML files to Peek- Built-in widgets: timezones, weather, stocks- MCP: let your agents make custom widgets and add them to Peek- Hide unwanted elements of a webpage- Auto-refresh webpages- Work over full-screen apps- Use separate layouts for different displaysPeek is a one-time purchase, currently available at an early believer price of $3.99.I’d love to hear what you’d keep in your Peek zone :)I also made a launch video on YouTube: https://youtu.be/yziiGo_Hq6Q
