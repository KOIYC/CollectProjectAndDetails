Home Starting Up Case Studies DB Products Ideas DB Vibe Coding Tools Subscribe to IH+ Starting Up Case Studies  Ideas DB Products DB Sign in Join Steer  Web Apps reimagined to be Voice First Visit Website Steer Web Apps reimagined to be Voice First  Post 1  Revenue $10 / mo  Website Twitter October 8, 2026  Websites Imagined Voice First Every interface we use was designed around what computers could understand, not how people naturally communicate. So we learned to click, scroll, fill in forms, and write bios about ourselves in the third person. It worked. It was never natural.  That's ending. Software can now listen and talk back like a person — in your voice, not a generic one — which makes voice the next interface the way touch was the last. Steer is built on that conviction, and it starts where the stakes are personal: the page about you.  Setup is a conversation. Steer asks you a few questions, you answer out loud for about a minute, and from that recording it writes your page in your own phrasing and clones your voice. Put the link in your bio and you're done. A recruiter at 2 a.m., a client asking your rates, your kids asking what's for dinner — each of them hears you answer…
