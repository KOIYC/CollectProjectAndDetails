## 摘要正文

When I posted v1.7.0 (I'm not on reddit a lot anymore, so apologies for the lack of updates here), I said Grimoire isn't becoming a VTT. The goal is to get your content into whichever VTT your table already uses. Six weeks and a few releases later, here's how that's going, plus everything else that snuck in along the way.  # What's new since 1.7 (which is my last update on Reddit)  * **Character sheets.** Install a sheet for your game from the community catalogue (D&D 5e 2024, Draw Steel, Pathfinder 2e & 1e, Call of Cthulhu 7e, Traveller, Cosmere, Dungeon Crawler Carl, Cairn, Basic Fantasy) or paste in your own. Modifiers, saves and totals work themselves out as you type, and picking a class or species fills in what it grants. You can import existing characters from D&D Beyond, Pathbuilder 2e, DiceCloud and Forge Steel. Content packs add searchable spells, classes and feats (5e SRD, Draw Steel, PF Player Core), and each campagn can carry its own house rules. * **Built for the whole table.** Characters live in campaigns so the party can see each other's sheets.
