## 摘要正文

Hi!I made Meshword, a daily puzzle combining crosswords and nonograms.Each row and column has groups of scrambled letters. You need to work out the words, where they fit, and which squares are blank. There’s a new puzzle each day, and 60 practice puzzles across Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty.The app is built with Vue/Quasar, with PocketBase serving the puzzle catalog. I have been very happy with this stack across my various daily games.Give it a try! Would love any feedback, especially related to puzzle difficulty, I am still fine-tuning that.
