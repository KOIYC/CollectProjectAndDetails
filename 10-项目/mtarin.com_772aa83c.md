## 摘要正文

I have built a note taking, to-do application, what makes this different then others?This app targets to make note taking fun, and efficient. - Most important feature it's fast. Performance wise, and usage wise. It's a combination of convenient, clever (actually obvious) keyboard shortcuts, and focused optimizations to make it extremely simple to take notes fast.  - This is completely hand crafted application, Not an AI slop, nor AI assisted. And, a lot of thought has been put to architect the app, and design.  - Stores all notes in a simple text file, so personal data is never locked down.
