## 摘要正文

Been a minute since I showed anything on HN, but I find myself back. I finally got around to scratching an itch I've had for a very long time: making a simple, clean, easy-to-use macOS Git client that "just makes sense."With that, I present: TinyGit!!!I've tried so many UI-based Git clients over the years, and none of them ever felt "good" to me, so I created TinyGit with my vision for a super-clean, super-intuitive experience: no crazy Git graphs, easy-to-understand upstreams/commits, super-easy squash-and-merge locally before pushing.Anyhow — it's a paid app on the App Store, but I'll drop free codes in as comments throughout the day or two for people to check it out.
