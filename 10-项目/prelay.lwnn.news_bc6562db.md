## 摘要正文

A couple of years ago, I left aerospace (SpaceX then Varda) to found a weather data company called Local Weather News Network (LWNN).Today, we're launching our first commercial offering, targeted at outdoor contractors who need to know the weather and site conditions before they go. What most site managers do for landscaping and roofing is just to check their phone's weather app for the various locations they need to dispatch crews, and try to figure out if any jobs should be cancelled or postponed. We're using ensemble models for weather, and a custom ground-wetness model for soil conditions.
