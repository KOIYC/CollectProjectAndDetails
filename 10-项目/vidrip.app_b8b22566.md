## 摘要正文

I kept sending friends videos and getting back "lol" with zero clue what their face actually did. So our small team built Vidrip.  You send a video, your friend watches it alone on their own phone, and the app records their reaction. You get it back synced side by side with the original.  Android, iOS, and a browser version with no sign up: [https://vidrip.app/?s=reddit-post](https://vidrip.app/?s=reddit-post)  We also made a few free staring contest games in the same family to test it on real friend groups. Some marketing art is AI made, the app is not.  Is the side by side reaction something you would actually send to a friend, or does being recorded feel weird?
