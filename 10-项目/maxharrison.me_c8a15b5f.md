Like a lot of people, I was sceptical of the marketing. I struggled to think of something concrete to build with it that couldn’t be done with traditional autoregressive LLMs. I did have one idea that had been sitting in the back of my head for a couple of years: a tool that shouts at you if you go off topic in a meeting (a common problem I have :/). I’d actually tried something similar with Gemini Flash before, though that attempt was pretty unreliable and not very reactive. Maybe I could have ironed the issues out, but not without significant effort. What is a decision model?  The traditional autoregressive LLM generates a response one token at a time, producing some text that can often be squeezed into JSON.
