## 摘要正文

I make editable PowerPoint templates at Evolune Digital Studio. I’m testing a pre-revenue slide for AI and SaaS founders who have customer conversations or pilots but no meaningful revenue yet. The example uses fictional data.  The idea is to show what customers have actually done, then name what still needs to be proven. For example, interviews show the problem exists, a signed pilot shows a stronger commitment, and repeated use during that pilot is a separate signal.  If you were putting together a first investor deck, which field would you need most: pilot scope and dates, a named success metric, or a place to show repeat usage? And what would you remove to make this fit on one slide?  The slide is in the public preview gallery for the paid kit I make. The images are viewable without buying anything: [https://digital.evolune.ai/products/ai-startup-pitch-deck-kit/](https://digital.evolune.ai/products/ai-startup-pitch-deck-kit/)
