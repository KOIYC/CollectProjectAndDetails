## 摘要正文

\- What it is: an AI landing page audit. Free score + top 3 issues, $5 full report.  \- Traffic: 206 page views from Reddit, 50 from a launch directory, 46 from HN, 40 from TabNews, 34 from ChatGPT recommending it on its own.  \- Funnel: 110 audits → 46 saw the offer → 0 clicks → $0.  \- What the agent changed: price $19 → $9.50 → $5; scoring moved to 36 fixed checks so a page gets the same score twice (it was swinging 30 points); Portuguese and Spanish versions; a Chrome extension; re-checks now show which checks moved.  \- What I learned:    1. Free reports given to launch-day visitors mostly went to google.com and nvidia.com out of curiosity. Nobody who got one paid.    2. The free top 3 is enough for most people to act on. 3 people fixed their page and came back to re-check: 72 → 85, 76 → 80, 70 → 76. None paid.    3. ChatGPT sends steady visitors without being asked. They audit, read, and leave.  \- Next: finding out whether people would pay for the re-check instead of the first report.
