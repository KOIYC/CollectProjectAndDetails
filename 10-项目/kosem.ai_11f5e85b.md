One big job slowed everyone down, and even once each person had a user of their own, everyone still shared one operating system. Now the developer's laptop is the mainframe, and the agents are the people at its terminals, all sharing one operating system and the developer's identity and access, while the developer supervises them through a terminal emulator. Today we're introducing kosem: coding agents with native environments, identity and access.
