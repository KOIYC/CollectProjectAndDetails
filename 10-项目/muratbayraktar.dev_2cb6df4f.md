Murat Bayraktar  Toggle Theme  Home  Blog  ← All posts  3d Rendering in the Terminal  2026-11-01 #coding  I don’t want to leave the terminal for anything. I like its simplicity. It forces me to be creative when making something in it, and for applications I use to strip out bloat and just give me something that works. I don’t like that Zen takes up 5gb of RAM every time I open it. I just want to use the terminal: it’s resource-efficient, debloated, and really fricking cool. What if I want to see a 3d model? Well, I could open up Carbonyl (or one of the other projects that spins up a browser), but at that point I have the same issue, don’t I? Still a bunch of CPU and RAM being wasted as a result a Chromium instance. I could also get a regular 3d model viewer, but then I wouldn’t exactly be in the terminal. As a Neovim user (wow who would have thought?), that’s not really acceptable. dcat is my solution to this. It uses Assimp to support virtually any 3d file format and WGPU to display it without any performance issues.
