THE SENTENCE A few small rules Finding your sentence…  What is The Sentence? Explore the Tree ↗ Close × A FEW SMALL RULES  Choose the word you want to change.  Leave every other word and punctuation exactly as you found it.  Type in your word.  You get one accepted change. Once accepted, it is public and permanent. Close × WHAT IS THE SENTENCE?  A strange little internet experiment.  One sentence is released into the world. Every person gets one irreversible choice: change one word.  Your sentence becomes a permanent branch. Someone else can inherit it and make another change. Tiny human choices grow into a history nobody, including its creator, can predict.  It isn’t about writing the cleverest sentence.  What survives when one sentence passes through a million humans? Some branches stop here. Others travel through hundreds or thousands of people. Words disappear, reappear and converge. Nothing is planned.  At exactly one million accepted changes, The Sentence stops accepting new words. Forever.
