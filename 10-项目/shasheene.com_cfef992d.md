## 摘要正文

I created this webpage to compare the rates for trash hauling across the San Francisco Bay Area.I wanted to see how Oakland California compares to the rest of the region. I knew the rates were high, but I wanted to quantify how high. Especially since the cost of proper trash disposal contributes substantially to Oakland's illegal dumping problem.I found some interesting insights between wealthiest cities on the peninsula and Oakland, because a city like Atherton actually pays substantially less than Oakland for 20 gallon trash service. I would have expected the operational expenditure related to service delivery to be lower in Oakland, so rates would be lower.Also, trash hauling has some well-publicized cases of corruption (including the current federal indictment aginst former Oakland Mayor Sheng Thao involving waste collection executives).I think the transparency provided by a webpage like this can only be a good thing.Please let me know what you think and any feedback.
