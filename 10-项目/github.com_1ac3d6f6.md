## 摘要正文

## I Write More Than I Read  I used to read a lot on DEV. Then I started writing my own articles, and somewhere along the way I stopped reading nearly as much as I used to.  I want to fix that. There are so many good writers on here that it's genuinely overwhelming, and I'd rather be reading them than not.  ## The Rules  Doesn't matter if you're a veteran tech writer or you published your first post last week.  👉 Pick one article you're most proud of. **Just one.** It doesn't have to be the community favorite or the one with the most reactions, pick the one you love the most. 👉 Drop the link in the comments. 👉 Optional: tell me why it's special to you. One paragraph is plenty.  I'll read it and give you my honest feedback. I promise! 🤝  ## Why Bother  Maybe your favorite post never got the attention you thought it deserved. Maybe it got plenty, and you'd just like an excuse to share it again.  Either way, it's sitting in your archive and nobody's read it in months. This is a chance to put it back in front of people.
