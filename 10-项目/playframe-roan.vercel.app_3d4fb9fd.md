# Steam Game Finder: Filter by User Ratings, Review Count, and Tags  Hey everyone!  A friend and I recently built a Steam game finder called **Playframe**, and we'd love to get some feedback!  It started as a small hobby project to solve a problem we kept running into while browsing Steam: finding games that match very specific preferences.  For example, let's say I want an RPG with Turn-Based Combat, an Overwhelmingly Positive rating, at least 1,000 reviews, but no Roguelike elements.  Sounds simple enough, right?  Steam does let you include and exclude tags, but things get tricky when you want to combine those filters with specific review ratings and review counts.  So we thought, why not just build something that lets us search for games exactly the way we want?  After some tinkering, we ended up with our first prototype: **Playframe**.  **Here's what it currently supports:**  * **Filter by Steam review ratings:** Select one or multiple rating categories, such as Overwhelmingly Positive, Very Positive, Mixed, etc., along with a minimum number of reviews. * **Filter by genres, features, themes, Steam tags, and content descriptors:** Both Include and Exclude are supported.
