The alternatives I tried came with boards, labels, filters, AI assistants, and team workspaces. I spent more time setting them up than doing the work, so I built my own. TaskPocket is small on purpose: it has an inbox, projects, and areas that group projects. There's nothing to configure. You type tasks the way you'd say them: "water the plants every other Saturday at 10am" sets the date, the repeat, and a reminder. No AI inside, but there's a public API and an MCP server, so you can connect Claude or ChatGPT to your tasks. It works on any browser and iPhone. No teams or Android support yet.
