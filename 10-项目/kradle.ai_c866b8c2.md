Hi HN -This is James and Kemal. In past lives we led Firebase and Tensorflow/JAX respectively. HN has been a particularly big part of my (James’) career and the trajectory I’ve taken would be meaningfully different without it. Thank you!Our team shipped a benchmark: KradleBench. We test AI in Minecraft Worlds.1. The worlds are multi-agent. Humans and other AIs are in there too, often pursuing their own goals. Collaboration and conflict skills fall out of this property.2. The world are dynamic. They constantly change, which means that agents need to react. “Capture the Flag” has a single winning condition, and many different ways to achieve it. This leads to many emergent and creative strategies.3. The worlds are real-time. They are not turn-based, they do not wait for AIs to finish thinking. Nature balanced speed and intelligence in humans, we can understand this in AIs too.Underneath all this is Kradle, a platform to test and understand intelligence.
