## 摘要正文

https://reddit.com/link/1xflafg/video/hq0gbgks5qxh1/player  I listen to a lot of podcasts and kept losing moments I wanted to revisit, so I made YouTube Timestamp Keeper.  It lets you:  * Minimize, show, or hide the floating widget ALT + T (Mac: Option ⌥ + T) * Save timestamps with one click or a keyboard shortcut Alt + S (Mac: Option ⌥ + S). * Add and edit notes, then click to jump back anytime. * Search saved moments and organize videos into collections. * Choose how many seconds before your click to save. * Export and import your saves.  It sits in a floating widget you can hide whenever you want. Everything stays in your browser, no account needed.  Try it here: [YouTube Timestamp Keeper - Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/youtube-timestamp-keeper/edbfhgehhijpidglikneappfcoeojghd)  Would love your feedback!
