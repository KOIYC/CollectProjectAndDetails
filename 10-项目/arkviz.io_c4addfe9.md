## 摘要正文

Hi HN, I’m Bryant, founder of ArkViz (https://arkviz.io). ArkViz is a product to automatically generate architecture diagrams from your codebase using AI.The vision behind ArkViz is to supercharge both AI agents and humans by providing AI agents deeper relational context using structured model diagram formats, and by providing humans with a rich visual representation you can interact with, with features like zooming, panning, search, and show composing files.In this new age of AI, you can generate a structured model diagram from your codebase in seconds, quicker and better than a human. Not only can the generated diagram be a visual documentation tool for humans, AI agents can also benefit from the context of a pre-generated architecture diagram that lives alongside a project’s codebase. This concept has already been proven by context files like CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, ARCHITECTURE.md living in the root of your repository.The differentiator between ARCHITECTURE.md and ArkViz is that ArkViz generates a structured model diagram of the system, while ARCHITECTURE.md is broader in scope and purely text.
