AI Research & AI Safety Programs Map - cleverhack dot com  ## AI Research & AI Safety Programs Map  Updated September 3, 2026  Most writing about getting into frontier AI assumes you are already in the pipeline — a PhD student, a lab intern, someone with a referral. This page is for everyone else.  These are the structured entry points into frontier AI and AI safety research: programs that take applications from outside, pay a stipend or cover costs, and end with you having done real work. Several were designed specifically for career-changers, and most say plainly that no PhD and no prior ML publications are required.   Considerations for inclusion: - Open to applicants from outside the lab and academic hiring pipelines - A defined cohort, curriculum, or mentorship structure — not just a job posting - Funded, or costs covered, or free to the participant - Currently operating, with a named upcoming or recent cohort  A note on timing. As of early September 2026, most cohorts for the coming winter have already closed.
