## 摘要正文

https://i.redd.it/42t933vbqoyh1.png  My series and movies lived in TV Time, my books in Goodreads, my games in Infinite Backlog. When TV Time shut down I decided to build a replacement I'd own, and it grew into **Loomkeep**: one library for everything you watch, read, play and listen to, that you can run on your own server.  **What it tracks**  * Series, films and anime (TMDB, AniList) * Games (IGDB), books (Open Library), music in beta (MusicBrainz) * Board games and podcasts are next on the roadmap  **Why you might like it**  * Every episode, rewatch, play session and reread is dated: your history is real, your stats are honest * Bring your past: imports from TV Time, Trakt, Simkl, Letterboxd, IMDb, MyAnimeList, AniList, Steam, Goodreads, StoryGraph and Babelio * Read-only public API with personal keys, iCal calendar and feed links * Social features, achievements and sign-ups are switches you control * Installable on your phone (PWA, plus an Android app)  **Self-hosting**  * Two Docker images (api + web) and Postgres: `docker compose up -d` * Free TMDB and IGDB API keys for the catalogs * Guide for HTTPS, backups, upgrades and optional services (monitoring, error tracking, SSO): …
