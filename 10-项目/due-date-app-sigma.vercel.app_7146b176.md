# Due Date  **Due Date** is a premium, full-stack gym subscription manager and payment reminder SaaS designed to help local businesses track payments and trigger automated, system-driven WhatsApp payment notifications. Unified Dashboard & Filter Controls * **Metrics Board**: Displaying **Total Members**, **Active / Paid** members count, **Overdue Fees**, and **Monthly Collected** revenue (current calendar month sum) alongside MRR estimations and All-time Ledger balance totals. * **Interactive Shortcuts**:   * Clicking the **Total Members** card opens a read-only **Members Directory modal** showing Name, Phone, and Joining Date.
