Swytch: Run a Redis-compatible cache with database-level | BetaList  ## Run a Redis-compatible cache with database-level consistency  Swytch is a leaderless, Redis-compatible cache that delivers database-level consistency across regions. Every node accepts writes, serves reads, and follows the same order of operations, eliminating single points of failure and leader elections. Keep your existing Redis clients and connect without changes. Self-host it free under AGPL or use Swytch Cloud for a zero-knowledge control plane and durability options, enabling active-active setups and EU data sovereignty.
