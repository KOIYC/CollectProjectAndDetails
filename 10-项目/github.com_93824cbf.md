## Cloud AI Cost Optmizer  This is  a SaaS that connects to cloud/AI providers, aggregates cost + usage data, tracks anomalies, and provides real-time insights into spend and profitability.  we will solve the following problems:  ## .Cost Visibility and Optimization Problem: Many organizations struggle with fragmented or unclear cloud and SaaS spending, leading to budget overruns and wasted resources.​  Solution: The tool centralizes all cost and usage data, making it easy to identify underutilized or redundant services, optimize spending, and eliminate waste.​  ## .Anomaly Detection and Risk Management Problem: Unexpected cost spikes or usage anomalies can go unnoticed, resulting in financial surprises and operational inefficiencies.​  Solution: Real-time anomaly tracking alerts teams to unusual spending patterns, enabling quick investigation and corrective action before costs escalate.​  ## .Profitability and ROI Insights Problem: It’s difficult to measure the true profitability of cloud/AI investments without granular, real-time data.​  Solution: The tool provides actionable insights into spend versus output, helping organizations assess the ROI of their cloud and AI investments…
