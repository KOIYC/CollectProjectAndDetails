ProudSend - Indie Hackers  # ProudSend  One cold message at a time, never sent for you  September 23, 2026 I built a cold outreach tool that refuses to send anything  Founder led sales, my own product, and the usual problem: a good first message takes twenty minutes, so by Thursday I am sending the generic one.  Every tool in this category solves that by increasing volume. I think that is backwards, and I think the reason outreach has the reputation it has is that the entire tooling market optimised for the wrong number. So I built the opposite and I am fairly sure it is a smaller business on purpose.  What it does: reads a LinkedIn profile and the recent posts, offers two or three hooks each carrying the exact line it came from, writes eighty words from case studies you upload once, and puts it on your clipboard. What it will never do: send, automate, bulk, CSV, or touch your LinkedIn account.  The constraint I keep having to defend to myself is one prospect at a time.
