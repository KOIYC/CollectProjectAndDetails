# Frog It Up Hops
...
HappyGamer
...
Vampire Survivors launched in Steam Early Access in December 2021 at $2.99. The game spent months as a footnote before breaking through via word of mouth in 2022. Starfall Games is making a similar bet with Frog It Up. The game arrived as a full release on Steam on September 17, 2026. It’s priced at $4.99 per its Steam store page, and Steam lists it as Casual and Indie on Windows.
...
The studio shared the news through a Steam post that doesn’t sound anything like a marketing release.
...
> “Frog It Up is out now on steam! We are very excited to finally see our Little game on full release, we hope you like the game as much as we liked developing it. Thanks a lot to everyone who supported us during the development. 🥹🥹 We wait you in the pond! 🐸🐸🐸” – Frog It Up on Steam
...
Shipping a finished game is harder than it looks from the outside. Many indie projects start enthusiastically and stall well before a release date becomes real. Completing Frog It Up and shipping it to Steam is a real milestone for Starfall Games. That milestone stands on its own, whatever the reception looks like.
...
Vampire Survivors took most of 2022 to find its audience after its December 2021 launch. Whether Frog It Up needs that kind of patience is the open question now that it’s on Steam. The community that followed it through development gives Frog It Up a head start that most small indie games don’t have.
