URL: https://happygamer.com/pop-pachinko-steam-launch-city-pop-roguelike-164749/
Published: 2026-09-18T07:18:49.000Z
Author: Zoe Carter
Highlights:
Fans of chill roguelikes and Japanese arcade games have a new Steam option today. Pop! Pachinko launched on September 17, 2026. It came from Pangolin Interactive, a solo dev studio in Vancouver. The game wraps pachinko in a roguelike loop and 90s city pop style.
...
Studio director Gordon Pang called the early response “incredible” in the launch press release. He said the positive feedback pushes him to keep making games fans enjoy. Pop! Pachinko is Pangolin’s debut title. Pang founded the studio after his time in mobile games. The game was co-developed with Robot Lion Games.
...
Pop! Pachinko is a single-player Windows game on Steam. The base price is $12.99. A 25% launch discount currently brings it to $9.74 at the time of writing. Gamersky Games co-publishes it alongside Pangolin Interactive.
...
The game launched today with 21 user reviews sitting at Positive overall. That’s a small sample at launch.
