# Shiba Sweet Launches on Steam as Solo Dev Releases Dream Game
...
If cozy cooking sims fill your playlist, Shiba Sweet just landed on Steam. Solo developer Ya Mao released it on September 17 under the Maowoo Studio label. You play as a Shiba Inu chef who runs a bakery and unlocks cute desserts.
...
> “People always say your first indie game shouldn’t be your dream game, but I feel super lucky, and maybe a little brave. As a solo indie developer, I’m so happy to say that Shiba Sweet really is the game of my dreams, and I actually made it to the finish line step by step, pure and simple out of my love for games.” – Shiba Sweet on Steam
...
Shiba Sweet is Ya Mao’s debut and their dream game at the same time. Getting any game to launch alone is an achievement. Getting your dream game there first is something more.
...
## Shiba Sweet Is Currently $15.29 on Steam
...
Steam lists Shiba Sweet at $15.29 as of today. That’s 15% off the standard price of $17.99. It’s a launch discount, not a permanent price.
