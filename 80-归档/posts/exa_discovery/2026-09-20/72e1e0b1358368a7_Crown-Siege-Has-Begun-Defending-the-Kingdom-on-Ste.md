## Welcome to Crown Siege
...
Ready to build an army and throw it into battle against seemingly endless waves of enemies? Crown Siege is officially out now on Steam, challenging players to protect their kingdom, expand their forces, and survive increasingly chaotic battles.
...
The game launched on September 18 for $9.99 and is currently available with a 15% launch discount. Players can also try the public demo, which will remain available for the foreseeable future. It also supports nine languages, including English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, and LATAM Spanish.
