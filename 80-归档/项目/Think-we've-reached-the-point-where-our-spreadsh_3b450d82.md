## 摘要正文

We're still early and our customer tracking isn't completely broken, but I can see where this is going.  Right now leads are in a spreadsheet, notes are scattered between email and docs and the founders remember most of the important context because we're involved in nearly every conversation.  The part I'm worried about isn't really storing more contacts. It's that someone new joins and suddenly has no idea why we've been talking to a company for three months, who the important person is or what happened on the last call without asking one of us.  I don't want us spending weeks designing a CRM around a sales process we're still figuring out though.  For people who introduced one fairly early, what made it useful enough that the team kept using it instead of drifting back into spreadsheets?
