## 摘要正文

Day 6 of my startup-building journey  Today I learned about APIs and how modern applications communicate with each other.  Before today, I thought companies built everything themselves.  Now I realize most products combine different services together:  Authentication  Payments  Maps  AI models  Databases  Notifications  through APIs.  One thing that stood out:  You don't need to reinvent everything.  You need to connect the right tools to solve a problem better than anyone else.  For experienced developers and founders:  What API or service saved you the most development time when building your first product?
