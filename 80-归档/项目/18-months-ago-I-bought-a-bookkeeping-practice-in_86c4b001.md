## 摘要正文

Everyone in my feed is building. Another ai content generator, another wrapper, another "I shipped a SaaS this weekend." 18 months ago I did the opposite. I bought a small bookkeeping practice, 5 clients, one retiring owner, and I've been running it since. Numbers guy, no hype, so here's the honest scoreboard.  Then: 5 clients. Now: 14. One person, me. Revenue roughly tripled and it's boringly recurring. People don't cancel their bookkeeper the way they cancel an app.  What worked, unglamorously: \- The existing 5 clients came with the business, so I started at "has revenue" instead of "has an idea." That head start is the whole pitch for buying vs building. \- Growth was all referrals. Do the books right, be reachable, and accountants and clients send you the next one. No funnel. \- The moat is trust and switching pain, not tech. Nobody wants to hand their financials to a stranger, which works in my favor once I'm already in.  What I underestimated: \- It's a service, so growth is capped by my hours until I hire. An app scales while you sleep, a practice does not. That's the real trade. \- Some months are just grind.
