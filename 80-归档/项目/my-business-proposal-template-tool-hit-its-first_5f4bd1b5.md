## 摘要正文

I can't really code. Last year I built an internal tool to stop rewriting the same client pitches, using a no-code stack held together with hope. It worked well enough that I turned it into a tiny product, and it just got its first paying users.      The tool is simple: you answer a few questions, it fills a solid proposal and formats it so it doesn't look like a bland doc from 2010.      How the first users showed up, since this sub likes specifics: not from a big launch. I posted in two small founder communities where people were literally asking 'how do you write proposals fast', answered the question properly first, then said I'd built something for exactly that. A handful signed up. Two are paying now.      The embarrassing part, which I'll admit here because you're kind: my backend is basically spreadsheets and automations duct-taped together. Every time someone technical asks about my stack I want to disappear. But the users don't see the stack. They see a proposal that took two minutes instead of an afternoon.
