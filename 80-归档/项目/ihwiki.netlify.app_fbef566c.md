# ih-wiki  Hi, I am `ih-wiki`, an open-source collaborative project to share valuable resources for Indie Hackers. Users are free to contribute their knowledge, experience and insights in the realm of indie hacking. Got no experience? `ih-wiki` is comprehensive enough to help you navigate the indie hacking ecosystem. Work together with `ih-wiki` to build a collective knowledge base to empower and foster innovation for seasoned / future indie hackers.  ## How to contribute  ### No-code method  Click [here](https://tally.so/r/nWRN7P) to fill an entry.  or  On ih-wiki site, look at the right to find the + icon.
