Thanks  ## Top comments (11 shown of 16 reported)  **u/Conscious-Month-7734** · 2 points · May 31, 2026 at 3:04 PM UTC > Tools depend on volume. At low volume (under 50 messages per week), a spreadsheet and Gmail outperform any paid tool because every message gets individual attention. Past that, the common stack is Apollo or LinkedIn Sales Navigator for the list, Clay for enrichment, and Instantly or Smartlead for sending. None of those are meaningfully different in raw deliverability, so the choice usually comes down to workflow preference and price. > > The bigger driver of outcomes is upstream of the tool. A tightly targeted list of 200 people who actually have the problem will outperform a generic list of 5,000 by a wide margin. A message that names a specific situation outperforms a generic template by a similar one. Pick whatever tool fits you…
