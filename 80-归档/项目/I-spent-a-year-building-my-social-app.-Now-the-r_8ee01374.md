## 摘要正文

For the past year I've been building **konnectbysetara**, a social app around a simple idea:  **Social media connects profiles. I wanted to build something that connects feelings.**  Konnect is now live on iOS and Android, and I'm entering the part of building that I've been looking forward to most: seeing what happens when people who had nothing to do with building the product actually use it.  The core is pretty simple.  **Sparks & Vibes** let you share what's happening in your life together with how you're feeling.  **eCards** give you a more intentional way to tell someone you're thinking about them, appreciate them, miss them, celebrate them, or simply make them smile.  **Buddy** gives you someone to talk to when you want to express something privately — through text or real-time voice, including conversations across 60+ languages.  **Emotional Journey** quietly turns the moments you already share into a picture of how your days and emotions have evolved over time.  And around that core I've built ways for people to actually **do things together** — private conversations, Hangouts, games, shared interests and lighter daily experiences.
