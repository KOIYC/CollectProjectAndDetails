## 摘要正文

Grimoire has long had support for organizing maps and tokens alongside being great at reading and organizing all your TTRPG books. But I'm building to something bigger, and have added some new tools to that end.  **A token editor.** Now you can edit the tokens you have uploaded to grimoire directly in the application, no need to download and then re-upload to a different service, only to download again and re-upload it to your grimoire campaign. And do you have a favorite map and token artist who has created hundreds of frames for your tokens? Well now you can put those frames to good use.  **A Map Editor.** With most VTT's supporting uvtt files, you can now take your boring old 2d pngs and jpgs and make them into uvtt files in the application. Need to edit a uvtt and add some lights, delete a wall, Grimoire makes this process a lot easier now. The map editor is designed to be fast and responsive, so you don't have to deal with the overhead of editing a map in a full VTT which can be slow.     No Grimoire is not becoming a VTT.
