## 摘要正文

Hello everyone.  I'm a senior SWE, and I've spent the last 6 years in software, primarily in building B2C and B2B eCommerce stores.  Over that time period, I have noticed a pattern - most eCommerce businesses are vendor locked, tied to a specific company or SaaS.  Monthly fees (much much higher than they're supposed to be), per-sale cuts, many monthly subscriptions that all hit at the same time. Ew.  So I decided to change that paradigm - that's how Noxuz was born.  Over the last year, we've helped many businesses transition (mostly) from Shopify to custom builds, significantly reducing their monthly costs.  When I started the company, I was alone and the first 2-3 month were a nightmare. No clients, no work, no money.  But then everything changed once it went off. Now we're a team of 4 full-time developers and 1 project manager.  I haven't imagined it would go this way so soon. Thank God it did.
