## 摘要正文

I think people misunderstood what ‘build something people want‘ actually means. I spent 6 months doing probably what a lot of technical founders do, like building, improving, changing, booking demos, adding features, and then **build** again. Well, and whenever growth was slower than I wanted, my instinct was basically:  ‘maybe the product needs another improvement’  (stop right there, this is a trap)  So, I finally started looking into a different problem. Why are there so many genuinely decent SaaS products being built, but such a small percentage are actually getting consistent customers?  turns out no one really does distribution. And it’s not because of lack of knowledge, but actually lack of time. That sent me pretty deep into GTM/distribution for the last month. And the biggest thing I noticed is that distribution shouldn’t really start after the product is finished. It should start before, or at least at the same time.  I also started looking less at ‘How do I reach more people?’ and more at where are the people already expressing the problem I’m solving?  Then I started looking at current GTM tools, but it’s either AI slop, mass UGC that everybody’s sick of, or cold emails…
