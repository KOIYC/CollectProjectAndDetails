## 摘要正文

Been putting off launching for months because I can't really code, and kept telling myself I needed a proper developer first. This weekend I finally just used a no code website builder and got a real page live.      Honest version: it wasn't as instant as the demos make it look. The drag and drop part was easy, but I still spent most of Saturday on the copy and figuring out what the page should even say. The actual building was maybe two hours of the whole thing.      It's live now. A handful of people from a group I'm in have clicked through, one signed up for the email list. Tiny numbers, but a week ago there was nothing.      The lesson for me was that the tool was never the blocker. Deciding what to put on the page was. I could have shipped something rough months ago.      For those who launched with no code, did you go back and rebuild properly later, or does the first version just keep running? Trying to figure out if I'm going to regret not learning to code the real thing.
