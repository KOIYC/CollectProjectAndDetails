## 摘要正文

I designed and manufactured a bikini that I would now love to share with the niche market I am targeting. Obviously, the immediate choice was to create content for social media, the best way to reach a specific target audience. The plan was to create various pieces of content and schedule their release to intercept early adopters before the official launch, which is theoretically in a few weeks, and from there focus on sponsored ads to reach a larger chunk of potential leads.  Well, I started by posting my first video -> ban.  Appeal -> profile ban.  I create another business profile -> ban.  This is pretty much the routine for almost all social networks with a large audience, sometimes they don't bring my content down but they softban it showing it to none. I manage to publish some things on other social platforms, but the content still reaches a limited audience.  it's frustrating setting all up and being stopped by some algorithm that holds you down, especially when you see around plenty of definitively NSFW content.
