## 摘要正文

I got engaged recently and while thinking about our wedding I realized something pretty simple.  Almost everyone there is going to have a good camera in their pocket and will probably take a bunch of photos and videos.  The problem is getting all of those afterward.  Some get texted, some end up on Instagram, some in Google Photos, and a lot just stay on people's phones.  So I started building a small SaaS around that problem.  The idea is:  * Create an album for an event * Get a QR code * Put the QR code on tables, signs, invitations, screens, etc. * Guests scan it and upload their photos directly into the shared album * No app download required  It started as something for our wedding, but I quickly realized the same problem happens with birthdays, graduations, reunions, company events, trips, sports events and pretty much any group event.
