## 摘要正文

Started testing LeadIQ about 3 weeks ago after our Apollo credits ran out. The contact data has been pretty solid, maybe 85-90% accurate on emails which beats what we were getting before. The chrome extension is smooth and the Salesforce sync works without breaking every other day.  The mobile numbers though - that's where it gets rough. We're getting maybe 40% of contacts with mobile data and half of those are disconnected or wrong. For roughly 90 bucks a user that's pretty disappointing when mobile is becoming huge for outbound. My manager keeps asking why we're paying for a sales prospecting tool that can't deliver phone numbers and I don't have a great answer.  The other weird thing is their credit system. You burn credits even when they don't find anything, which adds up fast when you're doing bulk exports. Support told me that's "how the platform works" which felt like a cop out.  Anyone else running into the mobile data issue? I've been poking around at a few other lead generation tools - Prospeo, Snov.io, etc. Curious if others have found workarounds with LeadIQ or if this is just the reality of their data.
