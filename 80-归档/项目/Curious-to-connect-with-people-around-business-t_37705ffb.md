## 摘要正文

I’ve been slowly building a small electronics resale side business and I’m trying to learn more about what happens to good equipment when businesses upgrade. I’m mainly interested in clean, original-condition iPads, MacBooks, and laptops that are still perfectly usable but are being replaced during normal refresh cycles. I’m not looking for pallets, ITAD inventory, refurbished devices, or large liquidation deals. Smaller quantities are actually more useful to me — usually just a few devices at a time. I’m open to connecting with people **anywhere in the United States** who naturally come across this kind of equipment through IT consulting, managed services, rentals, office technology, or business relationships. I’m not asking anyone to give up a good supplier or post anything sensitive publicly. I’m mostly hoping to meet people who already run into this type of equipment from time to time. If that happens to describe you, feel free to reach out privately.
