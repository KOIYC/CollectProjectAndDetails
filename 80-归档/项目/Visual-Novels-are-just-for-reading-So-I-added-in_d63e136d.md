## 摘要正文

**Hi everyone!** I'm an indie developer creating an interactive visual novel in the dramatic thriller genre. One of my main goals is to break the stereotype that visual novels ***have to be passive.***  I've been working hard on active mechanics and the **achievement system!** Now players will earn rewards not only for choosing text, but also for finding hidden objects, completing minigames, and discovering Easter eggs!  It took a lot of time to polish the interface, but the fact that the pop-ups work perfectly is worth it. I even managed to add a few references to my favorite games. *(Are there any fans of Undertale and Pressure here?)*  I'd love to hear your thoughts on the style of the pop-ups and the layout of the achievements menu!
