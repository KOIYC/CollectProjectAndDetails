## 摘要正文

Usage-based SaaS founders: would you ever make your prepaid usage transferable?  Example:  You sell 100k units for $500 instead of $1,000 at smaller-pack pricing.  A customer buys the block, uses 40k, and can later transfer some or all of the remaining 60k to another user.  You still control the units, the accounts and the transfer itself. No cash refund from you.  Would you allow that?  I'm curious what the instinctive objection would be:  * it cannibalizes direct sales * it makes pricing harder to control * fraud / abuse * technical or accounting complexity * no real downside, I'd consider it * something else  Especially interested in founders already selling $300+ prepaid usage packs.
