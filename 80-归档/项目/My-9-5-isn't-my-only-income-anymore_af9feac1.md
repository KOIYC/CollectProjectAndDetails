## 摘要正文

31M here, been at a pretty normal corporate job for about 7-8 years. Nothing special, mostly emails, spreadsheets, meetings, following up with people and dealing with whatever comes up that day. Make around 72k, have an apartment, a car, some savings. Financially I'm okay, but I felt like something was missing.  What started bugging me was how predictable the next 10 years looked. Work hard, decent review, get a 3-5% raise, feel good for a week, then back to the same routine. If everything went normally I'd probably be around 95-100k in 10 years, which isn't bad, but I didn't want to look back and feel like I'd spent a decade waiting for the next raise. Watching people around me quietly get replaced by AI for parts of their jobs also made me realize that even a "stable" job isn't guaranteed forever.  I'd always wanted to build something of my own, but for years that mostly meant reading about other people building things instead of actually doing it. I'd get home, tell myself tonight's the night, then spend a couple hours on my phone instead. Eventually I got annoyed enough with myself to just start.
