## 摘要正文

I started an insurance agency 15 years ago by myself and now have grown to 9 employees. Once I got managers I started having a lot of free time and eventually started looking for additional businesses to buy.  I was looking for something that could compliment my insurance business which could mean less start up costs, utilizing tools we already had, and possibly even overlap of employees and marketing.  I eventually found a document prep business for sale. I called the owner and it began a long negotiation. We talked about seller financing and got as far as me having a lawyer to draw up the contract we had agreed on which he eventually got cold feet and declined.  In that time period I had already obtained the necessary licensing to buy his business.  I kept working on him and we eventually came up with the idea of starting a new doc prep business together that he would mainly set up and I would take over.  Once again he backed out right when we found office space. That was it for me.  But after realizing I had the credentials and got to know somewhat of the operations, I decided to start on my own.
