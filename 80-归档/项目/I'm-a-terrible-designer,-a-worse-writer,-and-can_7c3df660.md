## 摘要正文

I'm 36. Most of my friends own apps or ecommerce stores, and for as long as I can remember I wanted to be like them. Or at least pull in some real money from something digital instead of staying salaried forever.  So I tried. A lot. Over the years I had 4 ecommerce sites, one Amazon FBA, 3 apps, and I put out 2 Amazon KDP books. Most of it worked, technically. It just barely worked. A few hundred bucks here and there, and three of them flopped completely.  The worst part wasn't even the money. It was that I never felt that "I made it" feeling, and honestly I wasn't even enjoying it. The whole time I felt like I was walking in the dark, constantly running into stuff I had no idea how to do. I'm one of the worst designers you'll ever meet, I can't edit video, and I'm a clumsy writer at best. Every project hit a wall that was basically just my own missing skills.  Then AI showed up and things slowly started to change.  A friend and I built a small add on for a project management tool, a feature that didn't exist yet, just letting people copy-paste columns and bookmark things (it does a lot more now).
