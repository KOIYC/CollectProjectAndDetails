## Track competitors’ pricing, features, and messaging with AI alerts

Rival Radar monitors competitors’ websites for meaningful changes and explains them in plain English. It tracks pricing pages, feature lists, messaging, changelogs, careers, integrations, and more, filtering out noise like cookie banners. You add competitors or describe your business and let AI find them, then receive prioritized alerts and before-and-after diffs. Start free with three competitors checked daily, and upgrade for more pages, faster checks, and support.
