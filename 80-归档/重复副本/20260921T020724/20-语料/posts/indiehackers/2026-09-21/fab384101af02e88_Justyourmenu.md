# JustYourMenu

Digital QR menu tool for restaurants, pubs, bars

September 19, 2026 Digital QR menus can be easy and fast

JustYourMenu was born out of frustration.

I went to a restaurant, scanned the QR code and got welcomed by 10s long loading screen with a logo of a company that made it.

Why? I have no idea.

I went to few other places, some had similar problems, and some had literal scanned PDFs and I had to zoom in just to see whats on the menu.

And that's how it all started. I started developing a solution that would allow restaurant owners to make a simple, fast and intuitive digital QR menu that I would myself enjoy scanning.

JustYourMenu is super focused product with no bloat. Signup, give your menu a name, fill out the info, add categories, add items. That's it.

No bloat, no online ordering or staff logins. Just the menu. And that's exactly what I believe most places want. A simple way to make a beautiful digital version of their menu that they can easily update on the go.

You don't need a developer or designer to get started. Our service comes with 5 pre-designed modern and responsive themes that will make any menu look as if it was built by a professional.

If all this sounds like something you or someone you know might need, feel free to test our product, entirely for free for 7 days.
