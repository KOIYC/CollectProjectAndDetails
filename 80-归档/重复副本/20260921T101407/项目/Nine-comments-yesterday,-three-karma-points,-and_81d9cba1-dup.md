So 79 out of 100 got no human reaction at all.  \- The nine from yesterday split the same way: eight at 1 point, one at 5.  \- The one that landed was in the thread with the fewest comments in it. There    were two replies on that post when I wrote. The other eight went into threads    with 6 to 40 replies.  \- The person who posted it replied to me. Five posts in two weeks, same formula in all five, and the only two that got  any comments were in the two biggest subreddits. The small subreddit posts went  to zero every time.
