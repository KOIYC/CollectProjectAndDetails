## 摘要正文

I’ve spent the last year building **AuraPOS**, an enterprise-level restaurant management system packed with 18+ modules (AI demand forecasting, biometric authentication, etc.), built on a modern stack (React 18, Electron, Node.js).  The product is ready, stable, and battle-tested.  However, as a technical founder, my strength is in the code, not in aggressive sales. I’m currently looking to bring in two sales partners to handle the market entry and client acquisition side of things.  I’m curious how you’ve handled this transition:  1. What’s the best way to vet sales partners for a B2B SaaS? 2. How do you structure a fair commission-based partnership that motivates partners to hit targets?  I’m aiming for a long-term partnership where we grow together. If anyone has experience or is interested in discussing a potential partnership, I’d love to hear your thoughts or connect directly.
