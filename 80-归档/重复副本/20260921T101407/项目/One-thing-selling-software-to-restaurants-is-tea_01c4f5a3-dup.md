## 摘要正文

I've been working on a vertical SaaS for restaurants and caught myself making a mistake that I suspect a lot of technical founders make.  I kept describing features.  "AI menu extraction."  "Automatic translations."  "Analytics."  "Digital loyalty."  To another SaaS founder, those sound pretty understandable.  To a restaurant owner working a 12-hour shift, I'm starting to think they're almost meaningless.  The better versions seem to be:  "Take a photo of your current menu instead of typing everything."  "Tourists can read it without asking your staff."  "Know what dishes people actually look at."  "Give regulars a reason to come back."  Same functionality.  Completely different framing.  The lesson I'm taking from it is:  **Vertical SaaS positioning should probably use the customer's verbs, not the software's nouns.**  I'm testing this on a restaurant product I'm building called Menumigo.  Curious if anyone selling niche B2B SaaS has seen the same thing.  What wording did customers use that completely changed how you positioned your product?
