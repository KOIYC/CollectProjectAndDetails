## 正文

Hi HN, I'm Samar, a software engineer.I built Junior because I was spending more time fixing CI issues, babysitting PRs and resolving merge conflicts.I thought maybe i should automate all of it, and that's why I started building Junior. Junior is an agent that runs locally on macOS and picks up that kind of work. It connects with my Linear, Slack, Github etc and autopicks work for himself. It works on it and shares a report for me to review.It's open source under. I'd love feedback, especially on the permission model and how autonomous should it work. Happy to answer anything.
