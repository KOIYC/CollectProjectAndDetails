What a pack holds
 Each pack is written once and runs every filing of its type.
 Form The questions you answer, such as the legal name, the fiscal year and the officers.
 Documents The files the filing needs. OpenComply reads each file into fields and compares them with your answers.
 Rules The rules decide if the filing applies to you. A rule decides, not the AI. A refused filing is refunded.
 Sections The text the AI drafts. Each sentence must cite a fact from your documents.
 Review The people who must approve before the pack is built.
 Files The completed form, the attachments, a source report and an audit log.
