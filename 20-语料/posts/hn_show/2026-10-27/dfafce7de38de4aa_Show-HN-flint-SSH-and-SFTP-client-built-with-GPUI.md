## 正文

i was using Termius, but it ate all my ram, lagged, and charged 20 bucks for the team plan. so (being impressed by how fast Zed felt) i tried to make an alternative with GPUI.me and my friends have used it for some time and now i want to show it to other people.
i tried to make the design look like Zed because i really like it, but i'm not sure it turned out well. would love to hear what you think.it's open-source, mostly built with ai but it doesn't eat my ram and isn't written in electron.
local-first (sync and teams are optional). the api is not open source, but if people start using the app, i'll add a self-hosted version.git: https://github.com/mousEHEHE/flint-gpui
mac and windows: https://flint.mouse.pw
