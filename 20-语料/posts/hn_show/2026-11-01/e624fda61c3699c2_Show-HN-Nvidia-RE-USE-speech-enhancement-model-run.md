# NVIDIA NemotronLabs RE-USE

Unofficial WebGPU demo

> NVIDIA NemotronLabs RE-USE is a 9.6-million-parameter multilingual universal speech enhancement (USE) model designed to restore speech quality across a wide range of real-world degradations while preserving speech fidelity.
