## 正文

Author: Ewan Wills

I spent 5 years building EWatch. Fully programmable smartwatch built from scratch on the ESP32-S3. This is the whole journey: the failed circuit boards, the parts I ordered wrong, the power design that nearly made me quit, and the moment it finally came to life.

It's a real smartwatch you can wear — colour touchscreen, motion sensor, haptics, USB-C — but it's open underneath, so you can write your own watch faces and apps. The drivers are all open, and because the code is documented you can even get an AI to write firmware for it.

EWatch is live for pre-order now (bare board, build-it-yourself kit, or fully assembled):
👉 https://ewanwills.co.uk/ewatch

Source code and schematics on GitHub:
👉 https://github.com/Ewan-Wills/EWatch_Dev

If you want one, the first batch is limited — link's above. More coming soon, including unboxing and what my beta testers think.
