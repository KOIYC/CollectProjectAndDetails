## Frequently asked questions

Is Subfaro free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit: up to 10 subscriptions, automatic prices with price-hike alerts, reminders before each charge and trial end, a charge calendar, Radar with 1 savings tip, a weekly summary and monthly report by email, CSV export and the Claude connector. Pro ($2.99/month or $24.99/year) adds unlimited subscriptions, the full Radar, a detailed monthly report, calendar sync with Google, Apple and Outlook, price history for each plan, custom categories, YouTube memberships and Twitch subs, webhooks and WhatsApp reminders (up to 100 messages a month).

Do I need to link my bank or card?

No. You add subscriptions by picking them from the catalog, or in one click with the Chrome extension. It takes seconds, and no bank password ever goes through Subfaro.

How do free trial reminders work?

Add a subscription as a free trial with its end date. Subfaro reminds you before the trial ends, in time to cancel if you don't want to pay. Renewals get a heads-up a few days before too, and you choose how many.

How does Subfaro catch price hikes?

When you pick a plan from the catalog, we keep an eye on its price for you: we check more than 100 services every week and, if your plan goes up, the subscription updates itself and you get an alert with the increase. Prices you typed in yourself stay exactly as you left them.

Can Subfaro cancel subscriptions for me?

No, and it never touches your accounts. For each service in the catalog, Subfaro gives you the direct link to its cancel page and the steps to follow. Once you've canceled, mark the subscription as canceled and the reminders stop.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Subfaro runs in the browser on any phone. On iPhone, open app.subfaro.com in Safari and tap Share › Add to Home Screen: it becomes an app, with notifications before every charge. On Android, Chrome offers Install. On your computer, the Chrome extension offers to add a subscription when you open your Netflix, Spotify or other account page. Google Play and App Store apps are on the way and use the same account.

Can I use it with an AI assistant?

Yes, even on the Free plan. Subfaro works with MCP-compatible assistants, like Claude: you connect it from your account, choose whether the assistant can only read or can also add and edit subscriptions, and disconnect whenever you want. The assistant never sees your password and can't delete subscriptions.

What if a service isn't in the catalog?

Create a custom subscription: gym, course, phone plan, anything. If you add the website, we fetch the icon for you.

How do shared subscriptions work?

Tell us how many people share it. Subfaro shows the full price and your part, and only your part goes into totals and alerts.

What data do you keep?

Your name, email and the subscriptions you add. No banking data. You can export everything as CSV or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

How do I cancel Pro?

Anytime, in Account → Manage subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for, then your account goes back to Free without losing any data.
