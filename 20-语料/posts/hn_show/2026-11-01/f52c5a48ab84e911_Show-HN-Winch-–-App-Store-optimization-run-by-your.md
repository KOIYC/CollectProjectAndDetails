Questions
Which AI do I need? The one you already pay for. Claude Code with your Claude Pro or Max plan, Codex with your ChatGPT plan, OpenCode, a local Ollama model, or an OpenRouter key. Winch finds them on your Mac and runs them for you. It has no model of its own and sells no AI credits.
Do I need App Store Connect or Play Console? No. Ranks, reach, difficulty, rivals, public listings and reviews all come from the stores' public pages. Connecting your account adds your own listings, reviews with replies, the store's numbers in Results, the Ship checklist and writing back.
What do reach and difficulty mean? Reach is how many people a search reaches. Difficulty is how hard its top 10 is to beat.
Can I track competitors? Yes. Add any app by its link or name, or let Winch suggest the ones that outrank you. You see its ranks next to yours, and every change it makes to its listing.
Does it work for Google Play? Yes. Ranks, listings, store art, reviews and the store's own numbers all work for Google Play apps too, side by side with the App Store.
Is there a free trial? Yes. Download Winch and choose Start free trial: a day with everything, once per Mac, no key or card needed. Enter a key any time to keep going.
Which Macs does it run on? Any Mac with macOS 13 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. Windows isn’t supported yet.
