Common questions
What does UPCLabelMaker cost?
 A single barcode as PNG (600 DPI) or vector SVG is free, with no account and no watermark. Full sheets, multi-SKU batches and thermal roll PDFs include 3 free sheets on sign-up, then Pro is $9 a month for unlimited sheets. One-off sheet packs start at $9 and never expire.
Can I mix multiple SKUs on one sheet?
 Yes. Open any barcode tool, switch to Labels to print PDF, and click '+ Add another item' to enter each product code and quantity. For example, 10 of SKU A and 20 of SKU B fill all 30 stickers on a single Avery 5160 page.
Why do Avery 5160 bottom rows drift?
 Set your print dialog to US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and Scale to 100% or Actual Size. Never use Fit to Page, which shrinks the sheet by 3.5% and pulls the bottom rows off their stickers. Print our free 1-page Alignment Test PDF on plain paper first to verify.
Do you sell new GS1 UPC numbers?
 No. We turn barcode numbers you already own—GS1 UPC-A, EAN-13, GTIN-14, or Amazon Seller Central X00 FNSKU codes—into scan-ready images and calibrated label sheets.
Are the blank Word & PDF templates free?
 Yes. Every template row above downloads directly—blank hairline PDF, fixed-cell Word .docx, and printer alignment test PDF—with no login required.
