## 正文

I have been moving off of large complex CMS platforms and I realized most of my sites could be static sites with a contact form. There are paid SaaS options out there, but miform is free self-hosted MIT!It is built on PocketBase https://pocketbase.io/, so you get sqlite db, backups, email, API, logging and ability to extend it in any way you can imagine via hooks.I have considered combining it into one project with mistatic, so you get the static website hosting component, and the forms handling in one binary/container
