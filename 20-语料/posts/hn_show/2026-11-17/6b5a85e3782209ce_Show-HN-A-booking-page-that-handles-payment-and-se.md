## 正文

Programming used to be the fun part. Sit with a problem for a week, get it working at night, the thing is yours. The idea of bootstrapping your SaaS and breaking out in the industry used to be exciting, but now everything is a prompt and can be built by anyone, in a weekend.What nobody wants to do is keep it running and maintain it. I think big SaaS will survive, but a small SaaS business is hard to sell. I know building is cheap, maintaining is the actual work, and the part I decided to take on.Since the end of July I have been building this alone. Consultants, Coaches, tutors, I know are selling sessions with linktree + calendly + stripe link or "dm me on discord", and also struggle using discord as the place to host the session, like Google Meet or Zoom. Which means making a voice channel by hand for every call. Now it's one page: people pick a time, pay if it's a paid session, and the call link is made for them on discord, zoom or google, sent in the email.I built a discord bot that turns discord into a meeting platform like Zoom or Google Meet. Add the bot to your server, run /setup, members can book with /book on the server or from the page, and 15 min before the start it makes a voice channel where only the creator and the booker can see.One thing I'm not sure about: no fixed price. A booking is a request with an offer starting from the base price, the creator accepts or declines. Clever or annoying, you tell me.Take a look at mine https://shinetr.ee/poty or type /book in https://discord.gg/SfEVf2yFPT. The dashboard lets you fully customize how you would like.I don't know if this takes off. Trying to get users right now and their feedback. But the few months of building it were the first time in a while that I closed the laptop and felt like I made something, and I wanted to put it in front of people who would get that especially with all the fear mongering of software development is over.If you sell sessions, or you want to build something like this together, write me. I will be in the comments all day.
