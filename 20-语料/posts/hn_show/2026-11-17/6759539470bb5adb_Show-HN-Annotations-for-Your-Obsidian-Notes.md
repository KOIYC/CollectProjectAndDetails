## 正文

Recently wished I could highlight and comment on my Obsidian notes so I could come back to them later. None of the existing plugins worked quite the way I wanted, so in the spirit of personal software I built one (with help from Codex).listing: https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/annotationsrepo: https://github.com/arugyani/annotationsMain features:- Highlight any part of a Markdown file (including LaTeX)- Comment on a selection, block, section, or a whole note- Read, search, filter, and resolve comments in a side panel- Lightweight (60.8 kB) and fast (44 ms median warm load)Feedback is appreciated!
