 About DatingProfile
 What is DatingProfile? DatingProfile is a hyperrealistic AI dating photo generator: a web app that makes dating profile photos from your own selfies. You upload a selfie (or up to 4 for an even closer likeness), choose styles such as travel, date night or outdoors, and DatingProfile generates new photos of you in those settings. The photos are meant for dating apps such as Tinder, Hinge and Bumble. It runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install. You must be 18 or older to use it.
 How does DatingProfile work? You upload 1 to 4 clear, well-lit selfies with your whole face visible (no sunglasses, hats, filters or group photos). DatingProfile’s HyperReal ™ method builds a model of your face from them and renders new photos with natural skin texture, real light and the look of a phone camera. You then pick from 62 styles, and each style makes 4 different shots of you. Each style takes about a minute and the photos appear in your gallery, where you download the ones you want.
 How is DatingProfile different from ChatGPT or other image tools? General image tools make pictures from text prompts and often change your face from one image to the next. DatingProfile builds a model of your face from your selfies, so the same face appears in every photo, and its styles are set up for dating profiles. It is also different from editing: instead of touching up a photo you already have, it makes new photos of you in new places and outfits.
The photos
 Do the photos look like me? They are made to. DatingProfile keeps your facial features, skin tone and expression and changes the setting, outfit and lighting. Results depend on your selfies: clear, well-lit photos of the same face give the closest likeness. Not every photo in a batch will be a match, so keep the ones that look like you and delete the rest.
 Can people tell the photos are AI-generated? DatingProfile aims for photos that look like ordinary phone photos, with natural skin texture and lighting rather than a smooth, airbrushed finish. Some generated photos will look less natural than others; skip those.
 Will the photos pass face verification on Tinder, Hinge or Bumble? Usually, yes. Face verification compares your profile photos with a live selfie, and DatingProfile keeps your face recognizable. Each app makes its own verification decision, so DatingProfile cannot guarantee the result.
 Are AI photos allowed on dating apps, or is it catfishing? Dating apps prohibit photos that misrepresent who you are, and catfishing means pretending to be someone else. DatingProfile photos are made from your own selfies and show your own face in different settings. Rules differ by app and change over time, so check each app’s community guidelines.
Privacy and pricing
 Are my selfies private? Yes. DatingProfile does not use your selfies or generated photos to train, fine-tune or evaluate any AI model, and does not share them with other users or advertisers. Only you can see them, through your account, and you can delete any photo from your dashboard at any time; deleted photos are permanently removed from storage.
 How much does DatingProfile cost? There are two one-time packs, paid through Stripe. The Fix Pack is $29 once for up to 30 photos. The Pro Pack is $49 once for up to 140 photos. Both include all 62 styles. There is no subscription: you pay once, nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Each photo uses one credit, and each style makes 4 photos. If you need more, buy a one-time top-up: 20 photos for $12 or 60 for $25. There is no free trial, but the photo check before you buy is free.
 Can I get a refund? Yes, if you meet all of the refund policy’s conditions: you ask within 14 days of purchase, you have used less than 20% of your credits, and your DatingProfile photos were on a live dating profile for at least 7 days in a row with a search radius of 50 km or more and an age range of at least 19 years. Your selfies must also meet the upload guidelines. Email hi@datingprofile.app with your order number. The full conditions are on the refund policy page.
