## 正文

Hi HN, I am Tigran (github.com/tigranbs)
I made Fakemetrics.com as a fun experiment while testing how well Claude Opus can build pixel-identical UIs. It turned out it wasn't as easy as just prompting it, but I spent time building the system so it works almost every time.
And I am pretty sure you are seeing a bunch of people posting weekend vibe-coded apps and saying "$50K/month after quitting my McDonnalds job...". Well, even my friend tried to fake it a few months back.
So, I kinda did this fakemetrics.com as an experiment to see how well I can have the system that clones UIs, and packaged that as a very simple (I think) usable product for the people that want to be a superstar "build in public" type of a person with no revenue, but a perfect Stripe user growth screenshot.
Useful? - Probably not that much
Fun? - YES!!
The best part is that Claude, ChatGPT, or Grok models aren't that good at UI clones by default, but with fakemetrics.com I kinda made a system that produces near-identical results from screenshots fairly fast. That was worth learning!
Let me know what you think? Should I move on with the UI cloning as an open-source product?
