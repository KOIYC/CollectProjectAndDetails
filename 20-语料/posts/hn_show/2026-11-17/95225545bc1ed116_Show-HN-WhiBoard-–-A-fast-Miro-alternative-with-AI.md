Questions
Straight answers.
What can the AI draw?
Plans with steps and tasks, flowcharts, mind maps, system diagrams with real cloud and brand logos, sticky notes, text and frames. Describe it in words, paste notes, attach images or video, or point it at a document on the board. It also sums up a board, sorts sticky notes into themes and suggests ideas. Everything it draws is ordinary content: edit it, restyle it or undo it one step at a time.
Is the AI free?
Yes. The free plan includes 3 AI prompts a day (up to 250,000 tokens a month), with no card. Team adds 1M tokens per editor a month and Business 3.5M, pooled for the whole workspace.
What if I want to leave?
Take everything with you. Every board exports to PNG, SVG, PDF, draw.io or plain JSON on every plan, and your account or a whole workspace downloads as one JSON file. Nothing is encrypted and a downgrade never deletes a board.
Can I import boards from another whiteboard app?
Yes. Import → From Miro… copies frames, sticky notes, text, shapes, cards and connectors from that app through its API, up to 10,000 items at a time; your token is used once and never stored. draw.io files and Whiboard JSON backups drop straight onto a board. Encrypted backup files from other tools can only be read by the tool that made them, so those cannot be imported.
Do my teammates need an account?
Viewers don't: anyone with a share link can open the board. Editors join the workspace; the free plan has five seats per workspace and you can own five workspaces. On Team, guests can also edit from a link without an account.
