## 正文

Hi HN, my team just released box by Simular, a cloud computer for all your agents, with fast computer use built-in.box gives your agents one remote Ubuntu desktop with Chrome and a shell, which your AI can then drive over MCP while your own computer stays unoccupied.That means you can turn your basic ChatGPT plan into a personal agent by giving it a box computer; let your Claude Code pick up what Codex has left off on the same box to maximize usage; actually close your laptop and let tasks run in one persistent, 24/7 environment with all your logins and setup.Some box demos:
https://x.com/SimularAI/status/2121329778896871665?a=U4U1yYT...We started on this because we believe computers shouldn't need human operation in the future. When agents arrived, people started running them on mini computers and you see colleagues walk around the office with their laptop lid open. We think there's a better solution - cloud computers engineered for agents.Simular started three years ago to build out the full stack of an autonomous computer. box is our attempt to give agents a body to live in.Our computer use outperformed Opus and GPT on OSWorld, costing fewer tokens. https://thenewstack.io/sai-agent-osworld-benchmark/Copy/paste one command into your agent to start:"Set up box from https://box.simular.ai/install"You can watch box's live action from a dashboard https://box.simular.ai/boxWe'd love for you to try it and share your feedback. I'll be in comments here to answer any question.
