The pre-built grai.html file (591 KB) ships with a strict Content Security Policy ( default-src 'none'; connect-src 'none' ) that allows inline styles, inline scripts, and data: fonts and images while blocking direct API connections from the page.

Want direct browser-to-API calls or local Ollama ( http://localhost:11434 )? Edit connect-src in source/src/app.html and rebuild with python3 scripts/build.py (included in the kit). Direct requests go from your browser to your endpoint, subject to browser CORS rules on file:// .

Questions
Nine questions people actually ask.
Why pay $69 when I already have ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro?
 Your subscription writes in a generic voice. grai does not replace it. It sits on top: it measures your cadence, then audits every draft against 6 structural rules and a CV ≥ 0.32 burstiness floor. You keep the model you already pay for and stop paying with your authority.
How does it write if the local app has no grai backend?
 Two paths. The shipped grai.html file blocks direct API connections by default ( connect-src 'none' ) and runs in Copy-Paste mode. It builds a compact prompt under 1,800 characters that you copy into your existing Claude or ChatGPT tab (or Gemini), then paste back into grai for local verification. If you want direct browser-to-API calls (BYOK) or local Ollama models, edit the connect-src directive in source/src/app.html and rebuild grai.html with python3 scripts/build.py (included in the kit).
Do I need technical skills or coding?
 No. In the default Copy-Paste mode, you open grai.html in your browser and paste your posts. You extract your Voice DNA and write. If you can use a browser and a text field, you can use grai.
What devices and systems does it run on?
Runs natively on macOS and Windows as well as Linux in any modern browser. No installer or admin permissions needed. Tablets load the application and run the profiler, while the full three-column Writing Desk is optimized specifically for laptop or desktop displays. Phones are for reading, never for serious writing.
Where do my texts go, and can they be used to train AI models?
The local grai.html app has no grai application backend and zero telemetry. Voice profiles and writing samples (along with drafts) are stored in your browser's localStorage when available. If your browser settings or private-browsing mode block localStorage on local file:// pages, grai falls back to in-memory session storage ( memStorage ), which lasts until the tab closes. Use the 1-click .grai backup export to save your profiles permanently to disk. Data leaves your machine in three cases: (1) when you paste and submit a prompt into Claude or ChatGPT (or Gemini) in Copy-Paste mode, or (2) when a custom BYOK build calls your selected API provider. It also leaves (3) when you voluntarily export and share a .grai file or PDF Dossier (such as over Slack or email, which sends the exported profile to that external communication service), or if your own browser syncs local data across devices.
How is it different from Custom Instructions in ChatGPT or Claude?
Custom Instructions are a paragraph of preferences the model may ignore. grai measures your actual sentence lengths, openings and rhythm, stores that as a profile, and runs a deterministic 6-rule audit and CV ≥ 0.32 burstiness check over every draft in your browser. It highlights matching spans and offers targeted span repair while you keep final control over editing and copying.
What happens if my style changes in time?
Re-run the profiler on recent posts. Compare the new metrics against your archived baseline with one click. Voice evolves, so grai adapts with your thinking instead of freezing past habits.
Can I make a backup if I change laptops or clear browser data?
Yes. Click Backup Profiles to export all voice settings into a single local file. Restore Backup loads the file onto any new machine instantly. No cloud login required. When sharing with colleagues, remember that Restore replaces existing profiles on that device. Export your working set first.
