Today we are launching SIE Serverless - a managed inference product focused on small models. It’s useful for spiky workloads on 20+ task specific workloads across Search, OCR and Agentic execution.Sign up for a $500 credits launch grant after making an account :-)It’s powered by our open source inference engine that has received thousands of github stars in the last few months, battle-tested across finance, legal & tech.
