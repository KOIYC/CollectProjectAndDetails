Navigate maze-like stages, strategically place bombs to destroy soft blocks and eliminate enemies, collect powerful upgrades, and find the exit to advance.
 Whether you grew up playing the original Bomberman on NES or are discovering this genre for the first time, Play Bomber delivers an authentic retro gaming experience with modern 2.5D graphics.
