## 正文

I’ve always had a problem remembering what I read. I tried Anki and a few other tools, but they were hard to use and didn’t work on all my devices. So I decided to build my own: bundle.inkYou save articles from your iPhone, desktop (Chrome extension) or e-ink reader, and they all end up in one place. At the end of each article you get a few flashcards to check if you understood it. The cards are saved, and it reminds you every day to review what you read.I’ve been adding features as I needed them, like offline reading, listening to articles, and asking AI about the article. In the end it’s a nice reading app with tools so you don’t forget what you read.I’ve been using and testing it for my own use case for a while. Would love to hear what you guys think.
