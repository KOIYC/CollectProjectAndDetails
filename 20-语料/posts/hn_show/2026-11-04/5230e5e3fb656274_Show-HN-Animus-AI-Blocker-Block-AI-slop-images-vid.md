# Animus AI Blocker

Overview

Hide images and videos labeled as AI-generated on supported social websites. Reveal filtered content anytime.

Hide declared AI images and videos on supported social feeds. Animus AI Blocker Early Access replaces matching media with a reversible notice when it finds an explicit generation declaration, trusted synthetic Content Credentials when available, or one of your saved filtering choices. You stay in control: - Select Show once to reveal filtered content. - After revealing a post, select Not AI to save a local correction. - Enable or disable protection for each supported website, or turn it off everywhere. - Optionally hide Reddit posts with an exact AI-generated community flair. This personal rule is off by default and is not verified detection. - Reset local decisions in Settings. This is not a universal AI detector. Unlabeled content may remain visible, and content left visible is not certified authentic. Previews without a supported declaration may remain visible even when the opened post has a label. Ambiguous editing disclosures and general discussion alone do not trigger filtering. Coverage and timing depend on the website and when it exposes evidence. Experimental visual models cannot automatically hide posts in this release. Local-first: filtering and corrections stay on your device. This release disables cloud analysis, community-report uploads and usage analytics. It retrieves a signed safety configuration; the hosting provider processes ordinary request metadata, including IP addresses. Diagnostic files are shared only if you choose to share them. See the privacy policy for details. This release is free, with no ads, subscription or Animus AI Blocker account required. It works on supported websites in Chrome, not every website or native mobile apps. If filtering is wrong or a page breaks, reveal the content, disable the affected site and contact support.
