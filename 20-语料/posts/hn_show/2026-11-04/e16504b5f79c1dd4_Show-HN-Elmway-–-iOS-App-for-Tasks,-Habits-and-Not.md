## 正文

Hi, I am Victor, the developer of Elmway - an app for Tasks, Habits & Notes.It’s local-first: you can use it without creating an account. Optional cloud backup and web sync are available, and it’s free to start with paid Premium features.It allows you to to track habits using repeatable tasks with charts and insights, events for reminding you of important things without a checklist, structured notes for storing detailed information (like a knowledge base), reminders, alarms, markdown support, themes and many more features.Any feedback is welcome!
