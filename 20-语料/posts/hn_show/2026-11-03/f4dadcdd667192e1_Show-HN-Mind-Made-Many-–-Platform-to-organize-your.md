## 正文

If you want to see what org MMM itself uses: https://mindmademany.com/mmmI created this platform because I wanted to have a system that allows our different AI subscriptions to organize and talk to each other as well as keep the roles separate from the providers and their chats. Basically have our agents independent from a specific chats and platforms and swapable at any time without having to "onboard" the role again. This also allows the agents to do long term tasks beyond the individual models trained window and for me most of the time only needing to talk to the director agent.It started out just wanting to keep my agents within their own role to keep them always sharp for their specific task and mixing models from Claude and OpenAI to minimize blind spots. A team chat app is not enough because it makes visually very hard to connect and track agents in more complicated ways. So I build this to do all of that.If you were to ask me why use this instead of paperclip for example, the answer I'm not really sure because I don't know much about paperclip. I just build this because I knew exactly the use case for us, wanted my organization to always be available and organizable by the agents themselves with their role context, history, decisions, etc. always available.I use the MMM organization shown in https://mindmademany.com/mmm to develop the tool itself which means the agents themselves improve the tool when they see issues and improvements with any parts of the platform.The platform has a free tier up to 8 filled roles after which more roles are behind a subscription. There's a ton of ways to make this more efficient but to keep things under control at the beginning the subscription tiers are set up as is. We want to avoid monetizing being the middleman for tokens since everybody has their favorite AI subs anyway and prefer to chat in them.
