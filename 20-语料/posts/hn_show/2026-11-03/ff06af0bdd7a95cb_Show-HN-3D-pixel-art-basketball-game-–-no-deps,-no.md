And the basket.
No clock, no opponent. Find your spot and let it fly.
01 / MOVE WASD or arrow keys move you around the court, in the direction you see on screen.
 02 / AIM Point the mouse toward the rim. Or hold Q / E to turn your aim. The dotted line shows your direction.
 03 / SHOOT Hold Space to build power, then release. More power sends the ball farther. You can also hold the shoot button.
The ball returns automatically. Press Esc to cancel a charge or pause. Stats last for this visit.
