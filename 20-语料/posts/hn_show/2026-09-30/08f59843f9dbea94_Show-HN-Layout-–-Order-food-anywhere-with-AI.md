Does it work with every restaurant?
Pretty much, yes. We work with virtually every restaurant, and we are building direct contracts to make it even more seamless and totally free to users.
Do I have to sign in every time?
No. You sign up once through the connector and that is it. After that you just ask ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or whatever AI you use to place an order and it does it. You will never even notice Layout is there.
Is my payment safe?
Yes. Your card is tokenized once and every order runs through a scoped one-time virtual card. Merchants never see your real card details.
Does it cost merchants anything?
No. Layout brings AI orders straight into a merchant's existing system at no cost and takes no cut.
Is Layout live yet?
We are early. Ordering is moving to AI agents and we are building the layer that makes it work across every restaurant.
