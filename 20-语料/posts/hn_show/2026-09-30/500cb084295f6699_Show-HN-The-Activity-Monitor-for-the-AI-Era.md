FAQ
 Questions, answered.
 Read the full FAQ
Is Cortex on the Mac App Store? No. Cortex needs to read other apps’ processes, which the App Store sandbox does not allow. It is distributed directly, signed and notarized by Apple.
Does it work on Intel Macs? Yes. Cortex is built for Apple Silicon, and Intel Macs are supported with fewer metrics: there is no Neural Engine and no power data. It needs macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
How much does it cost? $5, once, for the first 100 licenses. After that the price is $19. There is no subscription and no trial. Every future update is included, for life. See the update policy .
Can I buy Cortex for more than one Mac? Yes. Pick 1, 2, 5, 10 or 20 licenses on the pricing card . Each license is $5 and activates Cortex on one Mac at a time, so 5 licenses cover 5 Macs. You pay once, as with a single license.
How do I move my license to a new Mac? One license covers one Mac at a time. On the old Mac, open Cortex › Settings › License and choose Deactivate This Mac . Then enter your key on the new Mac.
If you enter the key on a second Mac first, Cortex tells you which Mac has it and when that Mac was last used.
Is my data uploaded? No. Your metrics, history and Ask Cortex conversations stay on your Mac. Cortex makes only two kinds of network request: the update check and the license check. There is no telemetry.
