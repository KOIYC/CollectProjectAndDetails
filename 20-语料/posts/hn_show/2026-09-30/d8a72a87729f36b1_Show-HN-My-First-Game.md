About This Game
A top-down parking sim about the last ten minutes of a fourteen-hour day. The lot is nearly full, the one open stall is between two sleeping rigs, and you have a 53-foot trailer to put in it.
 There is no story, no upgrade tree and no arcade handling. The tractor is a kinematic bicycle, the trailer is a rigid link on its fifth wheel, and everything that makes backing a semi hard — off-tracking, tail swing, the slow inevitability of a jackknife — falls out of those two equations. Learn to read them and the stall opens up. Fight them and you will be pulling up all night.
