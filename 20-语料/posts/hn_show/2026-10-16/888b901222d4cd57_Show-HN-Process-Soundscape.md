## 正文

Humans have the ability to detect when something is off based off patterns including sound patterns ("it's too quiet!"). This toy app generates soundscape from a simulated factory so that an operator can intuitively hear when everything is going okay or when something is wrong without looking at dashboards.Fiddle with knobs to generate your preferred volume and inject faults. Have fun!
