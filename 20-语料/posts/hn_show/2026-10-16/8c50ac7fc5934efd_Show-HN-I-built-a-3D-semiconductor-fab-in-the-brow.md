KNOWLEDGE BASE & ARCHITECTURE FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Direct technical definitions, cleanroom standards, and architectural
 specifications of the SemiconFab.org platform.
01
 What is SemiconFab.org?
SemiconFab.org is an open-access engineering digital twin
 platform for 300mm (12-inch) semiconductor wafer fabrication
 cleanrooms. It models architectural cleanroom elevations, subfab
 MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) utility networks, a 153
 OEM equipment engineering taxonomy across 13 process categories,
 real-time SECS/GEM & SEMI E10 industrial telemetry, and
 microscopic yield analytics directly in the browser.
02
 What cleanroom and architectural standards are modeled?
The platform follows SEMI standards and ISO 14644-1 Class 1
 cleanroom specifications (< 10 particles/m³ for particles ≥
 0.1 µm). It models a 4.5m process cleanroom with raised
 waffle-slab access flooring (600mm × 600mm perforated tiles), an
 amber-lit photolithography core, fan filter units (FFU), and a
 6.0m subfab utility basement (Class 10,000 / ISO 7) for vacuum
 pumps, RF generators, and scrubbers.
03
 Which semiconductor equipment categories and OEM tools are
 included?
The taxonomy covers 153 OEM equipment models across 13
 semiconductor manufacturing categories: Photolithography (EUV
 13.5nm, ArFi 193nm, KrF), Photoresist Track, Dry Etching (RIE,
 ICP, ALE), Thin Films (PECVD, LPCVD, ALD, PVD, ECD), Wet
 Cleaning, CMP, Thermal Processing, Ion Implantation, Metrology,
 Defect Inspection, Advanced Packaging, Test, and Other.
04
 How does the Yield Management System (YMS) & Defect Simulator
 work?
The YMS simulator implements Poisson ( Y = exp(-D0 · A) ) and Murphy yield models across an 8-stage cascading wafer
 flow. It includes interactive WebGL 300mm wafer maps that render
 WM-811K benchmark spatial defect fingerprints (Edge-Ring,
 Center, Scratch, Donut, Random) and STDF V4 datalog parsing.
05
 What industrial standards and telemetry protocols are
 supported?
The platform supports SEMI E10 equipment reliability states
 (Productive, Standby, Scheduled Down, Unscheduled Down),
 SECS/GEM (SEMI E30/E37 HSMS) equipment communication variables
 (SVID/CEID), and standard semiconductor factory automation
 interfaces.
06
 Is SemiconFab.org free and open to universities and research
 institutions?
Yes. SemiconFab.org is committed to open access for
 semiconductor engineering education, cleanroom workforce
 training, and digital twin research. It runs entirely inside
 standard web browsers without downloads or proprietary CAD
 software required.
