A higher Elo rating means a stronger team under this model. Results update each format separately; rating points should not be compared across formats.
The format pages show recent trajectories, full histories by team, and all currently active teams. Teams with fewer than 10 rated matches are marked provisional. These are baseline Elo estimates, without uncertainty intervals or venue adjustments.
