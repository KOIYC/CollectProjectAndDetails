Harnessing computer vision for martial arts training
What it is
Jabhook is an app for boxing training. Build a custom workout, follow
 spoken combinations, and get real-time coaching based on your movement.
 Use it for bagwork or shadowboxing.
Jabhook for Mac
$40 One-time
purchase
Buy Jabhook →
 Actively developed and maintained.
System requirements
Apple Silicon Mac
macOS 26.2 or later
Webcam or iPhone
Questions? johnny@jabhook.ai
Technology
Motion capture
Computer vision estimates your movement in 3D.
↓
Biomechanics analysis
Your movement is compared with a reference library of combinations
 recorded in collaboration with a professional boxer.
↓
Spoken coaching
A local large language model (LLM) turns the analysis into spoken coaching cues.
The entire process runs on-device.
“Drive from your back foot, turn your hips into the punch,
 snap the arm out — don’t just reach.”
Experimental Jabhookery →
Project Development
One planned development is a dedicated, wall-mounted version of Jabhook for gyms.
Jabhook is built to be scalable and other disciplines are being explored.
