## Methodology and Limits

- We list a cut only with a dated source: an SEC filing, a state layoff (WARN) notice, a company statement, or a named news report that quotes the company.
- Totals count jobs the source states. "About" figures are used as given. Cuts with no number are listed but add nothing to the total.
- Voluntary exit offers are shown in grey and never added, since nobody has lost a job yet.
- A company can appear more than once when it made separate cuts.
- "AI cited" means the company or its filing named AI or automation. We don't guess reasons.
- Other trackers include more small cuts and estimates, so their totals run higher. Ours is a floor, not a full count.

More in our Methodology. Missing a layoff or spot a mistake? Tell us.

## Common Questions

Which software companies had layoffs in 2026?

103 tech companies so far, including Amazon, Meta, Microsoft. The full list, with dates and sources, is in the table on this page.

How many tech layoffs have there been in 2026?

At least 71,183 jobs across 131 cuts through Nov 3, 2026. That counts only cuts with a dated filing, state notice, company statement or named news report.

Are software companies blaming AI for layoffs?

Some are. 22 of 131 cuts named AI or automation. Most companies give other reasons, or none.

Why do other trackers show bigger numbers?

Some include every small cut, rumors and estimates. We include a cut only with a dated source, and we never add voluntary exit offers to the total.

Do layoffs matter if I pay for the software?

They can. Watch for slower support, renamed plans or a narrower roadmap, and check your contract terms before renewal.
