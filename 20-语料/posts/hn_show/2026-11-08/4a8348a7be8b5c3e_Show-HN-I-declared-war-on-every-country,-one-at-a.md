Every country vs the whole world: who survives?
We took the real map of today and declared war on one country at a time, with every other nation on Earth against it. Then we let 300 years run. No scripting, no nudging: the same engine you play at playgod.earth . We did it for all 133 countries.

How this was made
Each row is one run of Play God on Earth's simulation, starting from today's real borders and cities. The chosen country is put at war with every other nation at once; then 300 years play out on their own. Nations attack, ally, split and make peace by the same rules in every run. One run per country, so treat each result as one possible history, not a prediction. Every run replays exactly the same way from its link, and you can open any of them and change what happens.
You are welcome to use these numbers. A link back to this page is all we ask.
