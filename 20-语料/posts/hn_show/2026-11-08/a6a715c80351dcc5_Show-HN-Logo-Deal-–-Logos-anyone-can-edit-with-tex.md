FAQ
Is the output a real vector file?
 Yes. Logos are generated as SVG (scalable vector graphics), so they stay sharp at any size and can be opened in Figma, Illustrator or Inkscape. PNG exports are included for convenience.
Can I try it without signing up?
 Yes. Every visitor can try one free generation (four watermarked concepts) in the Try it free section. After that, start the 7-day free trial on an annual plan.
Can I use the logo commercially?
 Yes — logos you download on a paid plan can be used for your business. AI-generated designs may resemble existing marks, so check trademarks before registering one. Free previews are for evaluation only.
What counts as a generation or an AI edit?
 A quick generation returns four SVG logo concepts and costs 5 studio credits. Each AI edit costs 10 credits. Manual changes to colours, font, layout and browser-side color shuffling are free. Monthly plan credits reset each billing month: Starter includes 160, Pro 420 and Agency 1,050. Pro subscribers can roll purchased extra credits over for up to 30 days.
How do I cancel?
 Anytime, from the customer portal linked in your account and in every receipt email. If you cancel during the 7-day free trial you are not charged. Access continues until the end of the period you paid for.
Do you also make logos for me?
 No. logo.deal is an AI generator. You make the logo yourself. There is no designer in the loop.
