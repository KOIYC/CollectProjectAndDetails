Frequently Asked Questions Got questions? We have answers.
 Everything you need to know about setting up and running Shiptape on your warehouse packaging line.
What is packing video software?
 Packing video software records a short video of each order while it is packed, links it to the order ID and tracking number, and stores it so you can share it as proof. Shiptape starts recording when you scan the order barcode and stops when you scan the shipping label.
Which marketplaces can I use Shiptape videos for?
 Shiptape videos can be shared for return and damage disputes on Meesho, Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra, Ajio, JioMart, your own Shopify or WooCommerce store, and with courier partners like Delhivery, Shiprocket, Xpressbees and Ecom Express. Each video carries the date, time, order ID and tracking number as a watermark.
Do I need special surveillance cameras or DVR hardware?
 No. Any standard USB webcam, laptop webcam, or overhead desk camera works directly through Google Chrome or modern web browsers. Plug in your webcam and barcode scanner, and your terminal is live.
How long are packaging videos stored in the cloud?
 Every video recording includes a standard 60-day encrypted cloud backup. During this 60-day window, you can view, stream, or download local copies anytime.
Will this slow down our warehouse packing line?
 No. Operators do not type or click anything. Scanning the packing invoice starts recording, and scanning the shipping label stops and uploads it. It integrates into the physical flow they already perform.
How do I share proof with Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart, or Delhivery?
 In your Shiptape dashboard, search the order ID or tracking number and copy the signed playback link. Paste that URL directly into your dispute ticket for instant proof.
What happens if our warehouse internet connection drops?
 Shiptape includes an automatic Fail-safe rescue mode. The video remains preserved in local browser memory so operators can retry upload or save a local copy without re-packing the box.
How does billing and wallet deduction work?
 Shiptape operates on a prepaid wallet model. Each successfully completed and verified packing recording deducts ₹1.00 from your wallet. You can top up anytime via UPI or cards powered by Razorpay.
