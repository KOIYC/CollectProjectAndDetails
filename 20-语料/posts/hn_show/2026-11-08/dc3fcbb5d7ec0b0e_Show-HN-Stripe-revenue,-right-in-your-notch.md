06 — FAQ A few things you might wonder.
 Contact support →
 Does it work on a Mac without a notch? Yes. Displays without a notch get a virtual notch at the top center. This version supports Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs, Windows and phones are not supported.
 Will I be notified as soon as a payment arrives? Notifications are not instant. The app syncs at startup, then checks again two hours after each sync finishes. You can also refresh manually. New payments are celebrated when a sync finds them, so notifications may be delayed by about two hours. Monitoring pauses while your Mac sleeps or the app is closed. Historical payments do not trigger celebrations on first connection.
 Which platforms and revenue figures are supported? This version supports multiple Stripe accounts. Today and this month use Beijing time. Amounts represent successful payments in their settlement currency, before fees and refunds. Currencies are shown separately. PayPal, Creem and other platforms are planned, with no promised release date.
 Can you access my Stripe keys or revenue? No. The app connects directly to Stripe from your Mac. Keys stay in macOS Keychain and revenue summaries stay on your device. Follow the guide to create a restricted, read-only key. Activation and updates contact our service separately from Stripe data access.
 What does the purchase include? Are refunds available? US$19.99 is a one-time payment with no subscription. Activate Macs used by you or internally by your team, and receive updates within the current major version. Change-of-mind refunds are generally not available.
