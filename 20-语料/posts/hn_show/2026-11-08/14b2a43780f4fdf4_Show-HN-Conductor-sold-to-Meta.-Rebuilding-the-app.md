## 正文

Re: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50369503#50370152---Still more to do, and lots of kinks to be worked out, but Conductor 0.92.1’s own frontend can run on this project’s Rust shell and sidecar. Every one of its on-screen strings, native commands and sidecar methods is matched or listed as a deliberate difference. On scripted scenarios, this project’s sidecar produces the same events as Conductor’s.Deliberate differences are documented in docs/decisions.md.Built with Opus 5.5 orchestrating Deepseek-flash agents with Umbel and Pleach (see plotplot.ai) with Sonnet reviewing each batch and Opus fixing what the reviews found.Size: about 197,000 lines of TypeScript and Rust, not counting tests.Cost: About ~50 USD of DeepSeek plus ~70% of a weekly Claude 20x subscription.PS: Oh, and it duplicates some of their cloud functionality by running agents on cloudflare workers/containers
