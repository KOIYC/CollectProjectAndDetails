In short
Trailback is a memory for your computer.
The Trailback app for Mac and Windows remembers what you did, so you can find it again by describing it.
On a Mac it is a Spotlight alternative that also finds web pages, screenshots and copied text by what was in them.
On Windows it is a Windows Recall alternative that works on any Windows 10 or 11 PC, without taking pictures of your screen.
What it does
Remembers the web pages you visit (free extension for Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Comet and Safari), the files and PDFs you open, the screenshots you take and the text you copy.
Reads the words inside PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, slides and screenshots, so you can search by what something said.
Describe it: type what you remember, like “the invoice from the designer last month”, and it finds the file, page, screenshot or copied text.
Quick Search from any app: press Option + Space on Mac (Alt + Shift + Space on Windows), find a file and drag it into an email or chat.
Pick a moment: choose a time, see everything you had open, and reopen it all in one click.
Your day, written up: connect Claude, Codex or Cursor if you want to, and they can write a short journal of your day. Or save a Day report for any day, in your own time zone.
Works in English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and Polish.
A cross-platform digital memory app for Mac and Windows: the same app and the same features on both (each license is for one computer).
Privacy
100% offline. No cloud, no account, no tracking. Works with Wi-Fi off.
Everything is stored encrypted on your own computer.
Passwords, one-time codes and card numbers are skipped. Private and incognito windows are never recorded.
You choose how long memories are kept, and you can delete anything.
A private AI memory for Windows and Mac. It never records your screen or audio.
For Mac (macOS 13 or later) and Windows 10 and 11.
Pay once, every future update included, 7-day money back.

The app
Is Trailback a Spotlight alternative? + Yes. Spotlight finds a file when you remember its name.
The Trailback Mac app finds it when you only remember what was in it, and it also finds web pages, screenshots and copied text. See Spotlight alternatives for Mac .
Is Trailback an offline alternative to Microsoft Recall? + Yes. Trailback is one of the offline alternatives to Microsoft Recall . It doesn't record your screen. It remembers the pages, files, screenshots and copied text you used, and keeps everything encrypted on your computer. Recall needs a Copilot+ PC; Trailback works on any Windows 10 or 11 PC and on Mac.
What app remembers everything I do on my computer? + Trailback. It's an app that remembers everything on your computer, offline: the web pages you visit, the files and PDFs you open, your screenshots and the text you copy. You find any of it by describing it in your own words.
Is there a private AI memory app for Windows? + Yes. Trailback is a private AI memory for Windows 10 and 11, from the Microsoft Store. Everything stays on your PC, with no account and no cloud.
Does it work with files in other languages? + Yes. Search works in English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch and Polish, including the words in screenshots and scans. Choose your languages in Settings → General.
