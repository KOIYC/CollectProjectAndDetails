WHAT IT DOES
A few highlights, recorded on a real Mac.
Download for Mac
01 Fresh live wallpapers, every week
New live wallpapers show up in Explore on their own, every week. Have a video you love? Import it, and Muro turns it into a live wallpaper too, right on your Mac.
Every week 4K Import your own
02 Desktop, lock screen, screen saver
One card sets all three, and every display separately. The lock screen and the screen saver play real video, not a still picture.
Per display Lock screen Screen saver
03 A playlist for your wall
Put wallpapers in a playlist and Muro changes them for you, in order or shuffled, as often as you like.
Shuffle Any interval
04 Automations that follow your day
Automations run two ways: every so many minutes, or at the times you set. One wallpaper for the morning, another for the evening.
By timer By the clock
05 Light on your Mac
Hardware decoded video with very low CPU and very low RAM. Then make it yours: Play only on desktop, Pause After, Smooth or Efficient, playback speed, and auto pause on Low Power Mode or a low battery.
Very low CPU Very low RAM Play only on desktop Pause After
06 Always one click away
Pause, play, change the speed, jump back to a recent live wallpaper or check for updates, right from the menu bar. Muro keeps running with the window closed.
Menu bar Speed Recents Updates
