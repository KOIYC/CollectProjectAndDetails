## 正文

Jev caught my attention since last week's announcement, so I decided to build a tiny experiment with it.You paste one or several URLs, optionally say what topics you care about, and Jev gives every block of each page a relevance score; you set the strictness and only the blocks above it are shown, laid out in one view. A quick way to sift through the filler, or see only what's relevant to you from a website.You can try it on HN: paste news.ycombinator.com (or click on "Front pages") and type a topic of your interest in "focus on...".This is simply a POC. There's no signup, no ads, and it's free while it lasts.Let me know your thoughts.
