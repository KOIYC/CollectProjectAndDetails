GhostDeck is an offline music player for macOS with the classic deck layout you remember and a living low-poly world behind your album that moves to every track.
Out now on the Mac App Store. One purchase, all five worlds, no subscription. macOS 14 or later. Follow the build on X .

01 Five worlds behind your album
Every track gets a jewel case that turns slowly while it plays, floating over a scene that scrolls forever and listens to the music. Pick a world; the whole deck re-skins to match, and you can shift its hue to any colour you like.
NEON CITY
02 On a real desktop
A minute of ordinary use, recorded straight off a MacBook: switching worlds, shifting the hue, picking an EQ preset, and docking the whole deck into a corner while it plays.
DESKTOP · 1:13
03 The deck
Four modules that snap together like magnets and move as one. Brushed metal, chamfers, rivets, glass, and a bitmap font drawn one pixel at a time.
Ten-band equalizer with presets Preamp, live response curve, and presets from Flat to Bass to Headphones.
Spectrum, marquee, LED time Thirty-two band analyser at your volume, scrolling title, elapsed or remaining time.
Playlist with M3U in and out Drop files or folders, save and load playlists, shuffle and repeat.
Windows that dock like magnets Drag a module near another and watch the field close the gap. Pick 1x to 4x pixel scale.
Media keys and Now Playing Works with your keyboard, the menu bar and the Now Playing widget.
04 Made the hard way
No web view, no engine, no downloaded assets. GhostDeck is a small native app that draws everything itself.
NATIVE METAL
 The 3D scene is a custom renderer written in Metal: retro pixel grid, low-poly geometry, real lighting, fog, planar reflections and a film-style vignette. It idles at a few percent of one core.
PROCEDURAL EVERYTHING
 City facades, shop signs, wet asphalt, coral, sand, the moon: all generated on your Mac at launch from code. The one bundled asset is a public-domain skull mesh. The whole app is about 12 MB.
YOUR FILES, OFFLINE
 MP3, AAC, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF and more, straight from your folders. Embedded artwork or a cover.jpg becomes the case insert; otherwise you get a generated retro sleeve.
PRIVATE BY DESIGN
 No account, no analytics, no network access at all. The app is sandboxed and only ever sees the folders you open.
THEMES AND HUE SHIFT
 Five worlds, each with its own palette for the deck. Rotate the hue of any theme through the whole spectrum from the View menu.
KEYBOARD FIRST
 The keys you remember: Z X C V B for transport, arrows for seek and volume, S and R for shuffle and repeat, L for the playlist.
05 Questions
Does it stream from Spotify or Apple Music? No. GhostDeck plays the files you own. Point it at a folder and it plays everything inside, recursively.
Which formats? Everything macOS decodes natively: MP3, AAC and M4A, FLAC, Apple Lossless, WAV, AIFF, CAF and AC3. M3U playlists load and save.
What does it cost? One purchase on the Mac App Store, all five worlds included, no subscription and no in-app purchases. The store shows the price for your country.
Which Macs? macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon and Intel. The renderer is tuned to be light on the battery.
Where does the artwork come from? From your files. Embedded tags first, then a cover.jpg or folder.jpg next to the track, otherwise a generated sleeve that is unique to the title.
Is anything sent anywhere? Nothing. The app has no network access. See the privacy policy .
