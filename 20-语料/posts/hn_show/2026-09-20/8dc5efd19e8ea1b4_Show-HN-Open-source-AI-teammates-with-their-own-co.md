## Your teammates. Nothing behind the curtain.

Errand is open source, powered by Runta Cloud Agents. Explore the code, build it yourself, or help shape what comes next.

## A few things to know.

What is Errand?+

Errand is a macOS app for working with persistent AI agents. Each agent has a conversation and its own Runta cloud computer, with files and tools that stay in its workspace.

What do I need to get started?+

A Mac with Apple Silicon running macOS 13 or later, a Runta account, and a model provider configured for your agents. Download the app, sign in, and create your first teammate.

What happens when I close the app?+

The desktop app is a window into the cloud workspace. Closing it does not delete your agents, conversations, or files. Reopen Errand to reconnect and retrieve their latest work.

Is Errand free?+

Errand’s source code is open and the app is available to download. Cloud runtime and model usage are separate and depend on your Runta and provider setup.

Is this a live agent on the website?+

The interactive preview uses sample conversations and documents. It does not connect to an AI service or send messages outside this page. Download Errand to work with real agents.
