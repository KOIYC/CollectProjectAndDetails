Guia.Social — Travel recommendations from people you trust

guia /gui·a · ˈɡiɐ/ n. the Portuguese word for guide.

# A postcard map of places worth going.

Guia.Social is the platform that allows you to easily see the travel suggestions from your friends. This way you can have a view of the reviews from the people whose taste you actually trust.

## See it in action.

Are you tired of sending WhatsApp links with your favourite places to your friends? Easy — just share your Guia profile, and your friends can filter for your places, or not! See only the reviews from the people you care about.

While logged in, you'll see reviews from only the friends you choose — and narrow them down to what's around you right now.
