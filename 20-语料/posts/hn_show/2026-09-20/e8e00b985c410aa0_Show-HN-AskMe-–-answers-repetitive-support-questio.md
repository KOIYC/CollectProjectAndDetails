Will AskMe replace my support team?

No — it takes the repetitive questions off your plate so your team can focus on the conversations that actually need a human. AskMe answers what it's confident about and escalates everything else to you, on the same channel, with full context. You stay in control of what it can and can't handle.

Which messaging platforms does AskMe support?

AskMe ships with five channels today: Discord, Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp, and a chat widget you drop on your own website with a single line of script. Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, GitHub, and Linear are on the roadmap. Build your AI agent once, then deploy it across any combination of the live channels from a single dashboard.

How long does it take to set up an AI agent?

Most users have their first AI agent live and responding to messages within minutes. Our visual editor guides you through each step: name your agent, upload your knowledge base, choose your channels, and deploy. The visual editor handles the rest.

How much does it cost?

AskMe uses a simple credit-based model. Every user gets 50 free credits per month — enough for ~50 AI messages. Need more? Buy a credit pack starting at $19 for 500 credits. No subscriptions, no commitments. Purchased credits never expire.

Does it work inside my existing Discord or Telegram server?

Yes. You invite the AskMe bot into the Discord server, Telegram group, Slack workspace, or WhatsApp number you already use — there's no migration and no separate app for your members. And when you want to reach the visitors who haven't joined anything yet, the same agent runs as a chat widget on your own website from a single line of script. It starts answering in the community you've already built.
