#### Current audience sentiment

Folks are digging this gritty Star Wars turn-based tactics game, praising its strong story and gameplay that clearly takes notes from XCOM. The main gripe is performance, with many on PC and Xbox Series X|S reporting a buggy, crash-prone experience that's dragging the whole package down. It's a solid single-player strategy game when it works, but some are waiting for patches before they'd call it a good value.

Written nightly by the scoring engine from 7,168 reactions' derived labels. Our own words: it never quotes or stores anyone's post.

#### What moved the number, 2026-09-19

The score held at 82 on 49 reactions, well under the week's pace. 73% of them came from people who had played it, and those ran 78% positive against 90% for second-hand reactions. Performance gained 17 points of positive share on 11 mentions.
