## What each market is actually good at

 Company counts are a measure of coverage, not of capability. What the rankings show is specialisation: each of these markets holds a different segment of the same supply chain.

### China

 56 companies · best rank #5

China has the most companies here and the widest spread across layers, because it is the only market building a full domestic stack. Breadth is not the same as depth: several of its most consequential firms are unlisted, and so unrankable.

- #5 Huawei
- #10 Tencent
- #22 Alibaba

 Concentrated in Edge AI & Devices and Enterprise Applications.

### Japan

 42 companies · best rank #4

Japan is strongest where the stack is least visible: lithography optics, photoresists, wafer handling, testing, precision components. Few of these firms sell anything an AI user touches, and most of the world’s accelerators cannot be made without them.

- #4 Advantest
- #6 Tokyo Electron
- #13 NTT

 Concentrated in Equipment & Materials and Silicon & Foundry.

### Taiwan

 35 companies · best rank #3

Taiwan holds the manufacturing chokepoint — leading-edge foundry and advanced packaging — plus the server ODMs that assemble most of the world’s AI racks.

- #3 TSMC
- #7 ASE Technology
- #8 Delta Electronics

 Concentrated in AI Server Hardware and Power & Infrastructure.

### South Korea

 31 companies · best rank #1

Korea is concentrated to an unusual degree. Two firms supply most of the world’s high-bandwidth memory, and that single dependency carries the country’s position in the index.

- #1 Samsung Electronics
- #2 SK Hynix
- #12 Hanmi Semiconductor

 Concentrated in Edge AI & Devices and Enterprise Applications.

## What the rank cannot see

The index scores disclosed financial scale. That is a real measure and a narrow one, and the distance between the two is where most of the interesting companies live.

 23 of the 164 companies profiled here carry no rank — privately held, state-backed, or a subsidiary whose numbers are folded into a parent’s. They are not absent from the stack. They are absent from the filings.

- 01.AI no published revenue or market capitalisation
- AUBO no published revenue or market capitalisation
- ByteDance no published revenue or market capitalisation
- Elite Robot no published revenue or market capitalisation
- EngineAI no published revenue or market capitalisation
- Fourier no published revenue or market capitalisation

 Two further limits worth stating plainly. The score is weighted toward revenue, so it measures the size of a business rather than the importance of a technology — a materials supplier with one irreplaceable product will always rank below a diversified conglomerate. And it is a snapshot: rank movement between updates is usually currency and disclosure timing, not a change in the industry. Read the methodology.
