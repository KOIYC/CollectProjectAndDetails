500–1500 words, about 30 minutes

Your writing is sent to Anthropic to generate this one coaching pass. We don't save it to our database, and it isn't used to train AI models.

To block automated abuse we derive a short-lived identifier from your network address. We don't build a profile from it, and we don't keep it beyond the rate-limit window.
