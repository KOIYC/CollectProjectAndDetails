Quizzes are free and require no registration. They are designed for entertainment and thoughtful self-reflection, with plain limits: results are prompts for curiosity, never medical, psychological, or scientific diagnoses or assessments.

## Loretypes FAQ

Is Loretypes free?↓

Yes. Every quiz currently available is free to take, and you can read your complete result without creating an account. Guides and color comparisons are also open for anyone exploring their patterns at their own pace.

What quizzes does Loretypes offer today?↓

The current collection includes a 21-question aura color test and a 24-question archetype test. Each one shows a leading pattern plus the surrounding picture, with more thoughtful self-discovery quizzes planned as the collection grows.

How are Loretypes results calculated?↓

Each answer contributes to a defined scoring dimension. The server applies one consistent method, identifies the strongest pattern, and returns the complete spectrum so you can see the result in context rather than as a rigid label.

No. They are reflective experiences inspired by familiar personality, color, and energy themes. Use the results to notice patterns or start a conversation, not as medical, psychological, or scientific advice or diagnosis.
