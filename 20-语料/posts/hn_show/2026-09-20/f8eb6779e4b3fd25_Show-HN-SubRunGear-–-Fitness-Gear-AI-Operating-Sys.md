# What Gear are you running ?

SubRunGear is the living gear locker for runners — every shoe tracked by real mileage, with an AI coach that reads your training and tells you what to run next.

Free to start · Strava stays private · live in under two minutes

## Know your gear. Then train on it.

### Every shoe, tracked by real mileage

Connect Strava and your locker builds itself — each run lands on the shoe you actually wore. The public number only ever comes from runs you logged, never guesses.

- Shoes and mileage import themselves
- Per-shoe run history, pace and lifespan bars
- Honest retirement when a pair is done

### A coach that reads your training

Ask about your week, get a workout for tomorrow, or pick the shoes to race in. It works from your real runs — after you opt in — and checks whether you actually did the session.

- Grounded in your own training, not generic
- Workouts, pace targets and race prep
- Opt-in, private, and deletable anytime

## Questions runners ask

What does “verified mileage” mean?

Public mileage only ever comes from runs you logged in the app or uploaded as FIT/GPX files, assigned to a specific shoe. Typed-in lifetime totals stay private to you — the public number is the one with receipts.

Is my Strava data shown publicly?

Never. Strava-synced runs are visible to you alone — they never appear on public pages or in public mileage, and they are deleted within 24 hours if you disconnect. See the privacy policy for the full rules.

What can the AI features see?

The Gear Advisor sees your locker and the shoe catalog — never your Strava activities. The Training Coach can use your training data only after you explicitly opt in, and you can opt out at any time. Details in the privacy policy.

Do I need Strava to use it?

No. Manual logging and FIT/GPX file uploads work on their own — connecting Strava just makes the locker build and update itself.

Does it cost anything?

It's free to start — no card needed. An optional Pro subscription unlocks higher coach limits. Claim your locker and see.

How do the shop links work?

Shoe pages link out to retailers. Some links are affiliate links — if you buy through them, the locker owner or SubRunGear may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Full details in the affiliate disclosure.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes — one button on your profile page deletes your account and its data immediately and permanently, and revokes Strava access. How data is handled along the way is in the privacy policy.

How do I get help?

Email hello@subrungear.com — we reply within two business days.
