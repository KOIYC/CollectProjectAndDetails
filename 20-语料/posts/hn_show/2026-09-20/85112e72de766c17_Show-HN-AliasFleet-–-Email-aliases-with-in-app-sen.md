The Problem

## Email was not built for multiple identities.

One email address connects your bank, your shopping, your social life, and your work. When any of them leaks — and they will — everything is exposed.

### The Master Key Problem

One leaked password or email exposes your entire digital life, creating a single point of failure for your security.

### The Tracker Trail

Companies use your email as a unique ID to follow you across the web, building a profile of your habits without consent.

### Infinite Spam Loops

Unsubscribe links just confirm you're active. Once your email is on a list, the spam never truly stops without killing the address entirely.

Most sites you trusted with your email already sold it. You just haven't found out yet.

## Your real email is nobody's business but yours.

One real inbox. Unlimited identities. Complete control over who reaches you.

Generate a unique address for any website in seconds — from the dashboard or your browser.

### Use it everywhere

Give`amazon.4821@aliasfleet.me` to Amazon,`uber.3947@aliasfleet.me` to Uber. Every email still lands in your real inbox.

### Cut off anyone, anytime

The moment an alias starts sending spam, you know exactly who leaked it. Disable it permanently in one click — your real email stays clean.

## Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Complete control over your inbox, with zero complexity.

### Alias per website

Every service gets its own unique address. One breach never compromises the rest.

### Instant kill switch

Disable any alias in one click. The spam stops immediately — no unsubscribe forms, no waiting.

### Leak detection built in

When spam arrives on a specific alias, you know exactly which service sold your data. No guessing.

Standard email forwarding breaks the moment you hit reply — your real address appears in the From field automatically. AliasFleet's built-in composer lets you respond directly from your alias. The recipient never sees your real email, ever.
