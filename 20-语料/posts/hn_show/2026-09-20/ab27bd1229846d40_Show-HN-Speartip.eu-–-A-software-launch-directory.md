# Submit your SaaS or software to Speartip.

Give your product a proper launch.

Speartip is a European software directory and launch platform for products from EU- and EEA-based companies. Submit your SaaS, AI tool, or business software for review. Every approved product receives a moderated 30-day launch and lasting product and company pages.

One submission, one clear path.

## Who can list software on Speartip?

Speartip accepts software from companies established in the European Union or European Economic Area. Every submission must pass review before it becomes a public listing.

### European company required

The provider must be established in an EU or EEA country. Company and product details are reviewed together.

### SaaS and software products

Submit SaaS, AI tools, developer tools, and other software products that buyers can evaluate from accurate public information.

### Free listing available

## Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about what Pro Launch adds and what payment never changes.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro Launch is a one-time purchase for one launch. It does not renew.

What does Free Launch include?

Free Launch includes review, public product and company pages, search and browse visibility, up to three categories, five alternatives, a 30-day launch, and the neutral leaderboard.

Does Pro improve my neutral leaderboard rank?

No. Valid upvotes and followers determine neutral rank. Pro Launch adds clearly labeled priority, a Pro badge, and a richer page without changing the ranking.

Is Weekly Drops exclusive to Pro?

Yes. Weekly Drops is reserved for eligible Pro Launch products. Inclusion still depends on an active campaign and available newsletter space.

What happens after the Pro launch ends?

Your approved product page, company page, launch history, and Pro page content remain. Time-limited priority ends when the launch does.

Does every submission go through review?

Yes. Payment buys launch services, not approval. Free Launch and Pro Launch both require review before publication.
