# Where is the weather good, and when?

Sun Season is a world map of holiday weather: pick the weather you like and a month, and see where it fits — or pick a place and see which months fit. Every verdict comes from 30-year climate normals, so it can tell a monsoon from a place that is simply wet all year.

## What is Sun Season?

A world map that answers one question: does the weather fit the holiday you want, in the month you can go? Pick beach, tropics, warm, cool or snow, pick a month, and the map colours every place on Earth by how well it fits — gold where it does, and otherwise the reason: too cold, too hot, too dry, too rainy, or the monsoon itself. Tap any place for its twelve-month rain and temperature chart and the months to go.

It runs on 30-year climate normals (WorldClim 2.1, 1970–2000) rather than forecasts, and judges rain by each place's own seasonal pattern, so it can tell a monsoon from a place that is simply wet all year. Read the method or fetch the open data.
