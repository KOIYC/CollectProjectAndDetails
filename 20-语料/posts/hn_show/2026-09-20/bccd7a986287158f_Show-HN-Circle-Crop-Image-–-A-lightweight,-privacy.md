### Crop Image to Circle Online — Tips for Best Results

For the best circle crop results, start with a high-resolution source image. Photos at 1000×1000 pixels or larger produce the sharpest circular output. When cropping a portrait, center the face within the circle and leave a small margin around the head. For logos, zoom out slightly so text and icons do not touch the circular edge.
