# Tola: A simple to-do list

Write. Plan. Do.

AVAILABLE NOW ON iOS AND ANDROID

OPEN THE APP · START WITH TODAY

01

Start right away No account required.

02

Works offline Your tasks stay available.

03

Reminders when needed Sent by your device.

## Say it. Make it a task

Speak a task, a date or a reminder. Watch your words appear, then check the details before saving.

> Send the quote tomorrow at ten. Remind me ten minutes before.

01 Speak naturally Your words appear as you speak. 02 Check, then save Review the task, date and reminder. Edit anything before saving.

Voice input needs an internet connection. Audio is processed online.

Your voice, becoming a task.

## Simple enough to use every day

Tola keeps only what you need.

01

### Start with today

See today’s tasks and the days ahead at a glance.

02

### Save it for someday

Keep unscheduled ideas in Later.

03

### Look back when needed

Completed tasks stay in Done.

## Write it down. Pick a day. Do it

Add a date or reminder when needed. Otherwise, leave it in Later.

Focus on today. See what’s next.

## The everyday essentials

01

### Choose your language

Switch between eight languages in Settings. Unsupported languages use English.

02

### Clear dates

Complete overdue tasks or move them yourself.

03

### Repeating tasks

Repeat by day, week, month, or your own schedule.

04

### Reminders when you want them

Set a time to be reminded. Everything else stays quiet.

05

### Paper or Ink

Switch between light Paper and dark Ink.

## No account. Your lists, on your device

Add, edit and complete tasks offline. Optional voice input processes audio online.

## The short version

Where can I download Tola?

Tola is available now on the App Store for iOS and Google Play for Android.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start.

Does it work without internet?

Create, edit and complete tasks offline. Voice input requires an internet connection.

Will my tasks sync to a new phone?

Not in the first version. There is no cross-device sync.

## Write it down. Get on with your day

Tola is now available on iOS and Android.
