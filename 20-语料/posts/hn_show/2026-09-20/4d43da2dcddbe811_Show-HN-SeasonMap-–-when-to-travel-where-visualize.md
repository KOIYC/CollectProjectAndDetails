## The score isn't the whole trip.

A number tells you the weather. It won't tell you the blossom is out, that the city is at peak crowds, that the smoke season starts next week, or that half of Lisbon is shut in August. SeasonMap says those too — beside the score, never inside it, and free on every place.

Lisbon, August

> “August reads fine on paper and is fine — the nortada wind off the Atlantic keeps it from feeling like Seville. What the score can't tell you is that half the city is shut for holidays and every restaurant that stays open is booked. Go for the beach at Costa da Caparica, not the food.”

Dubrovnik, July

The Dubrovnik forum's consistent line on July: hot, humid, and the Old Town is genuinely overwhelmed once cruise ships are in port — several regulars specifically recommend an early-morning walk before the crowds arrive, or shifting a visit to June or September instead.

None of it touches the score — a festival can't make a wet month dry. It sits beside the number so you can see the catch, and it's free for everyone. The forum notes are our own summary of a public thread with a link to the real one, never a copy of somebody's review.
