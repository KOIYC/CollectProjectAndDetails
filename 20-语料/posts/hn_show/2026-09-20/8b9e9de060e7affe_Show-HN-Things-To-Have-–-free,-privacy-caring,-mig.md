## More than a wishlist

- ### For everyone

A birthday wishlist to share with anyone, or a private list of the things you are saving up for
- ### For enthusiasts

Your desk setup, your vinyl shelf, your everyday carry — one link that shows all of it
- ### For creators

Gift guides and gear lists with your own affiliate links — you keep everything they earn

### Free & Frictionless

- Completely free with nothing hidden behind a paywall
- Public lists open for anyone — no account needed to read one
- Signing up takes only an email, and there is no password to remember
- Works in any browser, on any device, with nothing to install

### Feature-packed

- Flexible lists — a birthday wishlist, a private collection, or an article-like recommendation guide
- Paste a link and the title, photo and price will autofill themselves
- Add images and notes so each thing is exactly the one you mean

### Social-enough

- Notify, and be notified about birthdays
- Reservations, so two people never turn up with the same gift
- An activity feed of what the people you follow have added lately
- Follow like-minded people and find new things at your own pace
- A truly minimal interface that doesn't force a specific aesthetic

### Privacy-first

- Everything is private by default — you decide what gets shared
- Check what a visitor sees before you share anything
- Nothing recommended at you — it is your wishlist, not a feed
- GDPR compliant, with no ads and no tracking cookies
- Export everything you have added whenever you like — no lock-in

## FAQ

Is it free?

Yes — Things To Have is free to use without limits, and there's no paywall coming later.

How do I create a public wishlist?

Sign up with your email, add the things you want, and move them to a public list — all can be done in under five minutes.

Can I use it without social features?

Yes — keep every list private and skip the social side entirely.

Can I share my wishlist with someone who doesn't use the app?

Yes. Public lists open in any browser — just send the link. No account needed.

Can I export my data?

Yes — export everything you have added from settings at any time.
