Snail Walk: Step Tracking Survival Fitness Game

## FAQ

Who is Bob?

A snail. He’s large, he’s calm, and he’s not chasing you personally - he’s just going this way. If he reaches you, you die. Nothing personal.

When can I play?

Right now. Snail Walk is live on the App Store and Google Play.

Where can I play?

Snail Walk is a mobile game for iOS and Android. No web or desktop version - the whole point is walking around in real life.

What happens if the snail catches me?

You don’t want to know. Keep walking.

Is my health data shared with other players?

No. Only your daily step total is used - never GPS, location, or health details.

Does Snail Walk use GPS?

No. Snail Walk is a walking game without GPS - it only reads your daily step count, from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. No location, no routes, no map of where you went.

Does it work indoors or on a treadmill?

Yes. Steps are steps - treadmill, hallway, pacing while on a call. Bob does not care where you take them, only that you take them.
