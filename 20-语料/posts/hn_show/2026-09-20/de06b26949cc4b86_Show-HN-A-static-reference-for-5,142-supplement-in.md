# Common supplement interactions and timing

Get the short answer first, then see timing guidance and when extra care is needed.

Browse 5,142 supplement pairs, with common combinations first. Page 1 of 52.

## Can You Take Calcium and Iron Together?

Calcium and iron can be taken on the same day, but separate supplements are best taken at different times.

## Can You Take Iron and Zinc Together?

Iron doses of 25 mg or more may reduce zinc absorption when taken together; separate the doses.

## Can Zinc Affect Copper?

High-dose zinc taken for several weeks can reduce copper absorption; dose and duration matter.

## Can You Take Caffeine and Calcium Together?

Caffeine may slightly reduce calcium absorption, but most people do not need a fixed gap between them.

## Can You Take Iron and Vitamin C Together?

Iron and vitamin C can be taken together; vitamin C can help the body absorb nonheme iron.

## Can You Take Chromium and Vitamin C Together?

Chromium and vitamin C can be taken together; no fixed spacing interval is needed.

## Can You Take Iron and Manganese Together?

Typical iron and manganese doses can usually be taken together; no fixed gap has been established.

## Can You Take Calcium and Vitamin D3 Together?

Calcium and vitamin D3 can be taken together; vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium.

## Can You Take Vitamin E and Vitamin K1 Together?

Standard doses are usually compatible; long-term high-dose vitamin E may interfere with vitamin K-related clotting.

## Can You Take Magnesium and Vitamin D3 Together?

Magnesium and vitamin D3 can be taken together, but taking D3 does not automatically mean you need extra magnesium.

## Can You Take Vitamin D3 and Vitamin K2 Together?

Vitamin D3 and K2 can be taken together, but K2 is not a mandatory add-on for everyone who takes D3.

## Can You Take Magnesium and Vitamin B6 Together?

Magnesium and vitamin B6 can be taken together; a small study suggests a relationship, but no fixed gap is needed.

## Can You Take Vitamin C and Vitamin B12 Together?

Vitamin C and B12 can usually be taken together; official reviews do not confirm a mandatory two-hour gap.

## Can You Take Calcium and Phosphorus Together?

Calcium and phosphorus can usually be taken together; avoid chronically unbalanced high-dose supplements.

## Can You Take Choline and Folate Together?

Choline and folate can be taken together; they support related pathways and do not need to be separated.
