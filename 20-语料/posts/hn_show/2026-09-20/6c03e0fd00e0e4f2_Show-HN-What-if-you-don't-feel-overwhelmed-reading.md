# Kora – Focus Reader & Website Shield

Overview

Read one paragraph at a time, save your place, and block distracting websites. Private, local, and free.

Long pages shouldn’t feel like a wall of text. Kora is a calm focus reader that helps you finish more of what you open. It keeps your current paragraph clear, gently softens the surrounding text, and shows simple progress through the page. Start reading directly from the quiet page-edge button - no popup required. Move paragraph by paragraph, scroll normally, or use keyboard shortcuts. MAKE READING FEEL BETTER • Focus on one paragraph at a time • Resume where you stopped • Choose the original font, a clean sans, or a reading serif • Adjust text size and spacing • Mark pages as read and track today’s completed pages • Start directly from readable pages REDUCE DISTRACTIONS Kora also includes a website shield. Choose the websites that regularly pull you away and block them during a Kora focus session—or turn on the shield manually. PRIVATE BY DESIGN Page text stays on the page. Kora stores only your local preferences, a hashed page key, and reading progress. It does not collect article text, create browsing profiles, show advertising, or require an account. One page. One paragraph. Keep going.
