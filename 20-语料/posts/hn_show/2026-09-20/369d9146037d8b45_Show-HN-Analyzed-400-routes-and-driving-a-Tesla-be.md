## The short answers

### At what distance does flying beat driving a Tesla?

Across 430 trips compared on Fly or Tesla?, the verdict flips at about 350 miles of driving: beyond that, the plane wins more often than the car (252 trips are that long or longer). Under it, driving usually wins, and the margin grows with every extra seat.

### Is it cheaper to fly or drive a Tesla?

Of 430 trips, driving wins 274, flying wins 135 and 21 are close enough to call a toss-up. Where driving wins it keeps $301 on average for a single traveller, counting charging, tyres, maintenance, road food and any hotel night the drive forces against airfare, bags and getting to and from both airports.

### What does it cost per mile to drive versus fly?

under 300 miles: $0.12 a mile to drive against $1.74 to fly (145 trips); 300 to 600 miles: $0.16 a mile to drive against $0.84 to fly (141 trips); 600 to 1,000 miles: $0.39 a mile to drive against $0.67 to fly (43 trips); over 1,000 miles: $0.25 a mile to drive against $0.34 to fly (101 trips).

### How many Supercharger stops does a long drive need?

under 300 miles averages 1.0 stops; 300 to 600 miles averages 2.4 stops; 600 to 1,000 miles averages 4.2 stops; over 1,000 miles averages 12.9 stops, based on usable range between charges rather than a rule of thumb.

### At what distance does driving become more stressful than flying?

Around 900 miles. Below that the drive scores calmer than the flight on a 0-100 scale; above it the hours behind the wheel overtake the airport.

### Is driving an EV lower carbon than flying?

On 427 of 430 trips the car is the cleaner seat, averaging 7.4× less CO2e than the same trip by plane, counting grid mix where the car charges and contrails on the flight.
