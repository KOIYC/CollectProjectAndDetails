## About This Software

Never Lose Your Crosshair Again !

Tired of your static crosshair blending into the bright sky or disappearing in dark shadows? Smart Crosshair is the ultimate lightweight aiming utility designed to dynamically adapt to your screen, ensuring your crosshair is always perfectly visible, no matter the environment.

​ Core Features:

 Smart Adaptive Color Technology: Our real-time pixel scanning engine instantly analyzes the background behind your crosshair. It shifts to a contrasting color on the fly—turning dark against bright skies and glowing bright inside dark buildings. Important Setup Note: The color engine scans actual screen pixels. For perfect color adaptation, disable your default in-game crosshair or change its shape so it doesn't block the scanner.

 Absolute Zero Performance Impact: There are no heavy frameworks, no bloated background processes, and absolutely zero FPS drops. It runs incredibly fast and consumes virtually no CPU or RAM.

 Fullscreen Windowed Support: Renders flawlessly over any game running in Borderless or Fullscreen Windowed mode using optimized topmost window attributes.

 Anti-Cheat Safe: Operates strictly as a visual overlay without hooking into game files or memory, ensuring completely safe usage.

Deep Customization & Control:

 Built-in Pixel Crosshair Editor: Customize unique crosshairs using a 32x32 matrix (per pixel opacity tuning).

 Shape & Size Options: Fine-tune shapes, sizes, opacity, color dynamics, and color regimes.

 Right-Click Auto-Hide: Seamlessly hides the crosshair automatically when you press the right mouse button.

 On-Screen Positioning: Freely move and adjust the crosshair location across the screen with precise hotkeys.

 Game Presets: Save individual configuration presets for different games.

 Steam Workshop Integration: Share yours or subscribe to community-created presets on Steam, then instantly load and apply them in-app via your preset slots.
