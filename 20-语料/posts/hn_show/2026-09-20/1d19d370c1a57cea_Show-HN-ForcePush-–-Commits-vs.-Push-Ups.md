### No analytics backend

Your history stays in the app's local store. The app contacts GitHub for contribution counts and our licensing service for license validation.

### Only read:user

Reads your contribution counts, never your code. Token lives in the Keychain.

### Optional device-code sign-in

A GitHub username works with zero setup. To count private commits too, approve a short code on github.com: no password, no token to mint by hand.
