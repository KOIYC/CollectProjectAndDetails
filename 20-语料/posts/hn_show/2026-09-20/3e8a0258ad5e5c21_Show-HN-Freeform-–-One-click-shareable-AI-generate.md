Skip the scaffolding, the config, and the local setup, and go straight to shipped.

### Say what you want

Describe the app in plain language. No spec, no stack decisions.

### Watch it come to life

A sandbox spins up in seconds, frontend and backend already wired together.

### Ship it, right now

Every app gets a permanent link. Send it, show it off, no deploy step required.

## Working software, not a demo

Every generated app runs live, with its own server, ready to use, not just to look at.

### Build in whatever fits

Prompt your preferred backend, libraries and packages. The stack serves the idea, not the other way around.

### Live the moment it's built

The moment it's built, it's running. Submit a form, play a video, watch it work, not just render.

### One link, zero deploys

Every app gets a permanent link that just works. Send it to anyone, no hosting to set up or think about.

### Keep shaping it

Prompt again to change what you built. Freeform updates the live app in place, instantly.

### Safe to experiment

Every app is isolated, so you can try anything without risking everything.

### Built to last

Come back days later and it's still there. Your data persists, and the app boots right back up, no re-setup needed.

The gap between having an idea and showing someone a working app, closed.

## Questions

What can I build with a prompt?+

Full-stack tools and apps, not just static pages. Dashboards, client demos, signup flows, and more. Describe it, then keep prompting to change it. Freeform edits the running app instead of starting over.

Who can see what I build?+

You decide. Every app gets a link the moment it's built. Keep it to yourself, share it with anyone who has the link, or lock it down to invited people only.

Does my data stick around?+

Yes. Your app keeps its data between visits. It's not wiped on reload. Come back tomorrow, next month, whenever, and it's still there.

Can it connect to other services?+

Yes. Add API keys and secrets and your app can take payments with Stripe, send email, or call any API, just like a production app.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You describe what you want in plain language, and Freeform handles the setup, wiring, and hosting.
