About & privacy

Built on NYC DOT sign records for ordinary passenger cars in Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Queens. Records can be incomplete or out of date. Messages and matching sign records go to OpenAI. Your coordinates go to our server for parking checks, and to Mapbox when drawing a drive, not to OpenAI. Maps load from OpenFreeMap. Your location may be remembered in this tab for up to five minutes. We save generated routes, starting coordinates, parking criteria, generation times, and any optional parking feedback you submit to improve directions.
