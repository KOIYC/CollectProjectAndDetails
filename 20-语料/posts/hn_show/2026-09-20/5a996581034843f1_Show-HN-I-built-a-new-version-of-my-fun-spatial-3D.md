# Absurdly Delightful Virtual Spaces

Remote teams and schools use flat.social to socialize, collaborate and throw virtual socials

## Creatively Interactive & Full of Fun Features

Everything you need to bring people together online — from casual hangouts to polished presentations, all in one place.

Move around, bump into friends, and chat naturally with spatial audio

## Spatial Audio & Video

Attendees are not chained to a grid of tiny squares like they would in a classic videoconference.

Instead, they can move and gather in groups to talk with others around. Spontaneous creative moments are back in the game!

## Connect with a Smile

Fly around, bump into other attendees. Create new connections. Network casually and have fun!

It's a digital cocktail party in a creative playground that is built like a multiplayer game. Everyone is an equal participant in a virtual world that is playful and promotes spontaneous conversations.

## Games & Activities

Build a virtual venue for your next event or a digital office for your team where you can host events, happy hours, parties and team building activities.

It's quick and simple — just select the room that you'd like to add or use one of the templates. Those can be mini-games, team building activities or something designed especially for your guests.

### Remote Teams

Give your distributed team a shared space that feels like an actual office. Drop by a colleague's desk, have watercooler chats, and keep the team spirit alive — no matter the timezone.

### Online Schools

Turn passive lectures into interactive classrooms. Students move between group activities, raise their hand to ask questions, and collaborate on projects — just like on a real campus.

### Virtual Events

Host conferences, meetups, and socials that people actually enjoy. Attendees mingle freely, visit booths, and join breakout sessions in a space that makes networking feel natural.

## Your virtual space is ready when you are

Spin up a room in under a minute. No install, no credit card. Invite your team, students, or friends and just hit go.
