## The open-source email client you trust,now with AI superpowers

TabMail extends Mozilla Thunderbird — the privacy-respecting, open-source email client used by millions. We just make Thunderbird smarter and give it a fresh coat of paint.**

TabMail servers do not retain your email or calendar content. When you use AI features, this data may be temporarily sent for processing but is never stored at rest.*** You can disable AI features at any time with a single toggle.
