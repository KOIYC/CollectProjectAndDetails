 One vault for passwords, API keys, seed phrases, cards and SSH keys. Encrypted on your device. No account, no cloud, no network. Pay once for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Vaultis is a private, offline vault for everything you need to keep secret - passwords, API keys, cards, crypto wallets, software licenses, SSH keys, database credentials and secure notes. Everything is encrypted on your device with your master password and stored nowhere else. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no tracking. The app makes no network connections at all. WHY VAULTIS Fully offline. Your data never leaves your iPhone. Strong encryption. Your vault is protected with AES-256-GCM, and your master password is stretched with PBKDF2 before it ever touches a key. Face ID / Touch ID unlock, backed by the device keychain. Zero knowledge. Your master password is never stored or transmitted. Only you can open your vault. BUILT FOR MORE THAN PASSWORDS Pick a category and the form adapts to it: Logins - username, password, website API keys - service, key, secret, endpoint Cards - number, expiry, CVV, PIN Bank accounts, crypto wallets (address, recovery phrase, private key) Software licenses, SSH keys, databases, Wi-Fi, servers Secure notes and fully custom fields TOOLS Built-in generator for strong passwords and memorable passphrases Search and favorites Encrypted backups you can export and restore with a passphrase PRIVACY BY DESIGN Vaultis has no sign-up and collects nothing. Because your data is encrypted only with your master password and kept on-device, a forgotten master password cannot be recovered - keep an encrypted backup somewhere safe
