# AI Built for Businesses That Know Their Business.

AI shouldn't force a new way of working or replace your process. GrabThat is a configurable Windows desktop hub that connects local models, team directories, and automated workflows directly to your existing processes to help you achieve your strategic goals.

## AI Configured to Fit Your Business — Not the Other Way Around.

Most AI tools try to force a blanket transformation or change how your team operates. GrabThat takes a fundamentals-first approach: a configurable, local-first Windows hub that works alongside your existing operations, connecting directly to your local files and databases.

#### Distribute & Link Data

Distribute the launcher across your team and link everyone to a shared local database. Watch your proprietary operational data begin to link up, building a strong, unified foundation.

#### Empower Champions

Introduce the assistant to key team champions to ingest company policies, information, and SOPs. Champions analyze linked data from Step 1 to identify patterns and pinpoint high-value repeat tasks.

#### Scale Strategic Execution

Give everyone access to an assistant. Your champions develop agentic workflows and lead new efficiency strategies—using GrabThat as a tool to propel your flywheel from good to great.

## AI built to amplify the business you've already built.

A configurable, Windows-native desktop hub for teams focused on disciplined execution.

### What can you integrate?

Local Windows files and folders, network shared drives, secure SharePoint links, small local models trained on your data, private database tables, automated workflows, Python automation scripts, and custom internal systems.

## Local performance. Enterprise security.

GrabThat is designed for disciplined businesses that already know what makes them successful. Rather than asking you to abandon your proven processes for generic web-based chat windows, GrabThat operates as a configurable, desktop-native hub that amplifies your team's existing strengths to achieve strategic goals.

#### Windows-Native Shell Integration

A highly optimized system overlay that integrates with your Windows shell. Triggered with simple global hotkeys, active instantly with zero lag.

#### Small Local Models & Private API Access

Hook up small local models trained on your data to run 100% offline, or safely connect your private enterprise API keys. Keep your data on-premise.

#### Automated Workflow Integration

Extend what your AI can do. Connect local and team-shared automated workflows to directly hook into your local databases, secure file systems, and internal corporate tools with zero custom backend integrations.
