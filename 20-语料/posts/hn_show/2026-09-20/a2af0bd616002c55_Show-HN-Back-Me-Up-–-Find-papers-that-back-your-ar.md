## A TINYSCIENCE-ISHMACHINE.

### HOW THIS WORKS

- The site searches PMC, bioRxiv, medRxiv and arXiv for papers relevant to your claim.
- Relevant papers are sent to Luna. It checks the claim and pulls out the strongest supporting passages.
- Results are turned into plain-English answers with exact quotes and links to the original papers.

Made by Loom Labs ↗, largely using Sol + Opus models.
