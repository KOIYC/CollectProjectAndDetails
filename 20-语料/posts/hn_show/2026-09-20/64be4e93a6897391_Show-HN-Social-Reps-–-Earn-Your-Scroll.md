Social Reps: block Instagram until you do your push-ups

# Earn your scroll.

Your feeds start every day locked. Ten push-ups opens them. The camera counts, so there's nothing to negotiate.

One email the day it drops. Nothing else.

The camera runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

## You don't have a willpower problem. You have a friction problem.

Instagram is one thumb-tap away, all day, built by hundreds of people whose job is keeping you there. "Just be disciplined" was never a fair fight — the feed is free and instant. So we made it cost something.

### Lock your feeds

Pick the apps that eat your day — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X. They start every morning closed.

### Do your reps

Prop the phone up and start. It counts push-ups, sit-ups and burpees on its own.

### They open

Hit the day's goal and your feeds unlock until tomorrow. Miss it and the lock holds.

## Discipline you don't have to feel.

Real blocking Screen Time on iOS, a full-screen overlay on Android. Not a nudge you can swipe away.

The camera counts On-device pose detection counts full reps. Half-reps don't count.

Your rules Push-ups only? Abs only? All three? Easy, medium or hard? You set the toll.

Private by design The video never leaves your phone. You don't need an account to train.

Streaks that mean something Every day starts locked. The streak is effort, not a tap.

Move more, scroll less Trade the first ten minutes of doomscrolling for ten push-ups.

## The honest answers.

Does it actually block my apps?

Yes. On iOS it uses Apple's Screen Time to shield the apps you choose; on Android a full-screen overlay covers them. It's a real wall until you hit your goal.

Can I cheat the camera?

Not easily. On-device pose detection counts full reps — it ignores half-reps, wiggles and fake motions. If you didn't do the work, it doesn't count. Try the counter above and see for yourself.

Is my camera footage private?

Completely. Everything runs on your phone. No frame is stored, uploaded or sent anywhere. You don't even need an account to train.

Which apps can I lock?

Any you choose — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit and more. You pick them in a couple of taps.

iPhone or Android?

Both, today. iPhone on the App Store and Android on Google Play — same app, same price.

What does it cost?

Nothing to start: the free plan guards two apps and opens them with three push-ups. $29.99 a year unlocks every app, all three exercises and your own goal, with the first seven days free.
