## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Bulkgrid works and what it solves.

### What is Bulkgrid?

Bulkgrid connects your AI to knowledge from sources you trust. Index websites and GitHub repositories, track changes, and retrieve relevant passages with references through MCP, API, SDKs, or the CLI.

### How is this different from normal AI web search?

Web search is useful for discovering information across the internet. Bulkgrid gives your AI a persistent, searchable index of the sources you select, including private GitHub repositories. You control the content it can access, organize sources into collections, and retrieve passages with references.

### What sources can Bulkgrid index today?

Bulkgrid indexes public websites and public or private GitHub repositories, including documentation. Choose the pages or repository paths you need. Private repositories connect through a GitHub App, and their content stays private to your workspace.

### How often is data refreshed?

Sources refresh on their configured schedule. Bulkgrid checks for changes and updates indexed content as processing completes. You can also use Sync now to request a refresh.

### Do I need to build and maintain my own pipeline?

No. Bulkgrid handles crawling, GitHub syncing, content processing, indexing, and refresh workflows. Choose your sources and connect your application or agent instead of building an ingestion pipeline.

### Who is Bulkgrid for?

Bulkgrid is for developers and teams building AI experiences grounded in trusted content. Use it for coding agents, documentation assistants, customer support, and research workflows that need searchable public or private knowledge.
