Overview

Navigate to search results quickly by pressing their sequence numbers.

Key Jump lets you open search results instantly with a single keystroke. Stop reaching for your mouse every time you search Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Yahoo, or Yandex. Key Jump automatically adds small numbers (1-9) next to your search results. Just press the corresponding number on your keyboard to jump straight to that page. Key Features: - 🚀 Lightning Fast: Save seconds on every search by keeping your hands on the keyboard. - 🧠 Smart Detection: Automatically disabled when you are typing in the search bar. - 🎨 Customizable: Hide the numbers whenever you want and navigate from memory. Boost your productivity and browse at the speed of thought. Install Key Jump today!
