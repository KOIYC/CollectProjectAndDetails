# Digital Detox Lock: Disconnect

Focus timeout screen lockdown

Only for iPhone

Free · In‑App Purchases · Designed for iPhone. Not verified for macOS.

Share

iPhone

Discipline is giving yourself no choice. When willpower isn’t enough, lock the apps you can’t quit — and give someone you trust the key. Most app blockers fail for the same reason: they trust you to stop yourself. But research shows people are significantly more likely to break a habit when someone else is involved. Disconnect App is built on that insight. You set the rules. A trusted person holds the key. When you try to unblock a blocked app, they decide — not you. No bypass. No weak moments. Just a system that works when willpower doesn't. HOW IT WORKS Choose the apps you want to block and when. Then pick your lock level: Soft Lock — unblock instantly, but tap through a confirmation first. Enough friction to break the reflex. Medium Lock — wait 5 minutes before access is granted. Enough time to actually reconsider. Hard Lock — you cannot unblock it yourself. Your buddy gets a notification and decides whether to let you in. No workarounds. No escape hatch. THE BUDDY SYSTEM This is what makes Disconnect App different from every other screen time blocker. You choose someone you trust — a friend, a partner, anyone. When you try to open a hard-locked app, they receive a notification and approve or deny in seconds. It's not surveillance. It's accountability. One person who steps in when your willpower gives out. Your buddy never sees which apps you use or how long you spend on them. They simply get asked: yes or no? WHAT'S FREE · 1 rule · 1 buddy · All three lock levels including hard lock · No time limit — it's yours to keep DISCONNECT PRO · Unlimited rules · Multiple buddies · More features coming PRIVACY & LEGAL Disconnect App uses Apple's Screen Time API. The apps you choose to block never leave your device — we never see them. Only the minimum data needed to run the buddy system is stored: your display name, buddy relationships, and unlock requests. We do not sell your data. Privacy Policy: https://disconnectapp.co/privacy Terms of Service: https://disconnectapp.co/terms

## What’s New

New ways to stay focused without opening the app: • Home Screen widget — see your focus status at a glance: what's blocking now, what's paused, and when your next block starts. • Live Activity for timed unlocks — when you start a medium-lock countdown, it now lives on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, counting down and nudging you to stay on track. • Re-lock from the widget (Pro) — paused a rule and changed your mind? Re-lock it right from the Home Screen widget, no need to open the app. • Home Screen quick actions — press and hold the app icon to jump straight to creating a rule, reviewing buddy requests, or sending feedback. • Buddy request badges — the app icon now shows how many buddy requests are waiting for you, so nothing slips through. • Plus polish and reliability fixes throughout.
