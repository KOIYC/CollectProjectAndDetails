# Nerdulator

Put something in. Get everything out.

What have you got? Paste or type anything

What have you got? Paste or type anything; Nerdulator works out what it is.

Or don’t decide: surprise me picks a page at random, and today’s page is the same one for everybody until midnight.

## How it works

01

### It works out what you gave it

A deterministic parser (regular expressions, lookup tables and range checks) decides whether you typed a number, a date, a measurement, a colour, an address or a word. If it is genuinely ambiguous, you get both readings rather than a silent guess.

02

### It calculates everything it can

The matching engine runs every transformation it knows: factors, conversions, encodings, contrast ratios, subnet boundaries, calendar positions. Same input, same output, every time.

03

### It links you onward

Every derived value is itself a page. The square of 47, the complement of a colour, the day after a date. All real, all one click away.

## 22 engines, no models

Nerdulator makes zero AI calls. Every figure on every page is computed at request time from the input you gave it. That is the whole point: a reference you can check, that gives the same answer twice, and that will still give that answer in ten years.

- IP & networking

IPv4 and IPv6 address analysis, CIDR subnet and prefix calculations.
- Web

URL and email address structure, parts and encodings. Syntax only, never a network lookup.
- Developer identifiers

Cron expressions, UUIDs, semantic versions and checksummed identifiers, decomposed.
- Reference tables

Polyhedra, paper sizes, screen resolutions, solar-system bodies and musical pitches.
- Colour

HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV and CMYK conversion with contrast, harmonies and named-colour matching.
- Date

Calendar position, formats, Unix time and navigation for any date from year 1 to 9999.
- Units

Conversion across 20 categories and 202 units, all defined as data.
- Computing

Base literals, bit widths, Unix timestamps and Base64.
- Maths

Fractions, percentages, ratios, money arithmetic, statistics and expression evaluation.
- Geometry

Areas, perimeters, volumes and angles for the common 2D and 3D shapes.
- Science

Physical constants, chemical elements, molar masses and physics calculators.
- Geography

Country reference data, coordinates, great-circle distance and bearing.
- Algebra

Solves, expands and factorises single-variable equations exactly, in rational arithmetic.
- Dimensions, uncertainty and sequences

What a quantity is, how well it is known, and every rule that fits a sequence. Three questions whose honest answer is not a single number.
- Matrices and coordinate geometry

Determinants, inverses and eigenvalues in exact rational arithmetic, and what a set of points determines. Singular means exactly singular, not close to it.
- Chemical equations

Equation balancing solved as a null space, so "cannot be balanced" is a proof rather than a failure to find an answer: and "infinitely many balancings" is a real answer too.
- Electronics

Ohm's law, the power law and RC time constants. One line of algebra each, so the answers are fixed: but never a verdict on whether a part is safe.
- Cards, the chessboard and entropy

Exact counting: poker hands as ratios of binomial coefficients, knight distances by exhaustive search, and the two different things people call entropy.
- Games

Dice notation and dart checkouts, both by exact enumeration over a fixed rule set.
- Music

Scales, chords and key signatures in twelve-tone equal temperament, spelled correctly.
- Number

Integer and decimal properties: primality, factors, bases, words, powers.
- Text

Character counts, case conversions, encodings, frequency and word-game scores.
