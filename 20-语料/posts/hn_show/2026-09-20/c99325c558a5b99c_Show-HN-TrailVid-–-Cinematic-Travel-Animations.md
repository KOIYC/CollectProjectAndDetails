## Edit on your phone. See it on your TV.

Change a theme, adjust a title, add a stop. See the result on a big screen while you work, without exporting between revisions.

Your saved trips, ready to watch on Apple TV.

Route

Mix flights, trains, cars, buses, boats, walks and bike rides in one trip. Add waypoints when a place shapes the route but does not need its own scene. Car, bus, walking and cycling legs follow roads and paths. Trains follow rail lines; boats use shipping lanes.

Story

Write opening and closing titles, captions and credits. Add dates, distances, places and flags from the trip, then choose how labels and dates appear.

Series

Preview the whole trip or a single clip. Turn one itinerary into a connected series, with earlier legs already drawn when the next video begins.

Theme editor

The trip stays on screen while you change the globe, atmosphere, routes, type and title cards. Give cards different layouts for vertical, square and widescreen exports.

Export

Make 9:16, widescreen, square and 4:5 videos at 30 or 60 fps, up to 4K. Compatible devices can also export 10-bit HDR.

## Before you install.

What iOS version do I need?

Requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26. Install TrailVid through TestFlight using the beta link.

Does TrailVid upload my photos?

Photo import reads location and capture time on your device to draft the trip. It does not copy or upload the photos. You choose which places to keep before creating the trip.

Can I start with a GPX track?

Yes. Import one or several GPX files and TrailVid will draft the stops, dates and transport for you. Recorded tracks can also become the route shown in the video.

Can I use TrailVid on iPad?

Yes. TrailVid is optimized for iPad, and iCloud can keep your trips and themes in sync with your iPhone.

Do I need an account?

No. Install the beta through TestFlight and start creating.

## Practical routes into the first cut.

Cut one itinerary into several films, recover a trip from photos, or turn a GPX recording into a route worth watching.

## Everything is open during the beta.

Use every theme and export option, including 4K, with no TrailVid account or payment card.

You will still be able to make as many 1080p videos as you like, with every transport mode and a small TrailVid badge. Removing the badge will be paid; pricing is still undecided.
