## What Is Bracket Maker?

A bracket maker is a tool that turns a team list into a tournament path where every result feeds the next round. It is useful for teachers, club hosts, office organizers, and gaming communities that need structure fast.

Single-elimination brackets are popular because they are easy to explain and easy to schedule: one loss eliminates a team, rounds move quickly, and winners are always clear.

SnapBracket focuses on practical event flow: quick paste-to-bracket setup, automatic BYE handling for uneven team counts, live winner updates, and local-first exporting and printing without account setup.

## Bracket Maker FAQ

### What is the bracket generator?

SnapBracket is a free single-elimination bracket maker: paste your team list, pick a layout (single, two-sided, or regions), click winners, and export a clean PNG — everything runs locally in your browser.

### Is the bracket generator free to use?

Yes. You can create and edit brackets, export PNGs, and upload team images at no cost. No login is required.

### How is this bracket generator different?

It supports multiple bracket structures, custom team images, fast paste-to-bracket flows, BYE auto-handling, and runs fully in-browser so you can use it instantly for free.

### Can I use the generated bracket commercially?

Yes. You can export high-resolution PNGs for commercial decks, streams, or prints; just ensure you have rights to the names and images you upload.

### Are uploaded images safe?

Uploads stay in your browser storage only and never leave your device; clear your browser data to remove them.

### How many teams are supported?

You can run single-elimination brackets from 2 up to 128 teams. We auto-expand to the next power of two and fill empty slots with BYEs.

### What happens with BYEs?

If one side is empty and the other has a team, the real team auto-advances. Two empty slots stay empty.

### Can I change a winner later?

Yes. Clicking a different team in an earlier round clears downstream winners so brackets stay consistent.

### Is there a fullscreen view?

Yes. Use Full Screen plus zoom and drag to inspect large brackets, especially 64/128 teams.

### Do I need an account?

No sign-up required. Everything runs locally in your browser.
