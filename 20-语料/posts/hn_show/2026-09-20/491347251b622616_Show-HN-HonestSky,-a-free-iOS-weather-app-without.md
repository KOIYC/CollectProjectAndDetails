 Weather without ads, tracking, subscriptions, or paywalls. Accurate forecasts, radar, alerts, air quality, UV, widgets, and saved places—all free. Beautiful, accurate weather — without the noise. HonestSky is a free weather app with zero ads, zero tracking, and zero paywalls. Every feature is available to every user. No premium tier. No subscriptions. No behavioral tracking or data monetization. FEATURES: - Current conditions with feels-like, humidity, wind, UV, pressure, and dew point - 48-hour hourly forecast with precipitation probability - 16-day daily forecast with confidence indicators - Live animated precipitation radar with playback controls - Severe weather alerts from NWS, MeteoAlarm, and Environment Canada - Air quality index with pollutant breakdown (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2) - UV index with exposure guidance - Multiple saved locations with instant switching - Dark mode (OLED-friendly) and light mode - Home screen widgets (current conditions + forecast strip) - Full accessibility: VoiceOver, TalkBack, Dynamic Type PRIVACY: - No advertising SDKs - No analytics SDKs - No user accounts - Forecast requests use rounded coordinates and are not used to build profiles - If you enable push alerts, your push token and chosen locations are stored only to deliver alerts - Newsletter signup is optional and stores only the information you submit - Delete server-side data any time from Settings Data sources: Open-Meteo, National Weather Service, MeteoAlarm, Environment Canada, RainViewer.
