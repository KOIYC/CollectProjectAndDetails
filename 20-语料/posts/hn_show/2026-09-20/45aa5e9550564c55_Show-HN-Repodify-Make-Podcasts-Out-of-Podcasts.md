# Make a Podcast out of Podcasts

Stitch Together and Share Select the episodes you love and put them together in your own custom podcast feed

## Curate and Compile Audio

Mix and match multiple sources

### Authors & Guests

Compile all your interview appearances and guest spots into a single feed

### Podcast Fans

Curate your favorite episodes and greatest hits from any public podcast

### Education

Aggregate relevant episodes on specific topics for research or teaching

### Communities

Effortlessly share your personalized compilation feeds

## Repodification Technology

We built the tool we wanted to use ourselves

### Podcast Directory

Add episodes from any public podcast using our integrated PodcastIndex directory. No manual URL hunting required. (Although you can do that if you want.)

### Spice it Up

Add a personal touch. Record your thoughts, intros, or transitions straight from your browser. We host the audio for you. You can add existing audio files as well.

### Universal RSS

Repodify generates a standard-compliant RSS XML feed that seamlessly plugs into Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, Spotify, Overcast, etc...

### Podcaster Friendly

Repodify doesn't mess with the original podcasts - no re-hosting or other intrusions. Podcast creators retain full control over their content.

### Free!

This is a labor of love. If Repodify gets popular and costs us real money to keep online, we'll figure it out.
