## Skills aren't flat. Why is learning?

 Courses hand you one long lecture. Cubespotting turns each skill into a cube with six sides, one for each part of the skill, and deals every side a few cards each morning. Swipe through them: read the lesson, take the challenge, see the verdict. Five days later you have been around the whole cube, not down a list.
