# The web, back in your hands.

Follow the sites you love with RSS. Connect with people through AT Proto. Sunred brings them together in a reader that puts you first.

Open source. Self-hostable.

A new day for the open web.

Sunred / Your reading space Your feeds. Your pace.

A little less scrolling. A little more reading. Also at home in your terminal

## Open protocols. Not another platform.

The internet was made for people, not platforms. Let's get back to that.

### RSS

Your curiosity sets the feed.

Blogs, news, independent voices. Subscribe to the websites you care about and get their latest posts in one place. Newest first. Nothing ranked for you.

RSS, Atom & JSON Feed. Bring your subscriptions with OPML.

### @ AT Proto

Good reading is better shared.

Follow people and share articles across Sunred instances. Built on AT Protocol, the same open network as Bluesky, with an identity that isn't tied to one app.

## Your web. Your way.

Use our instance, or run your own. The code is open. The choice is yours.
