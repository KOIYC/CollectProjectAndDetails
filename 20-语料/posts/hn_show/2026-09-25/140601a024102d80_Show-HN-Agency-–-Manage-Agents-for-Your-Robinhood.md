> **rallies** · 2026-09-24T19:01:32.000Z　
> I'm very bullish on LLMs being able to invest in the market well, as long as proper safeguards are there.Robinhood agentic accounts is an amazing idea but you can't really automate stuff that can run on its own. Different companies have different ways for setting up automation which most retail traders are not aware of.Agency fixes a lot of this. It's a Mac OS app that keeps your account on your device, and lets you build agents that can run on their own every day to manage a sleeve of your cash.
