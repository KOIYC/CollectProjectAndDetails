Frequently asked questions
 How are prices obtained? Each price is shown as checked, with its source and time, or as estimated, with the date it was read. Where no partner covers the city, an estimated price by category and neighbourhood, shown as such. You check and book with the seller.
 Are the prices guaranteed? No. They are prices from official and partner sources, shown as checked, with their source and time, or as estimated, with the date they were checked. You check and book with the seller, under its terms. XYVEL books nothing and sometimes earns a commission on those bookings, at no extra cost to you.
 Do I need to install anything? No. The trip planner works in your browser, on desktop and on phone. An account (your e-mail and a code received by e-mail) and the XYVEL Trip Pro subscription are all you need.
 Where do my criteria go? What you write in the trip planner is processed by XYVEL and by the artificial intelligence service that writes the programme, without your e-mail. Details are in the privacy policy.
