### Microsoft Teams: Detect ESC1 Help Desk Threat

Microsoft Teams users face a new ESC1 Help Desk threat that can compromise accounts via malicious support interactions. The threat exploits social engineering tactics, leveraging deceptive help desk communications to gain credential access; no software vulnerability is required. Security teams should review telemetry, deploy detection rules, and apply the latest Teams patches to mitigate the risk.
