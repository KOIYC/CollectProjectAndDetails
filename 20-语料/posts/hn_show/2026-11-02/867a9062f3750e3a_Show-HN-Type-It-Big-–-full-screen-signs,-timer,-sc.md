FAQ
Is it free?
 Yes. No account and nothing to install.
Does my screen stay on?
 While your message is showing, the site asks your device to keep the screen awake. Support depends on your browser and device settings.
Can I type while it's full screen?
 Yes, on a computer with a keyboard. Just start typing and your words are added to the sign as you go, which works well for back-and-forth conversations. Ctrl or ⌘ + Backspace clears the sign so you can start the next line, and Ctrl or ⌘ + Z undoes. Press Esc when you're done. On phones and tablets, tap the sign to go back and edit.
Can I send a sign to someone else?
 Tap Copy link . The link includes your message, colors, font, style and speed. Visitors who prefer reduced motion start in Still and can choose to turn animation on.
Is my message stored anywhere?
 Your message and settings are saved in this browser on this device so you can come back to them. Use Clear to remove the saved message. Anyone you send a shared link to can read its text.
What if I'm sensitive to flashing light or motion?
 Choose Still if you or anyone nearby is sensitive to flashing light. Flash repeatedly swaps the screen colors. You can pause scrolling or flashing at any time, including while your sign is showing. Visitors who prefer reduced motion start in Still.
