> Hi HN! I've been reading a lot about games being de-compiled using LLMs, so I thought that I'd give it a go myself. Back when I was a kid my siblings and I would spend a ton of time playing Crystal Caves on our 286. Given it's age and relatively small footprint, I figured it would be a good target.- I mostly used DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017. If there was an issue it struggled with, I would switch to GLM 5.3 which could power through it.
> - It cost me about $20 to make the WASM port
> - I chose WASM for portability and so everyone can play it easily
> - Yes DosBox exists. This was for fun and experimentation
> - You can play directly at https://crystal-caves.re-cycledair.com/For next steps (if any) I might add a few quality of life improvements and work towards better code organization.
