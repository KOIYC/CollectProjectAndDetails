Frequently Asked Questions
 Everything you need to know about Castov.
Frequently Asked Questions
 Is Castov really free? Yes. All tools are free, with no account and no usage limits. The site may show ads in the future to cover running costs.
 Is my data sent to a server? Time, date and converter tools do not upload your input to Castov servers. Some preferences, such as theme, custom time zones and selected models, are stored locally on your device. Some features use the network by design: Env Vault uploads an encrypted copy, P2P Ghost Tunnel uses public STUN discovery with manual token exchange, and Developer Canvas connects to public WebRTC signaling servers (which may receive connection metadata; workflow graphs and payloads are never stored on Castov servers).
 What is a Unix timestamp? A Unix timestamp is the number of seconds since 1 January 1970 at 00:00:00 UTC. It identifies one moment in time regardless of time zone, which is why programs and APIs use it.
 Should I use seconds or milliseconds? Use whichever your system produces. A 10-digit value is in seconds and a 13-digit value is in milliseconds. The converters can detect the unit for you.
 Which time zones are supported? All IANA time zones supported by your browser, such as America/New_York, Europe/Paris, Africa/Algiers and Asia/Tokyo, with daylight saving time rules applied for the date you choose.
