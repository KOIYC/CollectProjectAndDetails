13 FAQ
 Quick answers.
 All questions and answers →
 What is DoppiNow? A Chrome extension that turns focus into a game for ADHD brains: XP for every focused minute, plus +25 XP when you come back after a drift (the first 8 per session).
 What is the Comeback ring? It's a ring that fills with the focus you win back after a distraction: once you're back on your goal, every focused minute counts as reclaimed. It closes when you've reclaimed at least as much time as the distraction took.
 Does DoppiNow block websites? No, never. If a page doesn't fit your goal, you get a calm reminder and the page gently ripples.
 How much does DoppiNow cost? Free for 7 days, with no card. Then $3.99/month or $29.99/year, with every feature in both. Cancel any time. Both plans include everything: all three worlds, the Comeback ring, the web dashboard.
 What happens when the free trial ends? New sessions pause until you choose a plan. Nothing is lost: your XP, streak, history and settings are kept, and you pick up where you left off.
 Do I need a card? Can I be charged? Not for the trial: it needs no card, and nothing is charged. You only pay if you choose a plan on your plan page . It renews until you cancel, and you can cancel any time.
 Is it good for ADHD? It's built around common ADHD hurdles: instant rewards, visible time and comebacks instead of shame. It's a productivity tool, not a treatment.
 Do I need an account? Yes. You sign in with Google once (no password to make). Your account keeps your progress safe and powers your web dashboard. We only use your name, email and picture.
 What data does DoppiNow send? Each finished session (its times, minutes, XP and the goal, unless you turn that off) is saved to your account for your dashboard. Page checks are off until you allow them; then, during a session, your goal and page details such as the address, title and main heading may be sent to DoppiNow's server, which asks an AI service whether the page fits your goal and doesn't store them. The page's full text is never sent. See the privacy policy .
 Which browsers does it work in? Desktop Chrome 120 or newer (other Chromium browsers such as Edge or Brave may also work). There's no phone app yet.
 Can I use it for studying? Yes: reading, writing, problem sets and lectures all work. Start with a 10- or 25-minute session.
