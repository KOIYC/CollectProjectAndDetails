# Don't just see the trade. Investigate the story.

Press ✦ Spy on any insider trade on InsiderPrint. Seven checks run on public SEC filings and our measured history, and you get the evidence for it, the evidence against it, and a plain list of what we couldn't check.

## The seven checks

Each one lights up only after it has actually run.

1 · Finding the SEC filing The Form 4 behind the trade: who, role, trade date, filing date, shares, price, and the link to the original.

2 · Reading the filing's qualifiers Pre-planned 10b5-1 sales, shares held through a trust or fund, late filings, amendments, prices that looked like typos.

3 · This insider's own history Their earlier buys and sales here: is this their first purchase, or several times their usual size?

4 · What other insiders did Everyone else buying or selling the same stock in the last 90 days, and how much of the selling was pre-planned.

5 · Similar past buys Buys since 2020 with the same traits, how often they beat the S&P 500, and real best and worst examples.

6 · What happened after The stock 7, 30 and 90 days after this filing, against the S&P 500. "Pending" until the days pass.
