# Supremath — Suprematism Art Generator

 Supremath is a small homage to Suprematism, the art of pure shapes and color that Kazimir Malevich launched in 1915. Every composition is built live by a generator that follows a reading of what makes a Suprematist picture — one dominant form, balance without symmetry, a shared tilt, a few free accents — and checks each one against those rules before showing it.

 Supremath draws Suprematist compositions with JavaScript. Please turn it on to see them.
