Right by your material
 Each right answer comes from your files, and the questions are spread over all of them, not only the first pages.
Wrong answers that teach
 Each wrong answer is a common misunderstanding of the subject, so a wrong answer shows what isn't understood yet.
Understanding, not copying
 The questions put what's in your material into new examples, instead of repeating its sentences with a word left out.
Only for you
 Your files are kept for you alone, and a quiz you make from them isn't public unless you choose.
