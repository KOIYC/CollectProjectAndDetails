## 正文

I have been posting on LinkedIn for more than 18 months and have had more than 4 million impressions.The one thing it took me a while to understand is that there is not a single algorithm.My belief is that the number of impressions highly depends on the one posting.I made a web game based on this thesis.You are shown real posts and have to guess the number of impressions.I find it quite entertaining, but it also questions our beliefs about how platforms like LinkedIn and X work.I will be available to answer all your questions and would be happy to hear your thoughts on the gameplay.
