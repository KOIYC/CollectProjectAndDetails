FAQ
Questions people ask first.
Do I need a household to use Cinnamon?
No. Every account starts with a private space that's yours alone. When you're ready, invite
 up to five family members or close friends into a group; shared tasks show up for everyone, your private
 space stays private.
What data do you store about me?
The tasks Cinnamon runs, and a memory of preferences you can read, edit or delete at any time. We do not store your conversation history with Cinnamon once a session ends. Passwords and payment details are never stored where Cinnamon can read them. We don't show ads and don't train models on your data.
What can Cinnamon actually do on my behalf?
Monitor and summarize your connected Gmail mailbox (surfacing financial transactions,
 school notices, youth sports, and other family activities), prepare draft inquiry emails in your Gmail Drafts
 folder when exploring options and reaching out to providers, check your schedule and help you add important
 events to your calendar, research and compare products and local services, design tailored meal plans and
 exercise programs, and help you prepare for job interviews. Cinnamon never sends emails on its own, modifies
 your calendar directly, or spends money without your direct action.
Can my household see my personal tasks?
Only what you put in the shared space. A job search, a gift or an appointment in your
 personal space is invisible to the group, and Cinnamon never mixes the two. You choose where each task lives
 when you start it.
What does it cost?
Every new account starts with a free trial (25 task credits—enough to complete 15–25 tasks depending on complexity, no credit card required). After your trial, plans start
 at $9.99/month for the Personal Plan (50 credits/mo for 1 member), $19.99/month for the Group Plan (150
 shared credits/mo for up to 6 members), and $99.99/month for Group Plan Max (1,000 shared credits/mo for up
 to 10 members). Both Group Plans include every member’s private Personal Space for $0.
Why isn’t there a free tier like other assistants offer?
Because “free” usually means you’re paying with your data. We don’t harvest your personal
 information or use it to train ad targeting. Instead, Cinnamon is funded by the people who use it, which
 lets us run a sustainable business and keep the experience fast, responsive, and high quality.
