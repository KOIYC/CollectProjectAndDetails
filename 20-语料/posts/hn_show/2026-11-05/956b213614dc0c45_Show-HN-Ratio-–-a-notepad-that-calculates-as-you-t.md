What is Ratio? Ratio is a notepad calculator for Mac, Windows and Linux. You type calculations as plain text, like "dinner 84 eur / 3", and the answer appears beside each line as you type. It understands currencies, crypto, units, dates and time zones.
Does Ratio run on Windows and Linux? Yes. Ratio runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and on 64-bit Linux (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, Debian, Fedora and others, as an AppImage or a .deb). It uses the same engine as on the Mac, so every line gives the same answer. The trial and the license work the same way everywhere, and a 2- or 3-computer license can mix a Mac, a PC and a Linux machine. iCloud sync, Liquid Glass and the Terminal command are Mac only for now.
Can Claude or other AI assistants use Ratio? Yes, on the Mac. Ratio comes with an MCP server, so Claude, Cursor and VS Code can work out currencies, stock prices, units, dates and VAT with Ratio instead of guessing. How to set it up
Does Ratio work offline? Yes. Every calculation happens on your computer. Exchange rates need a connection to refresh, and while you're offline, Ratio uses the last rates it downloaded.
Do I really pay only once? Yes. You pay once, and that's it. Updates are included.
Is there a free trial? Yes. Ratio is free for 7 days, with no account and no card. If you buy it and it isn't for you, email us within 14 days and we'll refund it.
How do I move my license to another computer? In the app, open Ratio Pro and click “Deactivate this Mac” (or “Deactivate this PC” on Windows and Linux). Then enter the same key on the new computer, whether it's a Mac, a PC or a Linux machine.
