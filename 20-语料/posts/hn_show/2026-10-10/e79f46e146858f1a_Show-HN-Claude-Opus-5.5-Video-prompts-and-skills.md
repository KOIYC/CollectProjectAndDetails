## How to use these Claude Opus 5.5 video prompts

Start with the video: watch its pacing, transitions, and final composition. Then read the creator’s prompt to see which details were specified. A short creative brief can invite exploration, while a detailed prompt can define the scene order, typography, timing, and rendering workflow.

To adapt an example, replace its subject, copy, and visual assets with your own. Keep the motion instructions that fit your idea, specify a duration and aspect ratio, and ask for a storyboard or a few key frames before a full render. Review the result and refine one issue at a time, such as text readability or the timing of a transition.

## Video prompts and skills: what’s included?

A prompt describes a particular video. A skill provides reusable instructions or tooling for a workflow. Where a creator has shared a skill, the example includes its description and available installation command or repository link. The resource badges distinguish prompts from skills; not every video includes both.

Each example pairs a playable video with its available creative instructions, model details, and original creator post. Requirements vary: some prompts call for reference images, music, code, or a separate rendering tool. Read those requirements before adapting a workflow. Videos and prompts are credited community work, shared here for creative inspiration.
