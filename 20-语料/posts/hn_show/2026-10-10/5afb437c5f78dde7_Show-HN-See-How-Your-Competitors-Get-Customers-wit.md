What is Winning Funnels?
 An AI research agent for marketers. You describe a niche; it finds live ad funnels in that market, screenshots each landing page, estimates what it makes per month and explains the hook, why it works and how to model it.
Where do the funnels come from?
 The agent searches the open web for landing pages and funnels that are live right now, then visits and analyzes each one. Funnels members save also appear — anonymously — in the Ads Community.
How is the monthly revenue estimated?
 It’s a model, not reported revenue: estimated visits × the share reaching the page × a conversion rate for that funnel type × the value per buyer. It’s shown as a range that widens when fewer signals are available.
What do “Proven” and “Rising” mean?
 Proven: the domain has been live for 3+ years and still runs ad pixels — an established business still paying for traffic. Rising: a young domain that’s already buying traffic and ranks in the top million sites.
Is there a free plan?
 Yes. The Free plan is $0 and includes 40 credits a month (≈ 20 funnels analyzed). No credit card needed.
What are credits?
 Research runs on credits: analyzing and saving a funnel uses about two. Paid plans refill every month, and one-time top-ups are there if you need more.
Can I cancel anytime?
 Yes. Cancel from Billing in the app whenever you like — you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
Does the community show who saved a funnel?
 Never. The Ads Community shows funnels, not people. You can also turn off sharing in Settings at any time.
Is there a real person I can talk to?
 Yes. Winning Funnels is built and run by real people, and a person — not a bot — reads every message. Use “Talk to a human” on any page, or email us, and we reply by email.
