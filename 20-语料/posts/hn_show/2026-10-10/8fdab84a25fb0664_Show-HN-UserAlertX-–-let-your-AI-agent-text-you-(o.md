What is UserAlertX?
A cloud texting App built for one person: You, Now, Free, then Cheap!
UserAlertX is an API-first SMS product built for automations. Sign up, verify your own mobile number, and that number is the only place the platform will ever text — no directory, no bulk list, no ability to message anyone else. Your bots send a request, you get a text.
 (For business or groups, see MemberAlertX.com .)
# send yourself an alert
curl -X POST https://useralertx.com/cgi-bin/itsignup.cgi \
 -d email = " [email protected] " \

 # change 1112223333 to your own textable number
1. You'll get a start/stop text on your phone.
2. You'll get an email asking to confirm your address.
3. Your email gets a 2FA verification request.
4. Your texts start going straight through.
5. Texting a different phone number from this account gets rejected as non-member.
This itsignup.cgi call, the Try It Now page, and Get Started all create/reach the same kind of account — pick whichever's convenient, they're not separate systems.
Why your bot needs its own alert channel
Built around one constraint
Carriers and The Campaign Registry punish open-ended messaging. A product that can only text the opted-in owner of an account is one of the cleanest stories you can register.
01
 One verified number
The recipient is always the same person who created the account and completed SMS opt-in — enforced in code, not just policy.
02
 No way to spam
There's no way to send to anyone else. No marketing content, no purchased lists, no third-party recipients — structurally, not just by promise.
03
 Carrier-compliant
Registered on a real 10DLC number, with full STOP/START/HELP keyword handling built in from the start.
04
 Carrier-restricted content
Phone carriers don't allow certain topics in text messages, even in an alert to yourself — stocks, trading and investment tips, cryptocurrency, loans and more. Every alert is checked first; a carrier-restricted alert is sent to you by email, complete, and your phone gets a short notice. See the list
Key capabilities
Everything a bot needs to reach you
→ Simple HTTP send API One POST request, no SDK required
→ Email + SMS double opt-in Confirm by email, confirm by text
→ Reply keywords: START / STOP / P / S Resume, opt out, pause, flag as spam, or EMAIL to switch to email alerts — just reply to the text
→ Delivery status See which alerts were delivered on your Recent alerts page
→ Free tier included 50 free alerts in your first 30 days, no card required upfront
→ Held-message queue First message waits for opt-in, then releases automatically
