## 正文

Hi everyone,When I first heard about WebMCP 6 weeks ago, I started thinking about what problems it might create. One question that came to mind was how developers would evaluate WebMCP tools and monitor them in production. That's when I decided to build Senro, a platform that helps devs test WebMCP tools using goal-oriented evals, trajectory evals, and more across LLMs, as well as monitor how those tools are used in production. Senro has a free plan (not a free trial). You can monitor up to 25K events/mo and run up to 250 evals/mo using your own API key (sorry, we're not venture-backed yet).
