## 正文

Use this to quickly get sleepy without taking any medicinal pill or long form meditation type things (It is as short as 7-minute session).Easy to use. Simply close your eyes and imagine the micro-scenarios you hear. Like if you hear "moonlight on a white flower", imagine that scenario until you hear the next one.(You may also use it to easily break nightly doomscrolling habit)
