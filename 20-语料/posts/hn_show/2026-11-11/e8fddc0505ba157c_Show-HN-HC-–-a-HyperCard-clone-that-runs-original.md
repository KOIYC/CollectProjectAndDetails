What HC does
It opens original HyperCard stacks , Apple's format from 1987
 to 1998, and runs their scripts as they are , with no
 conversion. On a corpus of real stacks, 99.7% of the scripts are
 accepted.
It is measured against the original. Every doubt is settled
 in the real HyperCard, under emulation, not from memory.
All of HyperCard, plus colour : paint, icons,
 backColor , foreColor , textColor .
 For games, polygon buttons and the keysDown .
Native and universal : a real Mac application, from High
 Sierra to today's Macs, signed by the Association Labynet and
 notarised by Apple. Free software, under the MIT licence.
Demo stacks
Download a stack, unzip it, then open it with HC: a double-click, or
 File › Open Stack… .
◉ Pinball
The pinball table from the video. The flippers are held with the
 W and N keys. It is a construction set:
 with the Button tool, copy a mushroom, paste it elsewhere, shape it
 with the mouse, and play.
▼ flipper.stack.zip
✎ Hangman
The hangman game: a gallows, a word to find, twenty-six letters.
 Play with the mouse or the keyboard; seven mistakes and the little
 man is hanged. The whole game lives in the stack script: open it,
 and add your own words.
▼ pendu.stack.zip
▦ Tetris
The Tetris we all know, in HyperTalk. The falling piece is a single
 polygon button that changes shape at every rotation. Play with the
 arrow keys, or with Q D to move,
 Z to rotate, X to drop faster, and space
 to let go. The whole game lives in the stack script.
▼ Tetris.stack.zip
▲ Space Invaders
Forty invaders made of polygon buttons, moving as one and speeding
 up as you shoot them down. Four shelters that crumble, a saucer
 that flies by now and then. Play with the arrow keys, or with
 Q D to move and Z or space
 to fire. The whole game lives in the stack script. The house
 record: 1,470 points.
▼ SpaceInvaders.stack.zip
▬ Brick breaker
Sixty bricks in the colours of the rainbow, a paddle and a ball
 made of polygon buttons. Off the middle of the paddle the ball goes
 straight back, off the edge it flies at an angle, up to sixty
 degrees; it speeds up a little with every brick. Play with the
 arrow keys, or with Q D to move, and
 Z or space to launch the ball.
▼ CasseBriques.stack.zip
◆ Boulder-Prout
A red-haired digger, boulders and diamonds: a homemade Boulder
 Dash, loosely inspired. Every cell is a button carrying a colour
 icon. Boulders fall and roll, but only a falling one crushes.
 Fireflies explode on contact, and a crushed butterfly turns into
 diamonds. Three caves, each one drawn as text in a hidden field.
 Play with the arrow keys, or with W A
 S D ; R restarts the cave.
 It needs HC 0.8.8.
▼ BoulderProut.stack.zip
● Gobe-Prout
A chomper, dots, four ghosts: a homemade Pac-Man, loosely
 inspired. The maze is our own, and each ghost hunts its own way:
 red charges, pink cuts you off, cyan closes in from the other
 side, orange dawdles. A power pellet turns them blue and edible.
 The characters are colour-icon buttons, moved point by point. Play
 with the arrow keys, or with W A
 S D . It needs HC 0.8.8.
▼ GobeProut.stack.zip
✦ Astro-Prout
A ship, rocks, a saucer: a homemade Asteroids, loosely inspired,
 drawn entirely in white-outlined polygon buttons. The ship turns
 and drifts on its momentum, space wraps around at the edges, rocks
 break in two and then in four, and the heartbeat speeds up.
 ← → or A D to
 turn, ↑ or W to thrust, space to fire,
 ↓ or S for hyperspace. It needs HC 0.8.8.
▼ AstroProut.stack.zip
▣ Pousse-Prout
A red-haired pusher, crates and targets: a homemade Sokoban.
 Thirteen warehouses, every one solved by the computer before it
 went into the stack. A crate can be pushed, never pulled, so it
 gets stuck fast. Play with the arrow keys, or W
 A S D ; U undoes
 a move, R restarts, and a click on a square walks the
 pusher there by the shortest path. The levels are written in XSB
 notation in hidden fields, so a level found elsewhere pastes right
 in. It needs HC 0.8.8.
▼ PousseProut.stack.zip
▤ Lingot-Prout
A runner, gold bars and guards: a homemade Lode Runner, on the
 original's 28 by 16 board. Run, climb, hang from the bars, and dig
 the brick to the left ( X ) or to the right
 ( C ): guards fall into the hole… which fills back in
 after nine seconds. Take the last gold bar and a hidden ladder
 appears. Eight levels. Run with the arrow keys, or W
 A S D ; R
 restarts the level. It needs HC 0.8.8.
▼ LingotProut.stack.zip

Installing
Download the
 DMG of the latest release
 (earlier ones are on the releases page ).
Open it, and drag HC.app into Applications .
Launch HC. The first time, macOS asks: "HC is an app downloaded
 from the Internet. Are you sure you want to open it?". Click
 Open : it asks only once.
Signed by the Association Labynet, notarised by Apple.
 Since release 0.8.3, macOS has checked HC: no more "damaged" message,
 no more command to type.
 For a release older than 0.8.3 ,
 which is not notarised, macOS still says "HC is damaged" — it is
 not. Once, in the Terminal:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/HC.app
