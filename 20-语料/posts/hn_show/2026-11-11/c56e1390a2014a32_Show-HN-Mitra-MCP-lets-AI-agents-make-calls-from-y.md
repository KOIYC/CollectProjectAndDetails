Questions and answers
 What is MCP? + MCP is a way for the tools you use to talk to Mitra. Connect once, then ask Mitra to handle calls and bring the results back into your conversation.
 What can I do with Mitra through MCP? + Ask Mitra to make calls for you, like booking a dentist appointment, reserving a table, or checking when your car will be ready. You can also ask what happened on calls Mitra answered for you. Do it all from your AI app.
 Which tools does this work with? + Tools that support MCP, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok. Look for the option to add a custom connector in your tool’s settings.
 Is MCP on every plan? + Yes. MCP is included in every Mitra free trial and paid plan. There’s no separate MCP add-on.
