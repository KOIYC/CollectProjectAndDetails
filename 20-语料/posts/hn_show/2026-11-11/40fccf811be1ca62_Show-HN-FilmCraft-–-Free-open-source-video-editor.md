What is FilmCraft?
FilmCraft is a next-generation non-linear video editing system (NLE) engineered from the ground up in systems Rust, designed specifically to address the stability, bloat, and recurring cost issues of proprietary commercial editors. Built originally by the ArtCraft open-source collective and actively maintained under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3), FilmCraft runs completely offline with zero telemetry, zero mandatory cloud account logins, and zero subscription fees.
Unlike legacy free editors that force non-standard timeline paradigms, FilmCraft deliberately implements the industry-standard track patching, 3-point ripple editing, multi-cam sync, and dual-monitor layout familiar to professional Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve editors. By adopting native GPU rendering via Apple Metal, NVIDIA CUDA/NVENC, and Vulkan/VA-API, FilmCraft delivers instantaneous 4K 10-bit ProRes and H.265 playback with sub-500MB idle memory consumption.

Knowledge Base
 Frequently Asked Questions
Is FilmCraft really 100% free forever?
Yes. FilmCraft is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3). There are no paid tiers, feature gates, watermark exports, or subscription charges.
Can I import my existing Premiere Pro projects?
Yes. You can export a Final Cut Pro XML (.xml) or EDL from Premiere Pro and import it cleanly into FilmCraft with cuts, audio gain, and tracks preserved.
Does FilmCraft collect any personal data?
Zero. FilmCraft contains no analytics tracking, no user ID beacons, and makes zero outbound network connections during editing. It functions 100% offline.
What are the minimum system requirements?
Any 64-bit multi-core CPU (Apple Silicon, Intel Core i5/i7, AMD Ryzen), 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended for 4K), and a GPU supporting Metal, DirectX 12, or Vulkan.
