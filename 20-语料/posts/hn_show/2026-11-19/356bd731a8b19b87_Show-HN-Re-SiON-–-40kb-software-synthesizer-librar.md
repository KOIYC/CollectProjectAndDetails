## 正文

Hi HN!Re-SiON is a re-implementation of an old software synthesizer created for Adobe Flash. It's incredibly tiny (at ~40KB) and runs on the web and desktop platforms. It plays easily editable plaintext MML (Music Macro Language) files which are insanely tiny, many example songs only taking up a few kilobytes.The project also comes with a playground to play and edit MML in real time, and a converter that can convert from tracker formats and NES/SNES/GameBoy/Genesis music straight to MML.It's all open source under MIT, and the link has a bunch of example songs to listen to. Happy to answer any questions.
