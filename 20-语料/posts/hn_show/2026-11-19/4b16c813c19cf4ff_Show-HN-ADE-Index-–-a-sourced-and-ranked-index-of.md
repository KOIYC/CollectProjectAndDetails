How the index works
Facts have sources
 21,449 sources behind 999 listings. Select any dotted-underlined fact on a tool page to see the sentence we read, where, and when.
Makers can claim, not rank
 A verified maker can keep the description, screenshots, links and pricing current. Their facts are marked as theirs. Score, rank, votes and quotes are out of reach.
The weights are published
 Hacker News mindshare, the same for open and closed tools. GitHub stars are shown beside it, not mixed in. Votes can only raise a score: up to 20% of it reflects upvotes, graded on a curve against the most-voted tool. The square-root curve has a floor of 100 effective votes. Votes decay with a 90-day half-life. Maker self-votes do not count. Counts below 10 are hidden for display only. How the score is built
Sponsors are labeled
 Sponsored placements say who paid and are never a row in a ranked list. Rankings pages carry none. No affiliate links. Rules and prices
