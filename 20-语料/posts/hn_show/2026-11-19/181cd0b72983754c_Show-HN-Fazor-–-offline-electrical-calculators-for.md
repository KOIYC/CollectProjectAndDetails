§ 02 — Calculators
 Eleven calculators
01 Ohm's Law
 Solves V, I, R and P from any two values.
02 Cable Size
 The smallest conductor that meets ampacity (installation method, insulation, temperature, grouping), the protective device (Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz) and your voltage-drop limit.
03 Voltage Drop
 Drop in volts and percent for a chosen conductor, with power factor and reactance.
04 Wire Color Codes
 IEC 60445 and NEC conductor colours for single-phase, three-phase and DC.
05 Resistor Color Code
 4- and 5-band resistors, with a live drawing of the resistor.
06 Breaker Sizing Pro
 IEC 60898 and NEC 240.6 standard ratings, with the 125% continuous-load rule.
07 Power Factor Correction Pro
 The compensation needed, in kVAR and in µF.
08 Three-Phase Power Pro
 The S, P and Q power triangle.
09 Motor Starting Current Pro
 DOL, star-delta, soft starter and VFD presets, with the starting kVA.
10 RCD / GFCI Selection Pro
 30 or 300 mA, type A or B, selective type S; GFCI under NEC.
11 Electricity Cost Pro
 Daily, monthly and yearly kWh and cost.

 § 06 — FAQ
 Questions and answers
Does it work without a connection? Yes, on site, in basements and plant rooms: every calculation runs on your phone. Only the free version's ads and Google Play purchases use the internet.
 Do I need an account? What does it keep about me? No account. Favourites and settings stay on your phone; what you enter is never saved or sent. The free version's ads come from Google AdMob, which processes some device data.
 I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone? Sign in with the same Google account, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.
 Does it replace a qualified person? No. Results are intended for field pre-calculation. Design and life-safety decisions must be verified by qualified persons under applicable regulations.
