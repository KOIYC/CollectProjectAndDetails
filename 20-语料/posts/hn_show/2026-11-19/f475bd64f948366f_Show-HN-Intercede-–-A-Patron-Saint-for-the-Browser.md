Intercede
A patron saint for your browser.
Set a daily limit for the sites that eat your time. When it runs out, a saint walks onto the page, raises a hand, and sweeps it away in a shower of pixels.
Add to Chrome
View on GitHub
No account, no servers, no tracking
youtube.com
How it works
Gentle until it isn't.
01
 Set your limits
Pick the sites that pull you in and give each a daily allowance, like 30 minutes of YouTube.
02
 Your patron steps in
When time's up, your saint walks on, raises a hand to pause the page, says a word, and sweeps it away.
03
 Return, if you must
"Return anyway" unlocks after a short countdown. Stay over your limit and they'll come back to check on you.
