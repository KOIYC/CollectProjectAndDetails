01 How it works
Your own room first. Then the whole home in 7 days.
No new drawings, no software and no contract to see it. Send one plan PDF and judge the result on your own house type, before you spend anything. How a floor plan becomes a 360 tour .
STEP 1 Send one plan PDF
 Email the floor plan of a house type you’re selling now and tell us which room to start with. Add finish specs or photos if you have them, or let us suggest a style.
STEP 2 Your demo room in 48 hours
 We build that room from your own plan as a 360 scene, with your name on it, and send you a private link. Free, with no deposit and nothing to sign.
STEP 3 A 15-minute call
 If you like what you see, we talk through the full model home, what’s included and a quote for your plan. You pay in full up front, before we build. No deposit, no subscription.
STEP 4 Live within 7 days
 Approve your finishes and the whole home is live within 7 days: on your website, with a link for Zillow and MLS and a QR sign for the lot. Add finish options or the rest of your catalogue whenever you’re ready.

05 FAQ
Questions builders ask.
What do I need to send you? The floor plan PDF for a house type you’re selling, and which room you’d like as your demo room. Elevations, photos of finished homes and finish specs help us match your look, but they’re optional. If anything on the drawings is unclear, we’ll ask.
 How long does it take? Your demo room within 48 hours of receiving a clear plan. The full tour is live within 7 days of you approving finishes. If something on the drawings needs checking, we ask first and the clock starts once we have what we need.
 What does it cost, and when do I pay? The demo room is free. There’s no deposit and nothing to commit to. If you want the full virtual model home, we quote per plan, with catalogue rates when you have several house types, and you pay in full before we build it.
 What if a room doesn’t match my plan? That’s our accuracy guarantee: if any room doesn’t match the plan you sent, we fix it free, as many times as it takes.
 Can you do our whole catalogue? Yes. Most builders start with one plan, then we build every house type at once, at catalogue rates. Extra finish options or colour schemes can be added per plan later.
 Is the tour an exact picture of the finished home? It’s an artist’s impression built from your plans. The layout follows your drawings; furniture, decor and landscaping are there to help buyers picture living in it.
 What happens to our plans? We use them only to build your demo room and, if you go ahead, your tours. They aren’t shared, sold, published or used for anyone else’s work. Demo rooms sit behind private links until you choose to share them. See our privacy page .
 Do buyers need an app or a headset? No. The tour opens from a link in any modern browser, on a phone, tablet or computer.
 Where are you based? London. We work with builders in the US and the UK.
