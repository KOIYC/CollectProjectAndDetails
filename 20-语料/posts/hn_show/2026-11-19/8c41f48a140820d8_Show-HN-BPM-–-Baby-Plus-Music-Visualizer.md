B.P.M. — Baby Plus Music ✕
A baby that dances to whatever music it hears. It finds the beat and the tempo, picks dances that suit the energy of the song, and dresses up along the way.
Give it music
Microphone listens to music playing in the room. Raise Sensitivity if the music is quiet.
Audio file plays a song from this device (you can also drag a file onto the page).
Demo plays a built-in track that builds from chill to a drop.
Steer it
Tap along to the beat (or press T ) if the tempo looks wrong. The outline pulses pink once your taps set it. If no music is being heard (for example, music playing on this same phone), the baby dances to your taps until it hears music.
Dance lets you tick which dances are allowed, or stick to just one.
Hats chooses the accessories: a surprise mix, a pack that stays on, or none.
Space pauses and resumes a song file.
Privacy
Everything runs inside this page. Sound from the microphone is analysed on the spot; it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The page works offline.
Microphone check
Microphone not in use.

3D rendering by three.js (MIT). Tempo second opinion by web-audio-beat-detector (MIT). Dances, beat tracking, accessories and demo track made for this project.
