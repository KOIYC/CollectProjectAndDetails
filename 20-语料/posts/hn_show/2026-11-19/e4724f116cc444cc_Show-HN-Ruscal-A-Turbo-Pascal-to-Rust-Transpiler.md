## 正文

Ruscal is a fairly-complete and well-tested Turbo Pascal to Rust transpiler, so you can run your old Turbo Pascal programs on modern computers. It supports mixing Rust and Turbo Pascal, embedding either way. It also supports graphics, including turtle graphics. I also included tools for pretty printing Turbo Pascal and converting old binary files to JSON. Great pain was taken to ensure accuracy with the original Turbo Pascal.
