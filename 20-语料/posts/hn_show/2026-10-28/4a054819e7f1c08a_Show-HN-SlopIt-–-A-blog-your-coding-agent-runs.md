## 正文

Hi HN! This is something I built out of my own need. I’m doing several projects on the side, and many of them need a CMS quickly (automated by my agents), and I thought there’s space for a very minimal thing that’s more agent-native instead of setting up the heavy blogging platforms.A few things that stand out here - you, as a human, cannot post or update your content. You need to tell your agent to do it. It's designed like this on purpose because that's where the industry is going.Although it's named SlopIt a bit tongue-in-cheek, obviously not trying to encourage actual slop here - I think if done the right way, LLM-assisted writing could be as good as human writing (and will only get better over time).Some of the example blogs I've setup for my projects:
- https://blog.rafikigo.com/why-we-started-rafikigo/ (Blog for a Kenyan tour company)
- https://blog.simbastack.com/ (Blog for my consulting firm)I'd love for you guys to try it out and provide feedback.--Getting started is super simple - just copy the prompt (shown in the Hero) and drop it into your agent and you have a new blog ready.I do recommend spending some time and effort in using Skills and projects like Humanizer (https://github.com/blader/humanizer) to make your content more readable for humans.Oh and the core is open source (MIT) - https://github.com/Simbastack-hq/SlopIt
