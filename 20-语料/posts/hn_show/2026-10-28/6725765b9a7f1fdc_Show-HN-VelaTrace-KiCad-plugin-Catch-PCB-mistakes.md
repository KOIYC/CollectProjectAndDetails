> **a2-_** · 2026-10-28T00:23:23.000Z　
> I design circuit boards as an hobby. Lot of times I would order one, wait for like two weeks, most of the time wouldn't work because it has a mistake, I have to fix it and order it again, it just keeps on going in a loop. To avoid this situation I built free KiCad plugin that checks your board before you order it.If you click "Check design" and in a second you get a list of things that you double-check, each with its suggested fix, since it is still in progress, it can catch around 4/10 problems, so I would recommend treating it as a first pass, like a linter. If you want more data you could check my KiCad forum post or Github, as more people contribute VelaTrace keeps on getting better.It also has a route feature which can route boards with its own built-in router: You can see the preview, KiCad checks it, and one Undo takes it back to where it was.It is free and open source(MIT). KiCad 10, tested on Windows. Installs from a zip. There's a demos board with mistakes planted on it if you wanna test it out.1) What mistake has cost you a board? any mistake, small or big would be helpful, for example recently I was using an mic for one of my PCBs which has 3 pins on bottom and top but, I accidently put all 6 pins in one row.GitHub link - https://github.com/F-Blaze/VelaTrace
