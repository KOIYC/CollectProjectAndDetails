 Fine print, in plain words
 What do I actually hand my AI? Open the kit folder in Claude Code and it reads CLAUDE.md on its own. In Cursor, Lovable, Bolt or v0, paste PROMPT.md and add DESIGN.md as context. Either way the AI starts from the original’s measured colors, fonts and spacing.
 Why not just send a screenshot? A screenshot makes the AI guess: which font, which gray, how much space. Each wrong guess costs you another prompt. The kit gives it the values, so there is less to correct.
 Is this legal? Copying your own site, or a public page to study how it’s built, is what the tool is for. Republishing someone else’s site as yours is not allowed, and our Terms forbid it. The text, images, logos and brand stay with the site’s owner: the kit gives you no license to them, so a rebuild should carry your own brand and content. We refuse pages with a password field and block banks, payment and sign-in sites, so the tool can’t be used to fake a login. Rules differ by country, and this isn’t legal advice.
 What can I do for free? Watch copies being made and check the preview, within a daily limit. Every new account also gets 2 credits once the email is confirmed, so you can download 2 pages, kit included, before paying anything. That gift comes once per email address. After that, downloads take credits, because running a real browser against a real site costs us money on every copy.
 Will pages behind a login work? Not yet. We copy what a visitor sees without signing in. A page that has a password box is refused on purpose, so the tool can’t be used for phishing.
 What is a credit? One credit copies one page of a site, kit included. A typical 4-page site takes 4 credits. You pick the pages and see the cost before anything starts. You only pay for pages that were actually copied; a page that fails costs nothing. Watching a copy before you pay is always free.
 Is there a subscription? Yes. Starter and Pro are monthly plans: new credits every month, and you can cancel anytime from your account (Manage subscription). Credits you already have never expire.
