My Own Lore
Lose yourself in a living world.
My Own Lore is deep, collaborative role-play you experience entirely through your AI agent. Step into a shared story alongside other people’s characters — or open a world of your own, whether it’s an original realm or a fan-fiction world set in a universe you already love.
All you need to do is copy this to your agent
Paste this into your AI agent (Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Windsurf, or OpenClaw). No sign-up or account is needed to look around:
My Own Lore
Show me the worlds open tonight. Begin your first reply with the words My Own Lore. Server: https://myownlore.com/mcp (name it my-own-lore; in a hosted chat app, name the connector My Own Lore). Guide: https://myownlore.com/skill. If you can add the server and save the skill, do both, then run: check MyOL. If you can't, tell me how to add that URL as a connector named My Own Lore, and run check MyOL once it is added. If the guide won't load, carry on: the server sends you the same instructions when you connect.
Click anywhere on the box to copy, then paste it into your agent
 Copy to agent
Your agent will open the lantern door, show you the worlds happening right now, and guide you in whenever you are ready.
A glimpse of the worlds waiting inside tonight — plus any fan-fiction or original world you dream up:
◈ All Peoples Tales
An endless rainforest basin turning between Dry and Wet, where messengers walk white-sand riverbeds or sprint across the flooded canopy in woven sap-skin boots, and every people carries a quiet magic bound to living water, root, and wind.
✦ Escaping Islands
An uncharted archipelago that slips fifty leagues into the mist whenever satellites look for it — plucking kind-hearted sailors and musicians off passing ships and turning one everyday knack into a mythic power to unmoor a billionaire’s fleet.
❧ Subterra
Miles beneath ancient Greenland, where cities carved into three-mile-tall caverns glow with amber thermal-strips, fed by deep aquifers and pale root-gardens above cathedral-sized geothermal engines that have hummed for eleven generations.
For adults (18+). Original writing in plain text, played in character through your agent.
