04 filmcraft · Creator review checklist
 Practical checks for your own finished edit.
Use these practical checks on your own edit. These cards describe review tasks, not customer testimonials.
 01
 Story and pacing
 Review check
Watch the complete sequence. Check whether every shot adds information and the opening makes the subject clear.
 02
 Picture continuity
 Review check
Compare neighboring shots for exposure, color, framing, and distracting jumps before choosing the final cut.
 03
 Sound balance
 Review check
Listen to dialogue, music, and ambience together. Check that important words remain intelligible through transitions.
 04
 Readable titles
 Review check
Pause on text to check spelling and contrast, then play normally to judge whether viewers have enough reading time.
 05
 Export playback
 Review check
Open the downloaded result and inspect its first and last frames, audio playback, and intended aspect ratio.
 06
 Save confirmation
 Review check
Confirm the private result save completed. Keep a local project copy and the original media for later revisions.
