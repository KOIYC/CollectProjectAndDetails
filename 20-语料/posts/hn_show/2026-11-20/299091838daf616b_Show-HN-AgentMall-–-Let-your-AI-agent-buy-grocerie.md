## 正文

Hey HN, we’re Nel and Kosi.We noticed personal agents like Instinct, Muse were terrible at buying stuff. Most of the time the checkout would fail.So we built reliable automations for agents to checkout at 9 leading grocery merchants.We grapple with anti bot, logins, OTP codes, and changing checkout structures.Loved revisiting this stuff given our backgrounds sneaker-botting and building computer use models. Honestly full circle haha.It currently works on these sites with ~70% checkout success rate:- Walmart
- Albertsons
- Ocado
- Wegmans
- FreshDirect
- Best Buy
- InstacartOur goal is to hill climb to 90%+ across each.Test keys are free and run the real flow without placing an order.New accounts get 10 free completed orders.https://docs.getagentmall.com/quickstart
