## 正文

Hi HN, I’m Imran, one of 3 founders building Relic AIWe started building Relic because we constantly experienced the problem of saving a lot of resources online such as websites, PDFs, Youtube videos and podcasts, but when we actually needed any of them, we couldn’t find them because they were burried in various places.We were saving stuff in open tabs, bookmarks, watch later lists and folders. But retrieving them when it mattered was a real problem for us. We spent a lot of time digging through our saves and redoing research that was already done just to find that one thing that we needed.So we decided to build a tool that helps you easily save resources, organizes them automatically and helps you find what you need when you need it.Relic lets you save web pages, PDFs, Youtube videos, notes, import bookmarks and open tabs, integrates with Notion and Google drive, and it even has an MCP so that you can conect it to AI tools you already use.We also have an AI chat inside Relic that only answers from your own saved resources with citations.Our free beta has been open since October and we want to hear as much feedback as possible.
