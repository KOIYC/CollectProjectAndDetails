FAQ
 Questions, answered.
 Anything else? Email hi@maglev.sh or ask on Slack .
What is Maglev?
 Maglev is an MCP server that gives AI agents 692 tools from 39 providers through one API key. You pay per call, at the provider's price, with no subscriptions. Tools cover Money and companies, Social, People and companies, Shopping and Business and SEO. How agents use Maglev
 How much does Maglev cost?
 You pay per call from one prepaid balance, at the price the provider charges, with no subscription and no monthly fee. The first 100 people who sign up get $5 of credit. The catalog shows every tool's price. See prices
 Which providers and tools does Maglev include?
 Maglev includes 692 tools from 39 providers across 15 categories. The largest are Money and companies, Social, People and companies, Shopping and Business and SEO. Browse the catalog
 How do I connect Maglev to Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or Claude Desktop?
 Claude Code: claude mcp add --scope user --transport http maglev https://app.maglev.sh/mcp. Codex: codex mcp add maglev --url https://app.maglev.sh/mcp, then log in. Cursor: add the server to ~/.cursor/mcp.json. Claude Desktop: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, then paste https://app.maglev.sh/mcp. Full setup steps in skill.md
 Do I need an account with each provider?
 No. One Maglev key covers every provider, so you never sign up with them separately.
 What is MCP?
 MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is an open standard that lets AI agents call external tools through a single connection. Maglev for agents
