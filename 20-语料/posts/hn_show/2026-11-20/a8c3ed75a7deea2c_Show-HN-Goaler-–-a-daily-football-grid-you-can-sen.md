What is Goaler?
 Goaler is a football trivia game you play against your friends. Play a puzzle, get a score out of 1,000, then send the link: your friend gets the exact same puzzle, on the same clock, and has to beat your score. There is no sign-up and no app to install; a nickname is all a challenge needs.
 Every puzzle is built from real careers: which clubs and which national teams a player appeared for, from Wikidata and English Wikipedia. Each cell has several correct answers, every board can be completed with nine different players, and samples of every new set are checked by hand before it goes live.
 You can send the same link to as many friends as you like. Each of them plays against your score, and your challenge page keeps the table of who beat whom. More about Goaler .
 The same data feeds our football stats and stories , and every past daily challenge, with all its answers, is in the daily archive .
How to play
 The board has three clubs or countries across the top and three down the side. For each of the nine cells, name a player who fits both: a club he played for, or the country he played for. Type any part of a name and pick him from the list.
 Each player can be used once. Every right answer scores, rarer answers score more, and finishing fast adds a time bonus. The best possible score is 1,000. You can finish early at any time; empty cells simply score nothing.
 Easy uses the biggest clubs and nations, Medium a wider pool, and Hard rare combinations. The full rules and tips .
 Bracket and Build Your XI have their own guides: how to play Bracket and how to play Build Your XI .

> 1. what is goaler - a soccer trivia with 3 games: tic tac toe grid game for players who played for two clubs or club and nationality, a head to head bracket game of "who has more?" with playoffs and final and build your XI based on specific conditions (like played for both Real Madrid and the Spanish national team)
> 2. why did I build it - Pure passion and love for football (soccer) and games. I wanted to create a social game where you can challenge your friends or people from around the world like in other games I used to play growing up (Song pop and Draw something)
> 3. How did I build it - mostly claude. I'm a product manager and used to be a game developer before that so I really enjoyed thinking about the UX here and delivering this as a full product although it still needs some work. I want to expand to more games and modes.
> 4. Some limitations - dynamic data about players moving from club to club and overall a dynamic database. Football fans are really strict about their stats so it needs to be treated with respect. I also think the difficulty levels might be too hard right now.
