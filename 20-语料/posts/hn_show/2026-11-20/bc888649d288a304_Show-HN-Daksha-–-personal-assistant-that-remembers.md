From a few words, or from your chats

Their voice, if you choose

 Daksha keeps personal things, so here is what happens to them, in plain words.
See
 Read every entry, and every memory Daksha made from them, with where it came from.

 If Daksha got something wrong, correct it, or make Daksha forget it.

 Download everything as plain files that open without Daksha.
Delete
 Delete any entry, and the memories made from it go a few seconds later. Or delete all your data.
Where it lives
 Your account, entries and files are stored with Neon, in the United States. Your memories are stored with Mem0. Cloudflare runs this website.
Who sees it
 You can read your entries. Daksha’s servers read them so they can search and answer. To write an answer, the matching pieces go to an AI company, and the privacy page names each one.
Honest limits
 Daksha is an early version. It is not end-to-end encrypted, because its servers have to read your entries to search them. We do not sell your data, show ads, or train AI on it ourselves. Some companies that help Daksha may keep data for a time, and we have not yet confirmed all of their rules. The privacy page says which.
 Read how Daksha handles your data
Questions people ask
Is Daksha finished? No. Daksha is an early version. Accounts, the journal, asking about your life, memories and talking out loud work today. Voice and video notes, connecting your apps, reminders, pictures and your own keys work too, but they are new and have had little real-world testing.
 Is it free? You can start for free. Each person has a daily limit on questions, so no one account can run up a big bill. Plans are not set yet, and we will tell you before anything costs money.
 Does Daksha make things up? Daksha answers only from what you have written, and shows the entry it used. When it finds nothing, it says it does not know instead of guessing.
 Can I use Daksha in Hindi? You can talk to Daksha out loud in English or Hindi.
 Who can read my entries? You can. Daksha’s servers also read them so they can search and answer. To answer a question, the matching pieces go to an AI company that writes the reply. The privacy page lists each company and what it does.
 How do I get my data out, or delete it? You can download your data from the app, and delete any entry or memory. The privacy page explains exactly what each delete button removes.
 Will Daksha do things without asking? Not outside Daksha. Before it sends, posts, pays, deletes or changes anything in your other apps, it asks for a clear yes on the screen. It also asks before deleting anything inside Daksha. Small changes inside Daksha that you asked for, like saving an entry, happen straight away.
