The Soapbox
 Sign in Next draw 08:00 tomorrow What would you say if you could email a million people?
 Every morning, one subscriber is picked at random to write a single email. It goes to the whole
 list. Tomorrow, it could be you.
 Email address
Put my name in the hat No passwords. One email a day, at most. Leave whenever you like.
How it works
 1 Join the list
 Drop your email in. We send a link to check it's really you.
2 The draw, 08:00 daily
 Each morning one subscriber is picked at random. Invite friends for extra tickets, up to 10.
3 Say your piece
 The winner writes one email. A human reads it, then it goes to everyone at 08:00 the next morning.
Bring a friend
 1 ticket for joining. +1 for every friend who joins with your link.
10 max
The small print, in big print
 Is it anonymous?
 Yes, unless you sign your name. We never show the writer's email address.
Who sees my reply?
 The writer. They see your message and your email address. Every Broadcast says so.
What if I'm picked and don't fancy it?
 Ignore the email. No penalty, you stay in the draw.
Can people write anything?
 No. A human reads every Soapbox first. Be kind, be legal, don't sell stuff.
Your ticket's waiting.
 It takes ten seconds and one email address.
 Join the draw
