## 正文

We wanted a way to track AI visibility locally in an agent first way, so we built Canonry.On top of AI visibility, it integrates GSC, Analytics, server traffic, and ads to give your agent a full view of your site's visibility.Some of the things we've built on top of Canonry: an automated content generation engine, in-depth site health check monitor, ads optimizer.We learned a lot about AI visibility and how to optimize sites during this process, happy to answer any questions!
