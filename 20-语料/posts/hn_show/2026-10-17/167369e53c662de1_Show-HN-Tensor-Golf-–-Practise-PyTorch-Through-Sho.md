## 正文

Hello HN!Excited to share Tensor Golf!I'm excited to share this with everyone here, I've been seeing a lot of requests for tensor manipulation exercises and so I thought I'd create a place to build confidence and fluency in working with PyTorch tensors!I've found that understanding ML concepts and mathematics is one thing, but implementing them in PyTorch is a totally seperate beast.Tensor Golf lets you practise working with tensors in a puzzle format, with the least number of operations giving you a better score - hence the "golf" part.The core set of practise questions are free and I'm considering open sourcing the core set of puzzles :)Let me know if you find this useful!ps:
