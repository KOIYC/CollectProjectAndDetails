## 正文

I’m writing my first novel and could not find an app that fit my workflow: quiet like iA Writer, organized like Scrivener, and with AI so I can discuss my book while I write it. It allows me to feel less “alone” and have a sounding board while I write.- A book is a folder of plain Markdown files. Musset commits to a git repo, tracks versions and drafts without you having to know git.- Revisions are CriticMarkup inside the text. It manages docx revisions too, so your editor can edit a Word doc and you import their comments.- PDFs are typeset using Typst, embedded in the app, following each language’s typographic rules.- Spelling and grammar are checked using LanguageTool, also embedded.- Mac/Word, WordStar, vi and Emacs keyboard modes.The AI is optional and uses your Codex or Claude Code CLI. It comments, leaves margin notes, and checks continuity and your chapters against your style rules.It’s GPL-3.0 and free during the beta. When or if it reaches 1.0, if there is sufficient interest, the idea is to make it a small one-time payment ($9.90). There is a paid sync service (git hosting) that lets you share a book with beta readers and editors.Download: https://musset.appI’d love to have some feedback!
