## 正文

I've seen a rising demand for context when AI queries data from different places, so I built a way to provide it.You basically describe the tables, write free-form markdown notes and set definitions (e.g. the SQL for how to calculate an active user, so everyone in the company calculates it the same way). It support Apache Ossie and the context can live in your codebase or my service or both.
