# Edgy: every edge and corner of your trackpad, mapped to whatever you want.

Edgy is a small menu-bar app for your Mac.

- Slide the right edge for volume.
- Hold a corner and talk to your agent.
- Double-tap a corner to open your terminal.
- The same edge does a different job in every app.
- Edgy tells you when it says no.

## Two ways to get Edgy

The signed app: $5 once, updates included. Nearly ready.

The source: same app, free under the MIT license. You build and update it yourself.

Both run on macOS 14 or later.
