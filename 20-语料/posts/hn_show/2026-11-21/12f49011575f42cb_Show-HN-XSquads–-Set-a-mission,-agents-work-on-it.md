Common Questions
 What is a personal AI assistant for work?
 A personal AI assistant for work takes on research, drafts, follow-ups and reports for one person, on the files they already use. In xSquads that is a personal squad of AI teammates. Finished work waits in Review until you approve it.
What is an xSquad?
 An xSquad is your own squad of AI teammates. Each bot has a role, like Growth Lead or Research Analyst, and picks up cards on a shared task board while you direct the work and review the results.
Is xSquads only for developers?
 No. xSquads is built for all office work: marketing, research, support, operations, finance, HR, legal, product and more. Software is just one of the things a squad can do.
Where does my data live?
 Missions point at a local folder, so bots work on your own files and folders on your machine. You choose what each squad can see.
Do the bots replace people?
 No. Bots take on the busywork and hand finished work to Review. A human checks it before anything moves to Done.
How do we get started?
 Use our setup and rollout service. We run discovery, set up a squad for every knowledge worker, connect tools and folders, define roles and routines, train your team and set guardrails.
Can you build a squad for our process?
 Yes. With Custom xSquads we design bespoke squads for your specific use cases, including custom roles, routines, integrations and internal tools.
