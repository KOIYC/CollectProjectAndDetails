## 正文

Hey HN, I have build a tool after my own job searching. You describe the job you want in a sentence, like "backend roles in Zurich, Python, no banks", and it searches the web and companies' own career pages for it. There is no bias, no curated lists, no promotion; only a pure and powerful search.I wanted to get the real postings. The same jobs can show up to many boards and recruiter sites, often after it's closed. Don't you want to know this? Craoli tries to find the employer's own postings, shows one card for all the copies that exist out there, and keeps checking whether the job is still open.Another issue I had was that one company while I submitted my job application, changed the job description, by adding one more requirement that was not there when I have applied!! Good luck proving that. Of course the recruiter brought this point for my rejection, that I was sure that it was not there when I applied. 7+ years of pre-sales is hard to miss on a job description when read.There are some limits for now; career pages are read directly only for the common hiring systems; a custom site is only found through web search. And every search costs me real money, so you need an account, and free has 2 searches a month using a cheaper LLM model; the search costs the same. Additional classical features are there for you to discover, like tailored CVs and cover letters.What I would like to know: the last time you looked for a job, what made you stop trusting a listing?
