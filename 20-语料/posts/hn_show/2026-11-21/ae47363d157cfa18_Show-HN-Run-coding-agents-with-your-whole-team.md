About Pangea What Pangea is
 Pangea is a hosted workspace where a team runs its coding agents together. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Antigravity and other agents work in shared sessions that several people can join; risky steps wait for a person to approve them; and every prompt, tool call and approval is kept in a tamper-evident audit trail. Each person signs the agent in with the plan they already pay for, and Pangea charges for the workspace, never for model tokens.
