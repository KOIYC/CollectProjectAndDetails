Several ways of looking, brought together
 Quality, valuation, the tape, risk and sentiment are each analysed on their own terms, then combined — so a company is judged on more than whichever number happens to be flattering this quarter.
 The same standard for every company
 Every covered name is measured the same way on the same day and ranked against real peers. Nothing is cherry-picked, and nothing is skipped because it was quiet.
 AI where it adds something
 It reads and interprets the news, the developments and the crowd, weighs them against each other and writes what it makes of them. What it does not do is set or alter the canonical scores, ratios and valuations — those come from the models and the data.
 We say when we don't know
 When the evidence isn't strong enough for a valuation, we publish no number rather than a confident-looking guess. Every figure carries the date and market state behind it.
