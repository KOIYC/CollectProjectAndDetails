Executive Inquiries
Frequently Asked Questions
Answers to common inquiries submitted by shareholders, founders, and regulatory agencies.
Is this game really free to play right here in my browser?
 ▼
Yes. Ultracapitalism runs directly inside any modern web browser via WebGL with zero plugins, zero downloads, and zero mandatory account creation. All seed funding is provided on credit.
How does the asynchronous ghost PvP system work?
 ▼
At the close of every fiscal year, your company snapshot—including your active asset board, policy lineup, and valuation metrics—is synced to the corporate server ledger. When you battle in an End-of-Year Review, matchmaking pairs you against a real executive's fleet recorded at that exact same fiscal year and record, simulated deterministically tick-by-tick.
What happens during the 30-Second Market Crash?
 ▼
If a boardroom battle lasts longer than 30 seconds, the macroeconomy implodes. Cash burns at a base penalty plus an escalating percentage every second. The only way to survive is through deep Moat reserves, fast-acting damage combos, or Surplus conversion.
Can I liquidate my co-founder mid-run?
 ▼
In Ultracapitalism, all assets can be sold back to liquidators for 50% of their initial purchase value. Whether you should sacrifice your co-founder to buy an Orbital Tax Haven is an ethical question outside the scope of our software license.
