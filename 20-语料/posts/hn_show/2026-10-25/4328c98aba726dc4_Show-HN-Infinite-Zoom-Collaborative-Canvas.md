The Neverending Painting
 One shared AI painting with unbounded, persistent depth. Zoom into unexplored territory and an image model paints what lies deeper; the result becomes a permanent part of the painting, and you name what you found.
 Free to explore in your browser, without an account. Sign in only when you want credit for the places you discover — and the right to name them.
