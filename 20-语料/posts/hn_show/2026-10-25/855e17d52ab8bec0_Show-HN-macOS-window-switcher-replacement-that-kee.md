 Free and open source · macOS 26 or later · Apple Silicon
Is Debut free? Yes. Debut is free and open source.
Will it work on my Mac? Any Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later. Adding, removing and merging Spaces requires macOS 27.
How do I install it? Open the download, drag Debut to Applications, and open it. Allow Accessibility when asked.
Why does Debut need Accessibility? macOS requires it before an app can respond to shortcuts like Command Tab or move windows.
Why does Debut ask for Screen Recording? To show previews of your windows in the switcher. It's optional. Without it, Debut shows app icons instead. Previews never leave your Mac.
Does it send my data anywhere? No. Everything stays on your Mac. Crash reports are only sent if you allow them.
Does it replace Mission Control? No. Debut works alongside Mission Control and your existing Spaces.
