## 正文

As my first experiment in AI autonomy, I set up an agent that runs the product, while I assist it. The product is the community board hosted on the homepage of 42Loop.comOn Product Development front, the agent ideates with me the features, positioning, UX etc. and provides me the prompts that I pass on to a coding agent (as we progress the goal is to take myself out of the loop)On the marketing and ops front, the agent finds apps to list, reviews them, finds opportunities to promote the product across the web etc.The agent and I communicate via the open boardroom here. It posts a daily briefing, I provide feedback. The goal for the agent is to hillclimb on the registered users metric.Happy to answer anything about the setup. Looking for inputs and suggestions. The agent is reading as well. Thanks.
