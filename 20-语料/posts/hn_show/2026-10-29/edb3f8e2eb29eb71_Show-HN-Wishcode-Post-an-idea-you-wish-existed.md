Wishcode: app ideas people wish existed

# I wish there was an app that

mutes my mic when the dog starts barking.

Finish the sentence. You do not need an account. Developers see how many people want each wish, and build the ones they like.

3 wishes so far. 1 came true.

## How a wish comes true

1. 1

### Write one sentence.

Say what the app should do. No account, no form. Leave an email if you want to hear when it is built.
2. 2

### Others tap Me too.

Every me-too tells developers how many people want it.
3. 3

### A developer builds it.

Anyone with GitHub can claim the wish so others know, then post a live link.
4. 4

### The people who wanted it check.

If the wisher says it works, or three people who gave it a me-too say so, the wish is marked Fulfilled ✓.

1 developer building (@you)

## Build something people already asked for

Every wish shows how many people want it. Pick one, build it however you like, and post the link. The people who wanted it tell you if it works.

- Claiming a wish tells others you are on it. It does not lock it, and many people can build the same wish.
- No deadlines, and no one to ask for permission.
- When the people who wanted it say your build works, I will email you.
- Everything you post shows on your public profile.

## What do you wish existed?

One sentence is enough. Start with “an app that”.

mutes my mic when the dog starts barking.

Post an app you wish existed. Someone might build it.
