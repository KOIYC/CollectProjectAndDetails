## 正文

I burned about $500 for this fun project: Small Python.
Initial PoC used 3 resets of Codex in one session but all later development happens in Claude Code, also in one Opus 5.5 session over three days.The outcome is a quickjs-like implementation for Python.
Almost full Python 3.15 language features plus builtins are covered.
No Lib/ support.
Great for embedding scenarios.No rust. Just plain C11 code.Repo is here: https://github.com/soasme/small-python/Ask me anything.
