Questions, answered
Planning your day with FluxTime
How is FluxTime different from a to-do list? A to-do list tells you what is left; it does not tell you whether it fits. FluxTime places tasks next to your calendar events on a timeline, links each task to a goal, and keeps a record of how the time was really spent, so you can see both the plan and the result.
Do I have to track my time all day? No. There is no stopwatch to start and stop. You add a short journal entry when you switch activities, from your Mac, iPhone or Apple Watch, and FluxTime fills in the durations between entries.
What does Flux AI do in the planner? Flux AI drafts a day plan from your goals, tasks, open time and energy patterns, suggests the next task when you are unsure, and answers questions about your history, such as where last week went. You review every suggestion before it changes your plan.
Is FluxTime free? Yes. FluxTime is free to download on the App Store. Flux Pro adds smart day planning, unlimited Flux AI, advanced Review insights, Pro widgets, cloud sync, Apple Calendar integration, and your data in Claude.
Can I ask Claude about my FluxTime data? Yes, with Flux Pro. FluxTime has an MCP server: add it as a connector in Claude or Claude Code, sign in and allow access, then ask where your week went or which goal is falling behind. If you also let it make changes, Claude can add tasks, move events and log time for you; it asks before deleting anything, and a deletion can be undone. How to connect .
