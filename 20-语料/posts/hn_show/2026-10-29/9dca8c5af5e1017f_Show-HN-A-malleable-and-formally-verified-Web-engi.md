## 正文

Hello HN,I've been working on a new Web engine, build around two ideas:- use formal methods from day one.
- make it modular.The modularity is orientated towards the following goals:- enabling shipping anywhere, including on IOS in the EU as per Apple’s guidelines.
- only write code for stuff the OS or application platform cannot do for you: keep binary size to a minimum and achieve native performance.
- developer choice: bring what you want, and only build what you need.For example you can choose a JS engine like v8 or Boa, or just bring your own. The entire graphics pipeline consists of components whose implementation can be swapped-out, with currently a relatively fast path on Mac integrating with Core Animation and a slower cross platform path.Project is at https://github.com/gterzian/formal-webIntro essay at: https://medium.com/@polyglot_factotum/how-i-am-building-a-we...Please let me know what you think.
