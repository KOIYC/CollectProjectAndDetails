Rocus - AI Knowledge Graph & Collaborative Bookmark Manager

# Turn everything you save into a memory you can search.

Links, PDFs, notes — Rocus weaves them into one private, searchable knowledge graph you actually come back to.

Save any page, note, or file

## Why Rocus?

 Three powerful reasons to transform how you organize your digital life

### Single Knowledge Map

 It turns scattered links and searches into a single, living map of knowledge, instantly showing relationships Google or bookmarks or tab grouping can't.

### Zero Mental Load

 Automatically clusters, labels, and connects all content, so users never dig through tabs, bookmarks, or repeated searches again.

### Hidden Connections

 Recommends related links, hidden connections, and trending content based on the user's own map, revealing information they wouldn't discover with standard search.

## One Graph, Every Feature You Need

 From your first saved link to a fully collaborative research hub - Rocus grows with how you work.

### Real-Time Collaboration

 Share any album with a live link. Invite collaborators, watch every change sync instantly, and see who's online - no more sending screenshots of your research.

### Notes That Cluster Themselves

 Jot down a thought and Rocus automatically connects it to the right topic in your graph - or drops it in a shared Notes hub if nothing matches yet.

### Save Any File, Not Just Links

 Upload PDFs and documents alongside your web saves. Rocus extracts and organizes them into the same knowledge graph - no manual sorting required.

### One-Click Browser Extension

 Save any page in a single click from Chrome or Firefox. Pick an album, or let quick-add sort it into your graph automatically.

### Discover Similar Websites

 One click surfaces related pages and resources you haven't found yet, powered by the connections already in your own graph.

### Local or Cloud AI - Your Call

 Run the AI entirely in your browser for full privacy, or switch to faster cloud processing when you need it. Either way, your graph stays yours.

## Built for Privacy & Freedom

 Your data stays yours. Open source, Local processing, and fully transparent.

### Complete Data Control

 Your browsing data never leaves your device.

### Open Source Transparency

 Review our code on GitHub and verify our privacy promises.

### Decentralized

 Browser-based AI model that runs completely on your device.

### Decentralized AI

 Rocus is a browser-based AI that runs entirely on your device. Your data stays private, no servers involved, and you stay in full control. Fast, Safe, and Decentralized.
