 Common questions
 WhatsApp chat export and sharing, explained.
 How do I export a WhatsApp chat? Open the conversation in WhatsApp and use Export chat. On Android, look under the three-dot menu, then More. On iPhone, open the contact or group information. Choose whether to include media, then save or share the export.
 Read the WhatsApp chat export guide Why is Export chat missing in WhatsApp? Usually because Advanced Chat Privacy is on in that chat, which stops anyone in it from exporting. In a group, the admins control that setting. Also check where you are looking: on Android, Export chat is under the three-dot menu, then More; on iPhone, it is near the bottom of the contact or group info.
 Troubleshoot a WhatsApp chat export How do I save a WhatsApp chat? Use Export chat in WhatsApp to create a copy of the conversation, then store the TXT or ZIP file somewhere you control, such as cloud storage or your computer. Saving a chat this way keeps the messages readable outside WhatsApp, which a backup on its own does not.
 Read the guide to saving a WhatsApp chat What is a WhatsApp chat export viewer? A chat export viewer turns the messages in an exported conversation into an easier-to-read layout. Preserve’s workflow brings together a chat view, message search, range selection, and a page to share with someone else.
 Learn about TXT and ZIP chat exports How can I share a WhatsApp conversation without screenshots? Instead of a folder of screenshots or a raw text file, a chat-sharing tool can present a selected part of the conversation as a readable page someone opens in a browser. Preserve is built around choosing a first and last message, previewing the exchange, and sharing that one link.
 Compare ways to share a conversation Can I turn a WhatsApp chat into a PDF? WhatsApp has no Export as PDF option. You export the chat, open the transcript, and print it to PDF from your phone or computer. A PDF suits a process that needs a fixed document to attach. When someone simply needs to read the exchange, a readable link is usually easier for them than a long file to download.
 Compare a PDF with a shareable link What is the difference between a TXT export and a ZIP file? A TXT file contains the conversation as text. A ZIP file is a container that can package the transcript with separate media files. A reference to an attachment in the transcript does not mean that the attachment itself is included.
 See what is inside a TXT or ZIP export Can an exported WhatsApp chat be restored into WhatsApp? No. A chat export is a copy to read outside WhatsApp. It is different from a WhatsApp backup or phone-to-phone chat transfer, and it cannot be imported back into WhatsApp as a conversation.
