 How does yume work?
 Pick a video template you like, add a few details, get your film. That's it. Think of it as a Hollywood film crew in your pocket :)
 Every video in the catalog is a template we wrote and directed ourselves, so your film looks like cinema. You supply the person and the facts. We take care of the full video production. There is nothing to edit and no software to learn.
 yume is the simplest way to produce high-quality personalized videos that capture attention and make people smile.
What do I need to provide?
 A few photos and a couple of details. Filling out the form takes about two minutes.
 The form is built for the video you chose and only asks for what that video needs. For the career videos, your LinkedIn link fills in most of it. For the rest, it's a few photos and a handful of quick questions, like a name or a favorite thing. We pull everything else from the template.
When will my video be ready?
 Usually about 10 to 15 minutes after you submit your details.
 You will receive your finished video via email as soon as it's ready :) 🍿
Can I change something after I get my video?
 Yes. Every video includes one edit.
 Once your video is ready, open it while signed in and choose Edit video. Tell our assistant everything you'd like changed and add new photos if you like. It sums up your changes, and once you confirm, we make them and send you the new version within minutes.
 The edit only counts once the new version is delivered. If we can't make a change, you keep your edit.
Can I buy a video as a gift?
 Yes. A yume video is one of the most personal gifts you can give.
 Add their photos and details instead of your own, and the film is about them. When it's ready, you get a beautiful personal page for the video that you can share with them in a message, or play it for them in person. They don't need an account, and they can watch and download it as often as they like.
