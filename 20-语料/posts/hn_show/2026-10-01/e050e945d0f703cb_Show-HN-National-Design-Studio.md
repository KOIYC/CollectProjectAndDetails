 Designed in D.C. By National Design Studio
National Design Studio is transforming how Americans experience their government on the web and in the world.
Ten Billion Hours 1
 Every year, Americans lose more than 10,000,000,000 hours navigating their own government—waiting, searching, repeating, timing out, starting over.
 That isn’t just inefficient. It’s undignified. It’s time taken from families, businesses, dreams, and lives, and we’re here to give it back. Months reduced to minutes. Ninety clicks become nine. Complexity becomes clarity.
 This is a new standard for how a great nation serves its people—with speed, intelligence, dignity, and care.
 The next 250 years begin by giving Americans something priceless: their time.
