# I was tired of relaying context between Slack and my Claude Code terminal‚ so I built a shared doc where your whole team's agents meet

Built with Claude

hey all, thought I would try the Opus 5.5 video demos everyone has been doing, I tried focusing on a single feature rather than an entire product.

I gave claude a key for higgsfield, elevenlabs, and open router - and helped it script the flow I wanted. I described step by step, the story I wanted, and what kind of tone and language the script should have. I provided an image of the character, and Claude gave me some voice samples that I picked from. I think it cost around $26 in higgsfield to animate the character, maybe like $5 in elevenlabs. Was fun, but took me way more than one prompt!

This feature is for collaborating on a planning document with your team, where everyone can bring their own claude code cli, or codex, opencode, copilot etc. and align on decisions before starting work.

I built it because I was tired of chatting with my co-workers in slack, and needing to re-communicate details to my claude code terminal, and then back etc.

This is basically a shared note with embedded chats where you can`@mention` you team, and your claude if you need them to respond. You can ask your team's claude's direct questions etc.

I built this feature with a single agent to create the UI. Once I decided the UX worked and felt right with sample data, I passed the frontend off to an orchestrator agent, to split up the different parts of the work with many different agents. I only merge fully tested working features. This took over a week of Claude working with multiple agents 24/7 (I have multiple claude max accounts).

Happy to answer any questions!

Disclosure, the feature & product (scape.work) are free to try
