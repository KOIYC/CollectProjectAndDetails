Features
 Built for the way a Mac actually works.
 More than just a screen scraper with a network socket bolted on.
Hardware encode, end to end
 Apple silicon captures and encodes your screen via media engine's fixed-function silicon. Your CPU cores stay free for actual work.
Headless Mac support
 Each session gets a virtual display at the client's exact resolution, so you get a desktop at the right size, not a scaled copy.
Multi-monitor support
 Use all of your monitors and get dedicated virtual desktops for each of them. Seamlessly move between then just like you're natively connected.
Choose your display mode
 Control your Mac, mirror it's screens, or create new extended displays for more working area. You're in control of your window configuration.
Input that feels attached
 Keyboard, mouse, and vertical and horizontal scrolling, with an optional Ctrl-to-⌘ swap. I-beams, resize arrows, and pointing hands are drawn by the client locally.
The Mac's sound, on your client
 Audio streams to the client as 44.1 kHz stereo, and the Mac's own speakers can be muted for the session so it doesn't play in two places at once.
Any RDP client
 Stock mstsc.exe connects with default settings: no .rdp edits, NLA included. The Windows App and FreeRDP work too, with no plugins or custom app.
Clipboard and files
 Copy text, rich text, HTML, and images in either direction. Files and whole folders copy both ways too, and paste in Finder like any other file.
No cloud required
 Just like Windows RDP, there's no cloud subscriptions or accounts to manage. Simply lanuch the server and connect from your RDP client of choice.
Try it on your own Mac.
 Free for home and personal use.
