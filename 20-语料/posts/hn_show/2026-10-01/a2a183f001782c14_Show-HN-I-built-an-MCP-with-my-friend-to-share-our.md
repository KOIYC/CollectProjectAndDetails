## 正文

Me and my friend (Fred) made 3x profit ($400 on ads and $1200 in profit) with X Ads this month and surprisingly, we did it well starting from very first week - we just didn't have enough money (we still don't have) to spend more in early steps. Many founders / builders asked as how we managed to be profitable from day one with ads. So, we decided why not to put all our knowledge in a structured way into one MCP and sell it in affordable price with license keys? So, that what we built - we launched few days ago and already made more than 10 sales, pretty happy with the result. So many builders in this community, sharing so that maybe we can help them as well (and ofc we get paid).Looking forward for community feedback at least.
