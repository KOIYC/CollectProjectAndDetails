# Kapwing

Caption, translate & dub video

Kapwing Translate this video into French Kapwing Dub this video into Korean and add Korean subtitles near the bottom Kapwing Add yellow subtitles on this video podcast episode

Translate, subtitle, and dub videos and audio from one language to another using Kapwing. Upload a video from your computer, transcribe it, add subtitles in the original or a translated language, and dub the speech into other languages in voices cloned from the original speakers. You can also browse your workspace's recent projects and media library, check your remaining credits, and export projects to MP4 with a download link when they're done. Every result links to the project in the Kapwing editor for further changes.

Limitations: the plugin only works with workspaces your connected Kapwing account belongs to, and it cannot delete projects or media. Transcription, translation, and dubbing use your workspace's existing Kapwing credits. Timeline edits such as trims and transitions happen in the Kapwing editor. Dubbing replaces the audio only and is not lip-synced.
