FAQ
Questions, answered.
What is Nysa? Nysa is shared memory for teams that work with AI agents. It turns your team's meetings, Gmail, and Google Calendar into one brain that every teammate's agents work from, and teammates and their agents can add to it over MCP.
Does Nysa record our meetings? Yes. When you invite Nysa's notetaker, it joins the call as a visible participant, records it, and transcribes who said what. It only joins the meetings you or your team choose, and recordings are visible to the teammates who attended and anyone you share them with.
Who can see what? Each fact is scoped automatically, and each agent sees only what its teammate can see. Admins describe scopes like HR or Board in plain words, projects can be private to their members, and agent API keys can be limited to read only or to specific projects or scopes. Raw email stays private to its owner; the team sees only the facts drawn from it. Who sees what
Is my data used to train AI models? No. Nysa uses your data only to provide the service and never to train AI models. See the Privacy Policy .
How has Nysa's security been tested? TAC Security, a lab authorized by the App Defense Alliance, put Nysa through a CASA Tier 2 security assessment, and we passed all requirements. Google also reviewed how we use Gmail and Calendar data and approved our access.
Which agents work with Nysa? Any agent that connects over MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Codex. Setup guides: Claude · ChatGPT · Other agents .
Can agents write to the brain? Yes. Agents read from and write to the same brain over MCP, each signed in as its teammate.
Can I import my existing brain? Yes. Bring in the memory you already have, whether it's GBrain, an Obsidian vault, or any folder of markdown notes. You review a preview before anything is added, imported notes follow the same scopes as everything else, and you can undo the whole import. How importing works
Which email and calendar accounts does Nysa support? Google accounts today: personal Gmail and Google Workspace accounts that use Gmail and Google Calendar. Outlook and Microsoft 365 are not supported yet.
How much does Nysa cost? Plans are priced per team, by team size. See Pricing for current plans.
How is Nysa different from GBrain? GBrain is software you run yourself: your server, database, API keys, and your own pipelines to feed it meetings and email. Nysa is managed for you. It records and transcribes your team's meetings, imports email history and syncs your calendar the day you connect, and scopes every fact to who should see it. Your agents keep using MCP; they just point at Nysa. Full comparison
