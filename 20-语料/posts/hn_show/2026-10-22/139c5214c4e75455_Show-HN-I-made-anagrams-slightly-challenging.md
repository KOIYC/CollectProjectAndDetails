Good to know
 Is the daily Anagrams puzzle the same for everyone? Yes. Everyone gets the exact same anagram challenge on the same day for a given board size, so you can compare scores and share results on an even playing field.
 How do I unscramble the letters? You can tap the letter tiles directly on your screen or type on your physical keyboard. Press Backspace to undo a letter, hit Shuffle to rearrange the letter bank, or hit Check Word to submit.
 What happens if I get stuck on an anagram? You can click 'Grid' at the top left to return to the board and solve other tiles first without penalty, or press 'Skip' if you want to forfeit that tile.
 How do tile timers work? Each colored tile on the board has its own time limit: Blue tiles give you 60 seconds for longer words, Yellow tiles give you 40 seconds, and Red tiles give you 25 seconds for shorter words.
