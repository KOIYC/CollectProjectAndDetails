FAQ
 Questions about the resume builder
Is the resume builder really free?
 Yes. Building, saving and downloading your resume as a PDF are free, with no watermark and no credit card. You get 20 free AI actions every day, such as improving bullet points or drafting your summary.
 Is the resume ATS-friendly?
 Yes. The template is a single column of real text with standard section headings (Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications), which applicant tracking systems read reliably. The PDF contains selectable text, not an image.
 Will the AI make things up?
 No. The AI only rewords what you write: stronger verbs, clearer phrasing, a concise summary. It is instructed never to invent employers, dates, numbers, tools or achievements. Always read the result before you use it.
 What are verified certificates?
 When you finish a free Nokku.ai course, you earn a certificate with a public verification page. The resume builder lists your certificates so you can add them in one click, each with a link employers can use to check it.
 Do I need an account?
 Yes, a free Nokku.ai account, so your resume can be saved and you can come back to edit it. You can delete your resume at any time.
 How do I download my resume as a PDF?
 Click Download PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. The PDF matches the live preview exactly.
