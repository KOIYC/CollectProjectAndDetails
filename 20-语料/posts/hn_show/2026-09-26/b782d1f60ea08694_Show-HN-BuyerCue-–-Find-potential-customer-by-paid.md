## What the evidence is, and isn't

- Every record shows the date we last saw it, so you always know how fresh it is. Anything we haven't re-confirmed in 90 days is flagged as stale.
- When we can't confirm something, we leave it blank instead of guessing. Pricing, country and contact details appear wherever we've found them, and gaps are clearly marked.
- Detected tools and ad pixels are strong clues about how a company operates. They show what's installed, which is a great starting point, though not proof of a paid plan or current ad spend.
- Scores help you decide where to start. They reflect general buying signals, so they work best alongside your own sense of fit for your product.
