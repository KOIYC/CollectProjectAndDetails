 How does Agent Jesse find leads that aren't in databases? + Agent Jesse crawls the live internet in real-time — permit filings, conference pages, LinkedIn posts, news articles, job boards, and hundreds of other public sources. That's why Agent Jesse surfaces buyers that no static database can.
What do I get with the free plan? + The Free Tier includes 1,000 Agent Jesse Credits, live people & company search, saved lists, and 1 daily alert — no credit card required. Email and phone enrichment unlock on Pro, Business, and Max.

Our average email deliverability rate is above 95%. Every lead is checked before it reaches your inbox so you never waste time on bounced emails.

What makes Agent Jesse different from Apollo or ZoomInfo? + Apollo and ZoomInfo rely on static, middleware databases — data that's often stale by the time you use it. Agent Jesse crawls the live internet in real-time to find leads who are actually in-market today.
