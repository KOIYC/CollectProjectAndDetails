# Choreoglyphs

our new writing system is invisible

 When we were writing by hand, we would draw the shape of each letter. Each letter would choreograph the movement of our hand. Now, when using swipe keyboard on our phones, we make a movement connecting all the letters in a word. Each word is created by this invisible movement, by this choreoglyphic notation. But how do these invisible word shapes actually look like? Play around below to find out.
