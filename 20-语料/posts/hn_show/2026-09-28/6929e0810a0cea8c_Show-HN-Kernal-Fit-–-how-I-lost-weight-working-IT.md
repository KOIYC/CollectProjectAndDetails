Kernal Fit
 I lost the desk-job weight without quitting my desk.
I'm an IT professional. No gym membership, no heavy dieting, no 5am boot camps. Just small changes to how I eat, built around a real workday. This is exactly what I did, written down.

Why I wrote this
I spend my days the way a lot of IT people do: long stretches in a chair, lunch at the keyboard, snacks within arm's reach, and a brain too busy solving problems to think about food. The weight crept on the same way the hours did - quietly, and then all at once.
Every plan I tried assumed a life I don't have. Two hours at the gym. Meal prep Sundays. Willpower I ran out of by Wednesday. So I stopped trying to overhaul everything and started changing a few small eating habits instead - ones that fit inside a workday instead of fighting it.
It worked. Steady loss, week after week, while still sitting at the same desk. Kernal Fit is the whole method: what I changed, why it worked, and how to make it fit your own routine.
What's inside
The habits that actually moved the needle The small, specific eating changes I made - no calorie obsession, no banned foods.
How to eat around a desk job Desk lunches, vending machine traps, conference room donuts, and the late-night debugging snack. Real situations, real swaps.
Why willpower isn't the plan Set your environment up so the easier choice is the better one, even on your worst deadline week.
Keeping it off without thinking about it How the habits hold up through crunch time, travel, and holidays - because they're small enough to survive them.
Who this is for
This is for you if...
You work in IT or spend most of your day at a screen
You've tried diets that didn't survive your schedule
You want steady progress, not a 30-day bootcamp
You'd rather change a few habits than your whole life
This isn't for you if...
You want a bodybuilding or athlete training plan
You're looking for meal plans with exact macros
You want medical advice (talk to your doctor for that)
