Description
Boonful is one place to build what you make, sell it, and run the business behind it.
Publisher Studio turns an idea into a real product page in minutes — apps, books, courses, podcasts, newsletters, films, digital downloads and physical goods. Add an icon, screenshots and a price, connect Stripe, and you have a page you can send to anyone, with checkout, subscriptions and free trials handled for you.
Business is your inbox and your customers in one view: a real @boonful.io address, every sale and subscriber, refunds you can issue yourself, and where in the world your readers are.
Search is a fast, private search engine built on its own index, with an assistant that answers from what it actually finds — and Posts sits alongside it, so you can follow, write and publish without leaving.
Library and Work hold everything else. Books, audiobooks, music, fonts and graphics in one shelf; your photos and documents edited right in the browser and saved to your account.
It all runs in a single window, on any device, with nothing to install. Your work stays yours, and you can take it with you whenever you want.
Start with a free trial — no charge until it ends.
