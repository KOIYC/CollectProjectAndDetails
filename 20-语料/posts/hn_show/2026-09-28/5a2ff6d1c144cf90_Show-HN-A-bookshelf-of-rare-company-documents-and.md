i kept collecting rare internal company docs + out-of-print business books in random folders, so i put them all on one page.top shelf has docs like intel's 1968 investor memo, apple's original marketing philosophy, bill gates' internet tidal wave memo, yahoo's peanut butter manifesto, facebook's little red book, valve's handbook, and mrbeast's onboarding doc.bottom shelf has hard-to-find books from the founders/leaders of ibm, hp, gm, fastenal and others.everything links to a free legal source. original pdf/scan when i could find one, otherwise internet archive.if you know any rare docs or books that belong here, send them my way.
