About this extension
 kosh is a modern, clean, and lightweight UI for your browser’s built-in bookmark manager.
It gives your existing bookmarks a fresh interface designed to make browsing and organizing them feel simpler and more intuitive. Kosh works with your browser’s native bookmarks, so you don’t need to migrate or manage a separate bookmark collection.
✨ Features
- Modern and clean UI
- Works with your existing browser bookmarks
- Simple bookmark browsing and organization
- Lightweight and easy to use
🔒 Permissions
Kosh uses only the browser permissions it needs to manage bookmarks, store extension settings, and export your bookmarks.
🛡 Open Source
Kosh is free and open source, licensed under GPL-3.0.
