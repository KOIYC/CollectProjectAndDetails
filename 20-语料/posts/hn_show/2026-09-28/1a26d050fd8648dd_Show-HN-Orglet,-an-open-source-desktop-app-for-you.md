## 正文

I’m building Orglet, a desktop app where you give AI workers (I call them orglets, and they are cute) their own instructions and talk to them individually or together in a group chat.You can build your own army of orglets using your existing Claude Code or Codex subscription, an API provider, or even locally hosted models.Like other agent harnesses, orglets can access a working folder you give them permission to use. They can split the work among themselves, and you can review the resulting diff before applying changes to your files. Each orglet has its own role, tools and permissions.Orglet is free under AGPLv3. Downloads are available for Windows, macOS and Linux. It is still very early and flaky, so I’d appreciate any contributions. Thank you!
