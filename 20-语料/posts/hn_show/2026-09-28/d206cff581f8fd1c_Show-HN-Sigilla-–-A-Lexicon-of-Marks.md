 A lexicon of marks
 Sigilla
 What a symbol is, where it came from, and what people have asked it to mean. The canon is a reading. Your margin stays on this device.
 Open one at random Search marks All Egyptian Hermetic Alchemy Astrology Indic Sikh Buddhist Christian Jewish Islamic Norse Hellenic Celtic East Asian Geometry Modern
 One closed line Marks that have no outside. Breath and beginning Life as something given, sounded, or grown. The quiet church Cut small, when a plain cross was a risk. Seen and shielded Eyes, hands, and stars set against harm. The septenary Seven lights, and the metals said to answer them. Principles and roots Three that a fire reveals, and four that the old physics named. World trees A cosmos you are asked to climb, not just to picture. Knots and threefold Drawings that will not come apart into one strand.
 The cabinet
 51 marks
A C D E F H I J K L M O P R S T V W Y
 A
Air Heat that does not scorch: blood, spring, thought.
Anchor Hope, carved where a cross would have been too loud.
Ankh Life held out as a gift, often to the nostrils.
 C
Caduceus Hermes’ staff: messages, trade, borders, and the road of the dead.
Chi-Rho A monogram that became a battle standard.
Cross Two lines, and for Christians the particular death they say turned.
 D
Dharmachakra A wheel set turning, with a hub that does not.
 E
Earth Weight, melancholy, and the body that remains.
Endless knot A line with no start, so nothing in it stands alone.
Ensō One brushstroke, usually left unfinished.
Eye of Horus An eye taken apart, restored, and used as a measure.
Eye of Providence A claim that you are seen. The dollar bill did not invent it.
 F
Fire Heat that rises, and the temper called choleric.
Flower of life Every circle born on the edge of another.
 H
Hamsa An open hand against the evil eye.
 I
Ichthys A fish that is also a sentence.
 ੴ
 Ik Onkar One, and that oneness is creative.
Infinity A typesetter’s sign that escaped into metaphysics.
 J
Jupiter Tin, kingship, and the god who enlarges.
 K
Khanda Two sovereignties, held together and not sheathed.
 L
Labrys A blade that faces two ways, and a cult object that became a banner.
Lingam A mark of Shiva that refuses a face.
Lotus What rises clean from the mud it grew in.
 M
Mars Iron’s mark: a blade’s metal, and later a sex.
Menorah Seven lights on one tree, and they have to be tended.
Mercury The go-between. Metal, planet, principle, and a god who travels.
Mjölnir A hammer that returns, and that hallows.
Moon What changes on a schedule, and still returns.
 O
 ॐ
 Om A sonic cosmology in one syllable.
Ouroboros The serpent that eats itself, and so has no outside.
 P
Peace sign A designed mark, with an author, a day, and a brief.
Pentagram Five lines in one stroke. The doctrines came later, and they disagree.
Phoenix A bird that burns and is the same bird after.
 R
Rod of Asclepius One serpent, one staff. The sign a hospital ought to mean.
 S
Salt What the fire cannot talk out of staying.
Saturn Weight, time, and the stage of the work you cannot skip.
Seed of life Seven circles: a center, and the ring that exhausts it.
Star and crescent A lunar blade and a star — a dynasty’s badge that went traveling.
Star of David Two triangles locked, asked to stand for a people.
Sulfur What burns, and what gives a thing its character.
Sun The center, and the metal that does not tarnish.
Svastika A bent cross that meant well-being, and was later occupied.
 T
Tree of life Ten stations of emanation, and a way back up.
Triquetra Three vesicae locked, so that parting them destroys the drawing.
Triskele Three spirals that share a center and refuse to be still.
 V
Valknut Three triangles locked, near what may be Odin, and we do not have the gloss.
Venus Copper’s mark, long before it was a gender icon.
Vesica piscis Two equal circles, each through the other’s center. A third shape appears.
 W
Water What sinks, gathers, and is felt before it is argued.
 Y
Yggdrasil The tree at the middle, which is also a gallows.
Yin and yang Two phases of one motion, each carrying the seed of the other.
