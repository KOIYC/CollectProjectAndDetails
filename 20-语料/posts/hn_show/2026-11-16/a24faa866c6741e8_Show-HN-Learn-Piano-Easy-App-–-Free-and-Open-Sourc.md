Learn Piano Easy

# Learn Piano Easy

Watch the notes fall onto the keys, then play them yourself, one song at a time.

- No account
- Works offline
- No ads

Hi, I'm your piano teacher. Shall we play?

## The notes fall, and you play

Every song looks like this. Each note falls onto its key, and you press the key as it lands.

Once it plays, press its keys to join in.

## Learn step by step, or just play

Pick the part that fits your day. You can switch at any time.

Guided course

### Learn Piano

A guided course of small steps. Each one teaches a skill, and a song puts it to use.

Any song

### Just Play Piano

Open any song, watch its notes fly towards a 3D piano, and play along.

## Four steps, again and again

Every lesson follows the same short loop, so you always know what comes next.

1. 1

### Learn a skill

A short lesson shows one new thing, such as a note, a rhythm or a hand position.
2. 2

### Practise it

Play it on your own keyboard. The piano waits for you and marks each right note.
3. 3

### Play a real song

Use the skill in a song from the library, at a level that fits you.
4. 4

### Earn badges

Each lesson earns XP, and each world you finish earns a badge.

## Every song grows with you

Each song of ours comes in four versions. Switch between them and watch Ode to Joy grow.

Beginner Easy Medium Hard

Your first days. One hand position, slow notes and a bass that waits for you.

## No piano? Start anyway

Any of these plays the notes. A real keyboard feels best, and the others let you start today.

- ### A MIDI keyboard

Plug it in by USB and allow MIDI once. From then on, the site finds it by itself.
- ### A touch screen

Tap the keys on the screen, with as many fingers as you like.
- ### Your computer keyboard

The letter and number keys play two octaves, and you can move any note to another key.

Touch a key to hear it.

## Nothing to pay, nothing to sign

The site is a gift from someone who loves the piano. It stays free.

- ### Free, with no account

Open the site and play. There is no sign-up and nothing to buy.
- ### Open source

Every line of code is public under the MIT License. Read it, change it, share it.
- ### Works offline

Once loaded, the site runs without a network. Songs you have opened play offline too.
- ### No ads, no profiles

Nothing you play leaves your device. We only count page visits, without cookies, and sell nothing.

## What people ask first

Do I need a piano?

No. A MIDI keyboard is best, but a touch screen or your computer keyboard plays every note too.

Which browsers work?

Any current browser plays. A MIDI keyboard needs Web MIDI, which Chrome, Edge and Firefox have and Safari lacks.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, and your settings stay in this browser.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Open the site once while online, and it runs offline from then on. A song plays offline once you have opened it.

Can I play my own songs?

Yes. Open any MIDI or MusicXML file in Just Play Piano. It is read on your device and never uploaded.

Which songs are in the library?

Our own arrangements of well-known tunes, each in four versions, a few pieces of our own, and classical pieces from open collections.

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT License, with no ads and no paid tier.

## Ready when you are

Pick a way in. You can switch at any time.
