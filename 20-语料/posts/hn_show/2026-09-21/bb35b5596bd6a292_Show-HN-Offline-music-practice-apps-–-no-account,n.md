The philosophy
Three rules. Every Buddha keeps them.
Whatever you play, the promises are the same.
01
Your music stays yours.
Everything lives on your device. No server, no account, no cloud. Analytics stay off unless you opt in — and even then they're anonymous. We can't see your songs, your takes, or what you're working on. We like it that way.
02
Practice isn't a game.
No streaks, no goals, no badges, no guilt. The moment practice becomes a game you can lose, you stop. Each app quietly remembers what you've sat with — that's all the tracking you need.
03
Free means free.
Every feature, in every app, forever. There's a tip jar if you'd like to say thanks — but nothing is locked behind it, and nothing ever will be.
that's it. that's the whole terms of service.
