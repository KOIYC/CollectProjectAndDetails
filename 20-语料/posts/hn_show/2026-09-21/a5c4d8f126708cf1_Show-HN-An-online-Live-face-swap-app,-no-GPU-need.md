FAQ
 Quick answers about online previews, privacy, performance, and using the app.
Is my camera data secure during live face swap?
Protecting your camera data is our highest priority. During an active live face swap session, your camera stream connects directly to the AI model provider over an encrypted connection and does not pass through any other third-party service.
Should I use the online preview or the Desktop app?
Use the online preview to test live face swap in your browser without installing anything. Use the Desktop app when you want to send the live AI output to OBS, streaming software, meeting apps, or video-call apps through a virtual camera.
Do I need a powerful GPU or local AI models?
No. The AI processing runs in the cloud, so you do not need to download AI models or own a powerful GPU. You only need a camera, a stable internet connection, and either a supported browser or LiveFaceSwap Desktop.
What kind of reference photo works best?
Use one clear, front-facing portrait with visible eyes, mouth, and facial outline. Avoid group photos, heavy blur, strong shadows, or a face covered by objects. Use only your own face or an image you have permission to use.
Does live face swap really happen in real time?
The browser or Desktop app receives and displays the AI output from the cloud in real time, with less than 500 ms of latency.
Does the browser record or save the live output?
The browser experience does not provide recording, downloading, exporting, or saving controls for the live output.
