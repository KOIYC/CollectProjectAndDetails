Questions
 The details, without the pitch.
What's the difference between AI Visibility and AI Readiness? + − Visibility is whether AI names you in its answer right now. Readiness is how well your site is built for AI to find, trust and cite you. You can be ready but not yet visible — readiness is the lever that earns visibility.
 Which AI engines does VisiScan check? + − Up to four engines: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini — with unavailable providers identified in the report.
 How is this different from SEO tools? + − SEO tools measure blue-link rankings. VisiScan measures the AI's answer itself — and the signals AI actually weighs: entity data, branded mentions and third-party citations, which matter far more to answer engines than on-page keywords.
 What's in the $49 full report that the free scan doesn't cover? + − The free scan gives you your scores and biggest problem. The full report maps every primary engine × question, traces grounding and cited sources, flags sources citing competitors but not you, compares local profile details when applicable, audits 30+ signals, and delivers a prioritized fix plan plus ready-to-publish schema and llms.txt. Brave cited-answer verification is an additional paid evidence surface and may be unavailable when Brave returns no data; it is not a fifth primary engine.
 What if the AI engines don't mention my business at all? + − That's exactly what the scan is for — it's the only way to know you're invisible. The report shows who AI named instead, which sources it cited, and what you need to fix to get named next time.
 How do you know which competitors are winning my customers? + − The scan reads your site to understand your market, generates sample buyer questions, then sees who each engine names in the answer. A mention shows what appeared in that sampled answer; it does not prove a lost customer.
 Are the schema markup and llms.txt really ready to publish? + − They are drafts generated from your confirmed business facts. Review them against your live site and implementation context before publishing. The report explains what was observed, what to change, and how to verify it.
 Is the scan really free? + − Yes. Your verdict and scores show without a purchase. Add your email to save the free evidence and its private link. The complete report with the full fix plan is a separate one-time $49 purchase.
 Is there a money-back guarantee? + − Yes. If the report does not reveal at least five useful findings, email us within seven days for a full refund.
 How does the Monitor plan work? + − For $29/month, VisiScan automatically re-runs your scan every week, tracks your scores over time, watches competitors, and adds citation monitoring for comparable source changes — with email alerts whenever something shifts.
 What kinds of businesses is VisiScan designed for? + − Local service businesses where customers ask AI engines for recommendations. Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, dentists, lawyers, accountants, roofers, landscapers, medical clinics, and more. If someone types 'best [your trade] near me' into ChatGPT, VisiScan tells you whether your name comes back.
 Does it work outside the US? + − Yes — the scan localizes automatically. It reads your site, works out where you operate, and asks the questions your local buyers ask, in your market, against your real local rivals.
 How long does it take? + − Completion time varies with provider availability. Keep your scan link to check progress. Full reports test up to 144 answers across four configured models; your report shows the actual coverage.
 I run an agency — can I use VisiScan for my clients? + − Yes. Many agency owners use VisiScan to audit clients' AI visibility, then hand them the report. You can run scans on any business URL and share the results — there's no per-seat license or client limit.
