## 正文

Claypot: Understanding AI systems through simple blocks.

# Young minds make worlds. AI is a new kind of building block.

Claypot lets curious creators combine AI with their ideas, test what happens, and reshape the project as it grows.

▶ Watch the explainer 56 seconds

## AI is sometimes astonishing. Sometimes absurd.

AI can make something remarkable, then miss something obvious. Claypot lets young creators discover both by building, testing, and trying again.

- Start with their own ideas
- Test capabilities and limits
- Decide what belongs

## The result is theirs to judge.

Observer shows what went in, what ran, what came out, and how much Clay it used. Young creators decide what to keep, change, question, or try again.

Visible inputs Read what the project sent.

Observable steps Follow what ran and in what order.

Comparable runs Run it again and test your hunch.

## Change one thing. Learn something real.

Make a prediction, run the project, inspect the difference, and explain what changed.

First run Speak like a fox

First result

> “Hello, traveler.”

One change Speak like a shy fox

Changed result

> “Um… hello, traveler.”

## Make something only they would make.

Stories, games, characters, experiments. AI can help. The idea, choices, and point of view belong to the creator.

## AI is too powerful to ignore. Too fallible to trust blindly.

Keeping children away from AI will not prepare them. Neither will handing them a chatbot. Claypot puts young creators in charge of building, testing, and learning what AI can and cannot do.

Make, don’t consume

Young creators begin with their own idea and use AI to help build it.

Test every result

They can change inputs, compare runs, and catch confident mistakes.

Stay in charge

They decide what to keep, question, change, or reject.

## Build

Snap together readable blocks for instructions, characters, information, and rules.

## Play

Run what you made, try a change, and keep shaping the result.

Follow each run and understand where the AI helped.

> Hi HN, I’m a solo founder building Claypot, an open-ended creative coding studio for ages 6–16. The learning approach is inspired by Scratch, but Claypot focuses on the concepts involved in building AI systems: inference, grounding and sources, tools, evaluation, and bounded actions.Kids combine visual blocks to build projects while the important AI-system choices remain visible and remixable. Claypot hides provider APIs and infrastructure, but not how the system is composed. I’d love feedback from educators, parents, AI builders, and anyone thinking about how children should learn to build with AI.Demo coming soon!
