## You don't mix it. You inhabit it.

Choose how you want to be, or where you want to be. The place composes itself and quietly follows your focus. No layers, no knobs, nothing to place.

Responds to you

In Focus, the room swells to mask as you work, and eases back when you stop.

Lives on its own

Voices come and go, wander, and shift. A bird sings from a new branch. The wind changes its lean.

Nothing to manage

No mixer, no placing. Pick a state or a place and stay.

The catalog is a set of finished rooms, each built from the sounds of one real place. A redwood forest, a black-sand coast, a lava forest at dawn. Pick one and step in.

## Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Free. No account, no ads, no feed.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Every sound is stored on your device, so the places keep playing with no connection. Online, places also follow their real local time and weather.

What data do you collect?

No account and nothing personal. Anonymous, session-only usage stats (no cookies, no persistent ID, no IP) help improve the product. Nothing is sold or shared.

Where do the sounds come from?

Pure, unprocessed recordings of the natural world. Rain, wind, water, birds. Every place is arranged by hand from them, and full credits ship with the app.

Does head tracking use my camera?

No camera. The motion sensor in your AirPods only, off by default.
