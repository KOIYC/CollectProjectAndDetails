When Foray isn't for you
 We'd rather tell you upfront than have you disappointed.
You need a complete business plan today
 Foray generates the strategic blueprint — market, model, competitors, risks. A formal business plan for investors or banks may need additional formatting and an accountant's stamp.
You want someone to validate your idea
 Foray stress-tests your idea, but it won't tell you it's great if the numbers don't support it. If you need reassurance, this isn't the right tool.
Your industry is highly regulated and niche
 Healthcare, legal, and financial services require domain-specific compliance knowledge. The blueprint will cover what it can, but verified regulatory data in narrow verticals is often unavailable.
