 Common Questions
All FAQ
 What is OEM in cosmetics? OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) means you supply the formula and the Korean manufacturer produces it under your brand name. You own the formula IP and retain full exclusivity — no other brand can use the same formula. It's the right model when product differentiation is the priority.
What is ODM — and how is it different from OEM? With ODM (Original Design Manufacturer), the manufacturer's R&D team develops the formula to your brief. You specify the product type, actives, and target market — they develop it, you approve and brand it. ODM is faster and lower-cost to start than OEM, and is the most common path for brands entering Korean manufacturing.
What is the difference between private label and white label? White label is a fully standardized existing product — you apply your label as-is, no changes. Private label allows minor customization: fragrance selection, color adjustments, or packaging changes, on an existing base formula. Both are faster and lower-MOQ than custom ODM formulation.
What is the minimum order quantity for Korean OEM? MOQs depend on the manufacturer tier and product type. Private label cosmetics: 1,000–2,000 units. Custom ODM cosmetics: 1,000–5,000 units. Supplements (drink ampoules, capsules): 3,000–10,000 units. Tier 1 industrial-scale Korean manufacturers typically require 5,000–10,000+ units. Most emerging brands start with mid-size Korean manufacturers at 1,000–3,000 unit MOQs.
How long does it take to launch a product from a Korean manufacturer? Private label (existing formula): 6–10 weeks from order confirmation. Custom ODM (new formula development): 16–24 weeks, including formulation, stability testing, packaging, and production. Plan for the longer estimate — the most common delays are sampling revision rounds and packaging decisions.
Why do global brands choose Korean manufacturers? Korea ranks #3 globally in cosmetics exports and sets the global benchmark for skincare formulation quality and regulatory compliance. Korean manufacturers are MFDS-registered, ISO 22716 certified, and export to the US, EU, UK, and Asia. The same factories producing leading K-beauty brands are accessible to international brands at 1,000+ unit MOQs.
