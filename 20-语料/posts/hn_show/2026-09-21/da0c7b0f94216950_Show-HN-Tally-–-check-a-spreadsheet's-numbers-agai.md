Tally by JieGou
 Client Books Report → · Connect a Google Sheet →
Tally is a verification tool for business reports and spreadsheets. It independently recomputes every figure from your source data and shows which numbers tie out — and which don't — with the exact rows as proof. Deterministic tools guarantee the same number every run — not the right number. That takes a check from outside the system that produced it; Tally is that check.

How it fits your workflow
Check. Watch. Prove.
Check Before you hit send
 Reviewed isn't reconciled. Drop a file — or connect a Google Sheet — and every figure is independently recomputed and reconciled against its own rows, marked ✓ or ✗, with the exact rows as the receipt.
Watch Then stop thinking about it
 Tally re-pulls a connected sheet daily or weekly, re-verifies every figure unattended, and tells you the moment something stops tying out — or the data changes under a number you already reported. Set up a watch →
Prove So they stop re-checking you
 Every verification becomes a shareable “✓ Verified” link. The person you send it to clicks any figure and sees the rows behind it — proof, not promises.
