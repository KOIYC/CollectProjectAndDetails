FAQ
 Things people ask before signing up.
Q 01 How fast can I be live?
 +
 Five minutes from sign-up to embed. Create a project, copy your collection link, paste the widget snippet on your site. That's it.
Q 02 Does the widget slow my page down?
 +
 No. The script is under 5kb gzipped, lazy-loads, and renders server-cached HTML. Lighthouse won't notice.
Q 03 Can I customize the look?
 +
 Inherits your font and color tokens by default. Five layout presets (grid, masonry, marquee, single, list) and a CSS escape hatch for full control.
Q 04 How does collection actually work?
 +
 You share one short link. Customers land on a clean page branded to you, leave a written or video testimonial, optionally connect their X or LinkedIn. You approve or pin from your dashboard.
Q 05 What about video testimonials?
 +
 Coming soon. The plan: record in-browser, 90 seconds max, auto-transcoded, plays inline in your wall like any other card. Not shipped yet — text and rating testimonials work today.
Q 06 Can I import testimonials I already have?
 +
 Manual entry works today — just type them in and they go through the same approval queue. Tweet URL and CSV imports are coming soon.
Q 07 Will the free tier stay free?
 +
 Yes. Anything available for free today stays free for you, forever. When paid plans launch they'll add new things on top — they won't take anything away from what you already have.
