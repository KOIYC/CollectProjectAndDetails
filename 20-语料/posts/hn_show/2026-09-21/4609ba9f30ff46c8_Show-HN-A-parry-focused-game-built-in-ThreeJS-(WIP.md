### Tuning

cut down

# 葦名

Ashina

 Tap deflect on the incoming blow — do not hold it. Parry the first cut of a flurry and the rest are carried for you. Three clean parries fill the gauge. Esc releases the mouse; F1 opens tuning.

## Controls

 Click rebind and press a key. Escape cancels. Mouse buttons are fixed. Rebinding a key that is already in use takes it from the other action, which is then shown as unbound.
