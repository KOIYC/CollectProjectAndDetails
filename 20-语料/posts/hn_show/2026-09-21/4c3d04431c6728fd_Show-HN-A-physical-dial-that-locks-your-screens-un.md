You sit down to work, but somehow the afternoon just slips away. A check here, a scroll there, and nothing gets done. It was never your fault, you just never had the right tool. Until now.

Meet MIRA Dial

Every app, blocker, and productivity device you've tried has the same weakness — the off switch is one tap away. MIRA Dial doesn't. You turn the dial to start a session, and every screen you own locks into focus at once. No menu to disable it. No bypass. The session runs until it's done.

Set it once. Then just turn the dial and work.

The Problem

You don't decide to pick up your phone. Your hand's already moving before the thought arrives, and by the time you notice, the time's just gone.

It isn't that you don't care, and it isn't that you're weak. You just never had a tool that could actually stop it. Every app lives on the same device as the distraction, so the way out is always one tap away.

Why Other Blockers fail?

Every blocker you've tried is sitting right there on the same phone you're trying to ignore. You set the rules, but you're also the one who can break them. You're the referee and the player. Of course you bend the rules in your favor.

Screen Time has an "Ignore Limit" button. Freedom app comes off in safe mode. Cold Turkey is a couple of clicks to disable. And the second you lock your phone, the distraction just slides over to your laptop. Blocking one screen was never going to be enough, because there was always a second one waiting.

Can't Quit on Impulse

MIRA Dial isn't software you can switch off the moment the urge hits. Once you press, your devices lock and the easy exit, the one your thumb takes before you've even decided, is gone. There's no "Remind Me in 15 Minutes," no menu, no quick setting to flip mid-session.

The Timer You Race

You can lose a whole afternoon to one annoying task. But tell yourself "I've got twenty minutes," and suddenly it's done in twenty minutes.

It works like running. On your own you jog, but the moment someone's next to you, you're not about to let them win. The timer is that someone. It's right there the whole session, and you just don't want it to beat you. Every break gives you a clean reset, so the next round feels just as short and doable as the first.

Automatic Intervals

Set your cycle once and MIRA Dial handles the rest. When your focus time's up, it changes to break mode and unlocks your distractions for your set minutes as a reward. Then it starts the next round on its own.

You never have to decide when to stop, remember to take a break, or summon the discipline to start again. You just keep moving, and you finish the day sharp instead of fried.

Blocks All Your Devices

Phone, laptop, tablet, browser, all quiet the moment you press. The distraction has nowhere to escape to, because the laptop's already locked, and so is the phone. For once, there's no second screen waiting.

Works across iOS, Android, Mac, Windows, and every major browser simultaneously.

How It Works

1. Set your rules once. In the app, choose what to block, apps, sites, notifications, across every device you want covered. That's the only setup you'll ever do.

2. Turn the dial. Set your focus time, your break time, and how many rounds you want to do.

3. Press to lock in. Every device goes quiet at the same time, and you can finally focus.

The cost

You lose a few hours a day to your phone or laptop. It never feels like much in the moment, just a check here, a scroll there. But it adds up to hundreds, even thousands of hours a year. For anyone whose time is worth money, tens of thousands of dollars are quietly gone.

But the money was never the real cost. It's the dinners you sat through but weren't at. The hobby still waiting. The version of you that keeps not showing up. Win back even an hour a day, and that's what you're actually buying back.

For ADHD Brain

You already know willpower advice is useless. You understand dopamine loops and attention hijacking better than most people, and you still can't get through two focused hours. That's not a knowledge problem. It's a mechanism problem.

Putting your commitment into a physical object is one of the few things that actually works for attention-diverse brains, because it takes the decision away from the moment the urge hits. MIRA Dial doesn't ask for your willpower. It just makes the easy escape disappear before the impulse can use it.

No Subscription

Freedom app costs $40 a year. Most focus apps nickel-and-dime you monthly. MIRA Dial has no subscription, no renewal, no features locked behind a paywall. Buy it once, and it's yours.

## FAQ

Why not just use an app blocker? You've probably already tried. App blockers live on the same phone you're fighting, so they're one tap to bypass, and they do nothing about the laptop sitting next to you. MIRA Dial blocks every device at once and moves the exit off the screen entirely, so quitting becomes a real decision instead of impulse. It's a different category.

What actually stops me from cheating? Let's be honest. Because you own your devices, a truly determined person could dig through settings to force their way out. No tool on earth can stop that. But that's not the point. MIRA Dial removes the easy exit, the one your thumb takes before you've even decided. To quit, you have to stop and deliberately dig your way out through system settings. That deliberate moment is all it takes for your brain to go "actually, never mind."

Won't the novelty wear off after a few weeks? MIRA Dial isn't built on novelty. It solves a problem that shows up every workday. You need to focus, and your screens won't let you. The physical ritual of turning the dial builds a real habit loop, the timer gives you something to race every session, and automatic intervals mean you never have to think about using it "correctly." It earns a permanent spot on your desk the same way a good keyboard does, not because it's exciting, but because it works every time you use it.

How is this different from NFC blockers like Brick? Brick only blocks your phone. MIRA Dial blocks every device at once. More importantly, Brick is something you avoid. MIRA Dial is something you work with. You turn it and race the clock. That makes you feel like someone's sitting beside you, keeping you honest and on track.

What if I have a real emergency? You get a small number of emergency exits each month. Enough for genuine emergencies, not enough to lean on every time you're bored.

Why do the work-and-break intervals matter? Short focused sprints get your best effort. Real breaks give you actual recovery instead of guilty half-working. Most people get more done in three focused 25-minute sessions than in four hours of trying to concentrate. And knowing a break is coming makes the focus easier to hold and not cheat.

Does it work with all my devices? Yes. MIRA Dial blocks simultaneously across iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows, plus every major browser.

Is there a subscription? No. You pay once and it's yours. No monthly fee, no renewals, no features locked behind a paywall.

## Risks and challenges

We took our time getting MIRA Dial to this point, and we want to be straight with you about exactly where it stands. What we have today is a working prototype we've refined over more than a year and tested on our own desks every single day. It does everything we promise on this page. What we don't have yet, and what this campaign funds, is the final step: bringing in professional engineering and manufacturing partners to take the design from "works beautifully on our desk" to "ready for clean, repeatable mass production at a premium standard."

Manufacturing. We could have rushed this. We chose not to. Rather than push a half-finished design into production and hope for the best, we want experts to test every component and refine the build, so the unit that arrives on your desk is the premium product we've promised, not a rough approximation of it. That process can surface things we'll want to fix properly rather than rush past, and timelines can shift because of it. If that happens, you'll hear it from us directly, with specifics. Quality is our first priority, on-time delivery our second.

Fulfillment. Some things can't be planned for. A supplier misses a date, a shipment gets held at customs, a package goes missing. We'll be working with an experienced logistics team to get MIRA Dial to you, and we'll keep open, honest communication the whole way, so you always know where your order is and when it's coming.

We'd rather be a little late than ship something that ends up in a drawer. You backed two real people, and you'll hear from two real people at every step, including the hard ones.
