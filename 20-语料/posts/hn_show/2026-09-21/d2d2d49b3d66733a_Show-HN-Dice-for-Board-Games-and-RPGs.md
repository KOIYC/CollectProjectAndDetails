What is Online Dice Roller?
This free tool lets you roll virtual dice anytime, anywhere in your browser. It's perfect when you don't have real dice handy or want to save time. Usually dice are used in many board games or Monopoly or role play games like Dungeons & Dragons. The real dice come in many shapes. The most common are 6-sided cubes. Our tool lets you roll any type.
How to Use This Online Dice?
Pick number of dice you want.
Choose the number of sides on each. We support 1-200.
Click roll the dice.
See your total score.
Frequently asked questions
Is it really random?
 Yes. Every roll uses the random generator of your browser, and nothing is sent to a server.
How many dice can I roll?
 Up to 100 dice in one throw, and from 2 to 1000 sides on each of them.
What is dice notation?
 It is the short way rulebooks write a roll. 2d6+3 means two 6-sided dice plus three. 4d6kh3 means roll four and keep the best three. You can type it in the notation box.
Do you save my rolls?
 Only in your browser. Clear the browser data and it is gone, or just press clear history.
