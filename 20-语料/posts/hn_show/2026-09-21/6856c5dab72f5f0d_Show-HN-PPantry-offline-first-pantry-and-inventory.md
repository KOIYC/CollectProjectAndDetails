## 正文

Works fully offline
 Know exactly how many days your supplies will last .
 Track your emergency supplies, bug-out bags, and everyday pantry in one place. Get rotation alerts before food, meds, or batteries expire — and see at a glance how long your household could hold out.
 Open PPantry No account needed · Free forever
Know how long you’ll last
 See food and water duration in days based on your household. Start-from-templates for bug-out, vehicle, and first-aid kits.
Never miss an expiration
 Configurable reminders for food, meds, and batteries. A rotation queue shows what to use next before it expires.
Scan to add, scan to use
 Barcode scanning auto-fills name, brand, and nutrition from Open Food Facts. Scan-out decrements when you use something.
Local-first & private
 Your inventory lives on your device. No account needed. Cloud sync is optional.
Free forever. No account. No ads.
 PPantry will always be free — no premium tier, no upsells, no tracking. Your data stays on your device unless you turn on sync. If the app is useful to you and you'd like to help shape the roadmap, you can back the project on Patreon.
 Support on Patreon
