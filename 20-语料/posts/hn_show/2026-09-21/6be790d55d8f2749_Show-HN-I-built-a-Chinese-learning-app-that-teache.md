Frequently Asked Questions
What is DouDou Chinese? DouDou Chinese (句式) is a mobile app that teaches Mandarin Chinese through the 100 most essential sentence patterns. Instead of drilling isolated vocabulary, it has you actively build sentences with each pattern and gives instant AI feedback, then schedules review with the FSRS spaced repetition algorithm so you retain what you learn.
 How is DouDou Chinese different from other Chinese learning apps? Most apps teach vocabulary in isolation. DouDou Chinese teaches sentence patterns — the reusable grammatical structures that natural conversation is built on — and has you actively produce Chinese sentences with those patterns rather than just recognizing flashcards. Beyond the built-in patterns, you can also generate custom patterns for anything you come across elsewhere, complete with AI-written structure notes and examples.
 Is DouDou Chinese free? Yes. The Free plan includes spaced repetition flashcards, spaced repetition audio loops, sync across devices, and basic settings. A 7-day free trial of Premium is available, which adds AI tutor chat, unlimited card access, background audio, and more. Premium is $5/month after the trial.
 What is FSRS spaced repetition? FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) is the algorithm DouDou Chinese uses to schedule flashcard and audio reviews. It times each review to happen just before you're likely to forget the sentence pattern, so you spend less time reviewing material you already know.
 Do I need to sign up to try DouDou Chinese? No signup is required to start. You can begin practicing sentence patterns instantly after downloading the app.
 Is DouDou Chinese available on iOS and Android? Yes, DouDou Chinese is available on the Apple App Store for iOS and on Google Play for Android.
