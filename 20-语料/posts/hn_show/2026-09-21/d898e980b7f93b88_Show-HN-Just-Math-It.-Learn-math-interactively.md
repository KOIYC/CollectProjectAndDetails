 Absorbing State probability
 A state i where P(i→i) = 1 — once entered, the chain never leaves.
 Absorption Probability probability
 The probability that the chain eventually enters a particular absorbing state.
 Aperiodic Chain probability
 A Markov chain where the GCD of return times to any state is 1 (no cycling behavior).
 Area-Based Probability probability
 $P(A) = \text{area of } A \,/\, \text{area of } \Omega$
 Axiom probability
 A fundamental rule accepted without proof
 Balance Equations probability
 $\pi_j = \sum_i \pi_i \cdot P(i \to j)$ — the equations that define the steady-state distribution.
 Bayes' Rule probability
 $P(B|A) = \frac{P(A|B) \cdot P(B)}{P(A)}$ — updates the probability of B after observing A.
 Bernoulli probability
 A random variable with exactly two outcomes: success (1) with probability p, and failure (0) with probability 1−p.
 Binomial probability
 The number of successes in n independent Bernoulli trials, each with success probability p.
 CDF probability
 Cumulative Distribution Function — $F(x) = P(X \leq x)$, always non-decreasing with $F(-\infty)=0$ and $F(\infty)=1$.
 Central Limit Theorem probability
 The sum (or mean) of many independent random variables is approximately normal, regardless of their individual distributions.
 Chain Rule probability
 Extends the multiplication rule: $P(A \cap B \cap C) = P(A) \times P(B|A) \times P(C|A \cap B)$, and so on for more events.
