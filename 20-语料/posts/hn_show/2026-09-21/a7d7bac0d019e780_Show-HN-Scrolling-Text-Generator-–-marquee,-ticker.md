 FAQ
 Common questions about exports, transparency, embeds, performance, and workflow.
 Is Scrolling Text Generator free to use? Yes. The core generator and exports are free to use in your browser. An account is only required for Pro features like cloud saves and billing.
 Do you upload or store my text or exports? No. Your text, preview, and exports are generated locally in your browser. We do not upload your content for processing.
 Can I change direction mid-project? Yes. Switch between right-to-left, left-to-right, bottom-to-top, or top-to-bottom at any time. The preview instantly reflects the new travel path.
 Does the preview match the export? What you see is what you export. Fonts, colors, spacing, and motion match GIF/WebM/Embed outputs, so you can trust the timing.
 What formats can I export? You can export an animated GIF or a WebM video, and you can also generate a web embed snippet to paste into a site or an OBS browser source.
 Is transparency supported? Yes. Use transparent backgrounds for overlays and embeds. For best transparency, export WebM; GIF support depends on the encoder and may look dithered.
 How do I use the embed code in OBS? Copy the embed snippet, add a Browser Source in OBS, paste the code (or host it on a page), and match the width/height. For transparency, use a transparent background and enable transparency in OBS where available.
 How do segments help with credits? Enable segments to split content into blocks, reorder them, or edit independently. Great for long credit rolls and multi-part announcements.
 Do I need to install anything? No installs required. Everything runs in your browser; exports are generated locally without uploading your text or assets.
 How can I reduce GIF/WebM file size? Use smaller dimensions, lower FPS for GIFs, fewer loops, and shorter durations. Solid backgrounds often compress better than transparency, and WebM is usually smaller than GIF for longer animations.
 Why is export slow or laggy on my device? Exports can be heavy at large resolutions or long durations (many frames). Try a smaller size, fewer loops, or shorter text. Closing other tabs can also help.
 Will the font look the same everywhere? Exports bake the pixels, so GIF/WebM will look consistent. For embed code, system fonts can differ across devices; web fonts depend on availability and loading.
 Can I export MP4? Yes. MP4 export uses a hybrid pipeline: WebCodecs + MP4 mux first, then ffmpeg.wasm if needed. If both engines fail, export stops with an explicit error.
 What does Pro include? Premium template access is sold by pack (one-time) or covered by the active pass. The pass also includes cloud save/load. Free generation and single export stay available.
 Do I need to sign in before upgrading? Yes. Checkout uses Google sign-in first so subscription ownership and Pro access can be linked to your account.
 What is the live ticker tool? It turns a public JSON or CSV feed into one live scrolling URL that keeps refreshing after you publish, so you do not need to re-export the ticker every time the source data changes.
