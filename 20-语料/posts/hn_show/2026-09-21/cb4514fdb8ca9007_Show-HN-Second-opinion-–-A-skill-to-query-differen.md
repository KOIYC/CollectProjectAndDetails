## 正文

Make this skill to help me cross examine suggestions made by claude to other Claude or opencode supported models mid session. It compiles relevant context, consult a second opinion for me and support following up with the second opinion. A pattern that I find quite useful.Currently it supports claude to claude, claude to opencode and opencode to claude invocation. These are the two harnesses I use.
