Foreword
 Storage is not a collection.
Your phone dumps every selfie, milestone, portrait, experience, receipt, screenshot ... into one big bucket. It will not tell you which ones are good, and it will never make the other 9,999 worth opening.
 Your phone needs a curator. Curation is why people love museums: interpret a collection, organize it, and make it worth visiting. Photos gives you a vault and a search box. Piqt curates and gives you the collection.

 Plate No. 01 · Rank
 Your best shot is somewhere around photo 4,732.
 Piqt scores every photo on five signals — Apple's on-device aesthetics, technical quality, a NIMA aesthetic model trained on 255,000 human-rated images, number and clarity of faces, and how rarely you shoot in that place — then reduces the blurred and the blown-out. The keepers float up. One pass through them and they're favorited in Photos for real, not in a private list Piqt keeps to itself.

05
 Plate No. 05 · Clear
 Piqt cannot delete your photos.
 Not "won't." Cannot. When you clear a photo it moves to a "Piqt: To Delete" album and stays in your library until you finish the job in Apple's Photos, behind Apple's own confirmation. Change your mind on the way? Recover the whole album in a tap.
 Not Your Best · Review Deletion Album
Certificate of safekeeping
 The Photos delete API isn't in our code.
 Apple's photo-deletion call appears nowhere in Piqt's source, and a test in our build fails if anyone ever adds it. The safeguard isn't a promise on a website — it's a condition for the app building successfully.
06
 Plate No. 06 · Upkeep
 One scan, then it keeps up.
 Cleanup apps are a weekend project you do once and never repeat. Piqt watches the library rather than a snapshot of it, so the second visit takes a minute.
First scan Piqt reads your library in resumable batches on the device's Neural Engine. Always on your device, not in a datacenter somewhere. Close the app and it picks up where it stopped. Tested on libraries to 100,000 photos.
After New photos are scored as they arrive. Edited photos re-score. Nothing gets re-analyzed for the sake of it. When Piqt sees you took a great picture, it will let you know.
Forever Every decision you make sticks. The keeper you swapped, the photo you saved, the one you favorited. Piqt only revisits them when you want to.
