Frequently asked questions
 Do I need a subscription or are there hidden fees? No subscription is required. You buy one-time credit packs only, and there are no hidden recurring charges.
 How do credits work? You spend 1 credit when you download a new SVG result. Re-downloading the same saved SVG is free. If you upload and export again, it counts as a new result and uses 1 credit.
 Can I preview the result before paying? Yes. You can upload and preview the vectorized result before spending a credit. A credit is charged only when you download a new SVG.
 Are the SVGs editable and ready for professional tools? Yes. Generated SVGs are editable and ready for Figma, Adobe Illustrator, CorelDRAW, and other vector tools. We also optimize colors for a cleaner editing workflow.
 Can I use generated SVGs commercially? Yes. As long as you have rights to the original uploaded image, you can use generated SVGs for commercial and personal projects.
 What image formats are supported? You can upload JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP files. The output format is SVG.
 Do you support transparent PNG files? Yes. Transparent PNG uploads are supported, and transparency is preserved in the conversion flow. For best edge quality, use cleaner source images with clear boundaries.
 Are my files and data safe? Yes. Uploaded files are processed securely and are not used to train public AI models.
 What is your refund policy? Because you can preview results before downloading, credit purchases are generally final. If you hit a verified billing or technical issue, contact contact@vectorify.co and we will review your case.
