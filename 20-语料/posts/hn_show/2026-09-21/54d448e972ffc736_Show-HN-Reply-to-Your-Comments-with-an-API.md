Frequently asked questions
Getting Started
Product Basics
Do you use official APIs for each platform?
Yes. Mallary uses the official APIs from each social platform. We do not scrape social sites or ask for your social media password. Mallary is a Meta Verified Tech Provider and a registered LLC in Lexington, Kentucky, USA.
Do I need to create developer apps for each platform?
No. Mallary manages the platform apps and the main setup for you. You only need to connect your social accounts and approve the access each platform asks for. Your Mallary plan and each platform's own limits still apply.
Which social platforms does Mallary support?
Mallary supports X, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit, Threads, and Bluesky. See our Supported Platforms page for the post types and options available on each one.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You can post from the Mallary dashboard, ChatGPT , Claude , n8n, Make, and other tools. Developers can also use the API , MCP , or CLI.
Does the Mallary name appear on my social media posts?
No. Posts published through Mallary do not show the Mallary name. Mallary is not sold as a full white-label service, but your audience will not see Mallary added to your posts.
What happens when a social platform changes its API or rules?
Mallary watches for platform changes and updates its connections when needed. A platform outage, new rule, or lost permission can still stop a post. If you need to act, Mallary will show a clear message.
Can I move from another social media tool to Mallary?
Yes. Connect your social accounts, then make new posts and schedules in Mallary. Posts that are already on your social accounts will stay there. Mallary does not import an old tool's post history or scheduled queue.
Can I post from my phone?
Yes. The Mallary dashboard works on phones, tablets, and computers. You can also use supported AI and automation tools from your phone.
