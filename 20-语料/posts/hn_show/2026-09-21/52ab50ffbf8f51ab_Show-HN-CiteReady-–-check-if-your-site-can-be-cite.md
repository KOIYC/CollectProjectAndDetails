What CiteReady checks
🤖 AI Crawler Access
 Are GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot & co. allowed in your robots.txt? Do meta tags block snippets? Blocked bot = invisible in AI answers.
🧩 Structured Data
 JSON-LD schema tells AI engines who you are and what your content is. FAQ & HowTo markup gets cited disproportionately often.
📝 Content Citability
 AI answers quote passage-sized chunks, not whole pages. We check heading structure, paragraph length, Q&A formatting, freshness signals and overall content depth — the traits that decide whether your text is actually quotable by an assistant.
⚙️ Technical Foundation
 The fundamentals AI crawlers still depend on: HTTPS, a clear title and meta description, canonical tags, Open Graph, an XML sitemap and the emerging llms.txt standard for machine-readable AI discovery.
Why AI search readiness matters now
Search is splitting in two. Alongside Google's familiar list of blue links, assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews now answer questions directly — often without the user ever clicking through to a website. When an assistant writes that answer, it draws its facts from a small set of sources it can crawl, parse and trust. If your site is not one of them, you are absent at the exact moment a potential customer is deciding.
These engines do not rank pages the way a classic search index does. They reward content they can extract cleanly: explicit crawler permission for GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, structured data that states who you are, passage-sized paragraphs that each answer a single question, and machine-readable freshness dates. A page that ranks first in traditional search can still be effectively unreadable to an AI assistant — different signals decide different winners.
CiteReady turns those signals into one 0–100 score with a concrete, prioritised fix for every gap it finds. The audit is fully deterministic — no language model and no randomness involved — so the same page always returns the same result, and you can re-run it after each change to confirm the fix actually landed. Free audits need no signup and finish in about ten seconds.
The signals also compound. Fixing crawler access first lets AI engines reach the pages whose structured data and citable passages you then improve, and machine-readable dates tell them the result is current rather than stale. That is why a single score across all four areas is more useful than checking any one of them in isolation — the weakest link caps how citable the whole page really is.
