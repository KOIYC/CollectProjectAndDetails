About This Demo
This is a free demo of Open Land , a cozy building and restoration game about transforming damaged landscapes into thriving ecosystems with the help of friendly eco-technology.
What's in the Demo? The demo gives you a taste of the full restoration loop. Start in a polluted landscape, clean up trash and debris, plant the first seeds of life, and watch nature begin to respond. You'll get access to the early eco-bot tools, unlock your first set of items, and see grass, plants, and the first hints of wildlife return to a world you've helped heal.
Place plants, structures, and restoration tools to shape the land. As conditions improve, nature responds. Grass spreads across the terrain, trees take root, and the ecosystem begins to sustain itself.
As the land recovers, animals start to return. Even in the demo, you'll begin to see how different species respond to the environment you create.
Clean, recycle, and restore to earn eco points and unlock new buildings and technologies: each one opening up new ways to reshape the land.
