FAQ
 Things people ask before signing up.
Q 01 How do replies actually reach me?
 +
 Through Telegram. You connect your Telegram once, and from then on every visitor message is forwarded to you there. You reply in Telegram like a normal chat, and your answer appears in the visitor's chat on your site instantly.
Q 02 Do I need to log into a dashboard?
 +
 No. That's the whole point. There's no separate inbox or app to keep open — you live in the Telegram you already check all day. A small web dashboard exists for configuration, but you don't need it open to chat.
Q 03 How fast can I be live?
 +
 About 30 seconds. Add one  tag to your site, connect Telegram, and the chat bubble is live on every page. No build step, no backend.
Q 04 Does the chat widget slow my page down?
 +
 No. The script is under ~5kb, loads after your page, and isn't a clunky iframe. It won't block first paint or drag down your Core Web Vitals.
Q 05 Does it work with Webflow, Next.js, or plain HTML?
 +
 If your site renders HTML, Eloqra works. Paste the snippet into Next.js, Webflow, WordPress, Astro, Framer, or a plain HTML file. No framework lock-in.
Q 06 Is this a cheaper Intercom alternative?
 +
 It's a lighter one. Eloqra does one thing well — live chat that routes to your phone via Telegram — without per-seat pricing, bots you have to train, or a heavy help-desk suite. If you just want to talk to your visitors, it's a fraction of the cost.
Q 07 Can I capture a name and email?
 +
 Yes. Turn on the optional pre-chat form to collect a name and email before the chat starts. Every message also arrives with the page, device, and country attached, so you always have context.
Q 08 Is it really free?
 +
 Free while we're in early access — no credit card to start. When paid plans arrive they'll add things on top; they won't take away what you're already using.
From the founder
 Hi, I’m Sev.
I built Eloqra because I wanted to talk to people on my sites without signing into yet another dashboard. I already live in Telegram all day — so why not let visitor messages just come to me there?
 That’s the whole idea: one tiny snippet on your site, every message in your Telegram, and you reply from your phone. No heavy help desk, no per-seat pricing, no tab you have to babysit.
