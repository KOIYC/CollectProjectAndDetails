# Ztab: The last tab manager you’ll need.

4.0

Overview

Manage tabs across Chrome windows, keep pinned tabs in sync, and move faster with keyboard shortcuts.

Ztab makes working across Chrome windows simpler: 1. Manage tabs in one place. View, switch to, move, and close tabs from a live side panel, or merge another window into your current one. 2. Keep pinned tabs in sync. Pin your favorite sites once and keep them ready across your regular Chrome windows. 3. Move faster with your keyboard. Open the panel with a customizable shortcut, navigate with the arrow keys, and press Enter to switch tabs. Hope you enjoy Ztab!
