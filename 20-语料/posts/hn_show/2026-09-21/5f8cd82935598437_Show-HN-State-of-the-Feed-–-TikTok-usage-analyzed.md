 State of the Feed
 "Wrapped for TikTok" gives people a personal recap of their time on TikTok. At the end, users can choose to donate their scrolling data to science. 16,981 people shared their data, and this report is what we learned from it.
 Each of them donated their complete, anonymized TikTok data export to our research program: 568 million logged events from 83 countries, some reaching back to 2015 .

09 · Methodology
 How to read this
 Everything above is computed from voluntarily donated, anonymized TikTok data exports. Here is exactly where the numbers come from — and where their limits are.
Watch-history coverage by month
Panelists contributing watch history per month. The two donation waves are clearly visible; statistics lean on the densest regions.
 Who is in the panel
 16,981 people who used Wrapped for TikTok and explicitly donated their anonymized TikTok data export for research. The panel skews toward our largest audiences (BR, US, SG) and toward people curious about their own screen time — it is not a representative sample of TikTok.
Watch time is inferred
 TikTok exports record when each video started, not how long it played. We measure the gap to the next video start and treat gaps up to five minutes as watch time; longer gaps end a session. True watch time can differ when a video is left running.
Two donation waves
 Most data arrived in two waves: December 2024 and March–July 2026. Any comparison across the waves compares different people, and every export only reaches as far back as TikTok's own retention for each data category. Where that matters, the report says so on the chart.
Local time is inferred
 Exports carry UTC timestamps. Each panelist's timezone is estimated from all data in their dataset.
Only aggregates are published
 Every number on this page is an aggregate over thousands of people. No individual viewing histories, videos, creators or sounds are identified, and monthly statistics require at least 50 contributing panelists.
The numbers
 568,385,037 events, of which 464,272,908 are video views, spanning 2015-06-25 to 2026-07-16 . Dataset release e317db0d , report generated 2026-07-30 .
