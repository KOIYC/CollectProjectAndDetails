## AI-Powered Investigation Network Topology

Reloadium Investigations is a research intelligence tool that turns any query into a structured matrix of entities, events, and their verified relationships — in seconds. Powered by Gemini AI and Google Search grounding, it gives you a bird's-eye view of any topic's key players and chronological events.

Whether you're investigating a public company, a news event, or a competitive landscape, the platform maps confidence scores and source citations so you always know how reliable each connection is.

- Entity–Event Matrix — Automatically identifies key entities (people, organizations) and events, then maps their relationships in a ranked confidence matrix.
- Google Search Grounding — Uses Google Search to ground results in real web sources, reducing hallucinations and surfacing verified citations for each relationship.
- Participation Score Ranking — Entities are ranked by their total participation score across all events, highlighting the most central actors in any investigation.
- Executive Summary — Generate a professional 3–4 sentence executive summary of your findings with a single click, synthesized from the full research context.
- Markdown Export — Export any investigation as a structured Markdown report with entity tables, event timelines, and source citations.
- Investigation History — All past investigations are saved locally in your browser so you can revisit and reload any previous analysis instantly.
- Send to Podcasts — Export any investigation report directly to Reloadium Podcasts — the generated Markdown is handed off automatically and used as the episode source.
