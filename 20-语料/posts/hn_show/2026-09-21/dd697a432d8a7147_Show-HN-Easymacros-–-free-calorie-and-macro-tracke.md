 Export everything, any time
What you actually get
 🔍 Food search that does the maths
 Three million packaged products from Open Food Facts, plus common foods from USDA. Pick “1 breast” or “1 tbsp” — no scales needed.
🧮 A calorie target you can check
 Mifflin–St Jeor, an activity factor, then your goal rate. Every step shown, so you can disagree with it.
🥑 Macros split properly
 Protein first by bodyweight, fat above its floor, carbs take the rest. The order most calculators get backwards.
🏋️ Training and weight in one place
 Minutes stacked by type, weigh-ins tracking your goal, and your latest weight feeding the calculator.
Questions
 Is it really free? + Yes. No tier, no trial, no card. No ads either, and your log is never sold or shared.
 Where do the food numbers come from? + USDA FoodData Central for common foods, Open Food Facts for packaged ones. The latter is crowd-sourced — treat a branded entry as a solid guess, not a lab result.
 Can I get my data out? + Any time, as JSON, from Goals → Your data. Import it back just as easily.
 Do I have to weigh my food? + No. Most foods carry portions — one breast, one slice, one tbsp. Grams when you care, servings when you don’t.
 Is the calorie target accurate? + It’s a well-validated starting point, not a measurement of you. Two people with identical stats can differ by hundreds of calories. Watch the scale for a few weeks, then adjust.
