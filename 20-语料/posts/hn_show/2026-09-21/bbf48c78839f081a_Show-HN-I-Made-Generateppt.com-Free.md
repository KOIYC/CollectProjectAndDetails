FAQ
Frequently Asked
Questions
What can i generate a deck from?
Topic, pasted text, URL, PDF, DOCX, TXT, or an outline. For YouTube, copy the transcript and paste it in Video mode.
Can i edit slides after generation?
Yes. Edit text directly, swap images, add slides, reorder slides, change themes, and refine with AI Director.
Who is GeneratePPT for?
GeneratePPT is for people who need to ship clear slide decks fast: executives, founders, teachers, students, consultants, and sales teams. It is built for practical work and deadlines.
Who is GeneratePPT not for?
If you want endless customization, design-perfect micro-adjustments, or a tool that tries to cover every possible workflow, GeneratePPT will feel too focused.
What does AI Director do?
AI Director edits the current slide based on your instruction. It can rewrite text, simplify, expand, translate, and also make layout-level changes like switching to two columns or making a section more visual.
How is my presentation data handled?
GeneratePPT is built with a private-by-default workflow. If you are not using AI features, your presentation content stays in your browser storage on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. That makes it a strong fit for decks containing sensitive company information. If you choose to use AI features, only the content needed for that request is sent to the provider handling it.
