# Ms. Penrose in the Library

A downloadable ebook reader for Windows and Android

Ms. Penrose in the Library is an e-book reader because I was looking for a custom e-book reader that would allow me to:

- build a library and fill it up visually (the bookshelves get populated as you add books) - journal in your books. Whenever I read books, I have very different experiences with them. It is a combination of when you read a book, what state of mind you're in etc. and so the ability to take notes, and make a journal per book you read - all your notes are stored in plain text and are exportable at any time, any where - use RSVP to "get into" a book without any distractions. RSVP is an amazing method for actually reading a book and being able to concentrate on it, if you keep the WPM reasonable - download a 64MB model in the app to enable full offline, local, text to speech option if you want your books read out aloud to you I used LimeZu's amazing assets for the pixel art!
