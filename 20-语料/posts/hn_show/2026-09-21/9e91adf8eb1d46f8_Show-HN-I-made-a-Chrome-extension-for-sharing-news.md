## 正文

If you are a writer or someone who likes to share news, this could help make sharing articles to your social media following much easier.Just highlight a quote, right click → Create Image and it generates a clean vertical card with the quote, the publication, the author, and the article title. The image can be either a 1x1 square or 9x16 vertical which is perfect for social media stories.
