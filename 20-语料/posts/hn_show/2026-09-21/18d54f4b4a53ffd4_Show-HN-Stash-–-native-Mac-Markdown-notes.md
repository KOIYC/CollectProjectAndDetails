Staunch Apps
Stash
A place for your notes
What is Stash?
Stash is a powerful note-taking application designed for anyone who values their ideas.
 Whether you're drafting documentation, journaling your thoughts, or organizing research,
 Stash provides a distraction-free environment that lets you focus on your content.
Key Features
Local First
Your notes live in a regular folder on your computer. No proprietary formats, no
 lock-in, just plain files you can access with any text editor.
Git Sync
Sync your notes across devices using any Git backend—GitHub, GitLab, your own server,
 or even a USB drive.
Native UI
Built with 100% native macOS interface elements. Stash feels like it belongs on your
 Mac, with familiar keyboard shortcuts and behaviors.
Multiple Formats
Supports plain text, Markdown, CSV, and MDSV (Markdown table) files. Use the right
 format for the right job.
Fast Search
Instantly search across all your notes with full-text search. Find that idea you had
 months ago in milliseconds.
One Time Purchase
$12 USD, no subscription necessary, includes all updates for version 1.
Privacy First
Your notes are your business. Stash doesn't upload anything to our servers. With
 local-first storage and optional Git sync, you maintain complete control over your data.
System Requirements
macOS 26.0 (Tahoe) or later
Apple Silicon Mac
