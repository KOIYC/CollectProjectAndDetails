## Questions, answered

How does Repolog scan my website?+

We launch a real headless browser that loads your page like a visitor would. Then we run automated checks across SEO, performance, accessibility, security, and AI visibility to give you one clear report.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Repolog works entirely from the outside - like a search engine crawler. To keep scans scoped to sites you control, we ask you to verify ownership once via a DNS TXT record. No code snippets or installs.

What is an AI Readiness score?+

It measures how easy your site is for AI tools to discover, understand, and cite. We look at the key technical and content signals that improve AI visibility.

How often should I run scans?+

After every major deployment or content update. For active sites, weekly scans catch regressions in SEO, performance, accessibility, or security before they impact traffic.

Is the security scan safe?+

Completely safe. We only make standard HTTP GET requests - the same as any browser or search engine. We never modify data, submit forms, or attempt authentication.

How is this different from PageSpeed Insights?+

PageSpeed focuses on performance. Repolog covers performance plus SEO, accessibility, security, and AI readiness in one scan, with prioritized fixes your team can act on.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes - every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. $0 is due today, you can cancel anytime, and you get the full product from day one: all five scores, checklists, and reports.
