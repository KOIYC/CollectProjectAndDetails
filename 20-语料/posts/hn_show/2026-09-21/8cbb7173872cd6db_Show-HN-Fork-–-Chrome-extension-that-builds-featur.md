We’re building Fork. Our first release is a free Chrome extension, allowing you to build features on top of Gmail and Google Calendar.50 second demo: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JQ292bncO_cChrome extension (free + no login): https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fork-for-the-web/mf...We keep limited trace activity of coding sessions for 30 days to troubleshoot and improve our offering.
