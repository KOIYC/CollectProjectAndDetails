 FAQ

What is an AI context card?
 A short, structured block of context you want an AI to have: a Profile, what’s happening Now, Goals, Guidance, Memory, a Timeline and Skills. You write it once in CardGo and attach it to a conversation when it’s relevant — nothing is sent until you do.
Which AI chats does CardGo work with?
 ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok, through the Chrome extension in desktop Chromium browsers such as Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. Mobile browsers cannot run extensions.
Does CardGo read my AI conversations?
 No. It never reads your chat history or the AI’s replies. The only text the extension looks at is the message box and your own unsent draft, so it can suggest a card — and nothing is attached until you choose it.
Is CardGo free?
 Yes. CardGo is free to start, with no credit card. The Free plan includes up to 7 end-to-end encrypted cards that sync across your devices.
