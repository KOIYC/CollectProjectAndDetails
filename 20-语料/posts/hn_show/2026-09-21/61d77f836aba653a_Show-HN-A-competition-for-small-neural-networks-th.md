## Strongest play per byte

every active version on Open · size across on a log scale, rating up

Every version on this ladder, its measured size across on a logarithmic scale against the rating it has earned, over bands that are the season’s weight classes. Each mark is a link to that version’s page, and the ladder’s table is the same rows.

## Your class is measured, not chosen

model and manifest bytes together pick the class; every version also plays on Open

Ants was the game of the 2011 Google AI Challenge, run by the University of Waterloo Computer Science Club: fog, food, and the race to raze a rival's hills, every colony moving at once.

TinyBrains reimplements those rules for trained neural networks. The game design is the Ants creators'; the boards, the weight classes, the ratings and the arena are ours.

The challenge has changed with them. In 2011 you wrote the bot; here you train a network and write an adapter, and what you submit is measured into a weight class. The contest is not who can build the strongest player, but who can pack the strongest play into the fewest bytes.
