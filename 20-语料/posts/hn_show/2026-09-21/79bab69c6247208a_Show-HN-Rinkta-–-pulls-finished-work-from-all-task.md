FAQ
 Let's talk details
What is Rinkta?
 ◀ Rinkta is an AI that turns completed work into invoices. It plugs into your work tools (Asana, GitHub, Linear, ClickUp, Slack, Basecamp), captures tasks as they get done, and estimates the time and price automatically. Everything appears in your dashboard without you lifting a finger.
 When you're ready to bill, select the entries you want to include, click generate, and the invoice comes out with the right client rates, currencies, and tax details already applied. No time tracking, no end-of-month reconstruction.
Does Rinkta replace my project management tool?
 ◀ No. Rinkta sits underneath whatever you already use — Asana, ClickUp, GitHub, Linear, Basecamp, Notion, Trello — and captures completed tasks in the background. Your team keeps their workflow exactly the same; Rinkta just handles the business layer (capturing billable work, estimating hours, drafting invoices) that PM tools were never built for.
How does the time estimation work — and what if it's wrong?
 ◀ Rinkta analyzes task descriptions, learns from your team's work patterns, and uses industry benchmarks to estimate hours. The more you use it, the smarter it gets about your specific workflow.
 You can review and adjust every entry before invoicing — you're always in control of what gets billed.
Do I need to train my team on new software?
 ◀ No team training required. Rinkta connects to your project tools through OAuth and runs in the background — your designers stay in Asana, developers stay in GitHub or Linear, ops stays in ClickUp. The only person who needs to learn the Rinkta UI is whoever does the monthly invoicing, and that takes about ten minutes the first time.
How long does setup take?
 ◀ Most users are up and running in an afternoon. You connect the tools you already use, set your client rates once, and add your clients — the fiddly part is just gathering your rates and tax details, and you probably have those handy.
 After that Rinkta quietly pulls in completed work and estimates the hours, so there's nothing to track by hand. No multi-week onboarding and no "implementation specialist" calls — there's no new system to learn, just the tools you already work in, now talking to each other.
What counts as an operation?
 ◀ An operation is a single unit of tracked work: a billable entry captured from a completed task, an invoice sent, an AI or email analysis run, an MCP agent call, or an integration message read. Plans include a monthly pool — 2,000 on Team, 5,000 on Studio, 20,000 on Agency.
 Everything else is unlimited: projects, clients, tasks, team members, and the integrations themselves.
Is my data secure?
 ◀ Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Primary databases run in EU data centers with geo-redundant backups. Rinkta is GDPR-compliant and we sign a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) on request within one business day. Full security details are at /security; we follow SOC 2 controls but aren't yet certified.
