Frequently asked questions
Don’t see your answer? Contact us using the email on your invoice or your account administrator.
Can I try FreeCal for free?
Yes. You start on the Free plan without a credit card. You can create unlimited calendars; public calendars show ads unless you choose Pro.
What’s the difference between Free and Pro?
Free includes all core features, with ads on the public view. Pro (€29 per calendar per year) removes those ads and suits organisations that want a polished, professional impression.
Can I upgrade per calendar later?
Yes. Pro is billed per calendar. Your other calendars can stay on Free if that fits better.
Do members see my internal planning?
Only if you set the calendar to public and share the link. Private calendars are meant for use inside your account.
How does Pro payment work?
When you move a calendar to Pro, you can pay securely via Stripe Checkout. You can also use a promo code if one is available for your organisation.
Where do visitors view my calendar?
Each calendar has its own shareable link. Members and visitors always see the current version without logging in (for public calendars).
