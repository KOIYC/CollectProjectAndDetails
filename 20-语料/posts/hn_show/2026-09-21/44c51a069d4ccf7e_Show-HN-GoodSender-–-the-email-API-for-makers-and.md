 The problem

 Anyone can write a post and queue an email. The only question that decides whether your marketing works is whether it reached the inbox.

Agents
 Your agent can run the whole loop
 Connect Claude, Codex, or any AI agent over the MCP server or the API. Every action in the app is a tool it can call. It drafts from your repo or docs, publishes, and with autosend on, delivers.

 Deliverability
 Earned deliverability
 A newsletter is permission, then sending, then keeping the people who stay engaged. We already run that engine for the largest senders in the world.
 Permission, enforced.
 Double opt in is the default, not a setting you forget to turn on.
Engagement, monitored.
 We watch opens, clicks, and complaints, and re confirm the quiet ones. Your list cleans itself.
Reputation, earned.
 You send from a high reputation shared pool, run by people who have done this at scale since 2009.
GoodSender owns its deliverability rather than renting it. Every send runs on Laneful, enterprise infrastructure from the same team that built SendGrid. It is not a feature we bought. It's the whole point.
Included
 What's included
 A hosted publication on your own domain: fast pages, clean URLs, sitemaps, RSS, full meta control.

 Subscribe forms, popups, and a hosted subscribe page, wired straight into enforced double opt in.
