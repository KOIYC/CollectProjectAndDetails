​
Owl VIP requests Gmail metadata access to detect new incoming emails and show notifications with sender + subject.

If you enable Email Snippet in the app's alert settings, Owl VIP requests Gmail read-only access so it can display snippet text in the notification.

Owl VIP is read-only and does not send, delete, modify, label, or move emails.
