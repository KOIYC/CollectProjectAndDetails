# 0.9.5: no computer has to stay on

**Send to dot 0.9.5 — no computer has to stay on**

Share a photo (and an optional question) from Android or iPhone, and the original appears in your existing ChatGPT dot conversation, where dot answers you.

What this release means for you:

- **No computer has to stay on.** Everything runs on your own free Cloudflare account. Your Mac can sleep or be off; sharing keeps working.
- **Set up by talking to your dot.** Paste the README “No Mac” message into your dot conversation. Dot finds this release and walks you through the few steps only you can do (Cloudflare Deploy page, your owner code, `/setup` for the phone code, adding the ChatGPT app), then creates its own task. No terminal, no server to maintain.
- **Your original photo, once.** The exact file, not a compressed preview, arrives as an attachment and is never sent twice. Uncertain sends are held and checked, not retried.
- **Private by default.** Encrypted, deleted from the inbox after 1 hour; the phone code can only upload; ChatGPT reads only after you approve with your owner code.
- **About 40 seconds** from tapping Send to seeing the photo in one measured case (server timings, not yet screen-recorded). Most of it is dot waking up.

No code changes since 0.9.1. Not yet verified: another person's independent deployment, screen-recorded timing, a 30-day grant renewal. The Mac install path remains available.

**中文说明**

从手机分享一张照片（可附问题），原图会出现在你正在用的 ChatGPT dot 对话里，dot 接着回答。

- **不需要一直开着电脑。** 全部运行在你自己的免费 Cloudflare 账号上，Mac 休眠或关机都不影响发图。
- **跟 dot 说一句就能装好。** 把 README 里“不用 Mac”那段话发给 dot，它会找到这个版本，只把必须你本人做的几步交给你，然后自己创建任务。不用终端，也不用维护服务器。
- **原图，只发一次。** 送到对话里的是原文件，不是压缩预览；不会重复发送，结果不确定时先暂停核实。
- **默认私密。** 加密存储，1 小时后从收件箱删除；手机码只能上传；ChatGPT 要你用本人确认码授权后才能读取。
- **约 40 秒可见。** 一次实测从点发送到看见原图约 40 秒（服务器计时，尚未录屏核实），主要时间在等 dot 被唤醒。

尚未验证：他人独立部署、录屏计时、30 天授权续期。
