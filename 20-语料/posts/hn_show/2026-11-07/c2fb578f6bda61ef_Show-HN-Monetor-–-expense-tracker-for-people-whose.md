Before you download.
What is Monetor?
 Monetor is an iPhone finance app for automatic expense tracking without a bank connection. Free includes eligible Apple Pay Shortcuts, supported payment-notification tracking, manual entry, budgets, savings goals and multiple currencies. Pro adds premium AI insights, advanced reports and financial tools.
Do I need to connect my bank?
 No. Monetor does not connect to your bank account or use Plaid. You choose supported Shortcuts automations, scan receipts or enter transactions manually. Setup, device compatibility and service availability apply to automatic tracking.
How does automatic tracking work?
 Automatic tracking is included in Free. Set up a Wallet Transaction automation and the Monetor Shortcut on a compatible iPhone with iOS 17+. Payment-notification tracking requires iOS 27+, supported source apps, setup, permissions, consent and service availability. These methods capture eligible new events, not past Wallet or notification history. Manual entry fills the gaps and works on iOS 15.1+.
What does Free include, and what does Pro add?
 Free includes automatic tracking where supported, manual entry, categories, search, two total wallets including the default wallet, multi-currency support, one successful receipt scan per account, week/month analytics, budgets, savings goals, subscriptions and manual loans with reminders. Pro adds more wallets, receipt scanning without the Free quota, premium AI insights, advanced reports and yearly comparisons, subscription overlap insights, loan document/refund tools and CSV, PDF and XLSX exports. Fair-use service limits apply.
Does the Free receipt scan allowance reset each month?
 No. Free includes one successful receipt scan per account, not one per month. A usable extraction consumes the allowance even if you do not save the transaction. Failed or unreadable scans do not consume it. Pro removes this Free quota, subject to fair-use service limits.
Can I track loans without Pro?
 Yes. Manual loan tracking, payments and reminders are included in Free. Pro adds loan document import and premium loan/refund tools. Existing imported loan schedules remain accessible after returning to Free; there is no separate Free loan trial.
How much does Monetor cost? Is there a trial?
 Monetor is free to download, and automatic tracking is included in Free where supported. See your local price and available offers in the App Store. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled; Lifetime is a one-time purchase. The purchase sheet confirms current plans, prices and terms. Any trial depends on the offer and your eligibility.
Is my data private?
 Monetor never asks for bank credentials and does not sell your data. Account data is encrypted at rest. Optional AI insights, receipt scanning and payment-notification tracking send the text or images they need to the processors named in the privacy policy, with your consent.
Do I need Pro for budgets or savings goals?
 No. Budgets and savings goals are included in Free. There is no one-goal restriction. Pro adds deeper analysis and premium financial tools.
Is Monetor available on Android or the web?
 Monetor is currently an iPhone app, available on the App Store. It requires iOS 15.1 or later for manual tracking, with newer iOS versions and additional setup for automatic tracking.
