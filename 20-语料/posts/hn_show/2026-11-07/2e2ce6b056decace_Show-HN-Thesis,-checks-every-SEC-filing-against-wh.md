Thesis analysis
 Challenged
 Revenue growth and mainframe-driven infrastructure have slipped off the original path, while free cash flow is still tracking close to the planned annual step-up. On balance, the thesis now depends on cash delivery more than broad operating momentum.
 Analysis breakdown
 Challenged IBM’s revenue grows above 5% a year in constant currency.
 Revenue growth above 5% is now challenged because quarterly results missed badly and management cut its constant-currency growth outlook to 4% to 5%, below the thesis target.
Broken The IBM Z mainframe cycle keeps Infrastructure revenue growing.
 The mainframe cycle keeping Infrastructure revenue growing is broken because mainframe sales swung from strong early growth to a sharp decline and Infrastructure turned down year over year.
Supported IBM’s free cash flow grows by about $1 billion a year.
 Free cash flow growing by about $1 billion a year is supported because first-half cash generation and management's outlook still point to that step-up, helped further by lower cash taxes, despite added investment demands.
