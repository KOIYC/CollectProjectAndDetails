Witnessory
Global view · take nobody's word for it
Witnessory measures how far the internet has moved to post-quantum cryptography, and how close quantum computers are to breaking what has not moved, from probes on several networks whose every reading is signed.
58.9% of the top 10,000 sites negotiate post-quantum TLS; 51.3% of SSH servers offer post-quantum key exchange. As of 2026-10-29, from 4 probes; signed readings, recomputable.
