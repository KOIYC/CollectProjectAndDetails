FAQ
Do I need subscriptions to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or Gemini?
 Axoris works with the AI services through their websites. You can sign in with your existing accounts, including eligible free accounts. Paid subscriptions to individual AI services are separate and are not included with Axoris.
Why not just use ChatGPT or the AI websites in my browser?
 You can. Axoris is for people who want faster, keyboard-first access to multiple AI services without searching through browser tabs or managing separate windows. It gives your AI workflow one dedicated home on your Mac.
Is Axoris safe? Are my passwords and conversations private?
 Axoris is designed with your privacy in mind. Axoris does not collect or transmit your personal data, passwords, or AI conversations to Axoris servers. You sign in directly to the AI services through their websites, and your conversations are handled by the respective providers under their own privacy policies. Axoris simply provides a convenient, dedicated place to access the AI tools you already use.
What if the Option shortcut conflicts with another app?
 No problem! You can customize Axoris's global keyboard shortcut in Settings to choose a shortcut that works best for your workflow and avoids conflicts with other apps.
