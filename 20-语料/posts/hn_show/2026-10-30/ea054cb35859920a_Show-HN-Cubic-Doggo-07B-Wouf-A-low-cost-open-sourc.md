## 正文

Cubic Doggo 07B Wouf is a ROS2-controlled 12-DOF untethered quadruped, built with ST3215 smart servos fully with off-the-shelf components and 3D-printed partsFeaturing:
* 12 ST-3215-C018 servos, usually on a bundled sale because of SO-ARM101
* Walking forward with a payload of 1 kg
* Smart servos as required for closed-loop reinforcement learning capability
* FreeCAD models fully available for editing
* M3 screws and locknuts are used extensively (instead of glue or heat set inserts) to strengthen and secure the PLA+ printed structures. Design iterates on strengthening whichever part breaks first. Check JLCPCB for stronger components
* No custom PCB (because I am terrible at it), only 2 required soldering points
* Runs on a Raspberry Pi with ROS2 Jazzy installed. Joystick control ready
* Walks on batteries
* Aesthetics of an exposed electronic box, but great as a development platform for stacking new components on top
