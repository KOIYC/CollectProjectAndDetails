## 正文

I was building a social media platform for users to raise their voices. I have made a special algorithm for them, and it works really well.

I got suggestions from @akhourianmolkumar, @effessdev, @technogamerz and more users to make the UI minimal.

I was very much interested in adding and showing features to users. Just in that curiosity, I forgot that the UI should be minimal and more comfortable.

I added a huge number of features to the dashboard. Whenever a user opens Crowdwide, they see a bunch of features.

Wherever they check, they get something like a feature.

They scroll, they get a feature.

They click, they get a feature.

They search, they get a feature.

They comment, they get features.

Their posts get reach, they get features.

Even after using Crowdwide for just 5 minutes, they look outside the window and see Crowdwide's features outside there... lol...

So, after noticing this...

I visited some other social media platforms, like Instagram, Reddit, Bluesky, and Twitter.

I noticed what they show when users log in.

I noted the features in their dashboards and decided to show only this much, with barely anything extra.

I made a new menu.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/wj6y233sdo18zbm0z8yb.png)

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/m1hgxvqplzkdkzw9cozd.png)

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/3ugbnd5g4n8sruac910y.png)

The top menu is clean now.

The side menu has a few useful options. Further, you can expand it to get others.

And on the other side, the communities section gets a new update, and now you'll be able to see communities there or join them. To create a new community, you have to open my community page.

And this is a major improvement in this.

Further, I noticed the post composer got a little more updated, and more options on the post page need to be put in a little dropdown.

And the second thing is about Widewave.

Many users aren't able to understand Widewave, and some feel it's not required at this stage.

Yeah, this was suggested by @akhourianmolkumar ,@effessdev and @technogamerz too.

So, I removed that too...

And now I feel it feels very, very much better and calmer than before!

UI is an important factor. I build the backend in a few days, but understanding the frontend takes longer.

And I think no frontend is perfect. Anything... anywhere... anyhow, something better exists in this case.

And I got some new users too. Yeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!

**18** members
**72** posts shared
**4** communities

It's growing, but sometimes it gets steady too!

And you might be thinking, why Day 25–26 together??

But on Day 25, I was working on both these features, so I hadn't updated anything. So, I just updated this and am now telling this to you!

I read and reply to every suggestion!
