## 正文

If you have been following me for a long time, you already know that I have been holding off on my Portfolio review for several months.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/55r34irqjs415ihk0o1e.gif)

&nbsp;

**Before you ask, yes, I will finally show my portfolio website.** First, I want to write about something we all may have been thinking about at some point.

&nbsp;

**Everyone needs a developer portfolio.** It's one of the big ways to stand out in the tech market because it shows your work and experience to recruiters in a friendly UI format. Some may say it's a great alternative to review a candidate's work **(Source: Trust me bro).**

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/7h8pveibft5pc28guc01.png)

However, in the age of AI, it seems that it's almost impossible to create a portfolio that stands out. **Why is that the case?**

&nbsp;

## AI Can do...anything?

We all (probably) have tried using AI at some point and even still use AI in our daily lives. Whether how much you have used it, you probably already know its capabilities.

**AI is trained so well that it can potentially one-shot a website portfolio.** Even better, if the person decides, it can add features and other stuff to make the website look like it was built in months (even though it was built in minutes). We even got to the point where **Claude was able to merge two games into one in a matter of a few weeks!**

{% embed https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xo3vpvbA8p8 %}

**It's crazy isn't it.** AI development has been rapidly growing in the last few years to the point where it is accomplishing tasks that may have taken months to do.

With that said, I thought to myself: **"Why consider building a portfolio if everyone can vibe code their way into making their perfect portfolio?"**

To be honest, it's a valid question. The market has been difficult because **everyone is using AI to their advantage to stand out from the market.** I have seen a lot of Website Portfolios and Resumes that have been assisted with AI. **As a result, the bar has never been higher.**

However, I still believe having a portfolio still matters, even in this age where it seems impossible. **Here's my reasoning.**

&nbsp;

---

&nbsp;

## AI Portfolios may look different, but they are the same.

**What do I mean by this?** I am talking about **"style".**

If you try to vibe code a portfolio, people can tell that the portfolio is vibe coded. Sure, it's not the EXACT replica compare to 100 portfolios out there, but you can easily tell by seeing some UI features that are defaulted if you vaguely prompt it.

A small example is this:

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/1floz2r2trb7qglvp6l7.png)

I have reviewed a lot of portfolios that has this (or something similar). Sure this may be a coincidence, but I want you to try to vibe code a sample website portfolio. Most of the time, they will add this feature.

This matters because if you think about it, a lot of portfolios out there are simply the same, **even though it may not look like it.** You have to have good "attention to detail" to see this for sure. However, at this point, coming from a person who has reviewed candidates before, it tends to be **really repetitive.** Hence, making it harder in the hiring process since everyone is doing the same thing and more research needs to be accounted for.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/otitvym1xxhlr917bsuf.png)

This leads to the next point.

&nbsp;

## "New Mask, Same Task"

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/iyhst5tknfvs08r0i8wn.png)

**Yes, I took this straight up from RDJ.** Though the whole point is that even though he plays a different character, **he is still RDJ.**

**What does this mean in this context?** Just because you have pretty portfolio, **it does not mean your work reflects that.** You can create many different portfolios under your name. At the end of the day, **it's still you that needs to improve and not the portfolio.**

**Portfolio is used as a presentation.** If you are presenting your portfolio and it looks good, I would assume you have a lot of work experience and projects that are noticeable based on your portfolio design and how you are selling yourself.

For example, you have a front-end developer portfolio, **but if I were to investigate further into your GitHub, I may see the following things:**

- You just joined as of this year on GitHub.
- You claimed you did Open-Source, but there is no PR that reflects that.
- Your Portfolio has good UI, your project doesn't.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/hqqedl0o60hmgy3vmbwq.png)

I can list a lot of things, but the main point is that if you have a good portfolio with a good list of projects you have done, **you better hope that your projects and work reflects on how you built this portfolio.** This is **ESPECIALLY** true if you tried to become a front-end developer. If your UI is good, you better hope your projects reflects that.

It's something to keep in mind when building your portfolio. Of course, if you are applying for a back-end developer role, having a good UI on your website portfolio isn't really required, but it's a nice to have. **The whole point is to not misrepresent yourself. Be careful on what you add on your portfolio. Always assume people will have questions on your work.**

&nbsp;
&nbsp;

Those are the two main things. Though you may be asking **"How do I make my portfolio stand out"?**

**Here are some key things that you can do (and yes, I am now showing my portfolio lol).**

## 1. Start with a basic portfolio template, then use AI to add onto it.

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/nl747eyej271ayfn2iit.png)

For my case, I have used an existing portfolio from **"brittanychiang.com and meikopoulos.com"**. From there, I grab existing features using AI and put them together into a portfolio I am proud of. Additionally, I added some new features that are not distracting, but is something I want to add onto my portfolio to make it unique to me:

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/y6mihaafktq4gfkff5ut.png)

**Instead of building it from scratch, I encourage you to find a portfolio that already exists (Bonus points: Proven that it stands out in the tech market) and build it on top of it.** This allows the portfolio to be unique and that **reflects you.** Additionally, building on to of a template is a good starting point because it already has the fundamentals on what makes a portfolio "good". In this case, I added a feature where the "Skills" section reveals Pokemon in each Skill Card, though I am not sharing how it works. **Can you find the Easter egg that does that?**

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/vvvd3q57f49bbfmctdxz.png)

&nbsp;

## 2. Usability and Accessibility!

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/kqbmxzxpo3fhlxop36kf.png)

**This is the most OVERLOOK aspect of making a portfolio.** Even though you can make your portfolio look nice, it's most likely that it's **not accessible.**

**For example, it can be very laggy on low-end devices, not responsive on mobile, not following best practices, etc.** There are a lot of things that is overlooked because we focus on building the best looking portfolio where we should be focusing on making our website portfolio optimized.

I would recommend spending time using AI to optimize your portfolio. In other words: **"Boil the lake to make the portfolio, but boil the ocean for website optimization and accessibility".**

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/shabozho5fs63ftadpah.png)

**For mine, I spend countless prompts where I tell the AI to make sure my portfolio is less "laggy" and being able to run on mobile.**

This will allow to make a much cleaner portfolio and being able to be used by many people such as people who have disabilities, devices that are not powerful, etc.

&nbsp;

## 3. Simplicity beats Complexity!

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/3jdxudp8a03vvlni8jvj.png)

**A lot of portfolios we have seen tends to be very complex where there are a lot of animations and such.**

However, the more you think about it, the more you realized that you can **stand out easily.**

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/8pa5pdohs07xc78ycnsv.png)

I have seen a lot of portfolios that are REALLY complex and have a lot of animations, but what people fail to realized is that: **You are doing what everyone else is doing.**

**At the end of the day, you are not standing out, even if you THINK that you are.** If I see a portfolio that is heavily designed using AI, I can guarantee that I can find the exact same person who can one-up that.

**The best advice is making it simple.** Yes, it's boring and yes it's common. But..that's the whole point.

I rarely see any portfolio that is simply built. **Usually the ones that are simple tends to stand out because it shows a lot of information really quickly.**

![Image description](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/yprh442j56lfeei9e71u.png)

**This is huge because recruiters tends to review portfolios and resumes in seconds.** They will not have time to look over your awesome UI and animation you have Vibe Coded. The UI should be the icing on the cake, not the whole cake. **The most important parts is your work and experience.**

Then why design your portfolio? It makes it much easier for people to navigate your work and experience than reading your resume line-by-line of course lol. The point is not spending time making the UI look nice, but **spending more time on making sure they are able to access your work and experience within a small amount of clicks.**
