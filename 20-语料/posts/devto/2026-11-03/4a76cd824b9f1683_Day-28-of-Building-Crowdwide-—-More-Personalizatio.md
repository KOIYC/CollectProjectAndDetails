## 正文

I was building a social media platform with a special algorithm that gives new voices or communities a better chance of being discovered.

And... it's Crowdwide!

Today is day 28 of giving updates on its v1 version, and its v2 version is going to release on 1 November 2026. Not too far...

So far...

I made a few more changes and made a few bugs...

While adding and setting up something for larger screens, I broke it for smaller screens!!

Just like the sidebar of Crowdwide.

Actually, I saw some space due to padding inside the sidebar, so I just removed it and didn't check it on my phone.

And YES!! That's where I made a bug... bug maker developer respect ++ MISSION RESPECT PASSES+++

Then it was reported by @effessdev, and I fixed it!

And in the sidebar... on larger screens like PCs, whenever I opened the sidebar, I saw that it was different in every section of Crowdwide.

So I made it fixed!

And everything was looking so set and satisfying!

But I thought...

Now I have 20+ users, why not increase personalization?

What did I do exactly?

I have added a few more things to the feed.

If your post gets more views, like 40–50 (initially shown to users in their feed), but no reaction, it will be ranked lower (not too low... just less preferred).

And...

Out of every 4–5 posts, you will get one post that you may be interested in...

Just to check your interest in other things as well.

There is a thing in social media platforms that checks the interest shifts of users.

So yeah, I added that...

Actually, I found that approach smart... smarter... smartest... lol.

Further, I haven't made any other very special changes...

Do you think with this much UI... and backend, Crowdwide is okay for now?

Like, I mean, can I stop giving it daily updates now?

Also...

I announced that... I want to make someone a brand ambassador of Crowdwide...

But no one talked about it... lol... so till now... I haven't got anyone...

> **mp3sss** · 2026-11-02T01:54:50Z　
> The sidebar bug is the classic regression I see in desktop apps too — you fix a layout for one window size and silently break another, because nothing checks the boundaries you're not looking at. Ranking by engagement (views without reactions downgrading a post) is a smart lightweight relevance model, and the interest-shift probe is a nice touch. Worth adding window-size snapshots to CI so 'works on my screen' stops being a per-developer claim.

---

> **Puneet Kumar Mishra** · 2026-11-02T11:10:00Z　
> That's exactly what happened 😂 fixed the layout for the screen I was testing on, and another size immediately exposed what I'd missed. Responsive bugs really are the ultimate “works on my machine” problem
>
> And yep, the engagement ranking is intentionally lightweight for now. I wanted something that could use real behaviour signals without jumping straight into an overly complicated recommendation system. Same idea with the interest-shift probe: small signals first, then refine based on what users actually do.
>
> The window-size snapshots in CI suggestion is actually a really good one That's the kind of regression protection that's easy to postpone until the same category of bug happens repeatedly Adding checks across a few representative viewport sizes would definitely make “I tested it” mean something more than “I opened it on my screen once”
>
> Thanks for the suggestion definitely adding that to the list of things to investigate!
