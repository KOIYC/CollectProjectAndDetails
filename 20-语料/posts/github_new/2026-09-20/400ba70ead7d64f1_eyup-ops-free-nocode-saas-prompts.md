- **Generate 5 micro-SaaS ideas** based on recent trends in automation, targeting remote real estate agents, using no-code tools.
- **Act as a SaaS product manager:** Analyze this feature list `[insert list]` and identify potential bottlenecks for an MVP built on Bubble/FlutterFlow.
- **Create a user persona document** for a B2B AI-powered invoicing tool built for freelance designers.

## 2. 🤖 AI-Assisted No-Code & Full-Stack Development (Lovable, Bolt, V0, Replit)
- **Write a detailed system prompt for Lovable/Bolt.new** to generate a clean, dark-mode dashboard for a subscription analytics tool.
- **Generate a step-by-step database schema** for a multi-tenant SaaS application using Supabase or Xano.
- **Draft a clear prompt for V0** to build a responsive landing page Hero Section with a high-converting email capture form.
- **Debug this specific workflow block** or custom JavaScript function in my no-code app: `[insert code/logic here]`.

## 3. 🎯 Growth Marketing & SaaS Traffic Generation
- **Write a high-converting Cold DM template** for Twitter/X targeting Indie Hackers who need automated SEO auditing.
- **Create a Product Hunt launch sequence:** Draft the primary description, first comment, and a 3-day launch outreach plan.
- **Generate 10 engaging Twitter/X thread hooks** explaining how a non-technical founder built a \$5k MRR SaaS in 30 days.
- **Develop a 3-step sales funnel copy** (Landing page -> Lead magnet -> Paid tier) for a new AI micro-SaaS.

---

## 4. ✍️ SaaS Copywriting & High-Converting SEO Content
- **Write a persuasive 500-word blog post** targeting non-technical founders, explaining why building an MVP with No-Code + AI is the future.
- **Create 3 variations of Facebook/LinkedIn ad copy** focusing on the pain point of "wasting months hiring expensive developers."
- **Draft an automated onboarding email sequence** (3 emails) for a SaaS tool, maximizing the trial-to-paid conversion rate.

---

## 5. 📊 Operations, Pricing Strategy & SaaS Scaling
- **Analyze these 3 pricing models** for a niche CRM SaaS `[insert models]` and suggest the best strategy for high conversion.
- **Draft a professional terms of service and privacy policy outline** specifically tailored for a micro-SaaS handling user data via OpenAI API.
- **Generate a 30-day operation checklist** for launching a SaaS on Product Hunt, HackerNews, and Microns.
