## SyKoHPaTh’s fantasy RPG launches with a 40% introductory discount, online and offline play, town building, dungeon exploration, PvP, guilds, and plenty of waifus to collect

August 31, 2026 | Indie developer SyKoHPaTh has officially launched Fantasy Waifu Collector on Steam today. The fantasy RPG is available for $19.99, with a 40% launch discount bringing the price down to $11.99 for the first week.

Fantasy Waifu Collector drops players into a world slowly falling into ruin as monsters spread across the land. Newly awakened and tasked with helping restore it, players recruit a growing army of Waifus, build a town for them to call home, strengthen their team, and uncover what is really happening behind the world’s decay.

...

Launch isn’t the end of development for Fantasy Waifu Collector. SyKoHPaTh plans to continue the update schedule established throughout development, with monthly content updates planned following release.

...

Fantasy Waifu Collector is available now on Steam for $19.99, with a 40% introductory discount available during its first week.

About Fantasy Waifu Collector

Fantasy Waifu Collector is an action, casual, indie RPG from developer and publisher SyKoHPaTh. Players recruit and strengthen a collection of Waifus, building a growing town, battle monsters, explore dungeons, participate in a variety of minigames and multiplayer activities, and work to restore a fantasy world overrun by monsters.
