## The woodland fantasy roguelike adventure game inspired by classic action roguelikes such as The Binding of Isaac, has officially arrived.

United States, 1 October, 2026 | Shroomwood, a twin-stick bullet hell game by indie developers Sporelite Games releases into Early Access today on Steam. Playing as a courageous little mushroom warrior named Shroomie, set in the ever-shifting depths of the mystical Shroomwood forest.

...

Play Shroomwood now on Steam! Shroomwood will be releasing with a 40% discount, making the game available for $5.99 for the first 7 days after release!

...

ABOUT Sporelite Games.

Sporelite Games began working on Shroomwood in December 2024. Owned and operated by Washington State University (WSU) student Martin Hundrup. Sporelite Games was founded in December 2024.

Martin aimed to create quality games that can be enjoyed by everyone. Being in the Game Development Club at WSU, he was the founder and president. Martin helped promote game development to the University, created a network of local game devs, started working on Shroomwood and Plant Panic, and went on to help other local student developers develop and publish their games.
