## The full story

I had around 10,000 Twitter followers and frequently mentioned I would be launching a new AI product soon. I didn't have high expectations.

### Multi-Platform Launch

When I launched SiteGPT on Twitter in March 2023, the launch tweet became viral. The next day, it got onto the front page of Hacker News. Then I launched on Product Hunt where it became #1 of the day.

### Growing SiteGPT

I went from zero to $15K MRR in just six months. The $250K exit people sometimes associate with this story was actually Feather.so, an earlier product I sold in June 2024 - SiteGPT itself was never sold and kept growing toward $1M ARR.

- Time to $10K MRR: 1 month
- Peak MRR: $15K in 6 months
- Feather.so (earlier product): sold for $250K in June 2024

### Key Takeaways

1

Build an audience before launching - my 10K Twitter followers were crucial

2

Launch on multiple platforms in quick succession - Twitter, HN, Product Hunt

3

Building in public creates momentum - people love following the journey

4

Don't underestimate your potential - I thought one customer would be success
