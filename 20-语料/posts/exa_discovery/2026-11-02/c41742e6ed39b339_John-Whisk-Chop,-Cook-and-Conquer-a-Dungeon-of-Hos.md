# John Whisk: Chop, Cook and Conquer a Dungeon of Hostile Produce

Slay hostile produce, harvest ingredients, and cook up permanent power in this delicious dungeon crawler

-

 Published 2 Nov 2026 By Rottie

What happens when a culinary artist goes on a vengeful dungeon crawl? John Whisk answers that question by letting you play as a chef wielding deadly kitchen tools against an army of hostile produce. Developed by Magirush and KazooKat, this freshly launched indie title blends action-adventure with survival simulation. As you hack your way through sentient fruits and vegetables, every defeat yields fresh ingredients.

- Battle through a bizarre dungeon of hostile produce
- Harvest loot directly from every defeated enemy
- Choose between safe extraction or risking a deeper run
- Cook ingredients for permanent stat boosts or sell them for quick cash

If you love high-stakes risk-and-reward loops mixed with quirky culinary themes, this one just landed on Steam today and is well worth throwing onto your radar. Resources: John Whisk on Steam
