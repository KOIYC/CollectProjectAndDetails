In 2024, one folder of starter code was selling about $126,000 a month for a solo founder living in Bali. On October 20, 2026, its creator posted the newer figure: $2,000.

...

#### AT A GLANCE

What it is: ShipFast, a paid boilerplate (a ready-made folder of code with login and payments already wired up) for building web apps with Next.js, a popular website framework

Founded: launched September 2023 by French solo founder Marc Lou (Marc Louvion)

Team: 1 person

Audience: indie makers and first-time founders, about 8,433 users listed on its TrustMRR profile (November 2026)

Makes money from: one-time licenses at $199, $249 or $299

Latest known milestone: $1,272,892 in all-time revenue verified through Stripe, with $1,395 in the 30 days to October 31, 2026

...

ShipFast went live on Product Hunt (a daily leaderboard for new tech products) on September 1, 2023, finished second for the day with 941 upvotes and brought in $6,000 in 48 hours. By the end of the month: $40,000.

...

ShipFast sells one thing: a license you pay for once and keep forever. Its MRR (monthly recurring revenue, the money subscriptions bring in each month without a new sale) is zero by design.

...

Marc’s own X account is the engine. At launch he had about 35,000 followers, earned over two years of building in public (posting launches and revenue numbers as they happen, flops included).

By November 2026 the count sits near 420,000. Every revenue screenshot works as an ad for the code that produced it.

This engine fits ShipFast in particular because buyers of a startup kit want proof that the kit produces startups.

Marc was the proof, posting income from products built on the same code he sold. In early 2024 he reported 500 to 1,500 visitors a day to the ShipFast site without paying for ads.

Two smaller channels added fuel. Skit-style launch videos got shared widely, and a viral YouTube feature in 2024 pushed monthly sales from roughly $50,000 to $135,000, by Marc’s own account on camera.

...

#### THE SOLO VERSION

A small version today: one person selling a $49 starter kit (code or templates) to people rushing into a tool that person already uses daily, built in public on X from the first day.

...

Month 12: $100 to $1,500 a month for people who kept shipping, and many people earn less.
