# Solo Developer's SpellChain Tops 2,000 Sales in Its First 24 Hours on Steam

Big Wiz Games' solo-made spellcrafting RPG SpellChain launched November 5 and topped 2,000 sales in a day, with two bug-fix patches already live.

Sam Ito Staff Writer · Nov 6, 2026 · 3 min read

Image: Big Wiz Games.

A one-person studio just had the kind of launch week most indie developers only dream about. SpellChain, a first-person spellcrafting fantasy RPG built and published by Big Wiz Games, released on Steam on November 5 for $8.99, and within 24 hours its solo creator was publicly stunned by the response: more than 2,000 copies sold on day one.

...

SpellChain casts players as an exiled wizard living in a tower above a hostile kingdom, tasked with crashing festivals, sabotaging patrols and generally making the king's life miserable on the way to an eventual confrontation with him. The hook is its chain-based spellcrafting system, which lets players combine effects to build their own attacks rather than pick from a fixed spell list, a structure the store page says draws on Magicka and Fictorum. The game ships with six bosses, 25 scripted level events and, according to Big Wiz Games, thousands of viable spell combinations.

...

The runway to launch was itself a small success story. Three days before release, Big Wiz Games announced that SpellChain had crossed 20,000 Steam wishlists, a strong number for a solo-developed debut with no publisher attached, driven in part by players trying the game's public demo ahead of launch.

When the game actually went live on November 5, the developer's own announcement kept things simple: SpellChain was out for $8.99, with a 10% launch discount running for its first ten days, and an invitation for players to bring feedback straight to the official Discord server. There was no elaborate marketing push behind it, just a solo creator posting directly to the people who had already wishlisted the game.

...

What happened next is the real story. In a patch note posted the morning after launch, Big Wiz Games revealed that SpellChain had topped 2,000 sales in its first 24 hours, a number the developer called "more than I ever expected." For a single-developer release priced under $10 with no major platform backing, that is a strong showing, and it tracks with the wishlist momentum the game had already built heading into launch day.

...

SpellChain is available now on Steam for Windows, with full controller support and Steam Achievements. Players chasing the current launch discount have a limited window before the 10% price cut expires, and the developer has said bug reports and suggestions are welcome at any time through the official Discord.
