Damon Chen reached $10K MRR in 11 months, faster than 66% of the 295 documented $10K journeys (median 12 months). The amber bar is this journey.
...
Built the MVP in about four days from the first commit (finished in under a week), reusing large amounts of code from the three prior 2020 projects. Damon says he sought no external validation before building; the Product Hunt / Indie Hackers launch itself, and the early lifetime-deal sales it produced, served as the validation signal.
...
Testimonial.to launched simultaneously on Indie Hackers and Product Hunt on 20 December 2020. Damon later said some of his early users told him directly they had found the product through Product Hunt.
...
The launch post itself carried a $199 "unlimited video testimonials" lifetime-deal offer, capped at 100 seats. Within ten days of the 20 December 2020 launch, Damon tweeted that Testimonial.to had generated $3,025, the only one of his five 2020 projects to make real money.
...
Damon credits Product Hunt's own daily-featured-product pages with driving organic search traffic well after launch day, on top of direct signups from people who found the product through the PH listing.
...
Continuous build-in-public updates over roughly a year took his following from about 70 to about 7,000, which he frames as the payoff of showing up daily rather than any single tactic.
...
Asked which acquisition channels were most productive, Damon ranked Twitter first.
