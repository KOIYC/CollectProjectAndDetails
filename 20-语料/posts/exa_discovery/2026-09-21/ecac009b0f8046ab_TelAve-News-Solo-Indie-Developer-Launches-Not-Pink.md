# Solo Indie Developer Launches Not Pink, a Couples App Built Around a Four-Phase Planning Journey
...
STUTTGART, Germany - TelAve -- Independent iOS developer Thomas Flad today launched Not Pink, a shared planning app for couples that organizes experiences around four phases: Align, Planning, Prepare, and Relive. The app is available now on the App Store in nine languages. Most couples apps target new relationships or long-distance use cases. Not Pink is built for the audience in between: established couples who want to plan and complete real shared experiences — trips, creative projects, and smaller adventures — together. Partners pair via invite code, swipe through a curated idea library to discover what they both want, and confirm together before each phase advances. "Most couples apps target new relationships," said Thomas Flad, founder of Not Pink. "I built Not Pink for couples who've been together a while and would rather plan their next trip than answer another daily relationship quiz." More on TelAve News
...
About the founder Thomas Flad is a solo indie developer based in Kornwestheim, Germany. He spent roughly a decade in mobile engineering, working across native iOS and Android, most recently as engineering manager at YAZIO, one of Europe's largest nutrition apps. In January 2026 he left full-time employment to build Not Pink independently. About Not Pink Not Pink is an iOS app that gives couples a shared space to plan and live out the experiences they keep talking about. Built solo by Thomas Flad, the app ships in nine languages and is available worldwide on the App Store. Media contact Thomas Flad Founder, Not Pink https://notpink.app App Store listing
