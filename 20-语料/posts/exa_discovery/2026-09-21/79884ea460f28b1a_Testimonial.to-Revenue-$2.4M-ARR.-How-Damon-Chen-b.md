### The Solution

Testimonial.to makes it dead simple - you send customers a link, they record a video directly in their browser, and you get an embeddable widget for your site. No friction, no downloads, no complicated setup.

### Building in Public

I shared my entire journey on Twitter - the good days and the bad days. When I hit milestones, I'd celebrate publicly. When features flopped, I'd share what I learned. This transparency built trust and attracted early adopters who wanted to support an indie maker.

### Product Hunt Launch

The Product Hunt launch was a turning point. We reached the top 5 products of the day and got hundreds of signups. But more importantly, the launch generated buzz that lasted for weeks.
