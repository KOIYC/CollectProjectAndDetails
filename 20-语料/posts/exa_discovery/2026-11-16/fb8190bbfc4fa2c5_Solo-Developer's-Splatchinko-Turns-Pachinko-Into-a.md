# Solo Developer's Splatchinko Turns Pachinko Into a Cosmic Rescue Roguelike

Stupidi Pixel's solo developer Fabio Taurisano launches Splatchinko on Steam, a pixel-art roguelike turning pachinko ricochets into a cosmic rescue mission.

Sam Ito Staff Writer · Nov 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Image: Stupidi Pixel.

A one-person Milan studio has turned a pachinko machine into a galaxy-spanning rescue mission. Splatchinko, the new game from solo developer Fabio Taurisano under the Stupidi Pixel banner, launched on Steam on November 11, pairing pixel-art roguelike structure with the bounce-and-ricochet logic of a peg board.

...

Splatchinko is Stupidi Pixel's second major release, following the studio's earlier delivery game Death Noodle Delivery. Where that project leaned on absurdist errand-running, Splatchinko channels the same irreverent streak into a tighter, score-chasing structure built around a single core mechanic. A free demo covering the opening stretch of the game has been available on Steam since the spring, giving players a chance to test the eye-and-mutation system well ahead of the full launch.

The game is published by Stratos Gaming, an Italian indie publisher that also backs titles including The Fortress and Two Sides of Hell, and whose stated role is helping small studios fund and ship games they could not get out the door alone. For a solo developer, that kind of backing can be the difference between a finished roguelike and an abandoned prototype.

## Price and availability

Splatchinko is available now exclusively on Steam for Windows, priced at $11.89, with a 20% launch discount bringing it down to $9.51 through its first week. There is no word yet on console ports; for now, the eyes stay on PC.

- Developer: Stupidi Pixel (solo project, Fabio Taurisano)
- Publisher: Stratos Gaming
- Platform: Steam (Windows)
- Launch price: $11.89, $9.51 with the first-week discount
