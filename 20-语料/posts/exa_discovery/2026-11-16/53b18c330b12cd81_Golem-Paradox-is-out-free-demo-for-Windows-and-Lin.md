# Golem Paradox is out - free demo for Windows and Linux news

Golem Paradox, a small factory automation + tower defense game made solo with Godot, is out. The free demo includes the first two planets and the whole factory.

 Posted by Puxin on Nov 14th, 2026

Hi everyone! I'm Puxin, a solo developer from Spain, and Golem Paradox is my first game on IndieDB. It mixes factory automation with tower defense, and there is a free demo for Windows and Linux.

### Build. Automate. Upgrade. Defend.

Humanity's Central AI needs resources to survive, so you mine distant planets. But every drill wakes up the Golems, ancient guardians of the stone... and they come in waves.

- Factory: place foundries, factories and assemblers, choose what each one makes and connect them with belts and walkways. Ore goes in through a funnel and comes out as ingots, gears, CPUs and ships.
- Defense: 7 towers, each with its own upgrade tree, against wave after wave of golems and their Titans.
- 11 planets, each with its own golems, a final boss and a chapter of the comic.

### Free demo

The demo includes the first two planets (Kepler-9 and Nexara V) and the whole factory:

- Golem Paradox Demo (Windows)
- Golem Paradox Demo (Linux)

The full game is on itch.io, and there is a game guide at puxin.habitantesdelcaos.com/golem-paradox.
