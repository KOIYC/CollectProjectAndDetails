# Nightware is out: a cute desktop assistant who won't let you log off news

## Nightware

### Sparekey Studio | Released Nov 14, 2026

Pal, a cute desktop assistant, will be deleted at 6 AM. He'd rather you didn't log off before then. A surreal horror adventure that lasts one night on an old computer.

Nightware, a surreal horror adventure that lasts one night on an old computer, is out now on itch.io, and it's 20% off for its first week.

 Posted by SparekeyStudio on Nov 16th, 2026

Nightware is out now on itch.io for Windows, and it's 20% off for its first week (until Sunday, November 22).

## One long night

A friend gives you their old PC and asks you to install one update. The update brings back Pal, a small, cheerful desktop assistant who has been waiting a long time for someone to show around. Then the notice arrives: old assistants will be retired at 6 AM. Pal has until sunrise, and he'd rather you didn't log off before then.

## A different game behind every window

Through the night the computer's forgotten programs wake up one by one: a fairground arcade, a neon city in 3D, a dollhouse where the toys are watching, and the hours nobody was meant to see. Some of them remember the last person who stayed.

What you draw becomes a real thing in the world, and what you say, keep and refuse stays with Pal until morning. There are several endings.

## Play it your way

- Larger text, sound captions, fewer flashes, less shake and softer jump scares
- Assist and Story settings for every fight
- English and Turkish (Türkçe)
- Streamer mode hides your computer's account name; you're welcome to stream and monetize

— Sparekey Studio, a one-person studio in Bordeaux
