### Press release

September 18, 2026 | The wait is over. After 6 years in development, Magic Puncher Games' Sunken Realms, a focused ARPG dungeon crawler with rogue-lite elements, is out now on Steam at $16.99 / £13.99 / €16.99, with a 10% launch discount for the first two weeks. The gates of Aedaria are open, with 45,000 wishlists already logged and adventurers ready to descend. The demo remains live until September 21st for anyone who wants a first taste before diving into the full journey.
...
"The game that inspired me to make my own was Outward," said Brad Warren, the solo developer behind Magic Puncher Games. "It has such a feeling of adventure and the wide unknown that I haven't quite felt anywhere else. Sunken Realms went in its own direction over time, but I hope it kept that same spirit." That spirit carries through the game today, alongside the storytelling influence of Hades and the sense of adventure found in The Legend of Zelda.
