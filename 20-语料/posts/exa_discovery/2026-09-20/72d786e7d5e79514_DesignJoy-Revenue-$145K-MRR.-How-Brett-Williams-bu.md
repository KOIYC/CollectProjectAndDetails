Brett Williams had been designing since 2009. In 2017 he launched DesignJoy, a subscription design service. Building in public on Twitter from early 2022 filled a 100+ company waitlist in three weeks and let him double his prices. By 2022, he broke $1.5M ARR as a true agency of one.
...
The breakthrough came in early 2022 when Brett joined Twitter and started building in public. He shared his revenue, his processes, his wins and his struggles, transparently. Within six weeks Twitter became his biggest lead source, and the surge of interest built a waitlist of 100+ companies in three weeks.
...
He doubled his prices after the demand surge. At $4,995/month per client, the math was extraordinary.
...
By March 2022, Brett had broken the $1.5M ARR mark as a true "agency of one" — handling 20-25 active clients with zero employees and just $95/month in tool costs. The margins were unlike anything in the agency world.
