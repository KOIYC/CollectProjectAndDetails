# 🦇 LaurEli & the Blood Dipped Manor Is OUT NOW! 💜

48 minutes ago by Arkd3v (@arkd3v)

# Hello, everyone! Ark here from Arkd3v Studios!

It's been a while since Otohime, and I'm excited to announce that our newest visual novel, LaurEli & the Blood Dipped Manor, is officially available on Steam and itch.io!

For those who enjoyed Otohime, LaurEli represents the next step in our journey as visual novel developers. We've taken everything we've learned from our previous projects and poured it into a much more ambitious experience!

...

### 🩸 Welcome to the Blood Dipped Manor!

Step into a gothic murder mystery filled with vampires, romance, secrets, and suspicious characters!

Inspired by games like Ace Attorney, LaurEli combines character-driven storytelling with investigation mechanics, challenging you to uncover the truth behind the manor's mysteries.

But be careful...

The vampire killer could be anyone in the manor, even the person next to you.

...

LaurEli is a standalone experience, so you don't need to have played Otohime to enjoy it.

...

fish

...

### 🎮 Available Now!

LaurEli & the Blood Dipped Manor is available for $4.99 on Steam and itch.io!

Check out the game, explore the manor, and see if you can solve its mysteries!

Thank you again for supporting our journey as indie developers. We hope you'll enjoy this next chapter!

With love,

—Ark Arkd3v Studios
