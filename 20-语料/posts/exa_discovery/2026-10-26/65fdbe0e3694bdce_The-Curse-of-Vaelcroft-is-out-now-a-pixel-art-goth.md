# The Curse of Vaelcroft is out now: a pixel-art gothic horror mystery (play the free demo)

A topic by treyz81 created 1 day ago Views: 28

treyz81 1 day ago (1 edit)

The Curse of Vaelcroft is a classic point-and-click adventure in the spirit of the old Sierra games, adapted from the gothic novel by Brad M. Kalensky.

"I write to you in desperation. I dare not sign my name..."

An unsigned letter draws former detective Samuel Darkwater and his partner, Rosalind Hollington, north to Vaelcroft, a remote village where those who touch certain objects stop being themselves. Something there has been waiting for them.

• A complete story across a Prologue, eight chapters, an interlude and an Epilogue

• Hand-crafted pixel art with candlelight, storms, fog and animated cutscenes

• Real detective work: gather clues, then solve seven case boards

• An original 29-track soundtrack, a case score and 24 achievements

The full game is only $4.99 and includes the soundtrack, the original novel, a hint guide, an artbook, three maps, wallpapers and one sealed file you shouldn't open until the end.

Free demo: the Prologue through Chapter IV, playable right in your browser. Your save carries over to the full game.

Runs in any modern browser on Windows, macOS or Linux. No install, works offline.

Play the free demo: https://treyz81.itch.io/the-curse-of-vaelcroft
