## MOGIZ

### Oreum Games | Released Oct 24, 2026

A 3D indie PC game where you are a mosquito in a family home. Bite the cat, the dog, the baby, the high schooler, mom, dad and grandpa in one life. Get swatted and you are reborn in the grass a few seconds later, a little faster and a little smarter.

MOGIZ is a free 3D indie PC game in the browser. You are a mosquito in a family home and you have to drink from all seven of them in one life. You will die a lot. That is how you get stronger.

 Posted by oreumgames on Oct 25th, 2026

...

That buzz right next to your ear. Everyone knows the human side of this. We wanted to play the other side, so in MOGIZ you are the mosquito. You hatch on a swamp in the front yard and slip into the house through a torn window screen.

...

## Made from notes in three days

MOGIZ was made from October 22 to 24. The person who runs our studio does not code. They play each build and tell us what is off, and a lot of the game came from that. The first version forced you to bite in a fixed order and always killed your first mosquito with the high schooler. Both came out after the note that those were just a way of describing difficulty. The mosquito used to sink into people's skin while drinking. Now it stands on the skin and only the needle tip goes in, the blood visibly climbs up the needle, and the bite swells as the itch grows. Every character got solid collision shaped like their actual body, so you cannot fly into the dog anymore. The swamp used to be a red plastic tub.

...

Then the first full run reached the ending in six lives, which was too easy, so every person now needs more blood, itches faster and raises a hand sooner. I also have to own one bug. The high schooler and grandpa could not hit you at all for a while, because I added the swatter's length to their reach even when they were not holding one. Their hands went right past you.

MOGIZ is free in the browser, in English and Korean, made for PC and playable on phones held sideways. Made by a very small indie team in Korea.
