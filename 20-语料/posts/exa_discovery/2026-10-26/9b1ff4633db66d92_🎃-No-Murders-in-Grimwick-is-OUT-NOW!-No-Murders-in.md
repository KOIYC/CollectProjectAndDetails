# 🎃 No Murders in Grimwick is OUT NOW!

4 days ago by Bee & Boo Games (@beeandboogames)

No Murders in Grimwick has officially launched and I'm so excited to finally welcome everyone to Grimwick! 🍂

What started as a little passion project inspired by the point-and-click mysteries I loved growing up has officially become my first released game on Steam and second title on Itch. It's been such a fun (and sometimes scary!) experience bringing this little town and its suspicious residents to life.

If you're new to Grimwick, here's what you can expect:

- 🍂 A cozy autumn harvest festival with a darkly funny mystery.
- 🔎 A suspicious death in a town that insists there are no murders.
- 🎃 Oddball townsfolk, finding and following leads, and a few unexpected twists.
- 🧩 Classic point-and-click investigation without combat or time pressure.

The full game is now available for Windows, macOS, and Linux for $6.99 USD on itch.io and Steam!

And if you'd like to try before you buy, the free demo is still available and will be receiving an update over the next few week to include quality of life features that can already be found in the full release.

I also want to say a huge thank you to everyone who wishlisted the game, tried the demo, shared a post, or offered encouragement along the way. As a solo developer, that support has genuinely meant so much and given me the confidence to follow my development dreams.
