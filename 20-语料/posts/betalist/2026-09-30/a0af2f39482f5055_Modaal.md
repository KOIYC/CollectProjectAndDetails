Modaal
Cross-platform native AI app builder for iOS and Android
Modaal is a cross-platform native AI app builder that lets product people build and ship real iOS and Android apps with their own AI agent. Instead of one React Native codebase drawing the same screens on both phones, Modaal shares the logic, not the screens. Your agent writes real Swift for iPhone and real Kotlin for Android from one project, based on a plan you approve before any code is written. This lets first-time founders or product teams take an idea to both stores with no credit meters or rebuilds.
