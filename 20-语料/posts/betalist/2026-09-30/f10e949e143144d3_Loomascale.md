LoomaScale
Run and audit Google Ads directly from ChatGPT and Claude
LoomaScale connects your Google Ads account to ChatGPT and Claude, letting you analyze and manage campaigns through normal conversation. You can ask it to find wasted spend, audit campaigns, check performance, create new campaigns, adjust budgets, or generate reports. LoomaScale reads live Google Ads data and prepares changes for your approval before anything that affects spend goes live. It is built for founders, marketers, and small agencies who want the power of Google Ads without constantly working inside the Google Ads dashboard.
