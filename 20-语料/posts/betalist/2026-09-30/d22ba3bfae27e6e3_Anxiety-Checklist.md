Anxiety Checklist
Anxiety support 24/7 with science-based tools to build coping skills
Anxiety Checklist brings practical tools for managing anxiety into one app so you can try different approaches and find what helps you. Use the free SOS Panic Button for grounding guidance when panic hits or the free CBT Thought Journal to work through anxious thoughts. Talk things through with an AI CBT Companion, face fears with exposure exercises, or unwind with breathing, meditation, and sleep audio. Check in before and after exercises to see which tools make a difference for you.
