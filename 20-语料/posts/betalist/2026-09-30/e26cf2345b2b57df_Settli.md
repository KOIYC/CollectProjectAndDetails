Settli
Step-by-step relocation guides in your own language
Settli turns relocating into a clear, personalized checklist. Tell it where you are moving from and to, and get step-by-step tasks for visas, registration, taxes, and healthcare, explained in your own language with the local term kept alongside (like BSN, the Dutch social security number). Track documents and deadlines, get reminders, and see only the procedures that apply to you. Available for 10 countries and 15 languages. Currently in early access; join the waitlist to be notified at launch.
