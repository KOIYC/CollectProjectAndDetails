monolayer
Turn a prompt or repo into a live, self-managed system on your AWS
monolayer turns code or intent into a running, self-managed system in your own AWS account. Start from a natural-language prompt or connect a repo, and it assembles frontend, auth, and runtime, validates drift, and deploys to your cloud. After deployment, Autopilot keeps the loop closed by monitoring runtime state and aligning it with your intent while the infrastructure stays within your account. Build faster while maintaining ownership and control in AWS.
