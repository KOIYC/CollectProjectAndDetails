GPT Image 2
Generate and edit photorealistic images with accurate in-image text
GPT Image 2 is an AI image generator that creates and edits high-quality visuals with accurate in-image text. Type a prompt, choose settings like aspect ratio and resolution up to 4K, and generate photorealistic or stylized images in seconds. You can upload images for smart, prompt-based edits, remove backgrounds, and export PNG, JPEG, or WebP files with optional transparency. GPT Image 2 is designed for creators, marketers, and developers who need production-ready visuals quickly and affordably.
