# CyberStock

## Generate data-backed microstock keywords and titles that sell

CyberStock is an AI microstock keywording platform that writes data-backed titles, keywords, and descriptions for photos, videos, and vectors. It is trained on over 50 million real buyer searches using Google Trends and SEMrush signals to match metadata with what buyers actually search for and each marketplace's rules. You can process files in about 1.3 seconds, predict performance with a 0–100 Selling Score, batch thousands to millions of assets, and automate multi-agency uploads via CyberPusher with 0% commission.
