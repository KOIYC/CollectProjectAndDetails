TellMyShop
Manage your PrestaShop or WooCommerce store using Claude or ChatGPT
TellMyShop is a module for PrestaShop and a plugin for WooCommerce that lets store owners manage their stores by chatting with Claude or ChatGPT, including free accounts. Store owners often spend hours on descriptions, SEO fields, promotions, and stock checks. With TellMyShop, you ask in plain words, the chat prepares the change and shows a before/after preview. Nothing is saved until you approve it. It offers 70+ tools, change history with undo, daily database backups, and customer data access off by default. There's a free edition with no time limit; Pro is a one-time license per store.
