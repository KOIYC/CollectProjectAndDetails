Squario
Run sales, projects, time, and margins in one customizable workspace
Squario is an all-in-one operations platform for SMEs that unifies CRM, proposals, project delivery, time tracking, budgets, and profitability in a single workspace. It lets you model your process with custom fields, data models, views, and automations, so teams keep everything in sync without juggling separate tools. Squaria, the built-in AI assistant, answers questions, builds dashboards, and makes changes with your confirmation, respecting your role and permissions.
