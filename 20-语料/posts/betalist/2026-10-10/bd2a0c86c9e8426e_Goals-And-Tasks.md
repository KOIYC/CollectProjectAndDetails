Goals and Tasks
Map goals into actionable tasks and track progress visually
Goals and Tasks is a visual workspace for turning big goals into clear, actionable plans. It lets you break outcomes into sub-goals and leaf tasks, then view the same structure as a radial map, outline, tree, bracket, timeline, or a focused task overlay. You can schedule, set due dates, add recurrence with streaks, and see progress roll up the hierarchy. Start free with core views, then upgrade for unlimited projects, exports, imports, AI sub-goal generation, and more.
