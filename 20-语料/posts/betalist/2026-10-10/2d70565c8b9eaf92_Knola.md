Knola
Turn your course idea into lessons, videos, and a sales page
Knola helps creators, coaches, and consultants build and sell online courses without filming. It drafts your course outline, designs slides, writes scripts, and narrates lesson videos using AI or your own voice. Knola also generates your sales page, funnels, and checkout, and lets you host your school under your brand. You pay once to launch, with optional fees only if you use Knola’s checkout, so you can focus on teaching while it builds everything around your course.
