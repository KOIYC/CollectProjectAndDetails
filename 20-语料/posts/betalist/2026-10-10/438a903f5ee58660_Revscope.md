RevScope
Track which channels and content drive real revenue
RevScope connects your website traffic to your payments, showing exactly which channels, pages, and keywords generate revenue. It attributes each sale to the source and session that caused it, helping you focus on what converts and fix what doesn’t. With funnels, subscription metrics, AI traffic detection, and GDPR-friendly tracking, RevScope reveals the full picture that ad blockers and cookie consent distort. See at a glance where to spend your time and energy to get the largest increase in revenue. Connect it to your favorite AI provider and ask any questions about your data.
Get the growth tier for starter pricing (~45% off) with an annual subscription.
