Save Genie
Free price drop and restock alerts for almost any online store
Save Genie is a free price tracker and wishlist app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Save products from almost any online store using Safari or Chrome, and Genie alerts you when the price drops, the item is back in stock, or it's cheaper elsewhere. You can organize items into collections and share wishlists with family.
