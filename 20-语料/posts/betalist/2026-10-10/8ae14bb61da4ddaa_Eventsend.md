EventSend
Event tracking and routing for solo founders and SaaS teams
EventSend lets product teams track important application events with a single HTTP request and route them to channels they already use. It records events for search and analytics, then sends them to your phone, Slack, Discord, Telegram, and webhooks with deduplication, retries, and delivery visibility. It ingests webhooks from tools like Stripe, GitHub, and Vercel, so no code is required. Alerting features like thresholds, quiet hours, and rollups provide usable signals without noise. It also offers AI assistant support to query event history with questions like "did anything fail in checkout today?" or "what did user cus_Xs4wA do before churning?" A forever free plan is available
