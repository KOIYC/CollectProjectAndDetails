Gordito.io
Get a daily plan that trains, guides, and doesn't accept excuses
Gordito offers a simple daily plan to help you take care of yourself without complications. It provides short home strength routines, real food guides, alcohol control with "passes," and adapts when life gets tough. It doesn't require coaches or tracking every detail, just clear actions with XP-style rewards to keep you engaged. Access it on your phone and follow the day's task to move, eat better, and live lighter with humor.
