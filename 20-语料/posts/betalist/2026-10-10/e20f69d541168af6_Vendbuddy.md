VendBuddy
Find profitable vending locations with direct contacts and close fast
VendBuddy helps vending operators find and close profitable machine locations. Search any US ZIP code to see ranked prospects with vending scores, foot traffic, and estimated revenue, then unlock the decision-maker’s direct email and phone. Build a product plan, analyze margins, choose machines, and generate contracts in minutes. Manage a pipeline, track route performance, and export tax-ready profit and loss reports to place machines faster and grow revenue.
