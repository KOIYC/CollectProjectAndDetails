CV Blender
Build, restyle and ATS-check your CV in one place, free to start
CV Blender lets you write your CV once and switch among nine ATS-safe templates with a click. It renders a live, print-ready PDF as you type, with region-ready layouts for the EU, U.S., Canada, Australia and India. A free ATS checker reads your CV like an applicant tracking system, scores it out of 100 across five categories and lists concrete fixes; the first check needs no account. A built-in AI polishes any field in one click, and a chat assistant tailors your whole CV to a pasted job ad. Customize colors, import an existing CV, and download three PDFs a month for free. One-time passes unlock Word export and higher limits. No subscription, no ads, no card required.
