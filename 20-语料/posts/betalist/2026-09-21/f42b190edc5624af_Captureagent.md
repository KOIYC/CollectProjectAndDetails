CaptureAgent
Streamline and automate government contracting with AI
CaptureAgent streamlines and automates federal contracting from lead discovery to post-award execution. It pulls solicitations from multiple federal procurement sites, scores fit against your company profile, and flags eligibility and compliance. Generate capabilities and full proposal packages with AI in minutes and a few dollars in model usage, then export Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files. After a win, convert awards into projects with schedules, deliverables, and earned value tracking. Manage CMMC and SPRS posture and explore accelerators and investors in one place.
