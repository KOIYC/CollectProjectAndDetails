LongTerMemory
Turn your study materials into quizzes with AI and spaced repetition
LongTerMemory is a web platform that uses AI to generate Q&A and an algorithm to schedule a spaced repetition program. This tool helps you study anything and prepare for exams. It turns your uploaded documents and web links into tailored questions, quizzes, and practice tests using AI. It builds a personalized study plan and schedules reviews with spaced repetition to lock in knowledge. Track performance, target weak areas, and prepare confidently for exams and professional certifications while retaining more in less time.
