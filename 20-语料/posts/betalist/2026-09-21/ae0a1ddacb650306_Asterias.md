Asterias
Answer Google reviews in your voice and save hours every week
Asterias automates replies to Google reviews for local businesses while keeping your tone and control. It connects to your Google listing, reads new reviews in real time, drafts responses that match your writing style, and publishes positive ones automatically. Mixed or negative reviews come through WhatsApp or Telegram for quick approval or edits. It supports multiple languages, sends periodic reports, and requires no installation, helping owner-run locations stay responsive and visible.
