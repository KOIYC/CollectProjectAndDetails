Flomera
Connect documents, devices, and rooms into usable home knowledge
Flomera is an AI-powered operating system for your home. It links rooms, devices, documents, maintenance, and events into a central knowledge layer so you can quickly find answers about your house. You can access invoices, warranties, and manuals, track connected device usage, plan maintenance, and build a clear house history. Flomera works even without smart home devices and emphasizes privacy.
