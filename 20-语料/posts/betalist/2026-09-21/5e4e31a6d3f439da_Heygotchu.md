Heygotchu
Pack smarter with AI outfits and lists from your own closet
Heygotchu helps you pack less and wear more by building outfits and packing lists from the clothes you already own. Snap photos of your wardrobe, and the app detects each item, understands color, warmth, and style, then generates cross-matched looks for trips or 50+ occasions with live weather. It follows rules like hijabi mode and avoids duplicates, suggesting what to pack and what to leave behind. You tap to remix or confirm until every piece earns its place.
