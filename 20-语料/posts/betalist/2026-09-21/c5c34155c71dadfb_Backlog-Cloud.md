Backlog.cloud
Meetings in, delivery artefacts out in the tool your team already uses
Backlog.cloud joins your Zoom, Meet, or Teams call and writes up the output. Uploaded recordings, transcripts, and images work too. It knows 26 ceremony types and produces up to 12 artefact types: user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria, edge cases, dependencies, bugs, tasks, epics, spikes, requirements, RAID and decision logs, action items, minutes, BPMN process models, and a living discovery pack. Every item cites its source quote and a confidence rating. Items push straight into Jira, Azure DevOps, Linear, GitHub, Notion, or ClickUp with fields mapped, or export to files. It eliminates the two hours spent post-workshop turning notes into tickets and docs. Free to start.
