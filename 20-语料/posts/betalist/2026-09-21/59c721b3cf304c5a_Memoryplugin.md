MemoryPlugin BOOSTED
Long term memory for ALL your AI tools
MemoryPlugin is long-term memory for all your favourite AI tools.
AI tools are incredible, but they suffer from amnesia and generic responses. MemoryPlugin is the cure.
MemoryPlugin allows AI tools to remember key facts, and use your conversation history to make AI more personalised and helpful over time.
With the browser extension, custom GPT, MCP server, TypingMind Plugin, and a public OpenAPI spec, MemoryPlugin is designed to work with ALL your AI tools, with dozens already supported. Memory is shared across your tools and features like Buckets help you organize memories for work, personal, or project contexts.
