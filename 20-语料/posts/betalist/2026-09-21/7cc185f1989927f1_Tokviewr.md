Free anonymous TikTok viewer for public profiles, videos, and comments
TokViewr is a browser-based viewer for public TikTok profiles, videos, comments, followers, and following lists. You can browse without a TikTok login, inspect public data anonymously, and export supported datasets to CSV. Public viewing is free; credits are used for optional exports. TokViewr is an independent third-party tool and is not affiliated with TikTok.
