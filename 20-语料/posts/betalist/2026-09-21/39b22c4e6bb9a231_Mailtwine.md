Mailtwine BOOSTED
AI email triage and assistant turning your inbox into an action list
Mailtwine is an AI email triage software and assistant that turns scattered messages into a prioritized queue. It prioritizes, summarizes, routes, and drafts replies while keeping humans in control with approvals before sending. Teams use plain-language routing rules, see thread context and related emails, and move work through clear stages from triage to done. Mailtwine works natively with Gmail, Outlook, Fastmail, IMAP, and Helpmonks. A browser extension makes it easy to access data from anywhere. Use it for support, sales, founders, and operations to keep critical email moving fast.
