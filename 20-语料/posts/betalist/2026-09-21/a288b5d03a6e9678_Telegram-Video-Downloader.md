Telegram Video Downloader for fast and bulk private media downloads
TG Video Downloader is a fast and easy-to-use tool for downloading videos, photos, audio, music, stories, and other media from Telegram. It supports public and accessible private groups and channels with link-based, bulk, and batch downloads. Designed with privacy in mind, TG Video Downloader does not store your downloaded media or personal information.
