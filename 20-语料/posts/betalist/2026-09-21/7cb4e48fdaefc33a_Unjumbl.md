Unjumbl
Snap a photo to get 15-minute decluttering plans for messy rooms
Unjumbl turns a photo of a messy room into a step-by-step decluttering plan divided into 15-minute zones, sorting items into collect, donate, or trash. If you're unsure about an item, Unjumbl asks questions like whether you've used it this year.
Currently in early testing on Android and iOS. Limited seats to become a founding tester, who can generate 20 free room plans.
First 50 users who save progress with email become founding testers and get 20 free room plans
