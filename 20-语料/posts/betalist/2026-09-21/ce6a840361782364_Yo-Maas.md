Yo MaaS
Stop guessing and start growing your business with AI
Yo MaaS is an AI-powered growth operating system that bridges the gap between automated website analytics and hands-on marketing execution. It runs continuous diagnostics across website performance, GEO/SEO structure, mobile experience, and security, translating complex performance data into a "Yo Score" with a prioritized action roadmap.
Beyond diagnosing technical issues, Yo MaaS solves them through an integrated marketplace connecting diagnostic alerts to vetted, on-demand specialists. Built for founders, solo practitioners, boutique agencies, and marketing teams, it delivers fractional agency power and automated growth intelligence without the enterprise price tag.
