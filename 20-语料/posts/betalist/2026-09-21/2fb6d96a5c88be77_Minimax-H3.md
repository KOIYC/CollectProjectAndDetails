MiniMax H3
Create 4K cinematic videos from text, images, clips, and audio with sound
MiniMax H3 is a multimodal AI video generator that turns text, images, video clips, and audio into coherent cinematic videos with native stereo sound. It lets you mix up to 12 references in one pass, maintain character and style consistency, and perform precise instruction-based edits without reshoots. You can generate videos in various aspect ratios up to 2k, clone voices, and keep on-screen text and product details crisp. Start free and iterate like a director using conversational in-chat editing.
