AI3DGen
Convert any image into a textured 3D model in seconds
AI3DGen transforms 2D images and text prompts into production-ready 3D models. Users upload a photo, generate a model in about a minute, and export in GLB, OBJ, or STL for use in Blender, Unity, or Unreal. The platform offers Base, Pro, and Ultra generators that deliver precise geometry reconstruction, clean UVs, and high-fidelity PBR textures. Creators use it for game assets, film, 3D printing, education, and VR/AR, with a free tier and upgrades for priority and commercial rights.
