InviteVia
Create stunning digital invitations and collect RSVPs instantly
InviteVia lets you design and share beautiful digital invitations for weddings, birthdays, and any celebration. Choose from elegant templates, add event details, and publish a single link you can share anywhere. Guests can RSVP in seconds without accounts, including plus-ones and meal preferences, while real-time dashboards track responses. The platform supports 19 languages and offers a simple free start with paid upgrades for more features.
