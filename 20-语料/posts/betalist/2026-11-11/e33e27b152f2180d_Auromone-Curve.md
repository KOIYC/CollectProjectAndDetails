Auromone Curve
A wristband designed to read your cortisol from sweat all day
Visit website
Save
 Saved
Auromone is building the Curve, a wellness wristband designed to read cortisol, the hormone behind energy, sleep, and stress, from the trace of sweat on the wrist continuously. Most wearables estimate stress from heart rate; the Curve is designed to measure the hormone itself, so you can see your daily cortisol pattern and how training, sleep, and travel affect it. It is in development and will publish no accuracy figures until validated against a laboratory reference. It does not detect, diagnose, monitor, or manage any condition. Reserving is free, with no card and no deposit.
