RemoteSup provides remote support from one screen for office PCs, field PCs, smartphones, and smart glasses. Technicians connect via the Connect app or directly from a browser without installation, with end-to-end encrypted sessions that never store media on servers. It supports crisp remote control, unattended access at sign-in, multi-participant sessions, automatic on-device recording, file transfer, clipboard sync, chat, voice, and on-screen pointing, all without account signup or cloud-stored device lists.
