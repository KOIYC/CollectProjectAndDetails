Bharat Software Solutions offers AI-powered Transport Management Software for Indian transport and logistics businesses. It centralizes booking, dispatch, fleet, GPS tracking, fuel, maintenance, billing, accounting, and analytics to reduce costs and boost productivity. With over 210 features and 22 integrated APIs like FASTag, Vahan, and DigiLocker, it helps manage trips, drivers, invoices, and compliance while providing real-time visibility and data-driven insights.
