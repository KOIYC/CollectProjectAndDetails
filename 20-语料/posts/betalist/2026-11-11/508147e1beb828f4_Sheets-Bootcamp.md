Sheets Bootcamp teaches Google Sheets through interactive, step-by-step lessons and free tutorials. Lessons start with a short video, then you type formulas into a live grid that checks your work and offers hints. The course focuses on essential skills like lookups, logic, QUERY, data cleaning, and charts, guiding you to build practical projects like a budget, dashboard, and mini-CRM. Unit 1 is free; full access is available via subscription or lifetime purchase.
