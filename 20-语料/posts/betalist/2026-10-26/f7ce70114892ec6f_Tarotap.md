Tarotap
Free AI tarot readings with illustrated decks and no signup
Tarotap is an AI tarot reading platform for questions about love, career, or life decisions. Type your question, draw cards, and get an AI tarot reading. Choose from several illustrated decks, including a cat deck and a witch deck. Try pick-a-card, yes/no, love tarot, and yearly forecast readings. Free readings require no signup on the web or Android app. A premium subscription unlocks more readings and features.
