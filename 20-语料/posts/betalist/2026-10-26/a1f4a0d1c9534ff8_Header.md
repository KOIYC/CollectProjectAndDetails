Header
Notes, meeting memory, and research in one Mac app
Header is a Mac app for notes, meetings, and research that saves your work as plain Markdown files. Keep them on your Mac or in folders synced through iCloud Drive or Dropbox. You can open and edit your files without Header. Write notes, save web pages, and turn meeting recordings into summaries, decisions, and action items. Link related notes, explore their connections visually, and see tasks and reminders on your Today page. Keep sources alongside your research and export your work to PDF, web, Word, or PowerPoint. For AI assistance, use local models or your cloud provider to ask questions about your notes and suggest edits, which you review before applying.
