Angebotsmeister
Speak the job to send a quote using voice-to-quote for German tradespeople
Angebotsmeister turns what a tradesperson says on site into a quote with proper line items and prices that show their sources, plus a PDF the customer can accept online. One tap then creates the invoice. It is designed for German trades, in German, and is free to start.
