Tancky AI lets you seamlessly merge multiple photos into a single, natural-looking image. Powered by generative vision, it analyzes subjects, lighting, depth, and perspective to harmonize shadows and edges without manual masking. Upload 2–4 images, describe the composition, choose an aspect ratio, and generate high-resolution results suitable for portraits, e-commerce backdrops, and concept visuals. Creators and teams can produce studio-grade composites in seconds with full commercial rights.
