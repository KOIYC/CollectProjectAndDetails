trimy
AI campaign analytics for your links
Most link shorteners only give you a click count. trimy.io provides a verdict.
trimy.io is an AI-powered link analytics platform that offers short links, QR codes, UTM tracking, and link-in-bio pages. It actively analyzes performance with a Campaign Health Score (0-100) and tips to improve, an A/B Test Interpreter that explains which variant won and why, analytics narratives in plain English, and an AI chat that answers questions about your click data.
It is built for SMB marketing teams and agencies running multiple client campaigns. The free plan includes 30 links per month, 1 custom domain, and AI analysis with no credit card required.
