The Final Tape
Audit your trades with AI to find and fix costly mistakes
The Final Tape is a trading journal and performance analysis platform that turns your trade history into actionable forensics. Its AI Council, made up of eight specialized agents, reconstructs completed trades, debates findings, and delivers a ranked Kill List of fixes quantified by preventable monthly dollars. Traders use modules like Trading Floor, Monte Carlo, Exit Oracle, and Quant Lab to review execution, psychology, risk, and setups, then follow a step-by-step plan to recover their edge with data-backed decisions.
