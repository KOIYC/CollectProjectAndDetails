Bamigos
AI photo booths for sale and rental, arcade games and brand experiences
Bamigos manufactures AI photo booths for events, brands, and venues. Their flagship Pikcha combines AI portraits, instant prints, and digital sharing for weddings, corporate events, and retail activations.
They offer photo booth sales and rentals and operate installations with select partners. They also build arcade and interactive games for family entertainment centers and custom branded games for app downloads, lead generation, and customer engagement. Based in India, they develop hardware, software, and games, combining sales, rentals, custom projects, and recurring operating revenue. They welcome investors and partners to expand internationally.
