Moonship
Turn analytics into action with intelligent signals and A/B testing
Moonship is a product analytics and experimentation platform that helps teams understand user behavior, uncover meaningful signals, and improve their products. The signal engine monitors your analytics for spikes, drops, trends, and opportunities you might otherwise miss. Instead of digging through dashboards for answers, Moonship shows you where to look.
When something is worth exploring, built-in A/B testing lets you turn insights into experiments, measure results, and decide what to do next.
