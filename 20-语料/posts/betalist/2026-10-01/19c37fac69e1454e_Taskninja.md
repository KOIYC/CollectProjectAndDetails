TaskNinja
A free task manager for to-dos, meetings, teams, and AI integration
TaskNinja brings your to-dos, meeting notes, and team members into one focused workspace. It's completely free.
You get tasks with priorities, due dates, subtasks, recurring tasks, and dependencies, viewable as lists, boards, or calendars. Connect colleagues and assign tasks that update in real time. Capture meeting notes and turn action items into tasks. Track what you've delegated and prepare for 1:1s. Get a calendar feed of due dates. Tasks are private by default, visible only when you choose to share. It also includes an MCP Server for AI integration.
