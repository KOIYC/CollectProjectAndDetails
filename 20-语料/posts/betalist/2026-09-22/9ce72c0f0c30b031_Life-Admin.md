life.admin
Organize your head, heart, and pocket in one calm place
life.admin is a calm personal life-planning app for the practical, emotional, and financial parts of everyday life. It brings together tasks, wellbeing, relationships, money, learning, purpose, home, and work across three simple areas: Head, Heart, and Pocket. Choose what deserves your attention today, keep useful plans and reflections in one place, and stay connected to the things that matter without turning every part of life into another productivity system.
I'm a social worker in Australia and built life.admin as an independent maker after wondering whether the different parts of everyday life could live together somewhere simpler, calmer, and more human.
