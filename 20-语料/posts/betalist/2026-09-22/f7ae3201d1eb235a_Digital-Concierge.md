Digital Concierge
Turn Instagram comments into on-brand replies and audience insights
Digital Concierge helps creators and brands manage Instagram comments with an AI that drafts on-brand replies, flags risks, and shows what audiences really feel. It reads every comment, runs a brand-safety check, and lets you approve, edit, or auto-send confident responses. The platform highlights sentiment trends, identifies personas and super-fans, and detects buying signals like purchase intent or shipping questions. Optional audience graphs reveal connectors and influence in your community.
