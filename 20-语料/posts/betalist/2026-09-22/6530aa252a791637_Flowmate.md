## Workflows made simple, no technical skills needed

You already use Claude every day for sales, support, or ops. FlowMate lets it run those workflows instead of just answering questions. Describe a process once in plain language, and Claude carries it through every app you use when you ask or a trigger fires. Unlike n8n, Make, or Zapier, there's no graph to build, no fields to map, and no technical skills needed. You can start right away, straight from a chat with Claude.
