Privacy Requests
Manage DSARs from intake to delivery in one guided workspace
Privacy Requests helps startups and growing teams manage data subject access requests from a single, organized workspace. It combines a hosted intake portal, identity verification, case ownership, deadline tracking, evidence tasks, approvals, and secure delivery via expiring links. Teams keep requesters informed, route follow-ups to the right people, and maintain an audit-ready history. It supports GDPR and CCPA/CPRA workflows without automating legal decisions or data discovery across systems.
