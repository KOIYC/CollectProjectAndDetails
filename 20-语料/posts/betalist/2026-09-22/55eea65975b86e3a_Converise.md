Converise
Manage and judge experiments with warehouse-backed evidence in one place
Converise centralizes experimentation so teams can plan, analyze, decide, and remember which tests actually moved the business. It connects to BigQuery, Databricks, and Snowflake to run SQL in your warehouse, refresh results on a schedule, apply quality checks, and surface recommendations with risks and confidence. Track hypotheses, variants, KPIs, guardrails, overlaps, and decisions, then log outcomes to avoid rerunning losing ideas and ship with clarity.
