LVLup Props
Find and research better bets and compete peer to peer
LVLup is a betting research platform that helps sports bettors discover and analyze betting opportunities faster. Explore NFL, NBA, and MLB player props using hit rates, matchup data, opportunity trends, cheatsheets, LVLup Scores, and advanced analytics. Compare bets across major sportsbooks, track your plays, follow top users, and compete in peer-to-peer Survivor, Pick'em, and playoff pool contests.
LVLup is the ultimate betting companion app. For our NFL launch, Premium Plus is free through September 28, and we’re running $2,500 in free NFL contests.
