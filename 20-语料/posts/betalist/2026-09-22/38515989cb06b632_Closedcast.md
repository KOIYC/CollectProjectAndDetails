ClosedCast
Create a private streaming video archive and watch on Roku
ClosedCast provides a private video streaming platform that lets you organize important recordings into a controlled archive and share them with specific people. You upload videos, group them into Collections, and manage access with invitations and optional expirations. Viewers get a clean streaming experience on the web or Roku without public feeds or ads. ClosedCast emphasizes ownership, exportability, and protected delivery so your videos stay watchable and easy to revisit over time.
