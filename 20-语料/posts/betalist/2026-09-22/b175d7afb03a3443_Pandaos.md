PandaOS
Run code, tools, and AI agents together in a single local workspace
PandaOS is a local AI workstation that unifies your AI subscriptions, code, tools, external apps, and AI agents in one desktop-first environment. It lets you manage multiple projects, preserve terminal and browser state, and create reusable agent workflows. You can switch AI keys to bypass session limits and build with persistent project memory tied to your stack. Use it to execute end-to-end workflows from inbox to deployment so you can ship faster without context switching.
