## 正文

ReClaim Miles: Rebuild IRS-compliant mileage logs offline | BetaList

ReClaim Miles is a Windows desktop app that helps US business owners, contractors, and accountants reconstruct IRS-compliant mileage logs without real-time GPS tracking. It uses odometer readings from service receipts, road network data, and fuel/EV physics to backfill missed months and generate clean PDF summaries. The app keeps all data local for privacy, avoids battery drain, and offers lifetime licenses for freelancers, small businesses, and CPAs.
