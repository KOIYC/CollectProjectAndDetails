Pasiv
Turn your idle PC into crypto paid directly to your wallet
Pasiv lets you earn crypto by mining with hardware you already have. Install the desktop app, paste a payout address, and start mining coins like Monero, Pearl, Verus, Ravencoin, Ergo, Zephyr, and Salvium using your CPU or NVIDIA GPU. The app auto-detects what your machine can handle, runs in the background, and pays you directly from the pool without accounts or custody. Pasiv charges a 4% fee on Monero only and 0% on other coins, with transparent on-chain fee logging.
