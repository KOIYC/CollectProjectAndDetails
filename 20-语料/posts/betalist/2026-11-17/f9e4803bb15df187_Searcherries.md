Searcherries is an AI SEO platform for small businesses, SaaS, and marketing agencies. It tracks your brand and competitors across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Grok, showing which sources each platform cites. It checks every answer about your brand for negative mentions and drawbacks. Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and Google Analytics add your SEO performance and real AI search traffic.
All this data goes into Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, or any other MCP client, where your agent analyzes it and sends content and technical SEO fixes. It works in any country and language. Plans start at $10 a month, with a 7-day trial.
