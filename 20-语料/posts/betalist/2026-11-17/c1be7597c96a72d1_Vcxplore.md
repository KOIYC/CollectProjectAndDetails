VCXplore analyzes your startup's website and ranks over 10,000 investors worldwide, including VCs, private equity funds, angel groups, family offices and corporate venture arms, by how closely their portfolios resemble your company. For each investor you see the portfolio companies most similar to yours and a breakdown of the match by area. The ranking is deterministic: the same inputs give the same list. Your top 3 matches are free, no card needed, and most reports are ready within two hours. The full report of 100 investors is a one-time €29.99. Questions and feedback: hello@vcxplore.com .
