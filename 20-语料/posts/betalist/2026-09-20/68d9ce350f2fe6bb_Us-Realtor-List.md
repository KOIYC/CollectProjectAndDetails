US Realtor List: Buy verified realtor emails by state with | BetaList

US Realtor List sells state and city CSV lists of U.S. real estate agents, where every row includes a published, current email. The database is rebuilt weekly from public brokerage profiles across major brands, so counts match reachable contacts and stale records drop out. You can preview 25 real rows for any state before paying, then purchase a one-time download via Stripe with transparent pricing based on list size. Files include name, email, phone, brokerage, city, state, and optional award tiers.
