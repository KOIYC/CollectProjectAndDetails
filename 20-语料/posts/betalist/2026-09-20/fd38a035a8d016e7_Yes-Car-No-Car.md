## Check a VIN and get a clear Good Car or Bad Car verdict

Yes Car No Car turns a used car's VIN into a plain-English verdict: Good Car or Bad Car before you buy. It combines the specific car's history, including accidents, title, odometer, and open recalls, with that model's known problems and real repair costs, with no dealer conflicts of interest. It's built for everyday buyers who want a straight answer, not a sales pitch.
