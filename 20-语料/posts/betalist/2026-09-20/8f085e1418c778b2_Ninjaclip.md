## Edit videos in the browser with automatic cuts, captions, and export

NinjaClip is a browser-based video editor that automates tedious tasks so you can publish quickly. It cuts pauses in real time without uploads, detects recording errors, generates synced captions, reframes to 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, and 16:9 formats, and adjusts audio and color. You choose features, review AI suggestions, and export in 1080p. Pause cutting is free and requires no signup; only audio is sent for transcription as per the privacy policy.
