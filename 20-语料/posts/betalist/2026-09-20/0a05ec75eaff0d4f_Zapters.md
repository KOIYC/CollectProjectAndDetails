## Create animated microdrama episodes on your phone with AI

Zapters is an AI microdrama studio that lets solo storytellers create short-form animated episodes and ongoing series from their phone. Start with Storymode to shape your premise, characters, locations, and script, then direct each zapter with beats, dialogue, and shots. Choose visual styles like anime or 3D, generate scenes, add voices and music, and export vertical videos. Keep your cast and world consistent across episodes and grow a show your audience returns to.
