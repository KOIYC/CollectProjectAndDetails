## Plan trips with expert-backed AI that validates and optimizes choices

FortripAI is travel decision infrastructure for complex trips. It helps travelers turn conversations into structured multi-city itineraries, then validate, optimize, and book them with real-world constraints in mind. FortripAI compares city sequences, transportation options, hotel combinations, transfer times, travel dates, and total trip cost while flagging hidden risks before booking. Travelers can book hotels, tours, activities, transportation, and destination experiences directly through the platform. For travel businesses, FortripAI breaks finished itineraries into bookable, supplier-ready units to support faster quoting and smoother operations.
