## Validate and shape your idea with a private AI advisory board

EntreDash helps you turn a spark into a protectable, buildable business. The platform uses over 10 AI agents across leading models to pressure-test your idea, search global patent records, pull market data, and deliver a cited, action-ready memo with a clear verdict. You record your idea, work through guided diagnostics, and get next steps on product, IP, and business. An Education Center teaches each stage, and a patent-practitioner directory helps you find counsel when you're ready.
