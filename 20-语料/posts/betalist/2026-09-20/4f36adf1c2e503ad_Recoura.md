## Recover failed Stripe and Square payments with manual and automated dunning

Recoura offers manual and automatic recovery of failed payments for revenue that Stripe and Square give up on. It includes custom email templates, multi-currency support, automatic and manual dunning sequences, an AI recovery assistant, and analytics to help with recovery campaigns. It supports failed invoices, subscriptions, and one-off payments, and is one of the few tools supporting Square.

Failed payments cause churn that many SaaS teams don't track and are easy to fix. Recoura helps recover hidden revenue with automation and a personal touch for important accounts.
