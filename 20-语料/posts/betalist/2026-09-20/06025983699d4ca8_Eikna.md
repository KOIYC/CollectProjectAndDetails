## Discover trusted romance reads by mood, trope, heat, and vibe

EIKNA is a human-reviewed romance discovery platform that helps you find books based on what matters to you. Editors read and evaluate every title, then map it by mood, trope, heat, pacing, and representation so recommendations fit your taste rather than popularity trends. You can browse curated lesbian and sapphic collections, explore by trope or vibe, ask Lyra for suggestions, and create a free account to save books, track reading, and refine your personal Reader DNA. EIKNA's editors also review and recommend lesbian and sapphic romance at LesbianRomanceReads.com, a companion site for readers who want deeper takes on specific titles.
