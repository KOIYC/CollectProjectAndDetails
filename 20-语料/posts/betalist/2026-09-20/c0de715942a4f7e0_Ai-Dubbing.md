## Translate videos into 20+ languages with natural, voice-aware dubbing

AI Dubbing lets you translate videos into over 20 languages while preserving speaker character and timing. Upload a short MP4, WebM, or MOV file, auto-detect the source language, and generate a new voice track aligned to the original pacing. Review the full multilingual result in your browser, then download the dubbed video. Creators, educators, and teams use it to localize interviews, tutorials, and product clips without rebuilding edits, paying by the second of processed video.
