## Turn screenshots into editable elements, drag, drop, and restyle on mobile

Edit Everything lets you drop in screenshots and turns them into editable elements so you can directly interact and use AI control to modify any part. You can rewrite text, drag and drop, reshape elements, or restyle the whole scene using a prompt, then export in the format you need. Create visuals based on existing designs or modify visuals if screenshots aren't suited for presentation or marketing. Refine visuals quickly and keep outputs on brand. It’s fully supported on mobile, so you can create anywhere using interactive and AI controls.
