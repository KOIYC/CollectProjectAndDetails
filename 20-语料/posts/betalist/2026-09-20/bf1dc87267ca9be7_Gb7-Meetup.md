## Meet locals and travelers, join events, and connect around interests

GB7 Meetup helps you meet people in the real world without the pressure of dating. Create a profile, choose interests and a location radius, then discover locals, travelers, events, and community posts on an interactive map. Start private or group chats, join event conversations, and make plans. The platform emphasizes safety with approximate locations, reporting and blocking, email verification, two-factor authentication, and clear community guidelines so you can connect with confidence.
