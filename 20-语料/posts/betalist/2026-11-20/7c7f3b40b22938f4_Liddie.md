Liddie is a crypto payment gateway designed for internet businesses, freelancers, and creators who want to accept cryptocurrency without running nodes or managing complex infrastructure. It supports 23 coins across 13 networks—including BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, USDT, and USDC—via no-code payment links, embeddable widgets, or a clean REST API with signed webhooks. Each order gets a unique deposit address verified on-chain before credit, with built-in wrong-network rescue, configurable underpayment and overpayment tolerances, and a 7-language hosted checkout.
