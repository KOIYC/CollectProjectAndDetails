Whereon
Track and improve your local business visibility in AI answers
Whereon helps local businesses see how often AI assistants recommend them and what to fix next. It checks platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Gemini, then compiles a visibility report with cited sources, competitor comparisons, and a prioritized action plan. Businesses add their location and services, review findings tied to real answers, and follow clear steps to strengthen proof and listings while Whereon measures impact over time.
