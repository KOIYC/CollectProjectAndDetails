Sekondo
Your next Adobe tool run by talking to it
Sekondo connects ChatGPT or Claude to Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, Premiere Pro, and Lightroom Classic. You tell it what you need, typed or dictated, and it works inside your own files with real layers, text, and styles you can keep editing. It doesn't generate separate images; it works on the real file, with you in charge.
In demos, it translates a 12-page brochure in InDesign in seven seconds and retouches 124 jewelry photos in Photoshop in one second each. Built by a graphic designer since 1992, it runs on macOS and Windows with your Claude or ChatGPT subscription. Ships in October 2026; reserving a seat is free.
