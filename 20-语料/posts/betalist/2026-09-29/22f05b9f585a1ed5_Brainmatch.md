BrainMatch
Know yourself first, a relationship app with no swiping or games
BrainMatch is a know-yourself-first relationship app created by Zach Lafer (2025 SDSU finance grad, Fowler Scholar in ethical business, ZIP Launchpad alum). Every question you answer lights up a spot on your own cartoon brain map. There are no scores, rankings, or gamification—just honest reflection, a private journal, a sealed letter to your future partner, and themed rooms with nightly questions for couples. Beta testing starts in two weeks and it's free during beta.
