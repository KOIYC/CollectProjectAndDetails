Papaya
Monitor and auto-optimize your AI agents from trace to fix
Papaya helps teams monitor and improve AI agents at scale. It ingests production traces from prompts, context, tools, models, and outcomes, then runs hundreds of analyses to find high-impact fixes. Findings are ranked by expected gains in quality, latency, and cost, supported by evidence from your own traffic. Use its SDK or connect existing observability data, review recommendations with human control, get Slack alerts, and ship changes via opened PRs you can merge with confidence.
