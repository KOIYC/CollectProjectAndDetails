AI Chatbot by Elfsight
Add an AI chat widget that learns your site and converts visitors
AI Chatbot by Elfsight offers an AI-powered website widget that answers visitor questions using your site content and uploaded files. It helps drive actions like capturing leads, taking bookings, or closing sales. You set plain-language rules to control its behavior, define off-limit topics, and guide how it handles pricing or unhappy customers. It logs every conversation with summaries, tracks unanswered questions, and installs with a single snippet. It offers simple monthly plans without per-seat fees.
