InfraGrid 3D
Civil engineering design in-browser with native BIM export
InfraGrid 3D lets civil and MEP teams design underground services and construction-grade roads in the browser. Users can verify routes with live long sections and clash detection, then export native geometry to Revit, Civil 3D, or AutoCAD. It supports pipe banks, drainage, lighting, grading, and LOD 400+ road components, with rapid IFC/DXF input/output and a Revit plugin for live sync. Use it standalone or inside Revit to route, coordinate, and deliver models faster while keeping elements native for downstream drawings.
