Furlyo
Run your grooming or daycare shop with unified booking, CRM, and POS
Furlyo is an all-in-one platform for pet grooming and daycare shops that replaces fragmented tools with a single pet-centric workflow. It offers 2-tap online booking with deposits to reduce no-shows, a CRM that stores full pet history, notes, and photos, integrated retail POS, automated marketing, and real-time analytics. Teams manage calendars, check-ins via QR codes, payments, and review requests in one place, while integrations with Stripe, Xero, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, and Mailchimp keep operations in sync.
