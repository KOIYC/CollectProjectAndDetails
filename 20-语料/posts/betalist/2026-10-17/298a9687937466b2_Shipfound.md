Shipfound
Get your product named by ChatGPT, Claude Code, or Codex
Shipfound turns Claude Code or Codex into your growth engineer. It audits your site and accounts it can access, then handles the work: fixing your site for Google and AI search, creating content pages and indexing as pull requests, and filling in directory listings for you to submit. It counts visitors and AI crawlers like GPTBot and PerplexityBot, asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, and Claude your buyers' questions to see if they name you, and checks every change on your live site. Plans include a free option, Starter at $49/month, and Growth at $99/month.
