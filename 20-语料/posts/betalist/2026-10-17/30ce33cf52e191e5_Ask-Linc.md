Ask Linc
AI for personal finance with zero tolerance for made-up math
Ask Linc is an AI for personal finance that does not allow made-up math. Connect your finances, ask questions, and explore decisions like retiring early, buying a home, or changing your spending. Ask Linc builds a financial model based on your situation, lets you change assumptions, and shows how outcomes change. It also shows its work so you can check the assumptions, calculations, and data behind every answer instead of blindly trusting AI.
