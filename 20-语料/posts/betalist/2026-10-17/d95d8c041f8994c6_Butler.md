Butler
Delegate bookings to a concierge that verifies every detail
Butler helps you hand off reservations and appointments with confidence. Tell Butler what you need in natural language, review a clear brief, and let a human concierge handle provider-facing details. The service keeps you updated until the arrangement is verified and confirmed. Use it for dining, care appointments, personal time, and travel across the United States, Australia, and Singapore. Membership is complimentary during prelaunch, with clear approval before any future billing.
