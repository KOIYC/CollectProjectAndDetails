QuotaBird
Run quick sales checks that reveal pipeline gaps and next steps
QuotaBird offers simple browser-based tools that help sellers and sales managers stress-test pipeline, deals, and coverage. Built around calculators like Pipeline Check and Deal Check, it asks a few precise questions, shows its math, and delivers a clear verdict with concrete actions. Nothing leaves your device and there’s no account or CRM connection. Created by Mark Flournoy, it serves teams in cloud, AI, cyber, and data, focusing on government sales and practical planning.
