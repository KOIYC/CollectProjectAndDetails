Aluseg
Manage rentals in Portugal with contracts, rents, and tax tasks
Aluseg helps individual landlords in Portugal manage their rentals legally and without paperwork. Letting a property in Portugal involves recurring obligations toward the tax authority (AT), including registering the lease (Modelo 2), issuing monthly electronic rent receipts, paying stamp duty, and applying the annual rent update on time.
