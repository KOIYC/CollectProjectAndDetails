Jaah
Delegate engineering work to AI agents with reviews, records, and rules
Coding agents write the code. Jaah makes it production-grade. It lets teams delegate engineering and related work to AI agents while keeping control over processes and outcomes. Jaah uses the same coding agent with added guardrails like reviews, audit records, permissions, and workflow rules that integrate with tools such as Jira and Slack. It sizes work, schedules recurring tasks, enforces standards across repositories, and returns merged, reviewed changes with reports. Jaah supports startups to enterprises on machines you choose.
