## Run a private sports prediction league with friends, no money involved

BeTeam is a social sports prediction app that lets friend groups create private leagues, predict match scores, and compete on a live leaderboard. You pick competitions like the Premier League, Champions League, LaLiga, NBA, and more, then submit predictions before kickoff while the app handles scoring and standings automatically. Chat and reactions keep the banter in one place, and strict lock-in and transparent history keep it fair. It’s free to play with no deposits or cash prizes.
