## Upload scans or lab reports and get instant plain-language answers

MyResultLab helps patients understand X-rays, CT scans, MRI scans, ultrasounds, and lab reports in clear language. Upload your images or reports to receive an AI-generated explanation of findings, key terms, and questions to discuss with your doctor. The platform uses Google MedGemma 1.5 for multimodal medical understanding with GPT for reasoning and summarization. Data is encrypted, can be deleted anytime, and you can ask unlimited follow-up questions for clarity.
