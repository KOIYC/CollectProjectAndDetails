## Create images, video, and music with 100+ AI models in one place

BudgetPixel is an AI creative suite that unifies image, video, and audio generation under one account and credit system. Use 100+ models like Flux, Midjourney, Kling, Veo, Qwen, and SeeDream to generate and edit visuals and sound, with pro tools for inpainting, ControlNet, upscaling, timelines, and audio mixing. Access AI companions, specialized apps like face swap and virtual try-on, and train custom LoRA models. Start free, pay as you go, and use outputs commercially.
