## Automate end-to-end testing with autonomous AI that maintains tests

Testifly is an AI-powered end-to-end testing platform that discovers your app’s user flows, generates and maintains tests automatically, and runs across every environment and release. It connects to tools like Jira, Linear, Notion, and Slack, heals brittle tests when UIs change, and provides root-cause reports with session recordings. Teams reduce testing time, catch regressions before production, and scale QA without writing or maintaining test cases.
