## Professional network with no ads, algorithm, or commission

DOER is a professional network built for people who get things done. It has no ads, no algorithm, and zero commission on what members earn. It focuses on four pillars: connect with profiles, groups, an activity feed, and messaging with no sponsored or boosted content. Learn by taking and publishing courses and running your own room as a mentor. Earn by offering services, products, and coaching to other members, keeping 100% of what you charge, minus Stripe fees. Build by listing your business, posting open roles, and finding customers. It's free to join, with paid tiers only for those who want to earn, and you pay nothing until you've earned double the membership through DOER. Built solo.

 Scott Barker
