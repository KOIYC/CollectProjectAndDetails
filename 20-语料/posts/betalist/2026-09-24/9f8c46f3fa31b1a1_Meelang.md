MeeLang
Translate Google Meet live with instant AI subtitles in your language
MeeLang is a Chrome extension that turns live speech in Google Meet into instant AI-translated subtitles so you can follow every word in your own language. It delivers sub-second latency, provides context-aware translation, and keeps bilingual transcripts organized locally in your browser for privacy. Install in one click with no account, join your meeting, and read along live.
