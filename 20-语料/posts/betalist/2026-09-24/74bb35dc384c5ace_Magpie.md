Magpie
Use AI agents to find, qualify, and prepare outreach to ideal customers
Magpie delivers AI-powered B2B customer intelligence to help you discover and qualify companies most likely to buy. It researches your ideal customer profile across multiple sources, verifies contacts, and drafts personalized outreach in your voice while showing evidence behind every claim. Magpie uses autonomous agents for research, qualification, and outreach with controls for suggest, approve, or auto modes. It enforces privacy-by-design and jurisdiction-aware communication controls.
