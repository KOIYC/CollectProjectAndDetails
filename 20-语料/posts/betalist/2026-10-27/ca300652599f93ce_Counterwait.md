CounterWait
Earn screen time by exercising and completing chores
CounterWait is an iPhone app that locks apps you choose and lets you earn minutes back by exercising and finishing tasks. The camera counts push-ups, squats, and jumping jacks on the phone, while chores and routines get checked off by a parent or accountability buddy. Use it alone, as a family, or with a friend keeping you honest.
There are no servers, ads, or tracking; everything stays on your phones and in your own iCloud. The beta is free on TestFlight for testers 13 or older.
