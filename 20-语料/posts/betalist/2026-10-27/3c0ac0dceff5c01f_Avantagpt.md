AvantaGPT
Unrestricted AI built for developers and cybersecurity researchers
AvantaGPT is designed for technical users who want AI integrated into their real workflow, not just another chatbot. Its combination of unrestricted AI, an OpenAI-compatible API, and OpenCode integration lets AI work alongside real codebases and development environments rather than being limited to conversation.
Unlike general AI tools, AvantaGPT focuses on developers, cybersecurity researchers, Linux users, and technical power users who need more flexibility and control. The platform is publicly available with no waiting list and evolves based on user feedback.
