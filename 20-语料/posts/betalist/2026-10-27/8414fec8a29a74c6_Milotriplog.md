MilO
Track business mileage on Android automatically via Bluetooth
MilO is an Android mileage tracker that logs trips automatically when your phone connects to your work vehicle’s Bluetooth. It records routes with GPS, classifies drives as Business or Personal based on work hours, and looks up start and end addresses. You can edit trips, add labels for purpose, manage multiple vehicles, and generate monthly mileage reports as PDF and CSV. MilO keeps data on your phone with no accounts or servers, and sends reports via your email app.
