ClipSeek
Find scenes in videos and photos using one sentence
ClipSeek helps video editors and creators find specific moments in their videos and photos. Describe a scene, like “someone running on a beach at sunset,” to find matching video frames and photos without relying on filenames or manual tags.
Indexing and search run locally on your Mac or Windows PC without uploading your original media. You can also search with a reference image or connect AI assistants like Claude, Codex, and Cursor through MCP to search and organize your library.
