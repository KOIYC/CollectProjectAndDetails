The Launch Directory
File your startup and keep its launch permanently on record
The Launch Directory is a weekly, permanent record of product and startup launches. Founders submit a launch to get a dated, searchable page that search engines and AI assistants can read and cite long after launch week. The site includes sections, archives, community-voted leaderboards, and a paid billboard for added visibility. Developers can integrate via an MCP connector and API to search launches, compare alternatives, and manage submissions in chat clients.
