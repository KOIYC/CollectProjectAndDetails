Marsell
Create content, run marketing, and find buyers on autopilot
Marsell runs content, marketing, and sales on autopilot for solo founders and small to medium businesses. It learns your business, voice, and visual style, then plans and creates posts, articles, emails, images, carousels, films, and landing pages. It publishes to connected channels, finds prospects, and prepares outreach and follow-ups. Teams can share organizations with admin, editor, and viewer roles, inspect the work, and keep selected actions for approval. Paid plans start at $49/month. Try the interactive demo: https://getmarsell.com/app/demo
