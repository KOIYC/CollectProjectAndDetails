StampyWallet
Stamp loyalty cards in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet without an app
StampyWallet replaces cardboard loyalty cards with digital ones in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, requiring no app for you or your customers. Customers scan a QR code at your counter and tap "Add" to get the card in their Wallet. At each visit, you scan it from your phone with no tablet or reader needed. Features include stamps, tiers or points, customizable card designs, lock screen messages to encourage returns, Google review requests, and a dashboard showing customer activity. Ideal for cafés, bakeries, restaurants, beauty salons, and neighborhood shops. Offers a 30-day free trial.
