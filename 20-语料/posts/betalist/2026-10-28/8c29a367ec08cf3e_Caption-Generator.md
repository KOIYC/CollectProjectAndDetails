Caption Generator
Add accurate, styled captions to any video in your browser
Caption lets you add accurate, styled captions to videos without installing anything. It runs entirely in your browser, keeping your video on your device while sending only a small compressed audio track for transcription. You can edit every word, control pacing, choose social-ready styles, and export SRT/VTT for free or burn captions into MP4. Caption supports popular video and audio formats and offers free, no-signup use with paid plans for higher-resolution, watermark-free exports.
