MyValuesMap
A guided app that turns your values into a compass any AI can follow
MyValuesMap is a guided ten-step app that helps you understand how you think and what you value. You select and prioritize your values, reflect on where they appear in your life, and end with a personal compass including your purpose, principles, daily guidelines, and instructions for AI. You can give the compass to any AI to align its behavior with you and build trust. It uses on-device AI by default, but you can bring your own key for a cloud model. Your work stays on your device. Coming soon to iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.
