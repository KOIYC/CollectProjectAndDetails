Unwind with voice-controlled games
Want something fun to do that doesn’t involve staring at a screen? Spellfort is an iPhone app featuring trivia and mini-games you play with your voice. Tap once to start, then lock your screen and close your eyes. The game gives you audio prompts, and you answer out loud. It's a new way to wrap up your day without another hour of doomscrolling.
