Timefolk
Schedule in-person sessions with ID-verified local Experts in public places
Timefolk is a peer-to-peer marketplace for practical skills. Pick a skill, see which ID-verified local Experts offer it near you, and schedule a one-to-one or small group session in a public place such as a cafe, park, or coworking space. Skills include interview practice, Business English, CV and pitch deck feedback, cabin crew preparation, photography walks, fitness, and 60+ more. The full session fee is shown before you pay, a QR or code starts each session, and you can bring a Folk, a trusted person who is told where you are going. Live on web, iOS, and Android.
