RobiTasks
Map your home, list chores, and prepare for your robot
Scan your home and list your chores to prepare for your robot. Home robots like 1X NEO are taking preorders, but new robots arrive knowing nothing about your home. RobiTasks solves this by letting you scan each room with your iPhone's LiDAR or a few photos to identify furniture and objects. You can set no-go rules by room and describe chores as you would to a person, then get editable step-by-step robot task plans. It's free on web and iPhone so your home is ready when your robot arrives.
