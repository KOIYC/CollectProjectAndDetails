SnapEX Pay
Crypto payment gateway for hosting, VPN, and online stores
SnapEX Pay adds a crypto payment method to your billing, store, or VPN panel. Customers pay on a hosted SnapEX Pay page in USDT on 16 networks like TRON, Ethereum, BSC, Solana, TON, Polygon, and more, or in 70+ other coins converted to USDT. The money lands in your merchant balance in USDT. No wallets, nodes, or blockchain setup is needed on your side.
There are 13 ready-made modules including WHMCS, BILLmanager, FOSSBilling, Paymenter, Blesta, WooCommerce, CS-Cart, Xboard / V2Board, Marzban, 3x-ui, Remnawave, a Telegram VPN bot, and an n8n node. It has a REST API with idempotency keys, HMAC-SHA256 signed webhooks, and a test mode. Pricing is 1% per payment with no setup or monthly fees.
