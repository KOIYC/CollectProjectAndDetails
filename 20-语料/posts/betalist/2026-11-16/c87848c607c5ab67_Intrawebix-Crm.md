Intrawebix CRM keeps a small team's conversations and sales documents in one place. WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Messenger, Viber, email, and website chat arrive in a shared inbox, and new chats become leads. Deals move across a Kanban pipeline, quotes and contracts are signed online, and invoices integrate with Xero, QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Odoo, SmartBill, FGO, or Oblio.
Paid plans add telephony, an AI voice agent that answers calls and creates leads, a helpdesk, and an AI assistant. From €7.50 per user/month (billed yearly; US dollars outside Europe), excl. VAT; free plan for 3 users with one channel. In English, Romanian, and Russian, hosted in Germany (EU).
