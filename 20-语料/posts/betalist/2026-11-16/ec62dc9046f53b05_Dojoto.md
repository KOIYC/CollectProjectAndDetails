Dojoto
Track attendance and fees for your martial arts club from your phone
Visit website
Save
 Saved
Dojoto helps martial arts instructors track attendance and monthly fees from their phones. Tap a student to mark attendance or record a payment, and see who still owes fees. Instructors share live updates across their groups, with fees set per club, group, or student. Import students from a spreadsheet or a photo of a paper list, and export attendance and payments whenever needed. Students and parents do not need an account. Dojoto records cash and bank transfer payments without processing money.
