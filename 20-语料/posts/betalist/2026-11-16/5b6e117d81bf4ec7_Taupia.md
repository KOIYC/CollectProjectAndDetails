Taupia helps UK students, renters, and young professionals manage household bills in one place. It sets up or reads your current energy, broadband, and mobile bills, tracks contracts and renewal dates, and compares the market to show savings you can approve with a tap. Start by entering an address, uploading a bill, or connecting email. Keep providers and prices visible, avoid hidden markups, and switch inside the app while monitoring continues. Taupia is free to use.
