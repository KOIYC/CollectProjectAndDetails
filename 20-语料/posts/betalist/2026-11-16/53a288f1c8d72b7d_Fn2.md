FN2 is an autonomous stock decision engine. Instead of asking questions every day, FN2 dispatches AI agents that watch the markets for you, do deep research on a schedule, and deliver the answers to your inbox, phone, or Discord. It was created to simplify the manual research loop that often requires checking tabs, earnings transcripts, FRED charts, and screeners before the market opens.
