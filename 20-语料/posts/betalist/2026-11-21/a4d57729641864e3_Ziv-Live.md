ZIV Live
Collect every guest's photos and videos from your event in real time
Visit website
Save
 Saved
ZIV Live lets you gather every guest's photos and videos from an event into one live, private gallery. Guests scan a code, shoot in full resolution, and see everything appear instantly on their phones or on a big screen without app downloads or accounts.
ZIV Live uses per-event pricing starting at $5 and automatically deletes galleries after a chosen time. It avoids feeds, followers, tracking, and ads so you can focus on capturing and sharing important moments.
