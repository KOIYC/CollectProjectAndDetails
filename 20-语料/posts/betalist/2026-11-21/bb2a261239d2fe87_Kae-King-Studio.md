KAE KING Studio turns your 30-second phone vocal into a finished, radio-ready track. Record a quick melody, choose genre and mood, add or generate lyrics, then create and share for free or purchase the master to release on platforms like Spotify, Boomplay, and YouTube. The service also offers an optional in-person residency at its licensed Zanzibar studio for acoustic booth recording and 4K cinematic video shoots, taking you from raw idea to global distribution with professional polish.
