Brickalike
Turn any building-set number into print-ready 3D brick files
Visit website
Save
 Saved
Brickalike turns a building-set number into print-ready 3D files. Type the number from the box and it lists every part and colour, then makes print files for the parts a home 3D printer can make, laid out on plates for your printer.
It also turns a photo, a few words or a 3D scan into a brick model with a parts list and building steps. A fit test helps you tune the studs. Free account to download; pay per set, or get Pro for every set. Independent, not affiliated with any brick or toy maker.
