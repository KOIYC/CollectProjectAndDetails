normakte
Draft and maintain EU conformity files for CRA and Machinery Regulation
normakte helps machine manufacturers prepare for the EU Machinery Regulation, which replaces Directive 2006/42/EC on 20 January 2027 without a transition period, and for the Cyber Resilience Act. It offers free in-browser tools to check old declarations of conformity for 2027 gaps, generate combined EU declarations, and calculate CRA reporting deadlines. The product file tracks 63 requirement lines across both regulations, stores versioned evidence, reuses it where allowed, and publishes nameplate QR pages for digital instructions.
