Themio
Upload documents or answer questions for EU compliance checks and fixes
Themio checks your documents against EU rules and shows where you fall short. Upload any relevant document, such as a privacy policy, AI-use policy, or security procedure. Themio reviews it article by article against GDPR and the AI Act, with more EU frameworks coming soon. If you have no documents, you can answer a few questions instead. Each requirement gets a verdict with the source text cited, and the gaps become a prioritized action plan. A report takes about two minutes.
