Evenitto
Discover and plan local events and activities across the Netherlands
Evenitto is an event calendar for the whole Netherlands in English and Dutch. It collects listings from hundreds of venues, city calendars, and organizers, so you can see what's on tonight, this weekend, or during school holidays. You can filter events by city, date, price (including free), age, and type, such as concerts, theatre, workshops, family outings, and exhibitions. Each listing links to the official organizer for tickets. The site also features profiles for over 100 concert halls and theatres, plus a blog with practical guides like Oktoberfest dates by city and free Christmas markets.
