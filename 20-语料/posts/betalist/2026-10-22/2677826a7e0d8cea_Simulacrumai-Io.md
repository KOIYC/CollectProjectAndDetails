SimulacrumAI.io
Simulate your career-to-wealth path and act on proven next steps
Simulacrum turns your resume into a career-to-wealth simulation so you can see outcomes before committing time. It maps five income layers over 24 months and uses forty-nine AI agents to produce deliverables like a rate card, outreach campaigns, course outlines, and an allocation plan. Start with a free SEO-ready bio page and 24/7 AI chat that captures leads, then run calibrated projections grounded in market data to choose and execute the path that compounds into wealth.
