Rezup
Learn why customers cancel using their own voice or text feedback
Rezup tells SaaS founders why customers cancel. Connect Stripe with a read-only key, and every canceling customer gets one short email from you. They answer by voice or text, in any language, with no app or account needed. You receive the reasons transcribed, translated, and ranked by the MRR you lost. The service is free up to $5k MRR (50 responses a month).
