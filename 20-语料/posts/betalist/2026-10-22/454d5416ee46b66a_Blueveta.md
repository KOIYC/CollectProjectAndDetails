BlueVeta
Turn one product photo into 25+ ready-to-post marketing assets
BlueVeta turns a single product photo into a complete marketing campaign in minutes. Upload a photo and the platform automatically generates studio and lifestyle images, a short promotional video, and copy sized for every major channel, all with built-in brand memory. Payments are per pack starting at $9.99 with no subscription required. BlueVeta supports 10 languages and learns from your edits and brand inputs to keep outputs consistent and true to your voice.
