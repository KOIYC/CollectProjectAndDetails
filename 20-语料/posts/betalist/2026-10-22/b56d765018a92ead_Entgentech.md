EntgenTech
Validate and de-risk your startup idea before you build
EntgenTech helps founders vet, score, and pressure-test startup ideas before investing months of work or capital. It combines an idea scorer and generator with a deep market test and a funding mapper to give you a founder-specific read, clear scores, and actionable outputs. With one credit per idea, you get an investor memo, a live-formula financial model, and a ready-to-send pitch deck. The platform avoids cheerleading and explains every score so you can decide to build or kill with confidence.
