Rey
Automate creator marketing workflows directly in Slack
Rey is an AI employee for creator marketing that works alongside your team in Slack. It takes ownership of recurring tasks like weekly reporting, campaign prep, creator and sample tracking, meeting follow-up, and ad insights. You define the scope, data access, and approval steps, and Rey delivers prepared reports, recommendations, and follow-ups on schedule. It flags blockers, asks for decisions when needed, and posts results where your team already works.
