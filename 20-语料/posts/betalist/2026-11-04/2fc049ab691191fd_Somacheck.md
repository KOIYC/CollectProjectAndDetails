SomaCheck
A 3-second body-based check-in for clear reflection using phone sensors
Hold a statement in mind, like "I'm ready for this conversation" or "This plan is right for me," make one phone movement, and see whether it reads as Aligned or Unaligned for you, measured against your own baseline. It uses only the iPhone's motion sensors—no camera, no wearable. After a short one-time calibration, each check takes three seconds, and your readings stay yours.
People use it before a decision, at the end of a long day, or when something feels off but they can't say why yet. It also connects to ChatGPT and Claude so your assistant can offer a check-in with your yes. Free for unlimited check-ins.
