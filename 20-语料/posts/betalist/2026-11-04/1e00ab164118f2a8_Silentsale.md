SilentSale
UK marketplace protecting buyers and sellers equally in second-hand sales
SilentSale is a UK marketplace for buying and selling preloved clothes, tech, homeware, and kids' items. Both buyer and seller pay the same small SilentCover fee (£0.30 + 3%), so they are protected equally. Payment is held by Stripe until the buyer confirms delivery.
Choose tracked courier delivery or PIN Pickup where you meet in person, inspect the item, then share a PIN to release payment. Sellers can add short video proof so buyers see the real item before paying. It is free to list, and sellers pay only when an item sells. Available on iOS, Android, and the web for UK adults.
