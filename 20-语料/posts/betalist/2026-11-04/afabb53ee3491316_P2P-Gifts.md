p2p.gifts
Send crypto gifts instantly without signups or app installs
p2p.gifts lets you create and share crypto gifts that recipients can claim instantly without creating an account or installing an app. It streamlines peer-to-peer gifting for communities, events, and friends by generating claimable links or cards. Built on mybucks.online, it focuses on a fast, simple flow with clear terms and documentation so you can distribute funds securely and recipients can redeem them right away.
