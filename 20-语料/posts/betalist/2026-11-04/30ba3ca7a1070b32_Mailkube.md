mailkube
Send transactional email via REST or SMTP with EU-hosted delivery
mailkube provides transactional email infrastructure for developers. It runs the sending stack and deliverability so you can integrate quickly over REST or drop-in SMTP. The platform includes templates with versioning, topics and tags, scheduled sending, real-time webhooks, CLI tooling, and official SDKs for popular languages. Data is stored in the EU and the service emphasizes reliable routing, detailed analytics, and fast integration for existing apps and frameworks.
