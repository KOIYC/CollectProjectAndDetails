ControlTheRoom.ai
AI meeting briefs for complex, high-stakes enterprise deals
ControlTheRoom.ai provides meeting prep for sellers handling complex enterprise deals. It analyzes over 35 sources using 17 frameworks to deliver a brief in about five minutes covering deal health, stakeholder psychology, competitive gaps, risk exposure, and a meeting playbook. There are three brief types for outreach, the meeting, and partnership deals, plus an AI Coach to help pressure-test your talking points. It's designed for people closing $500K to $5M deals with five or more stakeholders.
