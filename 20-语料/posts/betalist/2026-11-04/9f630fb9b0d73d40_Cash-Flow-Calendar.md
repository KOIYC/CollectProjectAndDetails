Cash Flow Calendar
The budget calendar designed for irregular income
Cash Flow Calendar is a budgeting calendar for people with variable income. It plots every paycheck, bill, and expense on the date it happens and projects your balance forward, so you can spot low-cash days before they arrive. Connect US bank accounts via Plaid or enter transactions manually, set monthly category budgets including scheduled spending, and review reports showing upcoming costs by day, month, and category to stay on track with confidence.
