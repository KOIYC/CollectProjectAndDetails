Cashd
Snap a photo, get an instant resale value, list it everywhere
Cashd helps you turn stuff into cash by identifying items from a photo, pricing them with real market data where available, and drafting listings for top marketplaces. It pulls live comps from eBay and TCGplayer and uses a brand-aware AI estimate for everything else, clearly labeling the source. Cashd generates titles, descriptions, suggested prices, and tracks your scans in a simple inventory so you can see what's listed, sold, and your total value held.
