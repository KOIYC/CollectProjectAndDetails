Chickadee Bandit
Centralize family calendar, chores, meals, lists, and location in one hub
Chickadee Bandit is a private hub for the people you coordinate with—family, couples, roommates, care circles, clubs, teams, or HOAs. Instead of a fixed dashboard with a large checklist of underused features, Chickadee Bandit works like an app platform. Start with an empty private hub and install apps from a catalog of 100+, such as shared calendar, agenda, chores and routines, grocery lists and meal planning, tasks, notes and whiteboards, expense splitting, home maintenance, document library, medication tracking, habits and streaks, and more. Add what fits, remove what doesn’t, and arrange the rest the way your group actually works.
