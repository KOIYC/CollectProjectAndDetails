OrbHeaven
Track real astrological transits and reflect on what happened
OrbHeaven is an astrology app that calculates the exact dates when planetary transits touch your birth chart and shows you upcoming windows. It provides the specific transits behind every reading, avoids sun-sign generalities, and lets you record how events actually went so it can read your own pattern over time. Use it to frame decisions, track four months of transits, ask questions, and keep a private record that remains yours to download or delete at any time.
