Pudding
Run paid, AI-guided work samples to assess real job skills
Pudding helps hiring teams assess candidates through realistic, paid take-home work samples paired with live, AI-guided screen share retrospectives. Its context-aware agent observes, asks targeted questions, and records explanations while candidates use their own tools, letting humans evaluate both the work and reasoning. Pudding integrates with popular ATS platforms, supports asynchronous scheduling, and ensures candidates keep ownership of their work while receiving automatic payouts via Stripe.
