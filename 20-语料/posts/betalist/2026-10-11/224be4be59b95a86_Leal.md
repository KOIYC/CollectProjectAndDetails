Leal
Create wallet-based loyalty cards that drive repeat visits
Leal helps small businesses build and manage digital loyalty programs customers actually use. It creates branded loyalty cards that live in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, so shoppers can add them with a tap and see progress from the lock screen. Staff issue stamps in seconds at checkout, while real-time updates, push notifications, and analytics keep customers engaged and support smarter decisions. Leal supports multiple languages and launches in minutes without technical setup.
