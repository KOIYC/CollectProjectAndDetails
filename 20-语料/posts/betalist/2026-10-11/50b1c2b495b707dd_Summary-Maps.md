Summary Maps
Turn books into interactive AI mind maps you can explore and retain
Summary Maps transforms books and long-form texts into interactive mind maps powered by Claude AI. Upload a book or search a title, and it extracts key ideas, chapters, and connections into a visual structure you can navigate, annotate, and share. Use study mode with AI-generated quizzes, audio playback, and book clubs to reinforce learning and collaborate. Export maps as PNG, PDF, or JSON to review anywhere and build a personal visual knowledge library.
