Flowy
Keep every saved link, reel, or screenshot findable in one AI inbox
Flowy is an AI-powered inbox that captures everything you save and makes it easy to find later. You can share links, reels, articles, screenshots, and more from iOS, macOS, browser, or email. Claude reads each item, extracts the gist, categorizes it, and indexes it for natural-language search. When you ask in plain English, Flowy answers with sources. It is designed to reduce friction for ADHD and busy minds by removing folders and tagging so you can capture fast and recall with a sentence.
