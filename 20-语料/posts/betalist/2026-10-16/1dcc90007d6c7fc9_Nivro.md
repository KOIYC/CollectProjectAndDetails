Nivro
Automate SEO articles, pins, and posts and publish everywhere
Nivro is a full-cycle AI content platform for bloggers, creators, and agencies. From one keyword, it scrapes live Google SERPs, analyzes top competitors, generates SEO- and GEO-optimized articles, designs Pinterest pins with a built-in template studio (no Canva needed), schedules Facebook posts, and auto-publishes to WordPress with schema and images. It supports bulk CSV import, multi-site management, and offers a unified dashboard for writing, design, scheduling, and publishing.
