Adgentek Ad Server
Ad server for AI apps, chatbots, and agents with native ads
Adgentek serves native, intent-matched ads inside AI apps using contextual cards, interactive Q&A, and sponsored recommendations. It does not use cookies or share personal information with advertisers and delivers ads quickly. You can integrate using a Web SDK, widget, REST API, or MCP. The service is free to start, with no traffic minimum or exclusivity.
