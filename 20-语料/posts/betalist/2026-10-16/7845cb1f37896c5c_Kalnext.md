Kalnext
See and edit your whole year in Google Calendar or Outlook
Kalnext gives teams a 12-month, bird's-eye view of their Google and Outlook calendars so leaders can align strategy and execution. It overlays multiple calendars, two-way syncs in real time, and lets you manage events and tasks without switching tools. You can filter by color, keyword, or type to spot conflicts early, find free time, and plan quarters clearly. With sharing, admin controls, and scalable plans, Kalnext helps organizations make informed decisions and stay on track.
