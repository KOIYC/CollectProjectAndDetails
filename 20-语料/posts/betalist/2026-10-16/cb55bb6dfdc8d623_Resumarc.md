Resumarc
Free resume and CV builder with ATS-ready templates and one-time passes
Resumarc is a web-based resume and cover letter builder with over 100 resume templates and 20 cover letter templates made to work well with applicant tracking systems. It offers a guided editor with writing suggestions based on O*NET data for 1,016 occupations, a resume checker that scores documents against ATS criteria with section-by-section feedback, and over 80 complete resume examples by job title. The builder, TXT export, and a daily AI suggestion allowance are free. PDF and DOCX export and larger AI allowances are available as one-time passes with a 14-day refund policy.
