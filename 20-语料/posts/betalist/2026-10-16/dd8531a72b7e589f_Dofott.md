DOFOTT
Automatic travel time blocks added to Google Calendar events
DOFOTT adds travel time to Google Calendar automatically. Connect your Google account once, set your home and work addresses, and every event with a location gets a travel block timed with real Google Maps durations for driving, transit, cycling, or walking. It handles recurring events, routes directly between back-to-back meetings when that's shorter, and adds an optional parking buffer. Travel blocks are regular Google Calendar events, visible in Apple Calendar, Notion Calendar, and anywhere else that reads your account. Booking links like Calendly also show the time as busy.
Free during beta; after that, free for 12 travel meetings a month, $5 if you go over, or $36 per year.
