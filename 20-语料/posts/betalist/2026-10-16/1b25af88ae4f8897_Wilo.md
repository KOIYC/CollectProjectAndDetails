AI clerk that helps shoppers find, compare, and choose products
Wilo is an AI shopping assistant for online stores that reads your entire catalog and helps each visitor find, compare, and choose products using natural language. It understands shopper intent, knows the page they’re on, compares items side by side, and recommends complementary products with both text and photos. It hands off non-catalog queries to your team and keeps conversations threaded across pages and devices. It installs in an afternoon on WooCommerce, Shopify, and Salla.
