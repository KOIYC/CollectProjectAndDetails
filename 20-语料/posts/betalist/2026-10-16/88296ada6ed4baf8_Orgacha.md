Orgacha
The employee comms platform people actually want to open
Orgacha is a corporate communication platform designed to make workplace communication feel intuitive and engaging like the apps people use every day. With personalized feeds, DMs, announcements, and gamification, Orgacha helps employees find, share, and engage with information naturally. Instead of relying on traditional top-down communication, Orgacha lets employees discover and interact with workplace information, making communication more accessible, searchable, and engaging.
