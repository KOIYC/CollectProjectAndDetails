Inwista
Professional subtitles in 70+ languages with an API
Professional subtitles in over 70 languages from any audio or video file. Broadcast-grade formatting is available in one click, along with an accurate transcript that includes speaker labels. Everything stays in one project. You can style the captions to match your brand, translate them, and cut the video by deleting text, since the timeline follows the transcript. Export SRT, VTT, DOCX, or a finished video with captions burned in, or push it directly to Drive, Dropbox, YouTube, or Vimeo. A public REST API and signed webhooks let you run the whole system from your own software, with an official n8n template if you'd rather not write code.
