Pitchlo
Find paid UGC jobs, pitch brands with AI, and land creator deals
Pitchlo is a UGC (user-generated content) marketplace connecting content creators with brands. Creators get fresh paid UGC jobs daily, a free portfolio page, and AI tools that write and send personalized pitches to brands, helping them spend less time searching and more time creating. Brands can post UGC jobs for free, review applicants with portfolios, and hire creators directly without agencies. Pitchlo also offers free UGC pay data, a weekly UGC Market Index, and creator tools like a rate calculator, contract template, and media-kit builder.
