Site Checkups
Find out why your website isn't bringing you customers
Site Checkups reads your homepage like a first-time visitor and explains why people leave without buying.
Paste a homepage address and in about a minute you get a free report written for the person who runs the site, not for a developer. It covers the first impression a stranger forms in the first few seconds, what visitors think you sell versus what you actually sell, the questions someone has right before they leave, the specific problems costing you sign-ups or sales with severity ratings, missing proof, and buttons that confuse or ask for too much too soon. You can run three new reports a day with no sign-up.
