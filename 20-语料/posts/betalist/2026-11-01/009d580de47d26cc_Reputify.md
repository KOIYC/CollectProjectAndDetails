Reputify
Collect and manage reviews via QR, email, and video in one place
Reputify centralizes your customer reviews from Google, Trustpilot, Facebook, Booking.com, and other platforms into a single dashboard. It automatically fetches new reviews, analyzes sentiment, topics, and suspicious activity on EU-based servers, and drafts reply suggestions you can edit and send. Reputify also lets you display a unified Reputify Score and recent reviews on your website via widgets or a WordPress plugin. You can invite customers to leave text or video reviews via email, QR code, or tablet kiosk, with tracking built in. A free plan is available, and no US AI services are involved.
