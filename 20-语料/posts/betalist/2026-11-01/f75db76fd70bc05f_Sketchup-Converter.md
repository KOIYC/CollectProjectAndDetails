SketchUp Converter
Convert .skp files to older SketchUp versions in seconds
SketchUp Converter lets you downgrade .skp files so they open in your installed SketchUp version. Upload a model, choose a target version from 2017 to 2025, and download a compatible copy without installing anything. It preserves geometry, groups, components, materials, textures, tags, and dimensions, and reports items not carried over like scenes, custom styles, and text labels. Use it on Mac or Windows, pay per file or monthly, and your files are deleted on schedule for privacy.
