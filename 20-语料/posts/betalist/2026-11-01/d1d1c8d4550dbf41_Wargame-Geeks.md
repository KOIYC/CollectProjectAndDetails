Wargame Geeks
Download 3D-printable terrain STLs for 28–32mm tabletop wargames
Wargame Geeks offers 3D-printable terrain for tabletop wargaming, focusing on 28–32mm scale and FDM-friendly, support-free STL files. You can browse modular terrain sets, scatter pieces, and tokens, or order select designs as printed terrain shipped to you. The site features large projects like Container City with hundreds of stackable, linkable parts, plus free sample STLs and print guides. Join the community on Discord and get updates when new files release.
