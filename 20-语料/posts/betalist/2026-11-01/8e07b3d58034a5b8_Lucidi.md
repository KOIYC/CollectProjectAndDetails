Lucidi
Zero-data-entry home asset tracker with proactive alerts
Lucidi organizes the documents that run your household into a living model of your home. Instead of manually updating spreadsheets or digging through folders, you simply forward your receipts, bills, and warranty documents. The engine connects the dots by linking documents to specific appliances or accounts and proactively alerts you before a warranty expires, a premium increases, or a return window closes. It is a strict push, not pull, system with full provenance, so you can always tap an alert to view the original source file. We built this with a strict zero-training policy (using Anthropic and Voyage AI) to ensure your personal household data is never used to train models.
