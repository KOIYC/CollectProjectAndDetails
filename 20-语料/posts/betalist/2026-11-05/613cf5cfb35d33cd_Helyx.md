Helyx
Everyday AI chat with organized tabs and app connections
Helyx is an AI chat designed for everyday use, with no jargon or setup maze. Keep your conversations organized in tabs instead of one endless scroll, and easily pick up any thread where you left off. Connect apps like Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and your notes to ask Helyx to find, summarize, or draft content from them. Use each answer as needed: copy it, turn it into an email or task, or build on it in a new tab. Choose the model per message, bring your own API keys, and see each message's cost. Helyx is in invite-only beta; request access on the site.
