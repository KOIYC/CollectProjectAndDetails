OLVA.ai
Bot-free real-time AI meeting assistant with live insights and coaching
OLVA delivers live meeting intelligence without joining your call as a bot. It captures audio from your device to provide real-time transcription, automatic question detection, instant answers from your documents, and live coaching during Zoom, Meet, Teams, and in-person conversations. OLVA supports multilingual meetings, generates summaries and action items, keeps a searchable meeting memory, and can turn sessions into editable PowerPoint decks for easy follow-up.
