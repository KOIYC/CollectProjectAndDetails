Puppetflow
Automate browser workflows with live control, 2FA, and auditing
Puppetflow is a browser automation platform that runs programmable workflows in the cloud and on self-hosted setups. It handles login walls, email codes, 2FA, and human approvals while letting you watch runs live, take control during execution, and replay sessions. Teams build flows visually, capture evidence with video and artifacts, and manage access and variables across environments. Puppetflow integrates with n8n to orchestrate APIs while driving real browsers where no API exists.
