Plot Travel
Plan group trips together, from dates to itinerary and expense splits
Plot Travel helps groups plan and enjoy trips together from the first idea to the final receipt. It gathers everyone’s availability, destination votes, and private budgets, then shows the overlap so you can decide quickly. Build a shared itinerary with wishlists, votes, comments, tags, maps, and travel times to keep everyone aligned. Track expenses with receipts, split costs fairly, and preserve memories with trip stories, stamps, and postcards so the whole group stays on the same page.
