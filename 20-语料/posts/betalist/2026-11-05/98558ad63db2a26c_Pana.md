Pana
Hand off tasks to an AI agent running on your private computer
Pana is an AI agent that works on your private computer to get tasks done across your apps. You describe what you need once, and it executes continuously, even while you sleep, keeping your data and passwords secure. Pana can prepare meetings, manage shopping, mark up documents, and build custom mini-tools like meal trackers, inbox consolidators, and timers. It learns from your feedback, asks for approval when needed, and acts across connected services.
