AI Shorts is an all-in-one AI short video generator that turns text prompts and images into videos in minutes. Generate videos from text or images, explore different creative styles, and create content for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, product ads, animation, and storytelling. For complex projects, AI Shorts offers editable storyboards, character references, and a visual canvas to organize scenes and assets. It helps content creators, marketers, and filmmakers make AI-powered video creation more accessible, flexible, and efficient.
