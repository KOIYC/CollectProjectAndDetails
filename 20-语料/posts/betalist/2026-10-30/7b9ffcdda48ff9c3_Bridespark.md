BrideSpark
Plan your entire wedding for free with website, RSVPs, seating, and budget
BrideSpark is a free wedding planning platform offering all features without limits or paywalls. Set up your wedding website, collect online RSVPs, manage guests and seating, track your budget and vendors, and build timelines and checklists. Design save-the-dates and invitations in 24 styles, then download them print-ready or have them printed and delivered. Plan with your partner, email invitations and updates, and keep up to 1,500 photos in your gallery, including those guests upload via a QR code. Your data stays private, and wedding planning is always free.
