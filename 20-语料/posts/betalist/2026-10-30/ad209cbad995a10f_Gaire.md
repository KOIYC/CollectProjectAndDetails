Gairė
Track chosen web pages and get precise change alerts
Gairė watches the web pages you choose and tells you exactly what changed: the item, and its old and new price. Paste a link and pick what to watch: prices, stock, new items in a list, or a part you describe in plain words, which AI then finds.
Alerts go by text message, email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, webhooks, or an API, and AI assistants can use it over MCP. Texts are included even on the free plan (numbers in Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, Poland, and Portugal).
Free users get 10 pages checked every hour during the day. The Plus plan costs EUR 15 a month for 50 pages checked every 15 minutes. No pages behind a sign-in.
