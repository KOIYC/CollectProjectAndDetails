Cullit
Cull photos faster with AI that flags, filters, and exports
Cullit is an AI-assisted photo culling app designed for photographers who need to move quickly after a shoot. It groups images by scene, shows every face, scores focus for each face, detects blinks and smiles, and lets you filter by sharpness and expressions. You can flag, rate, color-label, compare shots side by side or in a grid, and export picks to Lightroom with XMP sidecars. Cullit keeps everything local, runs fast on RAW files, and streamlines metadata, renaming, and batch actions.
