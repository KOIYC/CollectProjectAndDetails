RentalThread
One shared thread for every rental repair from request to payment
A tenant reports a leak on WhatsApp, the plumber emails an estimate, the owner approves on a call, and the receipt ends up in a drawer. We're building one shared thread for every repair, so the tenant, the pro, the owner, and the manager see the same photos, estimate, approval, and payment. Each property also keeps its rent, expenses, and documents in one place. Agents can share one link per listing so people book their own visits. AI turns messages and photos into clear requests and drafts updates, which a person reviews before anything is sent. Available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. We haven't launched yet: join the waitlist for early access and vote for the features you'd use.
