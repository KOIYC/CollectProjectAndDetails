Stella
An emotional wellness AI you can talk to by voice or iMessage
Stella is your emotional wellness AI, a friend to help you figure things out. Tell Stella what happened, even the parts you're still working through. Speak or text about what stayed with you. Explore another perspective, find words for a conversation, or choose a small step when you're ready. Talk by voice in the iPhone app or by text in iMessage with Text Stella. It’s free to download, but a subscription is needed to keep using it. Stella is not therapy and is not a substitute for professional care.
