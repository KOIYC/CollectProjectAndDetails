SubSplitHQ
Split shared bills fairly and automate reminders and payouts
SubSplitHQ helps households split shared expenses without awkward conversations. It supports equal, custom percentage, and fixed-amount splits, ensures 100% accuracy, and automates reminders before and after due dates. Roommates can settle up with one tap via Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or by copying Zelle details.
The platform tracks subscriptions, rotates supply purchases, shows spending insights, and enforces monthly budget limits to keep your group organized and on track.
