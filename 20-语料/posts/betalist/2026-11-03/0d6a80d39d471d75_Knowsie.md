Knowsie
Scan groceries to check allergies, diets, and goals with clear reasons
Food labels have a lot to say. We make them make sense.
Meet Knowsie, your curious little sidekick for smarter grocery shopping. Scan a barcode and see what’s really inside your food. Additives, allergens, and your nutrition goals are broken down into clear ratings, each with the research behind it. Get a heads-up when a product you buy changes its recipe, and see which additives aren’t allowed in the EU.
Can’t find a product? Snap its label for an instant score while our team checks the details for everyone. No jargon. No ads. No brands buying a better score. Just the fine print, finally explained. Free beta on iPhone and Android.
