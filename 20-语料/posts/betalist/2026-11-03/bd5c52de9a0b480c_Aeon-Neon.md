Aeon // Neon
Agents and infrastructure as your agantic network in cloud
Aeon Neon is to agents what cloud hosting is to your dockerized localhost:5000. It turns scattered AI work into a secure, visible system. Teams can map agents, people, tools, and handoffs in one place so ownership and access are clear before work starts. The platform connects real-world triggers, keeps essential human approvals, and runs executions with live visibility. It records status, decisions, and context so each run improves and stays auditable. Start with a concrete task and expand as your organization's AI workflows grow with confidence.
