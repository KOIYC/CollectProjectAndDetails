Haloscan
Track labs, symptoms, and doses for Hashimoto's and hypothyroidism
Haloscan helps people with Hashimoto's and hypothyroidism track labs, symptoms, and medication dose changes in one place. It creates a concise, doctor-ready report that highlights key questions, trends, and a clear summary of your results and history so you can have informed discussions with your endocrinologist. You can import lab values by photographing printed reports, see reference ranges from each lab, and export all data anytime. Haloscan prioritizes privacy, stores data in the US, and lets you delete everything with one action.
