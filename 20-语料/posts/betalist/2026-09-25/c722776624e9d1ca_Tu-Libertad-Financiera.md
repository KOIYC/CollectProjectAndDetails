## Centralize your money and get AI plans to reach FIRE

Tu Libertad Financiera gathers your salary, assets, investments, and real estate in one place to give a clear diagnosis of your financial health. The platform calculates returns, cash flow, and amortizations, syncs portfolios of funds, stocks, and ETFs, and shows visual indicators of savings and net worth. It includes an AI advisor that offers personalized strategies and lets you export PDF reports for tracking or bank presentations.
