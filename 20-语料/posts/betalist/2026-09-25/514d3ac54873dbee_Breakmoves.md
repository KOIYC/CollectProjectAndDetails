## Turn TV breaks and daily pauses into quick movement bursts

BreakMoves turns commercial breaks and everyday pauses into quick chances to move. The app listens for TV ad breaks, then cues you with short, space-friendly stretches or exercises so you build consistency without carving out extra time. Use TV Mode to move during commercials, Nudge Mode for gentle reminders on your schedule, or Move Now to start anytime. Skip the gym and equipment, fit motion into your routine, and feel better while keeping the life you already enjoy.
