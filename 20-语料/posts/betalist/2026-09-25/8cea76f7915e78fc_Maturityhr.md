## Track competence management maturity and see initiative impact

MaturityHR is a competence management tool that helps organizations measure maturity across six process areas and 24 sub-areas, each rated on operational, experiential, and impact dimensions. It gathers perspectives from executives, HR, managers, and specialists to reveal differences, track development initiatives, and show trends over time. Teams run assessment rounds via invite links, export reports, and keep EU-hosted data under organizational control to guide priorities based on evidence.
