## Plan, generate, and edit complete AI videos in one workflow

VlogMe helps you turn an idea, script, image, audio file, or existing clip into a complete video. Its AI Director prepares a script and scene plan. Then, you can generate or transform each scene, add voice, music, and captions, edit the sequence, and export the finished video. VlogMe also includes image creation, talking avatars, voice tools, and social publishing.
