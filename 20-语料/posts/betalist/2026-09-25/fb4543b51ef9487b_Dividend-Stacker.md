## Track high-yield ETF payouts and plan dividend income with real data

Dividend Stacker helps dividend investors research high-yield ETFs and plan income with data-driven tools. It shows estimated next distributions, full payout histories, and total-return numbers for families like YieldMax, Roundhill, NEOS, Defiance, and REX Shares. Use calculators to project monthly income, compare funds side by side, and measure actual results with and without reinvestment. Explore dividend stocks and ETFs to know when you get paid and what $10,000 could have returned.
