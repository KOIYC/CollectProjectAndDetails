## Reputation management, SEO, and AI engine optimization consultant

Matthew Kellogg is a twelve-year entrepreneur and applied search discipline practitioner working with clients across SEO, online reputation management, and AEO in AI search. He specializes in ranking highly contested, revenue-producing commercial SEO keywords and ORM negative search results suppression. He actively displaces unfair or damaging content off page 1 of personal or brand name searches for professionals and businesses whose reputation depends on their name. He is documenting a public, verifiable search engine reputation management case study.
