## Automatically time-block tasks into free gaps on your calendar

Auto-Scheduler helps you stop deciding when to do tasks by automatically booking them into open time around your existing calendar events. It syncs with Google Calendar, respects work hours and deadlines, splits long tasks into manageable chunks, and supports recurring tasks that move to where you're actually free. You can lock hand-picked times, push items to tomorrow, and reschedule with one click, so you stay in control while it handles the planning.
