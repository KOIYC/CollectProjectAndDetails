## AI-native LCM platform to draft, test, and run email marketing in your tone

Pombo is an AI-native lifecycle marketing platform that drafts, tests, and runs campaigns across channels in your brand voice. Set goals in plain language, and it generates sequences with segmentation, timing, and copy you can review and edit. Connect data to ask questions, spot opportunities, and turn insights into campaigns with one click. Run it via Claude, ChatGPT, or MCP agents, and migrate from your existing lifecycle marketing software in a day while preserving contacts and unsubscribes.
