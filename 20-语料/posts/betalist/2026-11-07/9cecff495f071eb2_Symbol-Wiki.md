symbol.wiki
Explore a curated lexicon of symbols across cultures and eras
symbol.wiki is a lexicon of symbols called Sigilla that explains what a mark is, where it came from, and how people have used it. It organizes cultural, religious, scientific, and fictional symbols into clear entries with histories and meanings. You can browse by traditions and themes. The canon is presented as a reading, while your notes remain on your device, making it a focused reference for researchers, designers, and the simply curious.
Add your mark to the lexicon by reaching out at raunaq@raunaqss.com
