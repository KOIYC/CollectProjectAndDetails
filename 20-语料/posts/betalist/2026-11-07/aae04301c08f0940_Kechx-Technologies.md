KechX Technologies
Check links, QR codes, and messages for scams before you act
KechX Technologies builds consumer fraud protection and scam intelligence. Its mobile app, KechX Safe Browsing, lets you paste a URL, scan a QR code, or share a screenshot to get an instant, plain-English verdict with reasons behind it. For institutions, the KechX Intelligence System delivers real-time dashboards with verified scams, brand impersonation alerts, threat maps, and AI-detected patterns tailored to their customers and regions. Both products help people decide safely before they click.
