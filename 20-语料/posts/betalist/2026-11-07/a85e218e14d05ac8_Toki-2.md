Toki
Your AI executive assistant for work and life
Toki is a free AI executive assistant that helps you manage your time by turning everyday information into notes, tasks, calendar events, and reminders, so fewer loose ends occupy your mind. Toki supports discovery, planning, and delivery across work and life without needing you to manage every step. It's designed for independent professionals, freelancers, and small-business owners juggling personal and professional commitments. Its core features focus on notes, tasks, calendars, and reminders from supported inputs. Always check the exact workflow on your platform, as prepared items are not the same as saved or sent.
