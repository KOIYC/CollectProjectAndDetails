Futureproof
Run your books with six AI agents and get decisions, not tasks
Futureproof is an AI finance team and general ledger designed for growing companies. Six specialized agents handle bookkeeping, receivables, payables, cash forecasting, margin analysis, and investor reporting on a unified ledger, while a human financial specialist reviews the monthly close. Connect banks, cards, and sales channels to see daily-closed books, cash runway, and profitability tied to source entries, replacing traditional accounting software with one auditable system.
