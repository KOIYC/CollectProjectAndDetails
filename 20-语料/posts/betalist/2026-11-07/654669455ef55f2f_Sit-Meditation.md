Sit Meditation
Meditation app that writes and narrates sessions for your moment
Sit is a meditation app with no library. Tell it what's on your mind in a sentence or a single word, and it writes and narrates a guided meditation for that moment in seconds. You choose the length, one of eight styles, how much guidance you want, a voice, and a soundscape. Every session is new; nothing is pre-recorded.
Sit is generated natively in 12 languages, never translated or dubbed. The free tier gives you one new session a day, up to 20 minutes, with no card and no trial clock. Sit is coming soon to iOS and Android. Save your seat at sitmeditationapp.com for first word. Made by a two-person team in Chicago.
