DeedSpring
AI transaction coordinator for real estate
DeedSpring is AI transaction coordination software for US real estate agents, teams, transaction coordinators, and brokerages. It reads executed contracts, pulls every date, follows up with lenders, title companies, and signatures, and keeps buyers and sellers updated in a branded client portal. Documents and e-signing are built in. Pricing starts at $15 per month per closing.
