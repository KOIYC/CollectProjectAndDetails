Prime AI Visibility
Track, improve, and monitor your brand’s answers across AI search
Prime AI Visibility helps you understand, fix, and monitor how your brand appears in AI search. It captures answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini to questions your buyers ask, then shows gaps and opportunities. Use Primey—the AI Search Race Engineer—to plan pages, draft content with research-backed guidance, and recheck visibility over time. Teams compare appearances, citations, and changes across checks, linking every page to tracked questions for measurable progress.
