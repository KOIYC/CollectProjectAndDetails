SilverSafe AI
The trusted voice older adults call before they get scammed
SAM is an AI phone and text companion for older adults. If you get a suspicious call, text, or email, hang up and then call or text 844-8-ASK-SAM like you'd call a trusted friend. SAM asks the right questions, flags warning signs, and tells you what to do next. Caregivers get a call or text when something looks off and can see past checks in the family dashboard. SAM checks links and images, including whether a photo is AI generated, helps document scams, and guides you to the right place to report them. No app needed, and it works with the phone you already have. Spanish and Canada support coming soon.
