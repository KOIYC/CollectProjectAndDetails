Among Our People
LGBTQ+ homestays, flatshares, roommate finder, and community pros
Among Our People is an LGBTQ+ community focused on practical life needs. Stay in a homestay with an LGBTQ+ host, find a room or flatshare, or use the roommate finder to meet the right flatmate before choosing your room. Explore co-housing and coliving where you can be yourself at home, or join a group retreat, tour, or cruise. Make local friends and find community-recommended doctors, therapists, lawyers, accountants, plumbers, and electricians, or list your own services. Creating a profile is free, and hosts keep what guests pay: 0% booking commission. Based in Barcelona, open to members worldwide.
