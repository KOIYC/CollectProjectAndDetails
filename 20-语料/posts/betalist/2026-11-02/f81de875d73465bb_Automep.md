AutoMEP
AI drafter for MEP engineers and designers
AutoMEP edits AutoCAD and Revit MEP drawings from your browser. Upload a DWG or RVT file, describe the change in plain language, and it drafts ductwork, piping, circuits, tags, annotations, sheets, and PDFs on the correct layers. AutoMEP reads your file, plans the steps, applies the edit, and checks results against your request, flagging items for review. It supports mechanical, electrical, and plumbing in native DWG and RVT formats, with no plugins to install and straightforward monthly plans.
