Smuggl
Shop abroad through friends who are already flying
Smuggl connects shoppers with friends or friends-of-friends who are already flying, so they can shop abroad at airport duty-free and approved retailers. Browse catalogs from 50 airports in 31 countries or choose from over 320 approved retailers. Someone in your network flying that route buys the item and hands it over in person. Your payment is held until you confirm the handoff, so no one pays on trust. Join the waitlist; the iOS app launches on 6 November 2026.
