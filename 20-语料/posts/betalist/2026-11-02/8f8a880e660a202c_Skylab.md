SkyLab
One shared home for your team's AI prompts and skills
Your team’s best AI prompts are stuck in people’s heads, chat threads, and personal notes. SkyLab brings them into one shared workspace where you work together on your use of AI. Save prompts, build reusable skills, and share them across the team. Developers can stop managing every prompt change. Marketing, sales, and operations can use and improve what works without dealing with anything too technical or too simple. SkyLab is for teams that want AI to improve for everyone, allowing all to contribute and get more from it.
