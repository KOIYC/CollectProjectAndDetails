ZoomMap
Build understanding with a layered knowledge map
ZoomMap is a tool for organizing what you understand as a layered knowledge map. Notes keep the points but lose how they fit together; ZoomMap keeps both. Each card holds one conclusion, with its explanation, examples, and evidence nested in the layer below. Cards on the same layer sit on an open canvas, linked to show how they relate. Expand or collapse layers to move between the big picture and the details. Cards can cite PDFs, EPUBs, notes, and web pages you import. The Agent asks questions, spots gaps, finds passages in your sources, then builds the cards once you decide. The thinking stays yours.
