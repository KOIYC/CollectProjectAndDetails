Play social prediction games on real-world events with virtual points
Problyx is a social prediction platform where you test your knowledge in sports, politics, entertainment, and current events. Use virtual Problyx Points (PP) to make predictions in hundreds of markets, earn points and XP as outcomes are verified, and compete on leaderboards, duels, minigames, and more. Badges and quests add challenges as you build your track record. Problyx is designed for friendly competition and bragging rights; points have no real value and there are no cash stakes, purchases, or cash-outs.
