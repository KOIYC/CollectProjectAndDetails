Anvil
Private and offline AI assistant running entirely on your iPhone
Anvil is an offline AI assistant that runs entirely on your iPhone. It creates AI images on your phone with no caps, no message limits, and no account needed. Download Anvil Dream to generate pictures even in airplane mode. Anvil Core adds offline chat, voice, notes, tasks, photos, and documents, and can read and translate signs and menus without a signal. Ask it anything without lectures, and your data stays private. Web search and iCloud backup are optional. It's in public beta on TestFlight and requires iPhone 15 Pro or newer.
