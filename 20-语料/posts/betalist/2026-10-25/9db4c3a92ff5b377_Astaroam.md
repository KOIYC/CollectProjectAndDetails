Affordable travel eSIMs with instant delivery and easy setup
Astaroam makes it easy to stay connected when traveling abroad. Choose a prepaid eSIM data plan for your destination, receive it instantly by email, and install it on a compatible iPhone or Android device without a physical SIM card.
With flexible data plans and 4G/5G connectivity where available, Astaroam helps travelers get online without costly roaming charges. Keep your existing SIM and WhatsApp number while using your travel eSIM for data.
