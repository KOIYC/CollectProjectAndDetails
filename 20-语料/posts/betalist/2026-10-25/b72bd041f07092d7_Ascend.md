Ascend
Net worth tracking for people who hate budgeting
I built Ascend because I wanted to see my net worth in one place without another budgeting app telling me how to spend. It's an iPhone app where you enter your balances manually — bank accounts, investments, debts — no bank linking required. You get a clean dashboard showing your total net worth and how it changes over time. That's it: your number, in focus, no budgets, no noise.
