Turn any TV into a vintage split-flap board with live updates
KarmaBoard turns any TV into a vintage split-flap message board that clacks each letter into place. Create custom messages and add live feeds like weather, sports scores, stock prices, and transit schedules to keep information fresh and eye-catching. KarmaBoard works in homes for family notes and daily updates, and in businesses for menus, specials, and announcements you can update from your phone for a low monthly per-screen price.
