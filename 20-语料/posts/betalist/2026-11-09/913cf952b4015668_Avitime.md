Avitime
Real product reviews for new online shops from verified testers
Avitime helps new online shops get their first genuine product reviews. Shops pick a product, Avitime selects verified testers in France, and testers order the product for free on the shop's site, use it, then publish an honest review on the product page marked "Product received for testing." Avitime handles tester selection, free orders, delivery tracking, and reminders until the review is published. No fake reviews: every review is published regardless of rating. It works with PrestaShop and WooCommerce; the Shopify app is under review. The first 5 tests are free for every new shop.
