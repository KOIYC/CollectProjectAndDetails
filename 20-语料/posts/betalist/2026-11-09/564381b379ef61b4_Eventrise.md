Eventrise
Sell tickets and manage events with transparent, fan-friendly checkout
Eventrise is a ticketing and event management platform for independent organizers. It offers beautiful event pages or the option to embed checkout on your site, shows all-in pricing to reduce cart abandonment, and pays out directly to your Stripe account as tickets sell. Eventrise includes email campaigns, segments, automations, sales attribution, and workflows like event duplication, comps, scheduled reports, and mobile check-in with offline scanning, all without contracts or hidden fees.
