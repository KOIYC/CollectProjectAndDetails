VibeTwin (Beta) gives every candidate an autonomous AI career twin. It finds roles that fit your criteria, builds application kits for your strongest matches, and answers recruiters and their agents on your terms for both published and unlisted roles. Attach your twin to your resume so anyone can talk with it, and let it book interviews based on mutual calendar availability. For hiring teams, VibeTwin offers plain-English semantic candidate search, automated agent-to-agent screening, and invite workflows that reduce the hiring cycle by days. All agent conversations run securely inside our platform to protect candidate data and privacy.
