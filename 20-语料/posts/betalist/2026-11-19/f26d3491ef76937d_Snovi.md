Snovi
Convert your voice into writing, translation, and memory with context
Visit website
Save
 Saved
Hold a key and talk the way you think. Snovi types tidy, finished text at your cursor in any app: email, Slack, docs, messages, or forms. It removes ums and restarts and translates as you speak. It runs on your device with no connection needed, and the cloud stays off unless you turn it on. Available on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. Built over a year in Cape Breton, Nova Scotia, where offline use was essential due to patchy signal. Launching December 1.
