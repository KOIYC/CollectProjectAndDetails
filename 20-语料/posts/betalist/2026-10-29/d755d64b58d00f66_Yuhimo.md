Yuhimo
Turn notes into long-term knowledge with spaced repetition
Yuhimo is a lightweight notes and learning app for people who want to keep useful knowledge and actually remember it. Write and organize notes as pages, choose which ones you want to learn, and Yuhimo schedules reviews using adaptive spaced repetition. During review, use your notes and questions, rate how well you remembered the material, and get the next review date. Optional AI can turn a topic, pasted text, PDF, or DOCX into draft study material and generate questions. No flashcards required.
