Schoolwize
Explore UK school data to compare subjects, trends, and admissions
Schoolwize helps families in England make informed school choices by turning official data into clear, comparable insights. It presents subject-level GCSE and A-level results, multi-year trends, local and national comparisons, admissions demand, inspection history, attendance, workforce stability, and destinations. You can start for free to search and review headline context, then upgrade with a Decision Pass to unlock full subject and grade details and side-by-side comparisons for your shortlist.
