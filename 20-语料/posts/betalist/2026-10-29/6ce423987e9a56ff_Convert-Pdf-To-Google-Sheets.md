Convert PDF to Google Sheets
Extract tabular data from PDFs directly within Google Sheets
Convert PDF to Google Sheets is a sidebar add-on that extracts tables from PDFs and images directly into your spreadsheet while preserving the layout. No more manual typing, copy-pasting, or uploading files to external converters. Just open the sidebar, upload a PDF or image (or paste from your clipboard), and click Extract Data. In seconds, your tables appear in the sheet, ready to edit.
It can process up to 5 PDF pages at once, placing each page in its own sheet, and works with images and screenshots too. Free credits are available to get started. It's ideal for bookkeepers, accountants, small business owners, and anyone working with bank statements, invoices, or financial reports.
