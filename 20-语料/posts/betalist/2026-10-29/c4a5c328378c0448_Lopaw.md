Lopaw
AI coach providing daily training for dog separation anxiety
If your dog panics every time you leave, Lopaw offers one short training session a day. It uses gradual desensitization, the method recommended by the ASPCA and most separation anxiety trainers. An AI coach plans each session based on your dog's previous sessions and adapts the plan to your dog's progress. Step by step, your dog gets used to staying home alone, and you get your life back. Lopaw is available on iOS and Android.
