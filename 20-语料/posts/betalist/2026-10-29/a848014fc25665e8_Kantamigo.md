Kantamigo
Run guitar nights with lyrics synced line-by-line to every phone
Kantamigo is a live songbook for guitar nights that syncs lyrics to everyone's phones as you sing. You start a session, friends join via a QR code, and each line appears in real time so no one stalls at verse two. It needs no app or account: just a nickname to follow along, suggest songs, and vote. You can keep a private songbook, paste chord sheets, and have them ready to play. Passing the guitar is easy as each player brings their own songs and leads the queue.
