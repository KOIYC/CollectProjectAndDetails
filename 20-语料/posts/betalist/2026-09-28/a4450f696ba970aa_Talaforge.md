Talaforge
Create AI characters in Telegram that remember, flirt, and speak
Talaforge runs AI character chat inside Telegram at @talaforge_bot. Describe a persona in one sentence, and it generates a name, voice, backstory, and opening scene you can edit. Characters remember facts about you across sessions, branch conversations into parallel threads, switch among five AI models, and can send images and voice messages. It supports 27 languages, starts free, and lets creators share characters, earn via referrals, and turn them into standalone Telegram bots.
