Currawong Web
Verify China suppliers with sourced, dated records you can check
Currawong provides supplier verification for global buyers sourcing from China. It checks company registration, factory capability, certificates, and bank beneficiaries using sourced and dated records, not scores or vague approvals. Buyers start from the decision at hand and purchase only the registry checks their order needs, with transparent scope and pricing. Currawong shows live data availability, discloses gaps, and traces every finding back to an identifiable source and check date.
