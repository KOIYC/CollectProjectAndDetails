Ln2CRM
Manage your CRM from LinkedIn with one-click save and AI follow-ups
Ln2CRM is a Chrome extension that lets sales teams manage their CRM without leaving LinkedIn. It shows who is already in your CRM, matches profiles to existing records, and lets you create or update contacts, companies, and deals in Pipedrive, HubSpot, Salesforce, or Zoho. You can capture notes, tasks, and communication history in a sidebar and draft replies with AI based on the conversation and CRM context. Keep your workflow in one place and reclaim selling time.
