Household Saga
Manage personal and shared finances in one clear, connected view
Household Saga brings personal and household finances together without treating them as the same thing. Track your income, expenses, budgets, payment accounts, cash flow, spending trends, and financial insights. Then create or join a household to manage shared money. Track shared expenses, split costs, manage shared budgets, see who paid, understand everyone's share, and reconcile balances through settlements.
Your personal finances remain separate from household finances while showing your complete financial picture. Whether you're managing your own money, sharing expenses with a partner, or living with roommates, Household Saga gives you one place to understand it all.
