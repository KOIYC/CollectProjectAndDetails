TravelPen
Read real trip stories tailored to your destination and reason
TravelPen collects first-hand travel stories organized by where people went and why. Instead of generic guides, you can browse trips that match your reason for travel: family holiday, mates' trip, solo backpacking, romantic weekend, adventure, or slow travel. Read what worked and skip what didn't. Every story includes honest details on accommodation, costs, food, and things guidebooks won't tell you. Search by destination and reason to find someone who just did the exact trip you're planning.
