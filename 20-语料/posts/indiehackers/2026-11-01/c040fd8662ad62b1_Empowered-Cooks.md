 Empowered Cooks: $1.2M+ a year, and what we built to run it
What we are
Empowered Cooks is a kitchen brand built around Cathy Yoder, a creator with 800K+ YouTube subscribers. Her air fryer cookbooks are the #1 and #2 best sellers on Amazon, and we’ve sold 80K+ cookbooks. The business now does $1.2M+ a year.
 How it makes money
 Physical products on Amazon FBA, Shopify, and ClickFunnels
Cookbooks and digital products
Tiered memberships and courses
Sponsorships, advertising, and affiliate
Underneath all of it is a cooking community that keeps people coming back.
 Where we're headed
Most people own an air fryer and barely use it. We teach them to cook healthier, better, faster meals with it. The long-term goal is to replace the microwave as the most used appliance in the kitchen.
 What we built behind the scenes
As we grew, the back office became the hard part. RFQs, purchase orders, freight, and landed costs were spread across too many tools. I reviewed 25+ operating platforms and talked to 6 e-commerce CFOs. Nothing fit, so we built our own ERP. It's live, it runs from the first RFQ through delivery, and it calculates landed costs and margins at every step.
 What's next
The mission for Empowered Cooks is simple: get people actually using the air fryer they already own, and make it the most used appliance in the kitchen.
The ERP is the second bet. We built it because the tools and the finance pros we talked to all accepted a status quo that didn't work for a real e-commerce business. I don't think we're the only operators living with that. I'm looking for a co-founder to take it to other brands while I keep running Empowered Cooks. More on that soon.
