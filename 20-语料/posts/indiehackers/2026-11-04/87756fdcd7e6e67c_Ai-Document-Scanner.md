 I Built an AI Document Scanner to Make Everyday Paperwork Easier
I've been working on AI Document Scanner as part of my journey building practical software that solves everyday problems.
 The idea came from something simple: people deal with documents almost every day, whether it's receipts, business paperwork, notes or other important records. I wanted to make scanning and digitising those documents easier without depending on a physical scanner.
 That's why I built AI Document Scanner .
 I'm still growing the product and would genuinely love to hear from people who try it. Your feedback, suggestions and ideas can help shape what comes next.
 Give it a try:
 https://www.ominiai.online/scanner
 I'm also open to meeting fellow indie hackers, developers, founders and businesses interested in collaborating, sharing ideas or exploring opportunities together.
 I'm building, learning and improving along the way. Happy to connect with anyone on a similar journey!
