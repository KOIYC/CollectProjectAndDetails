 I built BeaconMatch because targeted ads deserve better than generic landing pages
Here's something that never made sense to me about digital marketing.
 We spend hours optimizing ads. We obsess over keywords, audiences, creatives, and targeting.
 Then someone clicks.
 And we send everyone to the exact same landing page. 🤦‍♂️
 Someone searching for "CRM for startups" gets the same experience as someone searching for "enterprise CRM."
 Different needs. Different intent. Same generic message.
 That's the problem I'm solving with BeaconMatch.
 BeaconMatch is an AI-powered website personalization platform that automatically adapts your existing landing pages based on visitor intent.
 Here's what makes it different:
 One page, unlimited variations. No need to build and maintain dozens of landing pages.
Real-time personalization. Headlines, messaging, social proof, and CTAs can adapt to different visitors.
Built for your existing website. No redesign or migration required.
Intent-aware experiences. Match what visitors see to the reason they clicked.
Beyond paid ads. Personalize experiences for visitors arriving from newsletters, social media, and referral partners.
Imagine running five different campaigns targeting five different audiences.
 Instead of creating five landing pages, you keep one.
 BeaconMatch makes that page relevant to each audience automatically.
 The idea is simple: Stop making visitors figure out whether your product is right for them. Show them why it's relevant from the first second.
 We're building BeaconMatch for founders, SaaS teams, e-commerce brands, and marketing agencies that want to get more conversions from the traffic they already have.
 I'm still learning, building, and talking to marketers about how they approach personalization.
 I'd love feedback from fellow indie hackers:
 Are you creating separate landing pages for different audiences, or sending everyone to the same page?
 And what's been your biggest frustration with either approach?
