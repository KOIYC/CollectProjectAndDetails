October 10, 2025
 how I bootstrapped my services biz to $1M run rate
hey all! I'm Joe, the 24 yo owner of AppraiseItNow .
 I quit my job at Bain in December 2024 to build a tech-enabled services company, and this past month we've hit a $1M revenue run rate with just one full time employee.
 Keep reading if you want to see exactly how I did this
 Here's the backstory:
 I was a no-coder, helping businesses do more without hiring more
 Then I landed a job at Bain & Company to do some bigger league consulting work
 I met the founder of AppraiseItNow a year before we were both set to start at Bain, and joined him as a 33% owner.
 My job was to:
 Automate as much as possible
Find new growth channels
For the first 6 months, nothing happened. But then things started going crazy.
 We were getting 1,200 leads per month. (see below)
 At this point, I had stepped away from the business due to some partnership challenges.
 But I saw a clear opportunity to buy out my partner and 10X the company.
 So that's what I did.
 Here's exactly how.
 ** Before my acquisition:
 When I initially joined the company as a partner, we tested a lot of new growth channels: cold email, paid Google Ads, webscraping leads.
 Nothing was really working as well as we needed.
 Until we decided to try a niche corner of SEO called programmatic SEO.
 Context: our company offers any type of appraisal in any state. Think:
 Personal Property Appraisal in Maryland
Equipment Appraisal in Texas
Business Valuation in California
So we spun up over 10K landing pages with unique search terms in a wide range of appraisal offerings, across the country.
 Nothing happened for about 3 months
 Next thing you know, we were getting more leads than we could handle.
 We were doing about $10K in revenue at this point, but I saw a huge hole in our conversion rate.
 We also had a lot more juice to squeeze on the website traffic side.
 I thought I could 4x conversion rate and 2x the leads to get this business to $80K/month.
 So I then bought my partner out in November 2024 (hefty price tag, but already made money back)
 ** After my Acquisition
 Once I acquired the business, I implemented the following:
 Tons of software and automation to streamline customer fulfillment process and the CRM process
 Attio for CRM
Missive for email
Airtable for operations
Whalesync for programmatic SEO
make .com + n8n
AI agents to automatically follow up with leads (n8n + supabase)
Website refresh
New service offerings
Tons of new SEO content and landing pages
Backlink building
Getting rid of low value services
And in 10 months all of this led to:
 4X on conversion rate on leads
2X on average contract value.
And much much happier clients.
And we're now hitting $90K revenue per month over the past few months.
 There's obviously a lot more detail to all of this here, so I'm happy to share more! Just comment here or shoot me a note.
 Let me know if this was helpful :)
