 Out story
The idea for SavvyNomad sparked when one of our co-founders, Jameson, was on a nomadic journey in Morocco. While there, he crossed paths with a group of surfers from California who had been living abroad for years with no plans to return to the U.S., but were struggling to make the U.S. tax system adapt to their new lifestyle. Jameson, who is from Nevada — a state with zero income taxes — had studied accounting extensively at Northwestern University and knew there had to be a better way for Americans abroad to manage their taxes.
 With his brother, a full-time RVer and fellow CPA, Jameson realized that while there were some solutions for RVers and domestic travelers, no one was addressing the unique tax needs of expats and digital nomads. That’s when the vision for SavvyNomad was born — a service designed to help American nomads and expats legally reduce their tax burden by moving their residency to states with zero income tax.
