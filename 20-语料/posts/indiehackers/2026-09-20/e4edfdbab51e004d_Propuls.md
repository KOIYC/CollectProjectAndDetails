# Propuls

AI-powered business management for freelancers and agencies

September 18, 2026 I Built a SaaS for Freelancers — Now I'm Looking for My First Users

I’ve spent months building SaaS products with AI, no-code and code. Now I’m facing the part that comes after building: finding the right users.

One of the products I’ve built is Propuls, a SaaS designed for freelancers, consultants and small agencies.

The idea is simple: running a service business often means jumping between different tools to manage prospects, offers, proposals, invoices and client workflows.

Propuls brings these workflows together in one place, with AI features designed to help reduce repetitive work.

What Propuls currently helps with:

- Managing prospects and opportunities
- Creating offers and proposals
- Generating invoices
- Managing client-related workflows
- Using AI to speed up repetitive tasks

The product is already live.

Now comes the interesting part.

I want to find real users, understand how they use the product, discover what they actually need, and improve Propuls based on real feedback.

I’ll be experimenting with different ways of getting users: communities, content, direct outreach, partnerships and other acquisition channels.

I’ll share what works, what doesn’t, and what I learn along the way.

If you’re a freelancer, consultant or agency owner, I’d also be interested in hearing how you currently manage your prospects, proposals, invoices and client workflow.

Propuls: https://getpropuls.com
