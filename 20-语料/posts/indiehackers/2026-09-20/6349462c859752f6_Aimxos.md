# AIMXOS

AI Marketing Operating System

September 18, 2026 I built the first SEO tool made for AI Search Visibility

Hey IH, I'm Khushank, a techie + marketer, I run DigiBrandBox (a digital agency), and AIMXOS is the tool I kept needing and couldn't find.

Here's the pattern that kept showing up in client work: solid content, decent rankings, SEO basics all checked off, and still invisible the moment someone asks ChatGPT or gets a Google AI Overview instead of a normal search result. Not because the content was weak. Usually something nobody was checking for: an AI crawler quietly blocked in robots.txt, content that only renders after JavaScript runs (which most AI bots never execute), zero structured data telling AI models what the page even is.

Tools like Semrush and Ahrefs weren't built for this, AI visibility is a bolt-on feature for them, added after the fact. So I built AIMXOS specifically for it:

- Asks real ChatGPT/Gemini questions about your site's topic and checks if you actually get cited
- Crawls your whole site and flags exactly what's blocking AI visibility, blocked crawlers, JS-only content, missing schema
- Detects when your site only renders client-side (invisible to AI crawlers) most tools don't check this at all
- Generates the fixes for you: robots.txt, llms.txt, schema markup from your real crawl data, not templates
- Free PageSpeed/Core Web Vitals checker if you just want that piece

Been refining it against real client sites through the agency. Putting it out here now because it needs to survive contact with sites and setups I haven't seen yet.

If you've ever wondered why you rank fine on Google but ChatGPT's never heard of you, that's the exact gap I'm closing. Would genuinely love harsh feedback: what's confusing, what's missing, what you'd pay for.
