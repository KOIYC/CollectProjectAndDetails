# Birthday Reminder pro

“Never Miss a Birthday Again ”

September 18, 2026 Birthday Reminders Pro — Never Miss a Birthday Again 🎂

Hey everyone! 👋

I built Birthday Reminders Pro, a simple Android app designed to help you never forget the birthdays of the people who matter to you. 🎂

The idea is simple: add a birthday, get reminded at the right time, and make sure you never have that “Oh no, I forgot!” moment again. 😭😂

### What it does

🎂 Add and manage birthdays 🔔 Get timely birthday reminders 🎵 Birthday alarms with built-in sounds 🎶 Support for custom alarm sounds 📅 See upcoming birthdays easily 🖼️ Create birthday posters 📱 Simple, lightweight Android experience

I built this as an independent project under Bashyal Team and it's now live on Google Play.

I'd love to hear your feedback, suggestions, or ideas for features you'd like to see in future updates.

👉 Try Birthday Reminders Pro: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bashyalteam.birthdayreminderpro

Thanks for checking it out! ❤️

## About

Birthday Reminders Pro exists to solve a simple but surprisingly common problem: people forget the birthdays of the people who matter to them.
