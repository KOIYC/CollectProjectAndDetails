# Sigma Files

AI Powered platform for US sales tax filling & registrations

September 19, 2026 Save on your sales taxes

Greetings everyone,

I am introducing "Sigma Files"

An AI powered platform, the 1st of its kind. Developed specifically to issue one pain point of small and medium sized business owners"How to get rid of High Sales Taxes and related filing cost"

You heard it right! Sigma files automate your sales tax filing at a frictional cost of what conventional CPA firms charge. The good news is that the platform itself manage sales tax preparation, maximizing deductions. You will not even need even the basic understanding of sales taxes, the platform itself will manage everything using its AI powered process for sales tax registration as well as filing.

For just $24.9, the platform will manage unlimited returns for one state. Multi state subscription packages are available as well supporting all 50 states of US.

More details can be accessed on our official website : https://www.sigmafiles.com/

## About

Sigma Files was built to provide AI based sales tax compliance in all 50 US states at a fraction cost of what conventional CPA firms charge. It exist to facilitate business owners not to be a burden.
