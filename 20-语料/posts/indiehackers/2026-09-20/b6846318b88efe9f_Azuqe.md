# Azuqe

The OS for Reach

September 19, 2026 We’re building Azuqe an OS for reach

We started building Azuqe as an SEO platform.

But the more we worked on it, the more we realized that SEO is only one part of how a business gets discovered today.

People still search Google, but they also discover businesses through AI assistants, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Quora, Medium, communities, and other places across the web.

And this creates a messy problem for businesses.

You might have great content, but nobody sees it.

You might rank well on Google, but not appear in AI answers.

You might know exactly what content you need to create, but getting it distributed across the right channels is another problem.

You might discover an SEO issue, but fixing it requires another tool, another person, or a developer.

And suddenly, managing “SEO” means jumping between a dozen different platforms.

### That's why we're building Azuqe.

We think businesses need an OS for reach one place to manage how they get discovered across the modern web.

### Create

Azuqe helps you research keywords and topics, analyze competitors and search results, create content, optimize existing content, manage internal linking, and run your content workflow.

But we don't want content creation to mean “ask AI to write an article and publish it.”

Before generating content, Azuqe brings together search data, competitor research, search intent, ranking opportunities, related topics, and other signals to understand what people are actually looking for and what content has a chance of being useful.

The goal is content that is research-driven, useful, and human in how it communicates — not generic AI-generated text that loses the emotion and perspective that makes content worth reading.

### Distribute

This is one of the areas we're particularly excited about.

You can take an article, an idea, or a topic and create platform-specific content for:

- Reddit
- Quora
- LinkedIn
- Medium
- YouTube

The goal isn't to blindly post the same thing everywhere.

It's to make distribution part of the content workflow — while adapting content to each platform's norms and community rules rather than treating every channel like another place to dump links.

### Discover

Azuqe brings together the signals you need to understand where you're visible and where you're missing:

- Keyword rankings
- Technical SEO
- AI visibility
- AI citations
- Competitor visibility
- Backlinks
- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics
- Bing Webmaster Tools
- Traffic analytics

For AI visibility specifically, we don't just want to tell you:

> “Your brand isn't appearing.”

We want to help answer:

> Where are competitors being cited? What sources are influencing the answer? Where are the opportunities? What can you actually do about them?

### Execute

Finding an opportunity is only useful if you can act on it.

Azuqe can help identify technical SEO issues, recommend fixes, execute supported fixes through our website integration, generate implementation-ready changes through MCP, publish content, and automate indexing.

We're also building GitHub CI/CD workflows so SEO can become part of the development process instead of something checked after deployment.

### Connect

We don't want businesses to rebuild their existing stack around Azuqe.

That's why we're building integrations with platforms teams already use — including WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Ghost, Framer, Blogger, Notion, Google Docs, Dev.to, Hashnode, GitHub and more.

### Automate

AIM is our AI execution layer.

Instead of just giving you another dashboard, AIM can work with your SEO and search data to answer questions, identify problems, generate content briefs, create content, recommend actions, trigger indexing, generate reports, and automate repetitive workflows.

The bigger idea is:

Create → Distribute → Get Discovered → Measure → Optimize → Repeat

We don't think the future of search is just about ranking a page on Google.

And we don't think the answer is just another AI visibility dashboard either.

The web is becoming increasingly fragmented.

Search engines. AI assistants. Communities. Social platforms. Video. Content platforms.

Businesses need a way to manage their reach across all of them.

That's what we're trying to build with Azuqe.

We're still early, and we're actively looking for founders, marketers, SEO teams, content teams, and agencies who are dealing with this problem.

I'd genuinely love to hear from you:

How are you currently managing your company's reach across all these different channels?

And if you're already using multiple tools for this, what feels the most painful or fragmented today?
