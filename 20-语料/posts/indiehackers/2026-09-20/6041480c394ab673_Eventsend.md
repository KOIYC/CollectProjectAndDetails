# EventSend

Event tracking and alert routing for SaaS teams

September 19, 2026 Event tracking and alert routing for solo founders and SaaS teams

EventSend is event tracking and alert routing for solo founders and SaaS teams, the two halves of knowing what your product is doing. Send an event with one HTTP POST from any language, or write no code at all: paste one URL into Stripe, GitHub or Vercel and their webhooks arrive formatted and deduplicated. Every event lands in a live feed you can search and filter by name, level, source, customer and date, with operational analytics over it: volume, error rate, delivery health per destination, and twelve months of history on every plan. Filter to one customer and you have their timeline. Then rules route the events that matter to where your team already is - Slack, Discord, Telegram, a signed webhook, or a push notification on your phone - with retries and backoff, circuit breakers around a failing destination, and a log of every attempt. Your AI assistant can read the same history and tell you what happened. Free forever for 2,500 events a month, plans from $12.

## About

I got tired of finding out about failed payments from angry customers. Stripe knew the moment the card was declined, I found out days later, when someone emailed to ask why their account had stopped working.
