September 21, 2026
 Irony: the easiest way to stand out in today's job market is to be more human: genuine, specific, credible, and uniquely you.
Vikram here, founder of graph.
I run an 8-year-old specialist recruitment agency that helps well-funded startups and global enterprises hire Data, AI & Analytics professionals in India.
Over the years, I've realised that most candidates find it difficult to articulate what they've done . Whether it's on their resume, LinkedIn profile or even in interviews, candidates are either too generic, too short, too verbose or too vague, and often assume the reader has the time or willingness to understand what they've done and why it matters.
And tbh, things got especially worse over the last year or so. AI-polished resumes became very good at sounding impressive without necessarily adding any real value for the reader.
graph fixes that.
 You start by uploading your resume
Answer a few targeted questions (I promise this is not yet another long form)
And where possible add evidence from your public artefacts like GitHub, LeetCode, Kaggle, etc.
Along the way, you can claim your personalised URL, e.g. mygraph.id/ , and decide what to showcase publicly and what to keep private.
From there, graph can help you:
 Generate an enriched resume
Prepare for interviews
And build a much more confident answer to the question every candidate eventually faces: “Why should we hire you?”
So if you're a Data, AI or Analytics professional , I'd love for you to try graph and share your feedback in the comments:
 Were the questions around missing context actually helpful , or did they feel superficial?
Did graph help you think about or articulate your work better?
Did you compare your original resume with the graph-generated resume? If yes, what did you think?
Would love your feedback. 🙏
Vikram Parekh
