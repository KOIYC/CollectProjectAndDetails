 I’m building AppHall — a curated directory for useful AI apps and tools
I’ve been working on AppHall , a discovery platform for useful AI apps and tools.
 There are more AI products launching every day, but finding genuinely useful ones is getting harder. Many directories try to list everything, which often makes discovery more difficult instead of easier.
 With AppHall, I want to keep things simple: curate useful products and organize them so people can quickly find tools for work, creativity, productivity, development, business, and everyday tasks.
 The project is still early, and I’m building it as a solo side project.
 Current focus:
 improving the product discovery experience
building better categories, tags, and collections
adding high-quality AI products
improving SEO and product pages
The site is live at apphall.org .
 I’d love to hear how other founders approach curation and discovery for directory-style products.
