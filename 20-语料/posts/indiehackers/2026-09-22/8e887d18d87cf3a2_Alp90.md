 What Is Portable Solar Power?
 Portable solar power systems convert sunlight into electricity through the help of photovoltaic panels. Portable systems are foldable, movable, storable and can be setup anywhere there is sunlight. They can be plugged into a compatible portable power station, battery or another device to create and store energy for later use.

 Why Portable Solar Panels Are Useful
 Flexibility is undoubtedly one of the major benefits of a portable solar panel. A fixed installation uses a specific location, a portable panel can be moved to the site where electricity is required. The technology is especially beneficial to outdoor recreation and emergency preparedness.
 In fact, campers can use the solar panels on long trips without having to depend on fuel-powered generators. You can use solar power to power your RV's electrical systems to supplement them, or you can have portable panels in place at your home as an emergency back-up power source.
One advantage is that during operation solar panels do not burn fuel to produce electricity. After investment in equipment, the basic energy source is sunlight. This can make solar charging a more appealing alternative to traditional generators for those wishing to minimize their reliance on traditional generators.
 Choosing the Right Portable Solar Panel
 When it comes to choosing a panel, it's not just about the wattage. The energy required, the power station to be used, portability, connectors, weatherproofing and available sunlight are factors which should be considered by users.
 The wattage is the panel's rated power at a specific condition. The higher the wattage, the faster a compatible battery or power station can be recharged by the power of the sun, but this is dependent on sunlight, temperature, orientation, shading and other environmental effects.
 It is also important to make sure that the software is compatible. Various power stations will have various input-voltage and connecting needs. Users must verify the input specifications of the battery or power station they have in order to ensure that they will be able to operate safely with the panel they will purchase.
