 Afflemi: The Affiliate Marketing Directory
If you're a publisher, creator or affiliate, browse hundreds of affiliate programs by category, with commission rates, cookie lengths, payout methods and approval terms laid out so you can compare before you apply.
 Alongside programs, you'll find the affiliate networks worth joining and the tools that make the work easier: link cloaking plugins, tracking software and more.
 If you run a brand, Afflemi is where you find what your program needs to grow. Compare affiliate software and tracking platforms, browse affiliate recruitment tools and influencer marketing platforms to find new partners, and shortlist the agencies and OPMs that manage programs for a living.
 Every listing is written in plain language with the facts that matter, and when a vendor doesn't publish its terms, we say so instead of guessing. Vendors can get listed for a one-time fee, no subscriptions.
 One place to find programs, the tools to promote them, and the tools to run them.

 Hey All
I'm an affiliate marketer and a blogger/content creator. I write a lot of articles, on my own sites, for client sites, and just general content that has nothing to do with affiliate marketing. Every article needs links: affiliate links when I'm recommending products, and internal links to other pages on the site.
 Both of those were a mess.
 For affiliate links, I'd have to log into each individual program's dashboard just to grab a link. Multiply that across a dozen programs and it's a real time sink, especially mid-writing flow.
 For internal links, it was worse. Say I'm writing an article on the best solar panels, and I mention solar panel cells partway through. I know I wrote a whole comparison article on Monocrystalline vs Polycrystalline panels months ago, I just don't remember exactly where it is. So I'd end up scrolling through old posts, or on client sites, digging through the CMS or Google dorking (site: theirdomain.com ) just to find the page to link to.
 So I built Afflemi to fix both problems at once. You save your affiliate links and your internal site links in one place, tagged so you can actually find them again. Site links can be imported from a sitemap, uploaded via CSV, or added manually. Affiliate links are added manually or via CSV, since there's no universal API across affiliate networks.
 Then I built a browser extension on top of it. While you're writing, you fire it up, search, for example "Monocrystalline vs Polycrystalline," and drop the link straight in, no tab switching, no digging.
 It's not just for affiliates with blogs either. YouTubers and influencers who recommend products in every video can end up with hundreds of affiliate links scattered across descriptions and old content.
Afflemi gives you one searchable place to save and reuse them. Same for newsletter writers linking back to past issues, or anyone publishing regularly who needs to reuse links without hunting for them each time.
 It's the tool I wished existed while writing content .
