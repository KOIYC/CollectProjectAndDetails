 Connect all your platforms to your own AI assistant and run your business from one place
Hey IH 👋 We're Zeisoft, a small team from İzmir. This month we launched HeyMetra .
 The problem we kept running into. The data that runs a business ends up spread across dozens of separate platforms: CRM, ad panels, your store, subscriptions, search traffic. Each one is a separate login, a separate interface, and even a simple question like "How did last week go?" takes several tabs and several exports. By the time you've pulled it all together, the week is already over. We didn't want yet another interface to check; we wanted to see everything from one place.
 What we built. HeyMetra is a MCP server. You authorize your platforms once, and they all turn into tools that the AI assistant you already use can call: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity, Cursor, and more. You ask in whichever assistant you use, and the answer comes back in the same conversation. We run no model of our own, so there's no per-token cost.
 The best part: a single question can reach multiple platforms at once. It joins two pieces of data that live in different places and have never seen each other into one answer; something a tool that looks at a single source simply can't do.
 It helps different businesses in different ways. If you're an agency, you can connect your clients' ad panels and report on all of them from one place. If you run an ecommerce business, you can see your store, CRM, and search traffic side by side. If you own an app, you can join your attribution and subscription data. If you're a small team, you can get the whole picture with a single question, without needing a separate analyst. The same tool shapes itself around whatever stack you have.
 Key points:
 Read-first. The platforms you connect are read-only; none of them change anything in your account. The one thing it can send is a message to your Slack/Telegram/email, and even that comes to you with its full text for approval first.
Your credentials are never shared with the AI assistants. They stay secure and are never exposed to Claude, ChatGPT, or any other assistant.
An account is not a provider. You can connect the same platform multiple times, for multiple clients, each with its own name and its own access.
Where we are. We launched with an active set of connectors and we're constantly adding more; the list keeps growing across ads, analytics, ecommerce, CRM, subscriptions, and apps. Pricing starts at $9/mo, with a 7-day money-back guarantee.
 What I'd love from you: honest reactions to the "connect once, ask from the AI you already use" model. Does read-only-by-default feel like the right call, or a limitation? And which platform, if we supported it, would make you actually try it?
 Happy to answer anything in the comments. Site: https://heymetra.com
Zeisoft
