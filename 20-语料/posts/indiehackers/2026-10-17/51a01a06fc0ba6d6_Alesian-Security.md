October 16, 2026
 My Show HN was killed on submit
I've spent the last two years, on and off, building Alesian Security. Last Friday, I posted it as a ShowHN, and it never went live. It didn't fail, it never got a chance to.
 Alesian Security watches for lookalike domains (eg. yourcompany.co, y0urcompany.com, yourcompany-billing.com) that are or registered or have a change in disposition, evaluates the risk posed, and provides a path to have the risky domains blocked. I got into this having watched various paths that attackers used to perform Business Email Compromise and was tired of watching people get screwed.
 When I shared my project on Hacker News my post looked live, but I didn't see a comment field. Looking from a logged out window revealed that my post wasn't displaying and I emailed mods asking for help. So far I've not receive a response, my best guess is that a new domain tripped the spam filter.
 I don't think the opportunity on Hacker News is over, but I think my product requires some retooling (and repackaging) to give it another shot. The Hacker News FAQ says:
 If a story has not had significant attention in the last year or so, a small number of reposts is ok. Otherwise we bury reposts as duplicates.
 Please don't delete and repost the same story. Deletion is for things that shouldn't have been submitted in the first place.
 IMO it's fair to say that it just isn't the launch I hoped for.
 Right now I'm sharing my experience (building in public) here on Indie Hackers, commenting in relevant subreddits, working my own network, and basic SEO.
 I still have no accounts created on the site and I haven't observed much in the way of use. Time will tell which of these avenues will have been most success.
 Has anybody else had a launch quietly die like this?
 What actually brought your first ten users?
Jared Stewart
