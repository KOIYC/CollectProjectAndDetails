 Launching Hotel Lobby AI: How I Built a 9:16 Vertical Wan 3.0 Studio for Viral TikTok Meme Videos
Hey Indie Hackers,
 I’m excited to share the launch of my latest project: [Hotel Lobby Video AI]( https://hotellobbyvideoai.app) , a specialized cloud pipeline built to generate 9:16 vertical "Hotel Lobby" COLORS show rap meme videos powered by open-source Wan 3.0.
 The Spark Behind the Project
 Over the last few weeks, the "Hotel Lobby" rap format completely took over TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Creators upload a photo of themselves, their friends, or even their pets, and watch it turn into a cinematic rap performance in front of the iconic COLORS show orange booth with a single hanging condenser mic.
 However, when I tried to recreate these videos using existing tools, I ran into two major roadblocks:
 1. Local ComfyUI Hell : Running Wan 3.0 models locally requires heavy GPU VRAM (at least 16GB+), endless node debugging, and slow render queues.
 2. Stiff Face-Swap Templates : Mobile template apps only warp a 2D face onto existing footage, resulting in robotic motion and zero rhythm consistency.
 How I Built the Solution
 I decided to build a vertical-first web studio dedicated entirely to this workflow:
 - Zero Setup Needed : Users simply upload a single image (portrait, avatar, or pet).
 - Native 9:16 Vertical Format : Fine-tuned Wan 3.0 generation to output cinematic vertical video directly, eliminating clumsy horizontal cropping.
 - Natural Motion & Rhythm : The model synthesizes realistic head nods, mic interactions, and body language synchronized with rap beats.
 Tech Stack & Early Lessons
 - Core Pipeline : Fine-tuned open-source Wan 3.0 video generation models.
 - Frontend / Cloud : Modern Next.js stack with cloud GPU worker instances.
 - Key Takeaway : In the AI video space, hyper-vertical tooling that solves one specific viral format converts far better than general-purpose "text-to-video" generators. Creators want instant results, not prompt engineering homework.
 Check it out & Feedback
 The platform is live now:
 👉 Try it here: [ https://hotellobbyvideoai.app](https://hotellobbyvideoai.app)
 I would love to get your thoughts, bug reports, and feedback from fellow indie builders! What features should I prioritize next?
