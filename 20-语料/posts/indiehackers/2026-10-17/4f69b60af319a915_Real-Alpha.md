 I built a feed of the best minutes of long interviews
Why I built it
I follow the big AI interview shows (Dwarkesh, Latent Space, No Priors, Lenny's, BG2, All-In) to keep up for work, and I'm always behind. What I want from an episode is usually a few minutes somewhere in the middle.
 What it is
Real Alpha: https://realalpha.tv/?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_campaign=launch-post
 Every hour it checks different interview shows for new episodes.
It cuts the ones people are actually watching into one to three moments, about 4 minutes each.
Each moment plays in the show's own YouTube player from the right second, with the transcript underneath. On a phone it's a swipe feed. No account needed.
You can follow topics or people, and they come first. There's an optional email twice a week and one alert a day.
A few decisions
 No video is re-hosted. A moment is the show's own upload, started at the right second.
AI writes the short summary under each moment, but plain code checks every quote against the transcript word for word, and drops any quote that doesn't match.
It only cuts episodes whose YouTube views beat that show's usual bar
Where it is
Live since Oct 14. 185 moments from 73 episodes of 34 shows, with Jensen Huang, Satya Nadella, Yann LeCun, Tobi Lütke and Alexandr Wang among the people in them.
 What I'd like to know
 Did you find a moment worth your time? Which one?
Would you open it again tomorrow? What would make you?
