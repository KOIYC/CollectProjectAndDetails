 I built an internet radio player that lives in the MacBook notch
Hi IH, I'm Fatih. I make small Mac apps, and Notch Radio is the newest one. It's been on the Mac App Store since September.
 I listen to internet radio all day while I work. Every Mac radio app I tried wanted a window or a menu bar popover, so changing station meant finding something, clicking into it and closing it again. The notch sits right where I'm already looking and does nothing, so I put the radio there.
 When it's collapsed it's a thin pill against the notch. Hover over it and it opens into a player with album art, live track titles and controls. Move the pointer away and it folds back. There's nothing to open or close.
 - Thousands of stations from the Radio Browser community directory, searchable by name or genre
 - No account, no analytics, no ads
 - No notch? It runs from the menu bar or draws a notch of its own
 - Free for good with up to 5 stations, plus a one-time unlock for everything else. No subscription.
 I'll post updates here as it grows. I'd love feedback, especially from anyone with an external display or a Mac without a notch.
