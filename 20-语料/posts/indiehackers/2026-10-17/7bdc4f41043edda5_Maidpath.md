October 16, 2026
 We built a free training platform for cleaning businesses. Here's why.
Hey Indie Hackers! 👋
 I'm Amar, founder of ZenMaid, and I wanted to share something we've been working on.
 Over the years, we've worked with thousands of cleaning business owners and noticed a recurring challenge: training new employees is surprisingly difficult to get right.
 Most cleaning businesses rely on shadowing experienced cleaners, scattered documents, or simply hoping new hires remember everything they're taught.
 The result? Inconsistent service, repeated mistakes, and business owners spending more time retraining employees than growing their businesses.
 That's why we built MaidPath.
 MaidPath is a completely free training and onboarding platform designed specifically for cleaning businesses.
 Here's how it works:
 Start with 33 ready-made training modules covering cleaning procedures, safety, customer communication, and more.
Customize everything or create your own training materials.
Share a simple link with new hires. No downloads or employee accounts required.
Track training progress so you know exactly where each employee stands.
And the best part? It's 100% free. No credit card, subscriptions, or hidden fees.
 We wanted to remove the barriers that prevent small cleaning businesses from building consistent training processes.
 I'd love your feedback.
 If you're building a SaaS product for a specific industry, how do you approach solving problems that seem small but have a huge impact on day-to-day operations?
 And if you know anyone running a cleaning business, I'd love for them to give MaidPath a try.
 👉 https://maidpath.com/
 Thanks for checking it out!
 Amar
