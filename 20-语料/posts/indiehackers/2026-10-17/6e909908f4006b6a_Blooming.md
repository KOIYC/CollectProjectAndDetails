October 16, 2026
 We're building 40+ AI agents to replace an agency's 5 marketing tools.
Every agency we talked to had the same stack, repeated for every client: a social scheduler, an email tool, a CRM, a sales outreach tool, and a helpdesk.
 Five logins. Five bills. Client data split five ways. And a team spending a big part of every week copying content between tools and trying not to post one client's content on another client's account.
 So we asked a simple question: what if AI agents did most of that work, and the team only reviewed and approved it?
 That's Blooming ( blooming.sh ).
 What it does
 - 40+ specialised AI agents across social, marketing, CRM, sales and support
 - Social: drafts posts in each brand's voice, schedules and publishes them, and tracks competitors
 - Marketing: writes content, generates images, builds email templates and nurture sequences
 - CRM and sales: meeting summaries, spotting deals that are going cold, forecasting, cold email sequences and lead routing
 - Support: sorts tickets, drafts replies, monitors SLAs and flags VIP customers
 - Every client brand gets its own isolated workspace, with its own agents, brand voice and data
 The team stays in control. Agents do the work, and people approve it before anything goes out.
 It connects with tools like Zapier, Salesforce, Zendesk and Asana, and you can export your data at any time.
 Why we're posting here
 We're opening early access, and we're looking for 10 agencies who'll use it on real client work and tell us where it breaks. We'd rather hear "this agent wrote something no client would approve" now than after a big launch.
 What we'd love to hear
 1. Agency owners: which tool in your stack would you replace first, and which would you never give up?
 2. What would make you trust an AI agent to publish for a client? What's the deal-breaker?
 3. Fellow founders: how do you get early users to give real feedback instead of polite silence?
 If you run an agency and want to be one of the 10, reply here or book a demo at blooming.sh
