September 20, 2026
 Launched my first all-in-one social video downloader on Android (Built with AppCreator24 & AdMob Mediation
Hey Indie Hackers,I wanted to share my latest project that I just pushed live on the Google Play Store: ALL SOCIAL Video Downloader 🚀As a developer building out of Calabar, Nigeria, I noticed a constant friction point for mobile users. People love saving high-quality clips from TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook, and X (Twitter), but they are constantly forced to cycle through slow websites, deal with broken download bots, or settle for ugly, unremovable watermarks.I wanted to build a simple, clean utility app that acts as a 1-tap media saver for almost any major social platform, keeping files entirely local and private to the device.The Tech & Optimization Stack:Development Platform: Built efficiently using AppCreator24 backend dependencies.Monetization Architecture: Configured a high-yielding Google AdMob stack backed by active third-party network mediation. I recently optimized layout positions, transitioning to bottom adaptive banners and high-eCPM interstitials triggered on download completions to keep user experience smooth while driving daily revenue.Core Privacy Feature: Integrated a secure local folder encryption vault to let users lock their private downloaded media galleries away safely.Where I Am Right Now:The app is fully verified and live on the Play Store, holding a perfect 5-star rating from its initial user testing loop! I've spent the last few weeks squashing layout bugs (especially optimization fixes for Redmi status bar clipping) and tuning my App Store Optimization (ASO) keywords for long-tail search phrases.My immediate goal is to map out a clear organic-to-paid scaling blueprint to cross the 5,000 download milestone and unlock a steady, passive AdMob payment loop.I’d love your feedback:If you're on an Android device, please check it out or let me know what you think of the monetization layout and feature selection!👉 Check it out on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=elismandate.videodownloader
 Would love to connect with other indie developers scaling mobile utilities or optimizing ad monetization models in Tier 3 markets!
