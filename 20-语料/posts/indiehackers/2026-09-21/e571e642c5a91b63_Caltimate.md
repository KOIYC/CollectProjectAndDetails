September 20, 2026
 Say it out loud and it is on the calendar
Caltimate is a calendar that runs on the Mac itself and syncs with Google Calendar.
 Hit control option space from anywhere, say "tomorrow 3pm client meeting", and it's on the calendar. There's no window to find first.
 It pulls the meeting link out of the invitation so joining is one click, and it measures the travel time from wherever you are, so the alert to leave shows up before you'd thought to work it out.
 Fourteen days free, no card. Then it's 4 a month, 29 a year, or once and done. One licence covers three Macs. macOS 14 and later.
 The calendar that ships with the Mac is free and already open, which is a hard thing to argue with. If you left it, what was the thing that finally did it?
