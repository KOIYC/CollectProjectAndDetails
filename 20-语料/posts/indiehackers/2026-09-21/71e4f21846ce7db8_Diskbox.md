 I built a Windows app because I kept running out of disk space
So I built DiskBOX — a Windows disk analyzer that helps you visually understand your storage and find things worth cleaning.
 The idea is simple:
 See exactly what's consuming your disk
Search and analyze files quickly
Find duplicate files with content verification
Discover large Steam/Epic game installations
Clean unwanted files safely
Understand where your storage is going instead of randomly deleting folders
The goal isn't another "PC cleaner" that runs a mysterious scan and tells you to buy a subscription.
 I wanted something more transparent: show me what's taking space, let me decide what to do.
 I've just put it online:
 https://diskbox.app
 I'm curious about something from other indie hackers:
 When your own computer runs out of storage, what's the most frustrating part — finding what's taking space, identifying duplicates, or deciding what is actually safe to delete?
 I'd love to hear how you currently solve this.
