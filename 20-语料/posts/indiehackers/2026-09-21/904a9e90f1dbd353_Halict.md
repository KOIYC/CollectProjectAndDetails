 The form is the easy half
The form is the easy half. What falls apart is everything after it.
 Responses land in a spreadsheet. Someone adds an owner column and a status column by hand, the reply goes out from a mail client, and a week later nobody can say whether it was answered, let alone read.
 Halict keeps the response, its owner, its status and the reply on one screen. You reply from there, and the opens of that reply are recorded, so "did they see it" isn't a guess any more.
 Building the form is twelve question types dragged into the order you want. Say "a pre interview questionnaire" and the first draft comes out built, then you fix what's wrong with it.
 Free for one person. 39 a month for three, 99 for ten. Forms and responses are unlimited on every plan.
 Where do your form responses stop moving right now?
