 Eight kinds of board on day one, Gantt included, in the free plan
In most project tools the Gantt chart is what you get after you pay.
 Pinateca has eight kinds of board and they're all in the free plan. Kanban, Gantt, calendar, timetable, list and the rest, with the chat sitting next to them.
 What moves the price is the size of the team, not the feature you reached for. Five people and ten boards are free. Past that it's 5 per person a month, or 4 if you pay for the year.
 A Trello board comes across in one click, cards and lists intact.
 If you run a small team: which board do you open every day, and which one did you set up once and never look at again?
