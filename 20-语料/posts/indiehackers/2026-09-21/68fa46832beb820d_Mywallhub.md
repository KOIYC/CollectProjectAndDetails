MyWallHub Free creator-focused wallpaper ecosystem for Phone & PC
 Post 1

 Website
September 20, 2026
 Solo-built wallpaper ecosystem for independent artists across web, Android and Windows.
I wanted to see what happens if those pieces are connected.
 Creators can publish artwork for Phone, PC or both , build public profiles and collections, control downloads, create private galleries, and place their own external links alongside individual artworks.
 On the user side, the web platform connects with Android and Windows apps , with synchronized Favorites, wallpaper sources, controls and automatic rotation.
 There’s also an optional Original Sales system for creators who want to offer protected high-resolution files, but that’s an additional feature rather than the core of the product.
 MyWallHub is free to use and currently in public beta. The first outside creators have started joining and uploading, which means I’m now spending much more time on the difficult part: discovery, creator recruitment and figuring out how to get past the cold-start stage.
 I’d especially appreciate feedback on two things:
 Does the product positioning make sense when you first see it?
 And does the creator + wallpaper-management combination feel useful, or too broad?
