 Building Helion: a full exchange & DeFi hub on BNB Chain (looking for feedback)
Hey Indie Hackers 👋
I’m building Helion Network Exchange — a full exchange and DeFi hub on BNB Chain.
The problem
 Most people still bounce between wallets, DEXs, CEXs, and DeFi apps just to do simple things: hold, swap, trade, and earn. The tools are powerful, but the experience is fragmented.
What Helion is
 One product where you can:
 • use a Helion wallet
 • trade spot
 • swap tokens
 • stake / earn
 • send and manage assets
Goal: lower friction for everyday users, then scale the exchange side over time.
Why I’m building it
 I got tired of the “five tabs to do one task” workflow. I want trade + DeFi in the same place — clearer UX, fewer steps, one account.
Where I’m at
 Early public stage. Core flows are live and I’m iterating fast based on real usage.
What I need from you
 Honest feedback:
 1. Does the positioning make sense? (“exchange + DeFi hub” vs something narrower)
 2. What would make you actually try a new trading / DeFi product?
 3. What feels like the biggest trust barrier for custodial + on-chain products?
Happy to answer anything about the product, stack, or roadmap.
Thanks — building in public and learning as I go.
