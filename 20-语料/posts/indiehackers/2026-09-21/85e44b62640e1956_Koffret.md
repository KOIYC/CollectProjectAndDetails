 The menu bar icons you never read, put behind a divider
A Mac menu bar fills up with icons that are only there for a setting you change twice a year.
 Koffret pushes those to the left of a divider and frees the space around the clock. Press the crest and they come back exactly as they're drawn at that moment, so percentages, badges and little graphs are the real ones. Nothing gets swapped for a lookalike.
 Their menus open while they stay hidden, which is the part I use most.
 14.99 once. Not a subscription. Fourteen days free with no card, macOS 14 and later.
 How many icons are sitting in your menu bar right now, and how many do you actually read?
