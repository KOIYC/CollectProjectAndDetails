 I built a link in bio page with crypto tipping built in. Looking for feedback
Hey IH,
 I've been building TipaToken, a link in bio page where fans can tip creators directly in crypto.
 The problem: creators usually need one tool for their links (Linktree) and another for tips (Buy Me a Coffee), and the tipping side takes a cut of every payment. TipaToken puts both on one page. Tips go straight to the creator's wallet, on multiple chains, with zero platform fees.
 It's in live beta now, so it works, but there's a lot I'm still figuring out. I'd love your take on:
 Would you use this as a creator, or does crypto tipping feel too niche?
 What would make you switch from your current link in bio tool?
 What's the first thing that looks confusing or missing?
 Try it here: Tipatoken
 Thanks for reading, and happy to answer anything in the comments.
