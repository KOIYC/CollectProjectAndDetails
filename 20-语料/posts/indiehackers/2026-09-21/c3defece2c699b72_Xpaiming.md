 I built xpaiming — public X rankings so agencies don’t refresh profiles all day
A few weeks ago I kept hearing the same complaint from people who manage client X accounts: you only find out something broke after the client asks.
 Follower drops. Posting stops. An account gets limited or suspended. The “workflow” was still: open twenty profiles, scroll, screenshot, repeat.
 So I shipped xpaiming — a public rankings site for X accounts.
 What it is
 Public rankings (no login): today’s growth, champions, posts, and country views
Optional “My Monitors” (sign-in): watch the handles you care about
Honest cadence: data refreshes about every 6 hours — not live, not “realtime”
Product: https://xpaiming.com/en
 Countries: https://xpaiming.com/en/countries
 Why public rankings first
 I wanted a shareable front door. If the rankings are useful, people link them. If someone needs client-side monitoring, they sign in. Growth first, then conversion — not a locked dashboard from day one.
 What “good” looks like for us
 Rankings stay readable without pretending to be live ticks
When a data source fails, we show unknown instead of silently reusing old “healthy” numbers
Country views and account deep links are meant to be shared (with UTM when we post)
What’s next
 Stabilize collection, tighten the monitor path for agencies, and keep publishing daily ranking snapshots people can actually forward.
 If you manage competitor or client handles on X, I’d love blunt feedback: what’s missing before you’d trust this daily?
