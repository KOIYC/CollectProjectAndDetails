 I'm building the subtitle tool I've been wishing existed
If you're learning a language through shows/anime, you know the subtitle problem:
Subs don't match the dub. They're usually way simpler than what's actually being said - some are decent, most aren't.
Looking up a word kills your flow. Retype it into a translator (slow) or screenshot + AI (slower). Either way, you've lost the scene.
So I'm building a fix. Not a bloated app - a lightweight Chrome extension.
How it works: open your browser, hit play on any video. Subtitles generate automatically. See a word you don't know? Click it - a popup shows the full sentence with that word highlighted and translated, right in context.
No account. No setup. Just works.
 Would you use this? What's missing?

Wanna tell you that I'm kinda tired of subtitles nowadays, it sucks and the reasons are obviously simple.
 You see that I learn English by watching some videos / animes and there are two options:
 Subtitles do not match with characters' current speech, and what I saw, that subtitle variant is way simpler than dub one, some subs are decent, but most aren't.
If you need to translate some unknown word, you need to retype it into a translator (too looong) or get the screenshot, send it to AI and wait a translation (too long), also it ruins the flow

 Practical use will be like open your browser, turn on some video and enjoy. Subtitles are generated automatically, when you encounter some unknown word, click on it, and the popup with full sentence with highlighted word will appear translated.
