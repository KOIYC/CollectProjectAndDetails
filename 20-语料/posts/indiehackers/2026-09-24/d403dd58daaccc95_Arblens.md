# ArbLens

Where is my crypto capital right now?

September 22, 2026 You're running capital across multiple exchanges and wallets.

ArbLens fixes this.

Connect your exchanges via read-only API keys. ArbLens aggregates everything into one dashboard — current balances with live mark-price updates, open positions with real-time unrealized PnL, trade fill history, funding payments, and deposit/withdrawal records across 21 exchanges and on-chain wallets. Mark prices stream every 10 seconds via SSE. No page reload, no manual refresh.

Supported: 21 exchanges and chains Binance · Bybit · OKX · Gate.io · KuCoin · MEXC · Bitget · HTX · BingX · Phemex · LBank · CoinEx · XT.com · Backpack · HotCoin · Hyperliquid · Paradex · AsterDex · GRVT · Lighter · WhiteBIT · On-chain wallets (EVM)

What you get:

- Total portfolio equity across all accounts, current balances with live mark-price overlay
- Open positions: size, leverage, entry, liquidation, real-time PnL via SSE stream
- Trade fill history with incremental sync (active accounts every 5 min)
- Funding payment ledger per symbol
- Deposit & withdrawal tracker with TXID and network
- Telegram alerts: deposits, position events, API expiry, daily digest

Security: API keys are Fernet-encrypted before storage. ArbLens is read-only — it cannot place, modify or cancel orders.

Built for arbitrage traders, multi-venue operators and small funds who need capital clarity in under 3 seconds.
