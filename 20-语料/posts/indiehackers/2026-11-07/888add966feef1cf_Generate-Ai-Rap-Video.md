 I turned 1,003 credits of AI video testing into a 7-template product
I tested six AI video templates with real paid runs before selling anything: 1,003 generation credits total.
What worked: the Bulin 47 street freestyle template succeeded on the first paid run (493 credits) and became the flagship. A rap duo template took three attempts — the model provider's content filter rejected every run that referenced the copyrighted song, even the silent variant. Dropping the copyrighted audio entirely made it pass (510 credits), shipping silent so users attach the trending sound in TikTok themselves.
Three failure modes that actually matter:
1. Copyright audio is a hard wall, not a risk. The filter rejects the song reference and the silent render tied to the same performance. Product scope is the fix, not engineering.
2. Motion references are dependencies. Scenes with one central performer survive the face swap; multi-person scenes fight the model.
3. Photo quality decides most of the outcome. Front-facing, well-lit, both hands visible. The fix is a checklist on each template page, not a model tweak.
The result is a product with seven templates, one-time packs from $9.99, no subscription, and refunds on failed renders.
