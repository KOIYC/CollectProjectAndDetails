November 8, 2026
 Built and Launched this Tool in 2 Hours (20+ Visitors in Day 1)
Yesterday, I had a simple idea for a micro-tool: a quick way for founders to check their Domain Rating (DR4).
 Instead of adding it to a massive to-do list or planning it for "someday," I decided to sprint.
 In about 2 hours total , I:
 Coded the app DR4 Check
Bought the domain name
Deployed and hosted it live on the web
Within less than 24 hours of pushing it live, over 20 visitors have already used it to check their DR4 score.
 It’s not a huge number yet, but seeing real people use something that didn’t even exist 24 hours ago feels amazing.
 Here is what this mini-project reminded me about building software:
 Momentum is everything: When you get an idea, your energy is at its absolute peak. If you wait, that energy dies. Shipping fast keeps the excitement alive.
Overthinking kills projects: Spending weeks planning a micro-tool turns a fun 2-hour project into a stressful chore.
Tiny wins add up: Even a tiny project with 20 day-one users gives you quick proof of concept and a push to keep building.
If you have a small idea sitting in your head right now, don't overcomplicate it. Give yourself a 2-hour timer, build the simplest version possible, buy the domain, and put it out there!
