October 14, 2026
 Every idea deserves to be fully thought out
I guess it started out with how branching currently works on major AI Chat providers. You regenerate a message and boom, the message version is now at "2/2".
 Ok.
 What happened to the previous version of the message? What if it wasn't too bad and I want to reference things from it, while also keeping the newly regenerated messages.
 Why cant I have both?
And that was what led me on a journey to discover what an AI conversation could look like besides a chat.
 And the answer?
 A free-flowing canvas.
