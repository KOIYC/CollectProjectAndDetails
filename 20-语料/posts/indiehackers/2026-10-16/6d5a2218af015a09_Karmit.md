October 14, 2026
 I quit my job in July, shipped 6 products, and learned that building is the easy part
Hi IH 👋
 In July I quit my job to go indie. No prior experience shipping products, no audience, no network. Just a lot of motivation.
 Since then I’ve shipped 6 products. Here’s the honest lesson: building turned out to be the easy part. Distribution is where I kept failing.
 Reddit looked like the obvious answer
 Everyone says “go where your users are”, and for a lot of niches that’s Reddit. So I tried. Here’s what happened:
 My first launch post got removed within 20 minutes. No warning, just a bot message saying I didn’t meet the subreddit’s karma requirement. I didn’t even know that was a thing.
I spent hours scrolling through subreddits trying to find where my users hang out. I picked one with 500k members. Turns out most of them were lurkers, and the active ones were a completely different audience from who I was building for.
I posted the exact same text in three subreddits. It did okay in one and died in the other two. Same content, different culture, and I had no way of knowing that beforehand.
The problem wasn’t Reddit. It was that I was treating every subreddit the same. Each community has its own culture, unwritten rules and taste, and I had no efficient way to learn them before posting.
 What I wanted (and couldn’t find)
 Find the subreddits where my target users actually talk about the problem I solve
Understand what works in each community before posting, by studying its top posts
Know when people mention my product, my competitors or my problem space, in real time, while the conversation is still alive
Turn all those mentions into actual product and marketing insights
So I built it for myself. It’s called Karmit .
 Where it is now
 Karmit is in early access.
 I’m not looking for “great product!” comments. I’d love honest feedback:
 How do you currently find the right communities for your product?
What’s the worst thing that happened to you when promoting on Reddit?
Would real-time mention monitoring actually change how you engage, or is it noise?
I’ll share everything I learn (numbers included) as I go. Thanks for reading 🙏
