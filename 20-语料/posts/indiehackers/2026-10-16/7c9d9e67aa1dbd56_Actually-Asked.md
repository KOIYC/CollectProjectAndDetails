 I launched an AI FAQ last week: 7 signups, $12 MRR, and $20 for 14 clicks
Hey IH, Petar here. Solo, writing Rails for 12+ years, full-time on my own stuff since August.
 I wrote the plan for Actually Asked by September 30 and launched it on October 7. It's an AI FAQ section for your site. Visitors ask in their own words, or highlight a sentence and ask about it, and it answers from your own pages. The questions people keep asking get added to the FAQ by themselves.
 A week in: 7 people signed up. 5 of them installed it. $12 MRR that will hopefully grow.
 I think the problem is still signing up. The visitors number is good and steady, but getting people to signup is the main challenge. That's the first bottleneck I need to work on next.
 What I've tried so far:
 $20 for a day at #1 on outbid.lol . 14 clicks, 0 signups.
Posting about it on X every day. My views went from 200–350 a post to about 100. People followed me for founder stuff, not FAQ software.
Getting it listed where people already look. Claude approved it as a connector, Framer listed the plugin on Monday after three automated reviews in one day, and a few MCP listing websites approved it. Shopify rejected the app once. It's back in review, and so is the ChatGPT app.
The part I'm happiest with: setting it up on my other product took one prompt through the MCP server. By hand it was 20 to 30 minutes on all pages.
 You can try it without signing up. The FAQ at the bottom of actuallyasked.com is the product running on its own site. Ask it anything, or highlight any sentence on the website and ask about it.
 How did you get your first users?
Petar Risteski
