 Chartgaze gives Claude, Chatgpt, grok.. superpowers
I started ChartGaze because I ran into this particular AI short-sight problem myself.
 I’m a trader, and I wanted to make better-informed decisions without constantly switching between charts, news, broker dashboards, and AI chats. I first built TrainFlow AI to help traders automate parts of their workflow. But as I worked on it, I realized the market data and account tools behind it could help far more people if they weren’t locked inside one product.
 That idea became ChartGaze: a way to give Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, and other compatible AI tools access to live market information and the trading accounts a user chooses to connect. You can ask about price trends across timeframes, check economic news, create alerts and watchlists, and bring your account information into the same conversation. Trading is read-only by default; users can choose to require approval for proposed trades or enable live trading themselves.
 We’re opening up the SDKs and integration tools so developers can bring ChartGaze into their own apps, rather than having to build all of that market and account infrastructure from scratch. The goal is simple: give more traders access to the kind of tools we wanted for ourselves, and let them use those tools wherever they already work.
