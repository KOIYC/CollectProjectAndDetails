October 13, 2026
 My agents help me build apps. I want my apps to call them.
I want to take a useful agent workflow, give it an input and a result, and call it from my code. Then combine it with another workflow. Then share it so someone else can build on it.
FLOW is an independent standard for packaging those reusable methods. They can use code, AI agents, or both, and run through a compatible host.
What excites me as a builder is borrowing someone's great research method, adding my own checks, and turning that combination into a feature.
If you already use agents to build your product, what work would you want your product to call on its own?
