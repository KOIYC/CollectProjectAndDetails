 Documentation must not be that hard
Like many of you, I start multiple projects. Every new idea, after a short validation (sometimes without any), transforms into an application, and every application needs a website, and every website needs some way to be promoted.
 One of very effective way to be noticed (and to look professionally and trustworthy) is to provide an app documentation. Like this:
 It must not be complicated, it may consist only of several pages explaining how to work with an app. Even though it could seem unnecessarily for small project, for me, as an end-user, it's a crucial thing: before buying, not, before starting a trial, no, even before giving someone my email, I always check their documentation, and leave immediately if found nothing.
 So, as a developer, I always tried to make things myself. But the documentation is not that easy to make: not only it's still challenge to create a nice UI and UX, but adding controls to create and update content and change such things as palette or icons, are still not easy, even with AI.
 I tried several open-source solutions and even used one in production, but it was always a challenge to update it.
 The installation is another story: I see that the absolute most of founders install their documentation to the subdomain, not a subfolder which look much more professionally and brings benefits from SEO point of view.
 Why is that? Because it's crazy hard. I figured it out for Linux and provide this for my documentation, see what you should do to set up the documentation under your custom folder:
 But I guess not everyone is using Linux and may have direct access to it.
 I want to provide as many ways to integrate the great documentation into your website as possible.
 Can you please answer in comments:
 do you have documentation?
how is it organized - under subdomain or subfolder?
what do you use for it?
where do you host your website?
I also want to give those who answer free LIFETIME access to the PRO plan ($29/month, unlimited projects, 500 pages). All you need to do is to answer my questions and give me your email (to change the limits after you register for free), you can send it to info@kitedocs.com
