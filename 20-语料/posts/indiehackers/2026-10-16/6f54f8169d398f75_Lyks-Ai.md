 Launching Lyks: UGC videos for Shopify stores, no creators needed
The problem
 Short-form video is one of the best ways to sell online right now, especially UGC-style ads on TikTok, Reels, and Meta. But for small Shopify merchants, getting that content is hard:
 Creators charge [$100–300+] per video
Briefing, shipping samples, and revisions take weeks
You need lots of variations to test ads, which gets expensive fast
So most small stores end up with no video at all.
 What Lyks does
 Connect your Shopify store
Pick a product
Get ready-to-use UGC-style and product videos in minutes
No creators, no samples to ship, no editing skills needed. You can use the videos for ads, product pages, and social.
 Where we are
 We’re a small team, and Lyks is live
 What we’d love from you
 Feedback on the product and the landing page
If you run a Shopify store: what’s your biggest headache with video content?
Anything you’d want us to build next
Happy to answer any questions in the comments.
