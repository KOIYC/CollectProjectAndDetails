October 15, 2026
 Getting ready for our Oct. 20 launch
We not only wanted to show off some nice logo on our website, we also wanted to capture why these people were trusting us, what they saw in us and how Lens was solving a real problem for them.
 Here are two quotes we received (that we'll be able to show on our new website directly) -- one of them coming from The Linux Foundation:
 "Our buyers now start in ChatGPT and Claude, long before they ever hit our site and those engines were getting us wrong. They lumped us in with vendors we don’t compete with. When we tested our top buyer questions, all of them came back wrong. Lens lets us feed those engines from a governed AI knowledge layer, and we’re already seeing them describe us correctly. We expect that to show up as cleaner first calls, with less time spent correcting the record.”
 "I can't wait to put Lens to work. It gives us a better way to help customers in the moment, and every question they ask gives us insight we've never had before. Better experience for them, better decisions for us."
 On to the remaining tasks now (still a growing list haha).
