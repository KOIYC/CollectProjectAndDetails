October 10, 2026
 I built free screen sharing by link—can a first-time viewer join easily?
I’m the solo developer behind TabConnect, a free way to share a browser tab, window or screen by link.
 The host installs the Chrome or Edge extension. Viewers open the link in their browser, enter a name and join—without creating an account or installing anything.
 A quick walkthrough:
 1. Open the extension and start a sharing session.
 2. Choose the tab, window or screen to share.
 3. Copy the viewer link and open it on another device.
 There are host controls for the room, plus chat and pointer options. The website has screenshots of the host popup and viewer: https://tabconnect.app/
 The part I’d most like to improve is the first minute. If you try it, did the viewer link make the next step obvious? Please include your browser, whether you joined from a phone or computer, and any step that caused friction.
 TabConnect is free and one of my solo side projects.
