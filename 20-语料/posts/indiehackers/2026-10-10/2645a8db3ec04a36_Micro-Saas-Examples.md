May 7, 2026
 I’m sharing one micro SaaS idea worth building every week
I spent the last year collecting and studying micro SaaS ideas, and I noticed one pattern:
 The best ideas don’t look like startup ideas at first.
 They usually hide in plain sight:
 repeated Reddit complaints
small tools with ugly UIs
boring niches nobody talks about
failed launches with real reviews
communities where people vent every week
That’s what led me to build Micro SaaS Examples , a website where founders can discover real micro SaaS products, examples, and niche ideas.
 Now I’m launching a weekly newsletter for founders who want fresh micro SaaS ideas in their inbox.
 Every Wednesday, I’ll share:
 one micro SaaS idea worth building
interesting products from across the internet
market gaps and patterns I’m noticing
one fun product idea of the week
The latest edition is about a 3D city for Instagram creators, a fun example of how creator tools, gamification, and social proof can become micro SaaS opportunities.
 You can read it here:
 https://microsaasexamples.substack.com/p/a-3d-city-for-instagram-creators
 And subscribe here:
 https://microsaasexamples.substack.com/
 Would love to hear your feedback, especially from indie hackers who are always looking for their next small, profitable software idea.

July 30, 2024
 How I started Micro SaaS Examples
As a solopreneur with three years of freelancing experience, I love building quick product tests using no-code tools. This hands-on work has taught me how to turn ideas into real products fast.
 I've always enjoyed finding and sharing small software tools that solve specific problems. This led me to create Micro SaaS Examples - a simple directory where people can discover and share their own micro SaaS products.
 I built this platform to help others find new ideas and the right tools to build their own successful products. Whether you're just starting out or have been running your business for years, Micro SaaS Examples is here to help you see what's possible in the world of small software businesses.
