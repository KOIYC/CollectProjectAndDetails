 The smallest choices can make the biggest difference.
One meal doesn't define a healthy routine. But the small decisions made every day can add up, and understanding them is often the first step toward meaningful change.
 That's the thinking behind QuarkTracker , an Android app designed to make food and nutrition tracking simpler, more intuitive, and more meaningful.
 A clearer picture of everyday wellbeing
 Food tracking is more than counting calories. It's about understanding nutritional intake, recognizing patterns, and discovering how everyday choices contribute to overall wellbeing.
 QuarkTracker brings these insights together in one place, making it easier to:
 Track food and nutrition to better understand daily eating habits.
Monitor hydration and stay aware of water intake.
Follow physical activity and everyday movement.
Track sleep and habits for a broader view of wellbeing.
Explore progress and trends to recognize patterns over time.
Set personal goals and follow progress at an individual pace.
Built around simplicity and consistency
 The goal isn't perfection or tracking every possible detail. It's making useful information accessible, helping people build awareness, and encouraging small, sustainable improvements.
 Because meaningful progress doesn't always require dramatic changes. Sometimes, it begins with understanding what's already happening.
 QuarkTracker offers free core functionality, with an optional one-time Pro upgrade for additional features. No recurring subscription is required.
 Available on Android
 QuarkTracker is available on Google Play:
 https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=gr.aspectsoft.quarktracker
 QuarkTracker continues to evolve, with a focus on thoughtful design, practical improvements, and making everyday tracking more useful and accessible.
 What makes a tracking app truly valuable isn't necessarily how much it can measure, but how naturally it fits into everyday life.
 Feedback, ideas, and different perspectives are always welcome. Even the smallest insights can inspire meaningful improvements.
