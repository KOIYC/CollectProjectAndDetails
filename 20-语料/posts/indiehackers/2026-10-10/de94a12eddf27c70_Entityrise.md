 EntityRise: measuring how AI assistants describe and recommend companies
Hi Indie Hackers, I'm Kamil, founder of Smophy Labs. Some of you know SmophyAI. EntityRise is our second product.
 Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity for vendor shortlists before they visit a website. In a G2 survey of 1,076 B2B software buyers, 51% start their research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google. If an assistant describes you wrongly or leaves you out, you never hear about it.
 Each EntityRise audit asks 20 buyer questions across five AI providers, seals the question set with a SHA-256 hash and records the model versions, so a later run compares exactly the same questions. Then we fix what keeps a company out of the answers with technical, content and authority work.
 Here I'll share what we learn from measuring AI answers, including what does not work. Recent example: Hashnode's auto-moderation removed four of our syndicated posts in one evening, and the lesson turned out to be about topic, not wording.
 Happy to compare notes with anyone tracking how AI assistants mention their product.
Kamil Kępiński
