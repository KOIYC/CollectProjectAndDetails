 How a launch moves through IndieNeed
A quick look at how IndieNeed works right now, for anyone thinking of launching there.
 You submit your product with a short description and a launch date. It gets a quick review, then waits as scheduled until its day comes. On that day it goes live on the launch page, where makers and visitors can find it. Makers can also add a dofollow link to their listing if they want one.
 If you have something to launch, it is free to list on indieneed.com.
