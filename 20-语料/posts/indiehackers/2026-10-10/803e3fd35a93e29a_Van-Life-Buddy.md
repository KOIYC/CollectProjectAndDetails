October 6, 2026
 I built Van Life Buddy in a van — now it’s a real tool for nomads
I’m living the van‑life grind myself, and I kept running into the same problem: finding essentials fast — Planet Fitness, showers, overnight spots, water, gas, repairs, coffee, safe areas, all the stuff you need when you live on the road.
 So I built Van Life Buddy (VLB) in 3 days.
 It’s a free, lightweight companion app for nomads. No accounts, no friction, just open the map and go. It’s built for speed, clarity, and real‑world use. I’m already using it daily.
 Core features:
 Planet Fitness finder
Showers & overnight spots
Water & gas
Repairs & coffee
Saved locations
Clean mobile UI
Fast PWA load times
I’m launching it publicly today and would love feedback from fellow builders, travelers, and anyone who likes shipping fast.
 Live app: https://vanlifebuddy.com
 Happy to answer anything about the build, stack, or the van‑life chaos that inspired it.
