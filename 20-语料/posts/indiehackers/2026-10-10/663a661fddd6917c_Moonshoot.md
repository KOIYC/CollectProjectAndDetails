i almost gave up after 2 days of zero users.
hey ih, first post here.
 i'm beka, from georgia. i built moonshoot: one shared rocket to the moon. everyone pushes it 1 km, once, free, and your project rides on your km. it gets its own page with your logo, link and where you are right now.
 launched on sep 25.
 by sep 27 it was stuck at 12 km. two days, zero pushes.
 the feedback i got:
 "not clear"
 "looks vibe coded"
 "click, get a certificate, no value"
 all true.
 what i changed
 - your km became a page for your project, not a certificate
 - you push first, sign in after
 - the first 100 became the "space crew"
 the day after i posted about it: 128 visitors, 25 pushes. best day so far.
 on oct 6 we hit 100 km. today it's at 111. people from 47 countries.
 what i learned the hard way
 1. traffic = me posting. that's it.
 when i post, ~100 people a day come. i stopped for 3 days and it went 29, 16, 11, 7.
 2. the sign in window was my biggest leak.
 about 1 in 4 visitors hold the button. but of 30 who gonly 8 started signing in. 21 pressed close. on phones itwas 17 to 3.
 it wasn't the login. it was that the window never said
 3. design matters more than i wanted to admit.
 "vibe coded" came up again and again. so i redesigned everything. 4 rounds, threw away the first two. shipped it last night.
 visitors since: 10. a new design doesn't bring people, .
 my favourite part
 the one line people write on their km.
 "first user". "day 10". "trying to grow." "producthunt launch tomorrow!"
 i read every one.
 what i'm stuck on
 people push once and don't come back. only 12 of 111 evtheir project.
 if you've built something where people come once, how dto come back?
