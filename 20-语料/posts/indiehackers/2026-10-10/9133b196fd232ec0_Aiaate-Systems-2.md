October 7, 2026
 Have an idea but no clear next step? I built Aiaate to help.
Most founders don’t get stuck because there are no possible actions.
 They get stuck because too many actions seem possible — and it’s unclear which one matters now.
 At the idea stage, you may not know what to validate before building an MVP.
 After launching an MVP, you may not know why people are not using it or what to focus on next.
 I’m Anastasia, a founder building Aiaate Systems.
 Aiaate is an AI platform for aspiring and early-stage entrepreneurs. Tell us where you are, what you’re trying to build, and what’s getting in the way. Aiaate helps you identify your next practical step and find the right resources, support, and fellow founders to move forward.
 You can come with:
 only a business idea;
an idea you want to validate;
an MVP that is not moving forward;
a product with no clear path to the first customers.
For example:
 “I have an idea for a service for small businesses. I can work on it a few evenings a week, but I don’t know what to test before building.”
 Or:
 “I have an MVP, but I’m not getting consistent users. What should I focus on next?”
 Aiaate is in its first MVP stage, and I’m opening it to early users.
 Bring one real entrepreneurial question and try it here:
 https://aiaate.com
Anastasia
