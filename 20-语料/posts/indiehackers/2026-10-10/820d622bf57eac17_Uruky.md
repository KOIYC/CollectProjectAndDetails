October 9, 2026
 Uruky reaches 400 monthly active accounts!
Here are some of the top updates over the last few weeks for Uruky:
 New OURRS search provider for research-focused queries
Added advanced domain boosting (up to 500 domains/TLDs, CSV import/export)
Added advanced URL rewrites (up to 150 domains/rules, CSV import/export)
Added support for @tags (adds site:domain parameters to your query automatically), optionally supporting Kagi Snaps
Added scoped searches (our take on improving Kagi lenses)
Official Onion/TOR service
The indie/small web index has doubled and improved, still < 750k URLs, though
Many UI/UX improvements throughout the landing pages and settings
We’ve reached 400 monthly active accounts! Thank you so much for your support, it’s impressive!
Next up, we’re evaluating how to add a sort of “auto-complete” and “Did you mean X” optional features, which have been heavily requested. We want to make sure they’re good, private, and don’t increase our costs substantially, which is what’s making them hard to evaluate.
 Thanks for reading this!
