 Launching WanAI 3.0: Tackling 30-Second Long AI Video & Character Consistency
Hey Indie Hackers! 👋
 I’m excited to share a milestone project we’ve been working on: WanAI 3.0 ( https://wan30ai.app ).
 For founders, content marketers, and solo creators, video is the highest-converting medium—yet producing it remains one of the biggest bottlenecks. While generative video has advanced rapidly, mainstream tools still suffer from persistent practical issues:
 1. The "3-to-5 Second Wall": Most models only render micro-clips, requiring tedious stitching that breaks visual momentum.
 2. Identity Drift: Protagonists often warp or change facial features halfway through dynamic motion.
 3. Temporal Jitter: Unstable frame coherence that makes commercial deliverables unusable without heavy inpainting.
 What We Solved with Wan 3.0 Architecture:
 To overcome these hurdles, we developed WanAI 3.0 with native spatiotemporal coherence:
 - Extended 30-Second Generation: Generates long, unbroken takes that maintain lighting ratios and geometric consistency.
 - Multi-Character Consistency: Retains facial structure, wardrobe, and proportions across sequential actions.
 - Cinematic Controls: Smooth pans, tracking shots, and cinematic camera physics directly prompted in text.
 Try It Out & Share Your Feedback:
 We’ve set up a free trial (25 credits) so fellow indie builders can test prompt adherence and video workflows without friction:
 👉 Explore the tool here: https://wan30ai.app
 I’d love to hear from other creators and founders in the community:
 - What are your biggest hurdles when creating marketing video assets?
 - How are you incorporating generative media into your current product sprints?
 Looking forward to your thoughts and feedback!
