 Why We Built an Offsite Cost Calculator for Corporate Teams
Planning a corporate offsite often starts with too much guesswork. Teams usually begin contacting hotels, transport vendors, and activity providers before they know what a realistic budget should look like.
 We built the Offistrip Offsite Cost Calculator to make that first step easier. It helps companies estimate an approximate offsite budget based on factors such as team size, destination, stay duration, hotel category, transport, meals, and activities.
 The goal is simple: give HR, Admin, founders, and event teams a useful starting point before they spend time requesting and comparing multiple quotes.
 You can try it here:
 https://calculator.offistrip.com/
