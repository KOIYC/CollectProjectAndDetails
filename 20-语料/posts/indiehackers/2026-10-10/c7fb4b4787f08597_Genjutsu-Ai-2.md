 I built an AI video tool for changing what’s in a video, not recreating it
Most AI video tools start with a prompt and generate a new video from scratch.
 But I kept running into a different problem: sometimes the source video is already good — the motion, timing, camera movement, and composition all work. I only want to change one object, product, or part of the scene.
 That’s why I built Genjutsu AI .
 The idea is simple: upload an existing video, provide reference images, and transform only what you want while keeping as much of the original video as possible.
 I’m currently experimenting with workflows like object swaps and motion-based transformations, and I’m still figuring out where this is genuinely useful versus where a normal video generator is good enough.
 I’d especially love to hear from people creating short-form videos or product content: when would you rather modify an existing video than generate a new one from scratch?
