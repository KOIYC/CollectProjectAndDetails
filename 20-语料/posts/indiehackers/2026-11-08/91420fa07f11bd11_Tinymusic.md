 TinyMusic update: give a photo a soundtrack
New in TinyMusic: attach a photo in the chat and ask for music for it. The agent looks at the scene, colors and mood, turns that into a sound brief and lyrics, then generates the full song with vocals.
What I use it for:
 - A travel photo that needs a background track
 - Concept art for a game jam
 - A product shot where I want a 20-second teaser song
The image is only used as creative input for that request — you can also skip the photo and just describe the vibe.
Also shipped this week: password sign-in (Google account no longer required) and Dodo Payments at checkout alongside the existing payment options.
 TinyMusic is free to start, no credit card: https://tinymusic.ai

 Launching TinyMusic: turn a prompt or your lyrics into a full song
TinyMusic is live: https://tinymusic.ai
Most AI music generators hand you a short instrumental loop and call it a song. I wanted the full thing: vocals, a chorus, an ending. Something you could actually send to someone.
How it works: you type a prompt like "lo-fi study beat with rain" — or paste lyrics you already wrote — pick a style, and get a full song with vocals in a couple of minutes.
It's free to start, no credit card, and downloads are free to use commercially.
Why I built it: I kept meeting people with song ideas — a birthday roast, a soundtrack for a game jam, a lyric written years ago — who never hear them finished because studio time and music theory are out of reach.
I'm building TinyMusic solo as a side project. The next things I'm working on are better lyric control and longer tracks.
I'd love feedback, especially on how the vocals sound. Try it here: https://tinymusic.ai
