September 29, 2026
 Social Snitch is now open to everyone 🎉
I built it because I wanted to know when people were talking about my projects without checking a bunch of sites every day.
 Add a keyword, track mentions across sources like Hacker News, Reddit posts, Bluesky, etc. and get alerts in email, Discord, Slack, Telegram or a custom webhook.
 It’s free to start with three keywords. Give it a try, I’d love to hear what you think!
